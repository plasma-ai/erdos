**Source.** Conjecture 0.1, in the abstract on p. 475, of Peter Borwein,
Stephen Choi and Frank Chu, *An old conjecture of Erdős–Turán on additive
bases*, Mathematics of Computation 75 (2006), no. 253, 475--484, as
identified on the
[[additive_bases/borwein_et_al_2005_old_conjecture_erdos_turan_additive_bases/_index|source card]].

## Statement

**Conjecture 0.1** (p. 475; the paper attributes it to Erdős and Turán and
dates it 1941). Let $0=\delta_0<\delta_1<\delta_2<\cdots$ be an increasing
sequence of integers, $s(z)=\sum_{i\ge0}z^{\delta_i}$ and
$s(z)^2=\sum_{i\ge0}b_iz^i$. If $b_i>0$ for all $i$, then $(b_n)$ is
unbounded.

Here $b_n$ is the number of ordered pairs $(i,j)$ with
$\delta_i+\delta_j=n$. The introduction (p. 475) calls
$A=\{\delta_1<\delta_2<\cdots\}$ a basis of order two when every $b_i$ is
positive and rephrases the conjecture as: for any basis, the number of
representations is unbounded. The same statement reappears as Conjecture
2.3 (p. 477), and the paper states it equivalent to its versions 2.1 and 2.2
(pp. 476--477); see
[[additive_bases/borwein_et_al_2005_old_conjecture_erdos_turan_additive_bases/conjecture_2_1|Conjecture 2.1]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the statement was read clause by clause on
p. 475.

## Scope

A conjecture the paper records and does not prove or refute. Its proved
partial result is
[[additive_bases/borwein_et_al_2005_old_conjecture_erdos_turan_additive_bases/theorem_1_1|Theorem 1.1]],
that $\sup_ib_i\ge8$.

## Bears on

- [[../wiki/problems/additive_bases/E0028/_index|Problem 28]]: the
  conjecture assumes positivity of every $b_i$, the problem only of all but
  finitely many. The paper's finite-modification remark (p. 476), written
  out on the
  [[additive_bases/borwein_et_al_2005_old_conjecture_erdos_turan_additive_bases/conjecture_2_1|Conjecture 2.1]]
  page, makes the two equivalent. The paper reports that Erdős and Graham
  list the conjecture among the open problems (p. 476).
