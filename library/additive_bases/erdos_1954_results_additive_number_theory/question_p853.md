## Statement

**Question** (p. 853, unnumbered, quoted). "Does there exist a sequence
$b_1<b_2<\cdots$ satisfying $N(b_j,x)<c'_{10}x/\log x$ so that every
sufficiently large integer is of the form $2^l+b_j$?"

Here $N(b_j,x)$ counts the $b_j\le x$. The paper introduces the question as
seeming more difficult than its constructions for squares and $k$-th powers
on the same page (see
[[additive_bases/erdos_1954_results_additive_number_theory/theorem_p853|the remark on p. 853]]),
and does not answer it.

**Source.** P. Erdős, Some results on additive number theory, Proc. Amer.
Math. Soc. 5 (1954), 847-853, p. 853. The edition read is identified on the
[[additive_bases/erdos_1954_results_additive_number_theory/_index|source card]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the question was read clause by clause on the
printed page. Nothing here is independently reviewed.
