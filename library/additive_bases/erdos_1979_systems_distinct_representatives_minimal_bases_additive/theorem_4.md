## Statement

**Theorem 4** (p. 101). The sequence of squarefree numbers contains a minimal
asymptotic basis of order 2.

The introduction (p. 89) notes that the authors proved this in an earlier
paper (their reference [11]); here it is a corollary.

## Proof pointer

P. 101. A sieve argument from the literature (references [2], [14], [15])
gives at least $cn$ representations of $n$ as a sum of two squarefree
numbers for some $c>0$ and all $n\ge N_1$; then
[[additive_bases/erdos_1979_systems_distinct_representatives_minimal_bases_additive/theorem_2|Theorem 2]] applies.
