## Statement

**Theorem 5** (p. 101). Let $A$ consist of all numbers of the form $p$ or
$pq$, where $p$ and $q$ are odd primes. Then $A$ contains a minimal
asymptotic basis of order 2 for the set of positive even integers.

**Remark** (p. 101). If every sufficiently large even integer is a sum of two
primes in at least $cn/\log^2n$ ways, which the paper calls the strong form
of Goldbach's conjecture, then some subset of the primes is a minimal
asymptotic basis of order 2 for the even numbers. This is conditional.

## Proof pointer

P. 101. Chen's theorem (references [1], [16]) gives at least
$cn/\log^2n$ representations of $2n$ as a sum of two elements of $A$;
then [[additive_bases/erdos_1979_systems_distinct_representatives_minimal_bases_additive/theorem_1|Theorem 1]] applies with $U$ the positive even
integers.
