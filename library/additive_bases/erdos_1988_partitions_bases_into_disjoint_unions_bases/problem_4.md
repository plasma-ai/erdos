**Source.** Part 3 (Open problems), Problem 4, p. 8, of
P. Erdős and M. B. Nathanson,
"Partitions of bases into disjoint unions of bases," J. Number Theory 29 (1988),
no. 1, 1--9. The edition read is identified on the
[[additive_bases/erdos_1988_partitions_bases_into_disjoint_unions_bases/_index|source card]].

## Statement

Definition (p. 8). An asymptotic basis $A$ of order $h$ is *minimal* if no
proper subset of $A$ is an asymptotic basis of order $h$.

**Problem 4** (p. 8). The paper says it is not clear whether there is a
relationship between asymptotic bases that contain minimal asymptotic bases
and asymptotic bases that are disjoint unions of two asymptotic bases. As an
example it asks: let $A_1$ and $A_2$ be asymptotic bases of order 2 with
$A_1\cap A_2=\emptyset$, and let $A=A_1\cup A_2$. Does $A$ contain a minimal
asymptotic basis of order 2?

The paper gives no result on the question.
