## Statement

With $X(h)$ as defined on the [[additive_bases/plagne_2004_propos_de_la_fonction_d_erdos/theorem_1|Théorème 1]] page:

**Conjecture 2** (p. 1720). For every integer $h\ge2$,

$$
X(h)\le\frac{h(h+1)}{2}+1 .
$$

The paper's grounds (p. 1720): for $2\le h\le6$ the best known upper bound
on $X(h)$, and for $h=2,3$ its value, equals $h(h+1)/2+1$, and this
quantity appears in several places of the proof as the limit of the
method. It adds that if $X(4)$ is indeed $10$, as Li is reported to have
announced without publication (p. 1719), a proof of that would have to
break this bound for $h=4$.

## Scope

A conjecture the paper records and does not prove or refute. It holds for
$h=2,3$ by the known values, for $h=4$ by
[[additive_bases/plagne_2004_propos_de_la_fonction_d_erdos/theorem_1|Théorème 1]] ($X(4)\le11$) and for $h=5,6$ by
[[additive_bases/plagne_2004_propos_de_la_fonction_d_erdos/inequality_1_7|(1.7)]]. The paper says (p. 1763) that its
Conjecture 28, on Vosper-type subsets of $\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$, would
imply it.

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the statement and the sentences around it
were read on the page image of p. 1720.

**Source.** Alain Plagne, À propos de la fonction X d'Erdős et Graham,
Annales de l'Institut Fourier 54 (2004), no. 6, 1717--1767; the edition
read is named on the [[additive_bases/plagne_2004_propos_de_la_fonction_d_erdos/_index|source card]].
