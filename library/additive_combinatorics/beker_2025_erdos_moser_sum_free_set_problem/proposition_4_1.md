## Statement

A set $B$ is sum-free with respect to $A$ when $b_1+b_2\notin A$ for all
distinct $b_1,b_2\in B$ (p. 1).

**Proposition 4.1** (p. 17, quoted). "Let $k$ be a sufficiently large positive
integer and let $X\subseteq Y\subseteq\mathbb Z$ be such that
$|X|\ge\exp(k^{68+o(1)})$ and $|Y|\le(1+k^{-29})|X|$. Then there exists
$S\subseteq X$ of size $k$ which is sum-free with respect to $Y$."

The paper describes it as Proposition 2.7 of Sanders's paper with explicit
values for the implied constants (p. 17). The proof gives the size condition
in the form $|X|\ge k^{29}\exp(C'k^{68}(\log k)^{16})$ with $C'>0$ a
sufficiently large constant.

**Source.** A. Beker, *The Erdős--Moser sum-free set problem via improved bounds
for $k$-configurations*, arXiv:2501.10203v1 (17 January 2025; 23 pp.):
Section 4, p. 17. The edition read and the later version are identified on
the
[[additive_combinatorics/beker_2025_erdos_moser_sum_free_set_problem/_index|source card]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the statement and the deduction of Proposition
4.1 (p. 17) were read clause by clause on the printed page. Proposition 4.2,
whose proof the paper omits and attributes to the literature, and Theorem 3.1,
which the deduction applies, were not checked. Nothing here is independently
reviewed.

## Proof pointer

Page 17. If no such $S$ exists, Proposition 4.2 (a variant of Shao's
Proposition 6.2, with proof in Sudakov, Szemerédi and Vu's Section 6, omitted
in the paper) gives $X'\subseteq X$ with $|X'|\gg k^{-29}|X|$,
$|X'+X'|\ll k^{181}|X'|$ and $(2\cdot X')\cap Y=\varnothing$. The
Plünnecke--Ruzsa inequality gives $|2X'-2X'|\ll k^{724}|X'|$, and Ruzsa's
embedding lemma (Lemma 4.3) gives $X''\subseteq X'$ with $|X''|\ge|X'|/2$
Freiman 2-isomorphic to a subset of $\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z$ for an odd
$N\ll k^{724}|X'|$, so of density $\gg k^{-724}$ there. For $|X|$ large as
above,
[[additive_combinatorics/beker_2025_erdos_moser_sum_free_set_problem/theorem_3_1|Theorem 3.1]]
gives a non-degenerate $k$-configuration in the image; pulled back, it is
generated by distinct $x_1,\dots,x_k\in X''$ with every $x_i+x_j$ in
$2\cdot X''$, hence outside $Y$, and $S=\{x_1,\dots,x_k\}$ is sum-free with
respect to $Y$.

## Dependencies

Theorem 3.1 of the same paper; Proposition 4.2 and Lemma 4.3 (p. 17), both
taken from the literature (Shao; Sudakov, Szemerédi and Vu; Ruzsa); the
Plünnecke--Ruzsa inequality. The deduction of Theorem 1.2 from Proposition 4.1
is not written out: the paper says it is the same as Sanders's deduction of
his Theorem 1.2 from his Proposition 2.7.
