## Statement

**Definition** (p. 2). Let $G$ be an abelian group in which $x\mapsto2x$ is an
automorphism (for the paper, the rationals or a finite abelian group of odd
order, footnote 2), and let $k\ge2$ be an integer. A $k$-configuration is a
collection of $k$ elements $x_1,\dots,x_k\in G$ together with their pairwise
means $\frac{x_i+x_j}2$ for $1\le i<j\le k$; it is generated by
$x_1,\dots,x_k$, and it is non-degenerate when $x_1,\dots,x_k$ are pairwise
distinct. For $\alpha\in(0,\frac12]$ and $k\ge2$ the paper writes $F(\alpha,k)$
for the least positive integer $N$ such that every subset of
$[N]=\{1,\dots,N\}$ of density at least $\alpha$ contains a non-degenerate
$k$-configuration.

**Theorem 1.1** (p. 3, quoted). "There is an absolute constant $C>0$ such that
the following holds. Let $\alpha\in(0,1]$ and $k\ge2$. If
$N\ge\exp(Ck^{68}\log(2/\alpha)^{16})$, then any subset $A\subseteq[N]$ of
density at least $\alpha$ contains a non-degenerate $k$-configuration."

In the paper's notation this is
$F(\alpha,k)\le\exp(O(k^{68}\log(2/\alpha)^{16}))$. The paper (p. 3) compares
it with the earlier bounds it recounts on p. 2, the best general one being
Shao's $F(\alpha,k)\le\exp(\alpha^{-O(k^2)})$, and notes that the case $k=2$ is
the Kelley--Meka theorem on sets without three-term progressions. It also
notes that each dependence, taken separately, is optimal up to the exponent:
Behrend's construction gives $F(\alpha,2)\ge\exp(\Omega(\log(2/\alpha)^2))$,
and Green's bound on the clique number of random Cayley graphs gives
$F(\alpha_0,k)\ge\exp(\Omega(k))$ for some absolute constant
$\alpha_0\in(0,1)$.

**Source.** A. Beker, *The Erdős--Moser sum-free set problem via improved bounds
for $k$-configurations*, arXiv:2501.10203v1 (17 January 2025; 23 pp.): the
definition on p. 2, Theorem 1.1 and the comparison on p. 3, the deduction from
Theorem 3.1 on p. 8. The edition read and the later version are identified on
the
[[additive_combinatorics/beker_2025_erdos_moser_sum_free_set_problem/_index|source card]].

## Proof pointer

Page 8. Suppose $A\subseteq[N]$ has no non-degenerate $k$-configuration. Put
$N'=2N+1$ and let $\pi$ be the projection of $\mathbb Z$ onto
$\mathbb Z/N'\mathbb Z$, a cyclic group of odd order. The image $\pi(A)$ also
has none: a non-degenerate $k$-configuration in it would give distinct
$x_i\in A$ with $x_i+x_j\equiv2z\pmod{N'}$ for some $z\in A$, and since all
three lie in $[N]$ this congruence is an equality, so $z$ is the midpoint in
$A$. The density of $\pi(A)$ is at least $\alpha/3$, so the bound (4) of
[[additive_combinatorics/beker_2025_erdos_moser_sum_free_set_problem/theorem_3_1|Theorem 3.1]]
bounds $N'$, hence $N$, by $\exp(O(k^{68}\log(2/\alpha)^{16}))$, which a large
enough $C$ contradicts.
