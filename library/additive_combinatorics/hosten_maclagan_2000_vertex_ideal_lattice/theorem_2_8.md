**Source.** Theorem 2.8, p. 5, with Definition 2.5 (p. 4), Lemma 2.6
(pp. 4--5) and Corollary 2.7 (p. 5), of Serkan Hoşten and Diane Maclagan,
*The vertex ideal of a lattice*, arXiv:math/0012197v1 (2000), published in
Adv. in Appl. Math. 29 (2002), 521--538, as identified on the
[[additive_combinatorics/hosten_maclagan_2000_vertex_ideal_lattice/_index|source card]].
Page numbers are those of the arXiv print.

## Setting

Let $\mathcal L\subseteq\mathbf Z^n$ be a lattice with vertex ideal
$V_{\mathcal L}$
([[additive_combinatorics/hosten_maclagan_2000_vertex_ideal_lattice/proposition_2_1|Proposition 2.1]]).

- **Graver basis** (Definition 2.5, p. 4): for each sign pattern
  $\rho\in\{+,-\}^n$ with orthant $\mathbf R_\rho$, the monoid
  $\mathcal L\cap\mathbf R_\rho$ has a unique minimal generating set
  $H_\rho$, its Hilbert basis; the Graver basis $\operatorname{Gr}_{\mathcal
  L}$ is the union of all $H_\rho$.
- **Ordered Graver basis and positive circuits** (p. 5): write
  $\operatorname{Gr}_{\mathcal L}=\{\alpha_i-\beta_i\}$, listing each element
  and its negative ($\alpha-\beta\in\operatorname{Gr}_{\mathcal L}$ implies
  $\beta-\alpha\in\operatorname{Gr}_{\mathcal L}$). A positive circuit is a
  minimal positive linear dependency $\sum_{i\in\tau}c_i(\alpha_i-\beta_i)=0$,
  and for its support $\tau$ the paper sets
  $x^{m_\tau}=\operatorname{lcm}_{j\in\tau}x^{\alpha_j}$.

## Statement

**Lemma 2.6** (p. 4). If $\sum_ic_i(\alpha_i-\beta_i)=0$ with every $c_i>0$
and every $\alpha_i-\beta_i\in\operatorname{Gr}_{\mathcal L}$, where
$\alpha_i,\beta_i\in\mathbf N^n$ have disjoint supports, then
$\operatorname{lcm}_i(x^{\alpha_i})\in V_{\mathcal L}$.

**Corollary 2.7** (p. 5). Every minimal generator of $V_{\mathcal L}$ has the
form in Lemma 2.6.

**Theorem 2.8** (p. 5). With $\operatorname{Gr}_{\mathcal L}$ ordered as
above,

$$
V_{\mathcal L}=\left\langle x^{m_\tau}:\tau\text{ is the support of a positive circuit of }\operatorname{Gr}_{\mathcal L}\right\rangle .
$$

The paper notes (p. 5) that when $\mathcal L\cap\mathbf N^n=\{0\}$ the
ordered Graver basis may be replaced by the ordered universal Gröbner basis
of $\mathcal L$.

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the statement, Lemma 2.6, Corollary 2.7 and
Definition 2.5 were read clause by clause on pp. 4--5, with their proofs.

## Proof pointer

Pages 4--5. Lemma 2.6: if $v$ is the exponent of the lcm and a vertex of its
fiber, a functional $\omega$ maximized uniquely at $v$ would satisfy
$\omega\cdot(\alpha_i-\beta_i)>0$ for each $i$, contradicting the positive
dependency. Corollary 2.7: a minimal generator $x^u$ is a convex combination
of vertices $v_i$ of its fiber, and decomposing each $u-v_i$ conformally into
Graver elements gives a positive dependency whose lcm divides $x^u$. The
theorem combines the two.
