## Setting

Let $\mathcal L\subseteq\mathbf Z^n$ be a lattice of dimension $m$ with
vertex ideal $V_{\mathcal L}$, and let $B\in\mathbf Z^{n\times m}$ have
columns forming a lattice basis of $\mathcal L$ (p. 10).

- **Standard pairs** (Definition 3.4, p. 9): an admissible pair of a monomial
  ideal $M$ is $(x^u,\tau)$ with $\tau\subseteq[n]$,
  $\operatorname{supp}(u)\cap\tau=\emptyset$, and $x^{u+v}\notin M$ for all
  $v$ with $\operatorname{supp}(v)\subseteq\tau$; standard pairs are the
  maximal admissible pairs under the order $(x^u,\tau)\prec(x^v,\sigma)$ when
  $x^v\mid x^u$ and $\operatorname{supp}(u-v)\cup\tau\subseteq\sigma$.
- **Polyhedra** (p. 10): for $u\in\mathbf N^n$,
  $Q_u=\{x\in\mathbf R^m:Bx\le u\}$ and $R_u$ is the convex hull of
  $Q_u\cap\mathbf Z^m$; $z\mapsto u-Bz$ is a bijection from
  $Q_u\cap\mathbf Z^m$ to the lattice points of $P_u$, sending the origin to
  $u$. For $\tau\subseteq[n]$ with complement $\bar\tau$,
  $Q_u^{\bar\tau}=\{x\in\mathbf R^m:B^{\bar\tau}x\le u^{\bar\tau}\}$ omits the
  inequalities indexed by $\tau$, and $R_u^{\bar\tau}$ is the convex hull of
  its lattice points.
- **Critical** (Definition 3.7, p. 10): $Q_u$ is critical if the origin is a
  vertex of $R_u$ but not of $R_{u+e_i}$ for any $i$ (the print writes
  $i=1,\ldots,k$).

## Statement

**Theorem 3.6** (p. 10). The monomial $x^u$ is a standard monomial of
$V_{\mathcal L}$ if and only if the origin is a vertex of $R_u$. A pair
$(x^u,\tau)$ is a standard pair of $V_{\mathcal L}$ if and only if the origin
is a vertex of $R_u^{\bar\tau}$ and is not a vertex of
$R_u^{\bar\tau\setminus i}$ for any $i\in\bar\tau$.

**Theorem 3.8** (p. 11). The vertex ideal has the irreducible primary
decomposition

$$
V_{\mathcal L}=\bigcap_{Q_u^{\bar\tau}\ \text{critical}}\left\langle x_i^{u_i+1}:i\in\bar\tau\right\rangle .
$$

In the proof the critical $Q_u^{\bar\tau}$ correspond to the standard pairs
$(x^u,\tau)$ with $x^ux_i\in V_{\mathcal L}$ for all $i\in\bar\tau$; the text
before Definition 3.7 calls the decomposition irredundant (p. 10).

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the statements and the definitions they use
were read clause by clause on pp. 9--11, with the proofs.

## Proof pointer

Pages 10--11. The first statement of Theorem 3.6 follows from
[[additive_combinatorics/hosten_maclagan_2000_vertex_ideal_lattice/theorem_2_3|Theorem 2.3]]
and the bijection $z\mapsto u-Bz$; the second translates, through the same
bijection, the characterization of standard pairs in Proposition 3.5
(p. 10), which the paper says follows from the definition of standard pairs
(p. 9). Theorem 3.8 then applies the
decomposition of a monomial ideal by its standard pairs from the proof of
Lemma 3.3 of Sturmfels, Trung and Vogel.
