size of dissociated bases*, Electron. J. Combin. 18(1) (2011), #P117
(arXiv:1005.0155), as identified on the

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the statement and its two-line proof (p. 5)
were read clause by clause. Nothing here is independently reviewed.

## Statement

**Theorem 3** (p. 2). Let $A$ be a finite subset of an abelian group $G$ of
exponent $e:=\exp(G)$, and let $r$ be the rank of the subgroup $\langle
A\rangle$ generated by $A$. Then every maximal dissociated subset
$\Lambda\subseteq A$ satisfies

$$
r\le|\Lambda|\le r\log_2 e.
$$

The paper presents it as an improvement of
[[additive_combinatorics/lev_yuster_2010_size_dissociated_bases/theorem_2|Theorem 2]]
when the group has bounded exponent (p. 2).

## Proof pointer

Proof on p. 5. A maximal dissociated $\Lambda$ generates $\langle A\rangle$,
which gives the lower bound; its $2^{|\Lambda|}$ distinct subset sums lie in
$\langle A\rangle$, which has at most $e^r$ elements, which gives the upper
bound.
