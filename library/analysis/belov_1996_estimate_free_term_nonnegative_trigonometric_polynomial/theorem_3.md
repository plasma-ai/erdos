## Statement

Strict admissibility is defined on the [[analysis/belov_1996_estimate_free_term_nonnegative_trigonometric_polynomial/theorem_1|Theorem 1 page]].

**Theorem 3** (p. 629). Let $\{\xi_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ be a sequence of
independent random variables, each uniformly distributed on $(0,1)$. Then:

1. there is a constant $c>0$ such that if $q>1$ and $\lambda>cq^3$, the
   sequence $\{(n+\lambda-1)^q/\xi_n\}$ is strictly admissible with
   probability greater than $1/2$;
2. there is a constant $\nu_0>1$ such that for every $\nu\in(1,\nu_0]$ the
   sequence $\{\nu^{(n-1)^{1/3}}/\xi_n\}$ is strictly admissible with
   probability greater than $1/2$.

**Source.** A. S. Belov and S. V. Konyagin, *An estimate for the free term of a
nonnegative trigonometric polynomial with integer coefficients* (in Russian),
Mat. Zametki **59** (1996), no. 4, 627--629.
