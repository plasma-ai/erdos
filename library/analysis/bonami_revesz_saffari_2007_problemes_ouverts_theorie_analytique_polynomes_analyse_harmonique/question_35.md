## Statement

Setting (pp. 18--19). For an integer $n\ge2$, $T_n$ is the set of $n$-th roots
of unity, and $\psi(n)$ is the largest cardinality of a quasi-independent subset
of $T_n$ in the additive group $\mathbb C$ (quasi-independence as defined on p.
19; see
[[analysis/bonami_revesz_saffari_2007_problemes_ouverts_theorie_analytique_polynomes_analyse_harmonique/question_38|Question 38]]).
$\phi(n)$ is Euler's function, which the author recalls is the largest
cardinality of a subset of $T_n$ linearly independent over $\mathbb Q$ (pp.
18--19).

**Question 35** (p. 19, quoted). "La suite $(\frac{\psi(n)}{\phi(n)})_{n\geq1}$
est-elle bornée ?" That is: is the sequence $\psi(n)/\phi(n)$ bounded? The print
indexes the sequence from $n\ge1$, although $\psi(n)$ is defined for $n\ge2$.

The author notes (p. 20) that a positive answer would give negative answers to
[[analysis/bonami_revesz_saffari_2007_problemes_ouverts_theorie_analytique_polynomes_analyse_harmonique/question_38|Question 38]]
and
[[analysis/bonami_revesz_saffari_2007_problemes_ouverts_theorie_analytique_polynomes_analyse_harmonique/question_39|Question 39]].
The same contribution records
[[analysis/bonami_revesz_saffari_2007_problemes_ouverts_theorie_analytique_polynomes_analyse_harmonique/theorem_a|Theorem A]]
and Theorems B and C of Graham and Ramsey (pp. 20--21), and a remark attributed
to an informal discussion of Déchamps and Daniel Perrin ([DP]) that
$\limsup_{n\to\infty}(\psi(n)-\phi(n))=\infty$ (p. 21), which concerns the
difference, not the ratio.

**Source.** Myriam Déchamps, "Quelques questions ouvertes sur les racines de
l'unité et les ensembles de Sidon," pp. 18--21 of *Problèmes ouverts: Théorie
analytique des polynômes et analyse harmonique*, open problems of the Institut
Henri Poincaré working group organized by Aline Bonami, Szilárd Révész and
Bahman Saffari, 2006--2007; Question 35 on p. 19. The edition is identified on
the
[[analysis/bonami_revesz_saffari_2007_problemes_ouverts_theorie_analytique_polynomes_analyse_harmonique/_index|source card]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the question, the definitions and the remark on
p. 20 were read clause by clause on the printed pages. A question; the
collection proves nothing about it.

## Bears on

- [[../wiki/problems/integer_sequences/E0774/_index|Problem 774]]: only through
  Questions 38 and 39, the roots-of-unity analogue. If the set of all roots of
  unity is Sidon, the characterization recalled on p. 19 gives $\psi(n)\ge n/K$
  for one $K$ and all $n\ge2$, and the unboundedness of $n/\phi(n)$ recalled on
  p. 19 then makes $\psi(n)/\phi(n)$ unbounded; this is the remark of p. 20 in
  contrapositive form (an observation of this page). Question 35 says nothing
  about sets of natural numbers.
