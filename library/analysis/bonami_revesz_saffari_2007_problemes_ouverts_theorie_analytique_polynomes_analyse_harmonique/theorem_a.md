## Statement

Setting (pp. 18--19). Sets are subsets of the additive group $\mathbb C$.
*Independent* means linearly independent over $\mathbb Q$, in $\mathbb C$ viewed
as a vector space over $\mathbb Q$ (p. 19); an independent set is in particular
quasi-independent (see
[[analysis/bonami_revesz_saffari_2007_problemes_ouverts_theorie_analytique_polynomes_analyse_harmonique/question_38|Question 38]]).

**Théorème A** (p. 20, attributed to [GR]). Let $P$ be a set of primes and
$W$ the multiplicative subset of $\mathbb N$ generated by $P$, and put
$$
E=\{e^{2\pi ia/m}:a\in\mathbb Z,\ m\in W\}.
$$
Then $E$ is a finite union of independent sets if and only if
$\sum_{p\in P}1/p<\infty$. In that case $E$ is a Sidon set and is a union of
$$
M=\Bigl[\prod_{p\in P}\frac p{p-1}\Bigr]+1
$$
independent sets.

The print does not define the brackets in $M$; they are read here as the integer
part, as in the count $M_n=[n/\phi(n)]+1$ on p. 19.

**Source.** Myriam Déchamps, "Quelques questions ouvertes sur les racines de
l'unité et les ensembles de Sidon," pp. 18--21 of *Problèmes ouverts: Théorie
analytique des polynômes et analyse harmonique*, open problems of the Institut
Henri Poincaré working group organized by Aline Bonami, Szilárd Révész and
Bahman Saffari, 2006--2007; Théorème A on p. 20. The author attributes it to
[GR], L. Thomas Ramsey and Colin C. Graham, "Planar sidonicity and
quasi-independence for multiplicative subgroups of the roots of unity," Pacific
Journal of Mathematics 225, no. 2 (June 2006). The edition is identified on the
[[analysis/bonami_revesz_saffari_2007_problemes_ouverts_theorie_analytique_polynomes_analyse_harmonique/_index|source card]].
