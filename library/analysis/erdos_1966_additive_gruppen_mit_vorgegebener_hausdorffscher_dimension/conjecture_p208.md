## Statement

**Conjecture** (p. 208, quoted). "Folgende Verschärfung von Satz 1 ist zu
vermuten: Sei $S$ eine Borelmenge mit $\dim S = 1$, die zugleich additive
Gruppe ist. Dann gibt es für jedes $\alpha$ mit $0 \le \alpha \le 1$ eine
Untergruppe $S_\alpha$ mit $\dim S_\alpha = \alpha$."

In English: the authors conjecture, as a sharpening of
[[analysis/erdos_1966_additive_gruppen_mit_vorgegebener_hausdorffscher_dimension/satz_1|Satz 1]],
that if $S$ is a Borel set with $\dim S=1$ which is also an additive group,
then for every $\alpha$ with $0\le\alpha\le1$ there is a subgroup
$S_\alpha$ with $\dim S_\alpha=\alpha$. Here $\dim$ is Hausdorff dimension.

The Borel hypothesis separates the conjecture from
[[analysis/erdos_1966_additive_gruppen_mit_vorgegebener_hausdorffscher_dimension/satz_3|Satz 3]],
whose group $H$, built under the continuum hypothesis, has dimension 1 and
no subgroup of dimension strictly between 0 and 1; the paper does not say
whether $H$ is a Borel set.

## Proof pointer

None: the paper poses the statement and proves nothing toward it.
