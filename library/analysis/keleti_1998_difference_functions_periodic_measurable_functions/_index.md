# Difference functions of periodic measurable functions

[[analysis/_index|..]]

***

Tamás Keleti, *Difference functions of periodic measurable functions*,
*Fundamenta Mathematicae* **157** (1998), **15–32**.
[Published PDF](https://matwbn.icm.edu.pl/ksiazki/fm/fm157/fm15712.pdf).
[Retained PDF](keleti_1998_difference_functions_periodic_measurable_functions.pdf).
The retained PDF has 18 physical pages; its first and last printed pages
are 15 and 32. The file's text layer carries no copyright or license line; the
publisher's issue listing marks the article "Free download under CC-BY license",
as it marks every article in the issue, and names no Creative Commons version or
URL
(https://www.impan.pl/en/publishing-house/journals-and-series/fundamenta-mathematicae/all/157/1,
read 2026-10-02; the article's own page was not opened), so the term is the
Creative Commons Attribution license with its version unstated; the site footer
"Copyright © 2026 by IMPAN. All rights reserved." speaks for the site, not the
article.

Notation 2.1, printed p. 18 / PDF p. 4, defines $C^*$ as functions equal
almost everywhere to a continuous function. Corollary 2.18, printed p. 23 /
PDF p. 9, classifies the exact essentially-continuous-difference shift
subgroups for essentially bounded measurable periodic functions
($L_\infty$) not in $C^*$ as the finite subgroups of $\mathbb T$. Its
reverse-inclusion proof explicitly uses
$q_n(x)=\operatorname{sgn}(\sin(2\pi n x))$, with shift set
$\{0,1/n,\ldots,(n-1)/n\}$ modulo one.

Theorem 2.9, printed p. 21 / PDF p. 7, assumes $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$
measurable and every $\Delta_tf$ essentially continuous, and concludes $f$
essentially continuous. Theorem 2.13, printed p. 22 / PDF p. 8, gives the weak
difference property for $C^*$, using the definition on p. 21. No
mere-measurability-to-$C^*$ inference is made.
