# analysis/lewis_wu_1988_conjectures_arakelyan_littlewood

[[analysis/_index|..]]

[[analysis/lewis_wu_1988_conjectures_arakelyan_littlewood/littlewood_conjecture_bound|littlewood_conjecture_bound]]: Lewis and Wu's power-saving bound for the largest mean spherical
derivative phi(n) of a degree-n polynomial over the unit disk, as Hayman
and Lingham and Eremenko report it, phi(n) <= c n^{1/2 - 2^{-264}}; the
paper itself has not been read.

***

John L. Lewis and Jang-Mei Wu, *On conjectures of Arakelyan and Littlewood*.
Journal d'Analyse Mathématique 50 (1988), 259--283. doi:10.1007/BF02796126.
The Springer article page for DOI 10.1007/BF02796126 could not be read (it
redirected to a login endpoint), and the Crossref record names only Springer's
text-and-data-mining terms (http://www.springer.com/tdm) and no Creative
Commons license, every other right reserved.

The record of the paper rests on what later sources say of it. Update
4.18 of
[[polynomials/hayman_lingham_2018_research_problems_function_theory/_index|Hayman and Lingham's collection]]
(p. 77) says that Lewis and Wu proved Littlewood's conjecture
$\phi(N)=O(N^{1/2-\eta})$ for some $\eta>0$, for the mean spherical
derivative of a polynomial of degree $N$, by the method of their attack on
Problem 1.6 (Arakelyan's conjecture), and that the proof depends on harmonic
measure estimates of Bourgain.
[[polynomials/eremenko_1991_lower_estimate_littlewood_conjecture_mean_spherical_derivative_polynomial/_index|Eremenko 1991]]
(p. 713) says they obtained $\phi(n)\le cn^\alpha$ with
$\alpha=2^{-1}-2^{-264}$, improving the potential-theoretic method of
Eremenko and Sodin.

Read status: unread. The statement on the result page is taken from the two
secondary reports above and has not been checked against the paper.
