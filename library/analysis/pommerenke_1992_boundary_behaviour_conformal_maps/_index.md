# analysis/pommerenke_1992_boundary_behaviour_conformal_maps

[[analysis/_index|..]]

[[analysis/pommerenke_1992_boundary_behaviour_conformal_maps/capacity_diameter_continuum|capacity_diameter_continuum]]: The logarithmic capacity of a plane continuum E lies between a quarter and
a half of its diameter; the lower bound is attained by a segment and the
upper by a closed disk.

[[analysis/pommerenke_1992_boundary_behaviour_conformal_maps/koebe_distortion|koebe_distortion]]: The Koebe distortion theorem for a conformal map f of the unit disk:
|f(z) - f(0)| lies between |f'(0)||z|/(1+|z|)^2 and |f'(0)||z|/(1-|z|)^2,
and |f'(z)|/|f'(0)| between (1-|z|)/(1+|z|)^3 and (1+|z|)/(1-|z|)^3.

[[analysis/pommerenke_1992_boundary_behaviour_conformal_maps/koebe_one_quarter|koebe_one_quarter]]: For a conformal map f of the unit disk, the distance from f(z) to the
boundary of f(D) lies between (1/4)(1 - |z|^2)|f'(z)| and
(1 - |z|^2)|f'(z)|; in particular f(D) contains the disk of radius
|f'(0)|/4 about f(0).

[[analysis/pommerenke_1992_boundary_behaviour_conformal_maps/quasicircle_quasiconformal_extension|quasicircle_quasiconformal_extension]]: A Jordan curve J is a quasicircle, diam J(a,b) <= M|a - b| for all a, b on
J, exactly when it is the image of a circle under a quasiconformal map of
the plane (Ahlfors), and a conformal map of the disk onto the inner domain
of a quasicircle extends to a quasiconformal map of the plane.

[[analysis/pommerenke_1992_boundary_behaviour_conformal_maps/quasidisk_holder_continuity|quasidisk_holder_continuity]]: A conformal map of the unit disk onto the inner domain of a quasicircle
extends continuously to the closed disk and is Hölder continuous there,
with an exponent in (0, 1] depending only on the quasicircle constant.

***

Christian Pommerenke, *Boundary Behaviour of Conformal Maps*. Grundlehren
der mathematischen Wissenschaften 299, Springer-Verlag, Berlin, 1992,
ix+300 pp. doi:10.1007/978-3-662-02770-7. The publisher's book page
(https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-662-02770-7, read 2026-11-04)
shows "© 1992" with the copyright information "Springer-Verlag Berlin
Heidelberg 1992", and names no Creative Commons or open access license; every
other right reserved.

The book studies how a conformal map of the unit disk onto a simply
connected domain behaves at the boundary. Its chapters are: 1, Some Basic
Facts; 2, Continuity and Prime Ends; 3, Smoothness and Corners; 4,
Distortion; 5, Quasidisks; 6, Linear Measure; 7, Smirnov and Lavrentiev
Domains; 8, Integral Means; 9, Curve Families and Capacity; 10, Hausdorff
Measure; 11, Local Boundary Behaviour.

Read status: unread. The citation, DOI and chapter titles are
bibliographic metadata only. The result pages state classical results in
their standard form, each with the chapter it is attributed to; the
statements, their theorem numbers and those chapter attributions are
unread.
