***

**Source.** Erdős, Pomerance, and Sárközy (1987), paper III, introduction,
p. 1.

**Recalled statement.** The introduction says that the second paper in the
series proved, for all sufficiently large $x$,

$$
\#\{n\leq x:\varphi(n)=\varphi(n+1)\}
<\frac{x}{\exp\{(\log x)^{1/3}\}},
$$

and says that the same result holds for
$\sigma(n)=\sigma(n+1)$.

**Source-role guard.** This is a self-citation and contextual recall, not a
theorem proved in paper III. The direct statement is
[[arithmetic_functions/erdos_1987_locally_repeated_values_certain_arithmetic_functions_ii/theorem_2|Theorem 2
of paper II]], printed p. 253, whose proof is outlined in its Section 4 on
printed pp. 257--258.

**Relation to the problems.** The recalled bound bears on
[[../wiki/problems/arithmetic_functions/E1003/_index|Problem 1003]] but decides neither
infinitude nor finiteness. It supplies no block-distinctness statement for
Problem 1004.

**Bears on.** [[../wiki/problems/arithmetic_functions/E1003/_index|#1003]].

**Living verification.** Needs review. The recalled formula and attribution
were checked on p. 1 of paper III and against the direct paper-II statement.
No complete proof is supplied, reconstructed, or independently certified
here.
