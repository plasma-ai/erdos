Source: published paper, printed p. 381 (PDF p. 5),
Theorem 1.3. If a finite covering has distinct square-free integer moduli
$d_i>1$, at least one modulus is even. The proper-modulus condition excludes
the trivial one-class cover and is explicit in the separately cited source.

This paper states the result and refers its proof to the authors' separate
2021 article, *The Erdős–Selfridge problem with square-free moduli*.
That proof is recorded once, at
[[covering_systems/balister_2021_erdos_selfridge_problem_square_free_moduli/theorem_1_1|the square-free paper's Theorem 1.1]],
with its complete geometric and numerical dependency chain. This page is
an exact external statement and proof pointer, not another full proof.

In the
arXiv v1,
p. 4, the same assertion was labeled **Conjecture 1.3** and its proof was
announced for elsewhere. The published 2022 version labels it a theorem
and cites the separate published paper. The two PDFs are therefore not
presented as interchangeable versions.

**Bears on.** [[../wiki/problems/covering_systems/E0007/_index|Problem 7]], only for the
square-free case; arbitrary odd moduli remain outside this assertion.
