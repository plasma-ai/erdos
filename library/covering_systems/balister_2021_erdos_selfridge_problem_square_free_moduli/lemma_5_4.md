Source: published 2021 PDF, pp. 623–625, Lemma 5.4 and Tables 1–2.

## Statement

Let $Q_5=[2]\times[4]\times[6]\times[10]$, with coordinates indexed by
$2,3,4,5$. Let $\mathcal A_5$ be any family of hyperplanes in $Q_5$ with
pairwise distinct fixed sets, each of size at least two. There is a probability
$P_5$ supported outside their union such that

$$
c_5(3)-\frac34c_5(1)<9.018071<9.019,
\qquad
c_5(z)=\sum_{I\subseteq\{2,3,4,5\}}c(I)z^{|I|},
$$

where $c(I)$ is the largest $P_5$-mass of a hyperplane with fixed set $I$.
In particular, the uncovered set is nonempty. This is the stronger numerical
conclusion reached in the source's proof of the stated $9.019$ bound.

## Full proof with its finite certificate

### Reduction to the enumerated families

We may enlarge the union so that there is exactly one hyperplane $A_F$ for
every fixed set $F$ of size at least two, and none is contained in another.
Here is an explicit justification for imposing both conditions at once.
Process $F$ in increasing binary-mask order. If its existing plane is absent
or is contained in an earlier plane, replace it by one not contained in any
earlier plane. A contained plane contributes nothing beyond the earlier
union, so this operation never decreases the union.

Such a replacement always exists. Only earlier fixed sets $G\subsetneq F$
can contain $A_F$. On the $F$-coordinate box, their total uniform density is
at most the sum of $\prod_{i\in G}|S_i|^{-1}$ over proper $G$ of size at least
two. For $|F|=2$ the sum is zero; for $|F|=3$ it is at most
$1/8+1/12+1/24=1/4$; for $|F|=4$ it is

$$
\prod_{q\in\{2,4,6,10\}}(1+q^{-1})-1
 -\sum_{q\in\{2,4,6,10\}}q^{-1}-\frac1{480}
 =\frac{31}{80}<1.
$$

Choose a point outside those earlier projected planes as the fixed values
of $A_F$. At the end there is one plane for every required fixed set and no
containment. A measure avoiding this enlarged family also avoids the
original one.

In each coordinate, relabel values by the order of their first appearance
among the planes. If $m_i$ is the largest label already used, the next fixed
value in coordinate $i$ is at most $m_i+1$, and at most $|S_i|$. Thus every
family is isomorphic, by coordinate permutations, to one generated by the
following rule: process masks

$$
3,5,6,7,9,10,11,12,13,14,15
$$

on coordinates $2,3,4,5$; try every allowed value from $1$ through
$\min\{|S_i|,m_i+1\}$ at its fixed coordinates; and discard a plane if it is
contained in an earlier one. This enumerates representatives, not necessarily
one representative per isomorphism class; repetitions do not affect coverage.

### Certifying a partial configuration

For a partial family, let $R$ be its uncovered set and let nonnegative integer
weights $w_r$, not all zero, be supported on $R$. Put $W=\sum_r w_r$ and

$$
M_I=\max_{H:F(H)=I}\sum_{r\in H}w_r,
\qquad M_\varnothing=W.
$$

Then $P(r)=w_r/W$ is an exact probability and

$$
C:=c_5(3)-\frac34c_5(1)
 =\frac1{4W}\sum_I(4\cdot3^{|I|}-3)M_I.
$$

Let $\mathcal U$ be the fixed sets not yet assigned. Whatever hyperplanes
with those fixed sets are later chosen, their union has $P$-mass
$p\le u:=\sum_{I\in\mathcal U}M_I/W$. If $u<1$, delete this union and
renormalize. Deleting mass $p$ cannot increase any hyperplane maximum and
reduces $c(\varnothing)$ by exactly $p$. Hence the new figure of merit is
at most $(C-p/4)/(1-p)$. This is increasing in $p$ because $C\ge1/4$.
Thus every completion is certified by

$$
\frac{C-u/4}{1-u}
 =\frac{\sum_I(4\cdot3^{|I|}-3)M_I-U}{4(W-U)},
\qquad U=\sum_{I\in\mathcal U}M_I.
$$

This gives a proof for all completions of a partial configuration at once.
The published paragraph after (21) says renormalization increases mass by a
factor $1-p$; the multiplier is $1/(1-p)$, as its displayed formula (21)
correctly uses. The argument above uses that reciprocal factor.

### Exhaustive exact verification

The [retained certificate](initial_measures.json.gz) gives integer primal
weights at every leaf of a finite tree generated by the preceding rules.
After the first seven masks, try the displayed bound. If it is not yet
certified, extend by every allowable plane of the next mask and repeat.
The [standard-library checker](evidence/verify_bbmst_squarefree.py)
reconstructs the entire tree. At every leaf it expands all 480 point weights,
checks nonnegativity and positive total mass, verifies zero mass on the
specified planes, computes every $M_I$ by enumerating its hyperplanes, and
checks

$$
W>U,\qquad
10^6\left(\sum_I(4\cdot3^{|I|}-3)M_I-U\right)
 <9018071\cdot4(W-U).
$$

These are integer inequalities. A missing full-depth certificate is a
failure, and certificates outside the generated tree are rejected.

Weights are stored per orbit of unused coordinate values to save space. For
a point $r$, its orbit label is
$(\min\{r_i,m_i+1\})_{i=2}^5$. The stored list is indexed by the sorted orbit
labels of uncovered points. Each listed weight is a weight **per point**;
all orbit multiplicities are included when computing $W$ and $M_I$.
This compression imposes no unverified symmetry inference on the checker.

The checker reports:

| Unspecified final planes | Nodes checked | Nodes requiring another plane |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 4 | 7637 | 90 |
| 3 | 1083 | 12 |
| 2 | 312 | 3 |
| 1 | 216 | 2 |
| 0 | 142 | 0 |

There are 9283 certified leaves. The largest exact bound is

$$
\frac{18036140768809}{1999999999966}<9.018071.
$$

Exactly the two full configurations in the published Table 2 have certified
values at least $9.018$. In the source's notation they are

$$
11**,\ 2*1*,\ *22*,\ 121*,\ 1**1,\ *3*2,\ 13*3,
\ **34,\ 2*31,\ *232,\ 1233,
$$

and the same list with $2*31$ replaced by $2*33$.
Every branch either ends at a certificate valid for all remaining choices or
is expanded exhaustively. The reduction above therefore supplies the desired
measure for every original family.

The authors obtained proposed weights using Gurobi. The retained weights were
independently generated with SciPy/HiGHS and rounded to integers, then checked
as just described. Their validity does not depend on optimality, solver
correctness, or floating-point tolerances. The source's illustrative count
of 6025640717 fully specified representatives is not needed or separately
replayed; the exhaustive pruned tree proves the required universal assertion.
