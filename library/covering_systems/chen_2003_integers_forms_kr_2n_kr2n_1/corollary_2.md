**Source.** Corollary 2, p. 312, with its proof on p. 318, of Yong-Gao Chen,
*On integers of the forms $k^r-2^n$ and $k^r2^n+1$*, Journal of Number
Theory 98 (2003), no. 2, 310--319, as identified on the
[[covering_systems/chen_2003_integers_forms_kr_2n_kr2n_1/_index|source card]].

## Statement

**Corollary 2** (p. 312). Let $r$ be a positive odd integer with $3\nmid r$.

- *(i)* There are infinitely many primes $p$ such that $p^{2r}-2^n$ has at
  least two distinct prime factors for every positive integer $n$.
- *(ii)* There are infinitely many primes $p$ such that $p^{2r}2^n+1$ has at
  least two distinct prime factors for every positive integer $n$.

With $r=1$, part (i) answers the second question of the Introduction
(p. 311), on primes $p$ with every $p^2-2^n$ composite. As in
[[covering_systems/chen_2003_integers_forms_kr_2n_kr2n_1/theorem_2|Theorem 2]],
$n$ ranges over the positive integers and the prime factors in part (i) are
those of the absolute value.

## Proof pointer

P. 318: the paper says the proof is similar to that of
[[covering_systems/chen_2003_integers_forms_kr_2n_kr2n_1/corollary_1|Corollary 1]],
which applies Dirichlet's theorem to the progression built in the proof of
the theorem. Part (ii) rests on the construction for Theorem 2(ii), whose
printed conditions do not, read literally, give the needed divisibility;
see the note on the Theorem 2 page.
