***

**Source.** Conjecture 1, printed p. 390, also announced on p. 382
([PDF pp. 10 and 2](de_la_breteche_2013_non_intersecting_arithmetic_progressions.pdf#page=10)).

For $f(x)$ and $T=\sqrt{\log x\log\log x}$ as in
[[covering_systems/de_la_breteche_2013_non_intersecting_arithmetic_progressions/external_inputs|the definitions]],
the source conjectures

$$
f(x)=x\exp(-(1+o(1))T)\qquad(x\to\infty).
$$

The [[covering_systems/de_la_breteche_2013_non_intersecting_arithmetic_progressions/lower_bound|lower half]]
is proved unconditionally in that paper.
[[covering_systems/de_la_breteche_2013_non_intersecting_arithmetic_progressions/theorem_2|Theorem 2]]
proves the upper half under its separate intersecting-family
Conjecture 2. The unconditional upper coefficient in
[[covering_systems/de_la_breteche_2013_non_intersecting_arithmetic_progressions/theorem_1|Theorem 1]]
is $\sqrt3/2$.

This page preserves the claim's status *within the 2013 source*. It
does not assert that it remains conjectural in the current literature;
later solution and formalization evidence is separate from this
original-source compilation.

**Bears on.** [[../wiki/problems/covering_systems/E0202/_index|Problem 202]], and its
reciprocal-density counterpart [[../wiki/problems/covering_systems/E1190/_index|Problem 1190]].
