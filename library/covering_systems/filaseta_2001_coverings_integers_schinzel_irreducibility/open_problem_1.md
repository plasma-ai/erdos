**Source.** Open Problem 1, printed p. 1 (PDF p. 2) of the 22 May 2001 author manuscript.

## Statement

For every $c>0$, does there exist a finite covering of the integers with
distinct moduli and minimum modulus at least $c$?

**Bears on.** This is the question of
[[../wiki/problems/covering_systems/E0002/_index|Problem 2]] in its corrected form
(distinct moduli), asked for every bound $c$. The question is transcribed here
in its 2001 form; the manuscript answers no part of it, and it notes (p. 1)
Morikawa's announced covering with $c=24$.
