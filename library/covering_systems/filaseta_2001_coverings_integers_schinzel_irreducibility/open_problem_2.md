**Source.** Open Problem 2, printed p. 1 (PDF p. 2) of the 22 May 2001 author manuscript.

## Statement

Does there exist a finite covering of the integers consisting of distinct odd
moduli greater than $1$?

The source calls such a system an *odd covering* (p. 1).

**Bears on.** This is exactly
[[../wiki/problems/covering_systems/E0007/_index|Problem 7]]. The source's 2001 description
of it as open is historical and is not a fresh status review.
