**Source.** Theorem 1, printed p. 4 (PDF p. 5) of the 22 May 2001 author manuscript.

## Statement

For each positive integer $d$ with $4\mid d$, some polynomial

$$
f(x)\in\mathbb Z^+[x]
$$

makes $f(x)x^n+d$ reducible for every integer $n\geq0$; reducibility is
over the rationals, as the paper's problem is posed (p. 1). Here the source
uses $\mathbb Z^+[x]$ for polynomials with positive
integral coefficients.

The positive-coefficient condition replaces the usual nontriviality condition
$f(1)\neq-d$ in the source's formulation.
