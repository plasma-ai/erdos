## Statement

Let

$$
\mathcal A=\{a_s(n_s)\}_{s=1}^k
$$

be an odd covering system with

$$
1<n_1<\cdots<n_k.
$$

If its least common multiple

$$
N_{\mathcal A}=[n_1,\ldots,n_k]
$$

is square-free, then $N_{\mathcal A}$ has at least $22$ distinct prime
divisors.

**Proof pointer.** The proof begins in Section 2 on PDF p. 3. It was not
reconstructed or independently checked here.

**Bears on.** This is a necessary condition in the square-free special case
of [[../wiki/problems/covering_systems/E0007/_index|Problem 7]]. It neither constructs an
odd covering nor excludes all square-free odd coverings by itself.

The later
[[covering_systems/balister_2021_erdos_selfridge_problem_square_free_moduli/theorem_1_1|square-free exclusion]]
proves that every finite distinct cover by square-free moduli greater than one
has an even modulus, and therefore supersedes this necessary condition within
the square-free case. Its canonical page records a complete reviewed proof and
finite-certificate chain; that proof is linked here rather than re-reviewed.
