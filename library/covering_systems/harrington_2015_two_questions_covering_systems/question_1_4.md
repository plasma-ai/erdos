**Source.** Question 1.4, printed p. 1740, physical PDF p. 2.

## Statement

Harrington asks (p. 1740): "What is the largest positive integer $N$ such that
every integer satisfies at least $N$ congruences of a covering system with
distinct moduli greater than 1?"

The source takes Sun's definition (Definition 1.5): a system in which every
integer satisfies at least $m$ congruences is an $m$-covering; that definition
by itself does not require distinct moduli.

**Source result.** The paper's
[[covering_systems/harrington_2015_two_questions_covering_systems/section_4_construction|Section 4 construction]]
shows $N\geq3$. It does not determine the largest $N$.

**Bears on.** This question is motivated by the bounded minimum modulus in
[[../wiki/problems/covering_systems/E0002/_index|Problem 2]], but it is a different extremal
parameter.
