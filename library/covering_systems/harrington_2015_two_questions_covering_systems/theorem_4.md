***

**Source.** Theorem 4, printed p. 1747, physical PDF p. 9. Harrington
explicitly attributes this theorem to Chen; this page records Harrington's
restatement rather than assigning a new proof to Harrington.

## Conventions

For a positive integer $r$, define

$$
Y_r=\left\{k>0:2\nmid k,\ k-2^n\text{ has at least }r
\text{ distinct prime factors for every positive integer }n\right\},
$$

and

$$
G_r=\left\{k>0:2\nmid k,\ k2^n+1\text{ has at least }r
\text{ distinct prime factors for every positive integer }n\right\}.
$$

## Statement

"If there exists a $(2,1)$-primitive $m$-covering system, then the sets
$Y_{m+1}$ and $G_{m+1}$ each contain an infinite arithmetic progression."
(p. 1747)

Harrington combines this restated implication with
[[covering_systems/harrington_2015_two_questions_covering_systems/theorem_2|Theorem 2]]
to obtain Corollary 1: $Y_4$ and $G_4$ each contain an infinite arithmetic
progression.

**Proof scope.** Harrington's paper states this as a result proved by Chen and
does not reproduce its proof at this point. Chen's original paper was not
checked for this card.
