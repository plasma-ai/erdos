**Source.** Proposition 2, PDF p. 2 of arXiv:math/0604347v2.

## Statement

For two congruence classes $a_i\pmod{m_i}$ and $a_j\pmod{m_j}$, no integer
lies in both exactly when

$$
\gcd(m_i,m_j)\nmid a_i-a_j.
$$

**Proof scope.** The paper introduces the criterion as the one underlying its
pigeonhole argument for $k=3$ on pp. 1--2. No separate proof accompanies the numbered
statement, and no independent proof review is claimed here.

**Bears on.** [[../wiki/problems/covering_systems/E0202/_index|Problem 202]]:
the criterion decides pairwise disjointness, the condition on the families
that problem counts. The paper does not apply it to that problem.
