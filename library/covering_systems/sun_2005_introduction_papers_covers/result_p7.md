***

**Source.** Item 8 on p. 7 of the author survey, an unnumbered summary. The
survey attributes both statements to Zhi-Wei Sun, *On covering systems with
distinct moduli*, J. Yangzhou Teachers College (Nat. Sci. Ed.) **11** (1991),
no. 3, 21--27.

## Conventions

A cover is a finite system $\{a_s(n_s)\}_{s=1}^k$ of residue classes
$a_s(n_s)=a_s+n_s\mathbb Z$ whose union is $\mathbb Z$. It is minimal
(irredundant) when no proper subsystem is a cover.

## Statement

1. The survey reports that the following two questions are equivalent.
   The question of V. Billik and H. M. Edgar (1973) asks whether, for every
   $d\in\mathbb Z^+$, there is a minimal cover whose moduli are all distinct
   and have greatest common divisor $d$. The question of Erdős asks whether,
   for every $c>0$, there is a cover whose moduli are all distinct and all
   greater than $c$.

2. Let $\{a_s(n_s)\}_{s=1}^k$ be a cover with $n_1<\cdots<n_k$, and suppose
   that no cover has all its moduli distinct and greater than $n_1$. Then
   $3n_1$ or $4n_1$ divides some $n_s$.

**Proof scope.** The survey states both results without proof; it credits
them to the 1991 paper, which is their proof source.
