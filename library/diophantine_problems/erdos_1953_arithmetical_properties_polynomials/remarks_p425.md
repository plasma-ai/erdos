## Statement

All on p. 425, in the setting of the
[[diophantine_problems/erdos_1953_arithmetical_properties_polynomials/theorem|Theorem]]
($f(x)$ of degree $l$, integer coefficients with highest common factor $1$,
positive leading coefficient). None of these remarks is proved in the paper.

1. Sums. Citing Estermann (Math. Annalen 105 (1931), 653--662) for every
   sufficiently large positive integer being a square plus a squarefree
   integer, the paper says similar methods prove that every sufficiently large
   positive integer is an $l$-th power plus an $l$-th power free integer, and
   that its own methods would doubtless give an $l$-th power plus an
   $(l-1)$-th power free integer.
2. Primes. The paper says one can prove that $f(p)$ is $l$-th power free for
   infinitely many primes $p$, provided $f(x)$ is not the $l$-th power of a
   linear polynomial. It calls it reasonable to conjecture that $f(p)$ is
   $(l-1)$-th power free for infinitely many primes $p$ when $f(x)$ satisfies
   the conditions of §1, and says the methods of the paper do not seem strong
   enough to prove this.
3. The quartic (quoted). "I have also not been able to prove, for example,
   that $n^4+2$ is squarefree for infinitely many $n$."
