## Statement

Notation as on
[[diophantine_problems/erdos_1976_products_factorials/fact_1|the page of the sets F_k and D_k]]:
$D_6(n)$ is the number of $n'\le n$ in $D_6=F_6-F_5$.

**Conjecture** (p. 354, quoted). "We are reasonably certain that
$D_6(n)>cn$."

Here $c$ is, by the paper's convention (p. 338), a suitably chosen
positive constant. The statement follows
[[diophantine_problems/erdos_1976_products_factorials/fact_14|Fact 14]] at
the end of the section on six factors, after the remark (p. 353) that it
seems certain that almost all products of two primes are not in $F_5$.

## Proof pointer

None: the paper states the conjecture without proof.
