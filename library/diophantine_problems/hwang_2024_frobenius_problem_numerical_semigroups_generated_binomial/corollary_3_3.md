## Statement

Notation as on the page for
[[diophantine_problems/hwang_2024_frobenius_problem_numerical_semigroups_generated_binomial/theorem_0_1|Theorem 0.1]].
The genus $g(S)$ is the number of nonnegative integers not in $S$; an
integer $x\notin S$ is a pseudo-Frobenius number of $S$ when $x+s\in S$
for every $s\in S\setminus\{0\}$; $PF(S)$ is the set of them and the type
$t(S)$ is its size (Definitions 1.7 and 1.9, p. 4).

**Corollary 3.3** (p. 7). Let $n=p_1^{k_1}\cdots p_t^{k_t}$ with primes
$p_1<\cdots<p_t$ and $t\ge2$. Then

(a) $\mathrm{Ap}(S(B_n),n)=\Bigl\{\sum_{i=1}^{t}\sum_{j=1}^{k_i}c_{i,j}\binom{n}{p_i^{\,j}}\ :\ c_{i,j}\in\mathbb Z,\ 0\le c_{i,j}\le p_i-1\Bigr\}$;

(b) $F(S(B_n))=\sum_{i=1}^{t}\sum_{j=1}^{k_i}(p_i-1)\binom{n}{p_i^{\,j}}-n$;

(c) $g(S(B_n))=\sum_{i=1}^{t}\sum_{j=1}^{k_i}\frac{p_i-1}{2}\binom{n}{p_i^{\,j}}-\frac{n-1}{2}$;

(d) $PF(S(B_n))=\{F(S(B_n))\}$, the single value in (b), and
$t(S(B_n))=1$.

Parts (a) and (b) are parts (1)(a) and (1)(b) of Theorem 0.1. The print
again calls the $k_i$ "nonnegative integers"; the statement is read with
every $k_i\ge1$.

**Corollaries 3.4 and 3.5** (p. 8). For $n=p_1^{k_1}\cdots p_t^{k_t}$ the
semigroup $S(B_n)$ is telescopic and a complete intersection; the paper
derives both from results of D'Anna, Micale and Sammartano (its reference
[2]), computing the relevant invariants as $\tau_i=p_i-1$.

## Proof pointer

Pp. 7-8. The paper omits the proof of (a) "because it can be obtained
directly", from the generators of
[[diophantine_problems/hwang_2024_frobenius_problem_numerical_semigroups_generated_binomial/theorem_3_2|Theorem 3.2]]
and the residues of
[[diophantine_problems/hwang_2024_frobenius_problem_numerical_semigroups_generated_binomial/lemma_2_2|Lemma 2.2]].
Parts (b) and (c) follow from (a) and the Apéry-set formulas
$F(S)=\max\mathrm{Ap}(S,x)-x$ and
$g(S)=\frac1x\sum_{w\in\mathrm{Ap}(S,x)}w-\frac{x-1}2$ (Lemma 1.8, p. 4);
(d) from the observation that the largest element of the Apéry set is its
only maximal element for the order $a\le_S b\iff b-a\in S$.
