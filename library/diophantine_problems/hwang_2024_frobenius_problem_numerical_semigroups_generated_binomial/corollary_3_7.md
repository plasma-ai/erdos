## Statement

Notation as on the pages for
[[diophantine_problems/hwang_2024_frobenius_problem_numerical_semigroups_generated_binomial/theorem_0_1|Theorem 0.1]]
and
[[diophantine_problems/hwang_2024_frobenius_problem_numerical_semigroups_generated_binomial/corollary_3_3|Corollary 3.3]].
For $n=p^m$ the semigroup $S(B_n)$ is generated by
$\frac1p\binom n1,\ldots,\frac1p\binom n{n-1}$.

**Theorem 3.6** (p. 8). Let $n=p^m$ with $p$ prime and $m\ge1$ an integer.
Then $S(B_n)$ is generated by
$\frac1p\binom n1,\frac1p\binom np,\frac1p\binom n{p^2},\ldots,\frac1p\binom n{p^{m-1}}$,
and its embedding dimension is $e(S(B_n))=m$.

**Corollary 3.7** (p. 9). Let $n=p^m$ with $p$ prime and $m\ge1$ an
integer. Then

(a) $\mathrm{Ap}(S(B_n),p^{m-1})=\Bigl\{\frac1p\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}c_i\binom{n}{p^i}\ :\ c_i\in\mathbb Z,\ 0\le c_i\le p-1\Bigr\}$;

(b) $F(S(B_n))=\frac{p-1}{p}\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}\binom{p^m}{p^i}-p^{m-1}$;

(c) $g(S(B_n))=\frac{p-1}{2p}\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}\binom{p^m}{p^i}-\frac{p^{m-1}-1}{2}$;

(d) $PF(S(B_n))=\{F(S(B_n))\}$, the single value in (b), and
$t(S(B_n))=1$.

Parts (a) and (b) are parts (2)(a) and (2)(b) of Theorem 0.1, which the
paper's introduction (p. 3) cites as Theorem 3.7.

**Corollary 3.8** (p. 9). For $n=p^m$ the semigroup $S(B_n)$ is
telescopic, and hence a complete intersection.

**Remark 3.9** (p. 9). From Corollaries 3.3 and 3.7 the paper concludes
that $S(B_n)$ is telescopic of type 1 for every $n\ge1$, hence symmetric,
so that $g(S(B_n))=(F(S(B_n))+1)/2$, and reports checking this equality
against the values computed from the Apéry set for each $n\ge1$.
