## Statement

**Theorem 7** (p. 458). There is an infinite set of positive integers
$k_1,k_2,k_3,\ldots$ with

$$
1\le k_1<k_2<k_3<\cdots
$$

such that the number of representations of $k_\nu$ in the form

$$
k_\nu=pq(p+q)
$$

with positive integers $p,q$ is greater than $\sqrt[4]{\log k_\nu}$.

$G$ in

factors, and an angle with $0<A<B$, and change signs and take $\gamma<1$ as for
