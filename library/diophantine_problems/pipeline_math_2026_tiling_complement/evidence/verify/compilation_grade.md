### Mathematics

7. **OK: Lemma 1.4.** The gcd/conjugate base-point argument,
   $h(p_1^{13}:\cdots:p_r^{13})=13e$, support from equal-degree ratios,
   normalized nonconstancy and $|\Sigma|\ge2$ all hold. All four
   active-coordinate counts, the two-vanishing-pair classification and
   $[F:R]$ surjectivity check out. Both
   $13e\le3(4e-2)$ and $13e\le3e-2$ are impossible for $e\ge1$.
8. **OK: Corollary 1.5.** Coprimality forces denominator $q=1$;
   oddness transfers non-power status to $-c$. Thus $N=-c$ is nonzero
   and not a rational thirteenth power. The affine-to-projective
   extension is exactly the one supplied in Lemma 1.4.
9. **OK: Proposition 1.6 witness bound.** $Q$ is strictly positive on the
   compact max-norm unit sphere; the origin, where $Q=0$, is excluded.
   Homogeneity and $|u-v|\ge1$ give
   $\kappa\max(|u|,|v|)^{12}\le(1+|c|)T^{13}$, and $C_c\ge1$
   ensures the same $X$ also bounds $|t|$.
10. **OK: fixed-shift shell repair.** The shells $(R_{j+1},R_j]$ are
    disjoint and cover $(R_{J+1},X]$. The geometric sum contributes
    $K_F/(1-2^{-10/13})$ times $X^{10/13}$. The residual box has at
    most $(2\lceil R_c\rceil+1)^3$ points, absorbed because
    $X^{10/13}\ge1$. This proves the stated fixed-$c$ estimate.
11. **OK: stronger restatement not endorsed.** Journal PDF p. 2 defines
    the shell count on $B/2<\max|x_i|\le B$, whereas v1 PDF p. 1
    defines a whole-box count. Manuscript PDF p. 2, Theorem 1.3,
    states the whole-box form with an $F$-only constant while citing
    the journal. The shell theorem does not supply that constant, because
    the residual box scales with $|N|$. The report says the journal
    does not supply it, not that the stronger assertion is false.
12. **OK: exact external interfaces.** Corvaja-Zannier p. 438 gives the
    recalled three-term bound $H(1:u:v)\le\chi$ when $z=0$ in (1.1)
    and $u,v$ are not both constant, and the four-term consequence
    under no vanishing subsum on the right, with no printed
    nonconstancy clause. Heath-Brown journal p. 1580 agrees with the
    native shell statement. Adding projective nonconstancy to the native

    says natural number both strengthen the assumed hypotheses and are
    safe for this application.
