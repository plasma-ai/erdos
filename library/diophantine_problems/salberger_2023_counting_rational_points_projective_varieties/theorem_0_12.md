## Statement

**Theorem 0.12** (p. 1096, quoted). "Let $X\subset\mathbf P^{r+1}$ be a
geometrically integral projective hypersurface of degree $d$ over $\mathbf Q$.
Then there exists for $B\geq1$ a hypersurface $Y$ of degree
$O_{d,r,\varepsilon}(B^{(r+1)/rd^{1/r}+\varepsilon})$, not containing $X$
such that all rational points of height $\leq B$ on $X$ lie on $Y$."

The exponent $(r+1)/rd^{1/r}$ means $(r+1)/(r\,d^{1/r})$. Heath-Brown's
local determinant method (recalled as Theorem 0.10, p. 1095) covers the same
points by $O_{d,r,\varepsilon}(B^{(r+1)/d^{1/r}+\varepsilon})$ hypersurfaces
of degree $O_{d,\varepsilon}(1)$; Theorem 0.12 uses one hypersurface of larger
degree instead. The paper says (p. 1096) that the result was first presented
in a talk in 2002.
