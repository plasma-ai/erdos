## Statement

Normed triangles and monochromatic triangles are as in the setting of the
[[discrete_geometry/aichholzer_2019_triangles_colored_euclidean_plane/lemma_2_2|Lemma 2.2 page]].

The coloring (Figure 6, p. 10:6) tiles the plane by regular hexagons of
diameter 1 in 4 colors, in the pattern the figure shows; the three lower sides
and the two lower vertices of each hexagon take its color.

**Theorem 3.3** (p. 10:6, quoted). "In the 4-coloring in Figure 6, all normed
triangles with $\overline{AC} \le \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ are non-monochromatic."

With the zebra colorings of Theorems 3.1 and 3.2, the paper charts for each
position of $C$ the least $k$, $3\le k\le6$, for which its colorings make the
triangle non-monochromatic (Figure 7, p. 10:7); three colors suffice for
triangles whose sides do not differ too much (p. 10:6).
