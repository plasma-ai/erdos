## Statement

**Theorem 5.4** (p. 9). Let $h_1,\ldots,h_n$ be positive integers and
$G$ the set of points $(y_1,\ldots,y_n)$ with $y_i\in\mathbb Z$ and
$0\le y_i\le h_i$. A set of hyperplanes of
$\operatorname{AG}(n,\mathbb R)$ that covers all but one point of $G$
has at least $h_1+h_2+\cdots+h_n$ members.

The phrase "all but one point" means that exactly one point of $G$ lies on no
hyperplane of the set, as in the case $t=1$ of
[[discrete_geometry/ball_2009_punctured_combinatorial_nullstellensatze/theorem_5_3|Theorem 5.3]]. The paper attributes the theorem to
N. Alon and Z. Füredi, *Covering the cube by affine hyperplanes*,
European J. Combin. **14** (1993), 79–83.

## Proof pointer

P. 8: the paper calls it an immediate corollary of Theorem 5.3 with
$t=1$; take $S_i=\{0,1,\ldots,h_i\}$ and $D_i$ the coordinate of the
uncovered point.
