## Statement and external scope

For integers $M\ge N\ge2$ and polynomials of degree at most $D\ge1$ in
$N$ real variables, the number of realized sign patterns is at most
$$
(50DM/N)^N.
$$
The paper imports this form of the Milnor–Thom bound and points to Section
6.2 of Jiří Matoušek, *Lectures on Discrete Geometry*, Graduate Texts in
Mathematics 212, Springer (2002). Its historical citations are Milnor (1964),
Oleĭnik–Petrovskiĭ (1952) and Thom (1965).

**Proof scope:** this is an exact external input, not a reconstructed proof
for arbitrary polynomial degree. The only specialization used in Theorem
1.2 has $D=1$. The separate linear-sign-pattern page proves that case,
including zero signs, by an elementary hyperplane insertion argument.
Thus the reconstructed main proof does not depend on an unchecked
higher-degree assertion.

The two seven-page manuscript versions label this result Theorem 2.1;
the eight-page published article labels it Theorem 2.5. Citations here use
the published numbering.
