**Source.** Conjecture 1, p. 3, of Ákos Dúcz and Dániel Varga, *A unit-distance graph in the plane with
independence ratio below 1/4*, arXiv:2606.28157v1 (26 June 2026), the version
named on the [[discrete_geometry/ducz_2026_unit_distance_graph_plane_independence_ratio/_index|source card]]. A preprint.

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the statement and the paragraph before it
were read on p. 3 of the print.

## Statement

Setting (pp. 1--3). $f(n)$ is the largest integer such that every set of $n$
points in the plane contains $f(n)$ points no two at distance $1$;
$m_1(\mathbb R^2)$ is the supremum of the upper densities of measurable planar
sets with no two points at distance $1$; and
$\delta_{\mathrm{Croft}}=0.22936\ldots$ is the density of Croft's construction,
which gives $m_1(\mathbb R^2)\ge\delta_{\mathrm{Croft}}$.

**Conjecture 1** (p. 3). $f(n)/n=m_1(\mathbb R^2)+o(1)$, and in particular
$m_1(\mathbb R^2)=\delta_{\mathrm{Croft}}$.

## Scope

The authors' own conjecture, stated after the remark that estimating $f(n)/n$
accurately is still wide open (p. 3). It is distinct from Conjecture 1 of
Matolcsi, Ruzsa, Varga and Zsámboki, which
[[discrete_geometry/ducz_2026_unit_distance_graph_plane_independence_ratio/corollary_1|Corollary 1]] falsifies. The paper proves nothing toward it.
