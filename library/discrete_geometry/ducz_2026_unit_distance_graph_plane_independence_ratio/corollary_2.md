## Statement

**Corollary 2** (p. 1). $\chi(\mathbb R^2)\ge5$.

The paper calls it an immediate consequence and says that it recovers de
Grey's lower bound (*The chromatic number of the plane is at least 5*,
Geombinatorics **28** (2018), 18--31).

## Proof pointer

[[discrete_geometry/ducz_2026_unit_distance_graph_plane_independence_ratio/corollary_1|Corollary 1]] gives a finite unit-distance graph $G$ with
$\chi(G)\ge\chi_f(G)>4$, so $G$, and hence the plane, needs at least $5$
colors.
