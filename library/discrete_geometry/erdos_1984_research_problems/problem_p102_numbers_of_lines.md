## Statement

**Definition** (p. 102). $\alpha_1<\alpha_2<\cdots$ are the integers
$\alpha$ for which some set $X_n$ of $n$ points in the plane determines
exactly $\alpha$ distinct lines.

**What the note says** (p. 102). Several results on the possible values are
known, citing Erdős's 1972 paper *On a problem of Grünbaum*; for example
$\alpha_1=1$ and $\alpha_2=n$. As far as Erdős knows, the number of possible
values of the $\alpha_i$ has not been determined. He adds that much less
seems to be known about the possible numbers of ordinary lines, or of lines
containing exactly $r$ of the points.

## Proof pointer

None; the note states the known values without argument.
