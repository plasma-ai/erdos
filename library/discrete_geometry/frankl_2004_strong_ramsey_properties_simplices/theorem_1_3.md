***

**Source.** Published p. 216, Theorem 1.3, attributed to Matoušek–Rödl (1995).
(canonical PDF).

If a simplex $X$ has circumradius $\rho$, then for every integer
$q\ge2$ and every $\delta>0$ there is an ambient dimension $m$ such that
every $q$-coloring of $S(\rho+\delta,m)$ has a monochromatic congruent
copy of $X$.

**Scope.** This is an external historical theorem, not an additional proof
component. The present paper strengthens its qualitative color conclusion
to exponential growth in the dimension in [[discrete_geometry/frankl_2004_strong_ramsey_properties_simplices/theorem_1_6]]. Its proof uses
the separate spread-vector input [[discrete_geometry/frankl_2004_strong_ramsey_properties_simplices/lemma_2_3]], not Theorem 1.3 as a
black-box substitute for the main argument. The 1995 article is
[Matoušek–Rödl, DOI 10.1016/0097-3165(95)90078-0](https://doi.org/10.1016/0097-3165(95)90078-0).
