***

**Source.** Takashi Hara and Gordon Slade, Critical behaviour of
self-avoiding walk in five or more dimensions, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. (N.S.)
**25** (1991), no. 2, 417--423; Theorem 2.4 on printed p. 421. The edition
read is identified on the
[[discrete_geometry/hara_1991_critical_behaviour_self_avoiding_walk_five_more_dimensions/_index|source
card]].

**Statement.** Let $\mu(d)=\lim_{n\to\infty}c_n^{1/n}$ be the connective
constant of $\mathbb{Z}^d$, where $c_n$ counts the $n$-step self-avoiding
walks from the origin (pp. 417--418). Then

$$
\mu(3)\ge4.43733,\qquad\mu(4)\ge6.71800,\qquad\mu(5)\ge8.82128.
$$

**Context.** The paper says on p. 421 that the bounds were needed in the
proof of
[[discrete_geometry/hara_1991_critical_behaviour_self_avoiding_walk_five_more_dimensions/theorem_2_1|Theorem
2.1]], that they come from an elementary method which does not use the lace
expansion and is valid above two dimensions, and that the bound for $d=3$
slightly improves the previous best rigorous lower bound $4.352$ of
Guttmann (its [3]). The acknowledgments (p. 422) credit Theorem 2.4 partly
to Alan Sokal.

**Proof pointer.** The announcement contains no proof; Section 3 (p. 421)
refers all proofs to the authors' two-part paper *Self-avoiding walk in five
or more dimensions* (its references [7] and [8]).

**Dependencies.** None.

**Bears on.** No Erdős problem in the corpus.

**Living verification.** Needs review. The statement and the context on
p. 421 were checked against the print. The proof is not in the announcement
and was not checked.
