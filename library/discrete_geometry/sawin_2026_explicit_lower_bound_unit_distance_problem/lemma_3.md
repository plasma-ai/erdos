# Lemma 3 — ideal indices and archimedean sizes

***

## Statement

Let $F$ be a totally real number field, let $K/F$ be a totally imaginary
quadratic extension, and put $d=[F:\mathbb Q]$. Let $c$ be the nontrivial
member of $\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)$. For a fractional ideal $I$ of $K$, let
$N_{K/F}(I)$ denote the fractional ideal of $F$ generated by the elements
$x c(x)$ with $x\in I$. Write $\Sigma_{F,\infty}$ for the infinite places
of $F$.

If $0\neq\beta\in I$ and
$0\neq\alpha\in N_{K/F}(I)$, then

$$
\frac{\#(I/(\beta))}
     {\#(N_{K/F}(I)/(\alpha))}
=\prod_{v\in\Sigma_{F,\infty}}
 \frac{|\beta|_v^2}{|\alpha|_v}. \tag{1}
$$

## Proof

At finite places use product-formula-normalized absolute values; at real and
complex places use the usual absolute value, so a complex factor is squared
in the product formula. For a nonarchimedean place $w$ of $K$, let $|I|_w$
be the absolute value of a generator of
$I\otimes_{\mathcal O_K}\mathcal O_{K_w}$. Localizing the finite quotient,
then applying the product formula, gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\#(I/(\beta))
&=\prod_{w\nmid\infty}\frac{|I|_w}{|\beta|_w}\\
&=\left(\prod_{w\nmid\infty}|I|_w\right)
  \left(\prod_{v\in\Sigma_{F,\infty}}|\beta|_v^2\right).
\end{aligned} \tag{2}
$$

The analogous calculation over $F$ is

$$
\#(N_{K/F}(I)/(\alpha))
=\left(\prod_{v\nmid\infty}|N_{K/F}(I)|_v\right)
 \left(\prod_{v\in\Sigma_{F,\infty}}|\alpha|_v\right). \tag{3}
$$

At every finite place of $F$, the contribution of the norm ideal equals the
product of the contributions of the places of $K$ above it. Therefore

$$
\prod_{v\nmid\infty}|N_{K/F}(I)|_v
=\prod_{w\nmid\infty}|I|_w. \tag{4}
$$

Dividing (2) by (3) and using (4) proves (1).
