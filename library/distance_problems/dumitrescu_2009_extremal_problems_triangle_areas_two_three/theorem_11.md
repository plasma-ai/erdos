## Statement

**Theorem 11** (p. 20, quoted). "The number of triangles of maximum area spanned
by $n$ points in $\mathbb R^3$ is $O(n^{7/3+\varepsilon})$, for any
$\varepsilon>0$. For all $n\ge3$, there exist $n$-element point sets in
$\mathbb R^3$ that span $\Omega(n^{4/3})$ triangles of maximum area."

The paper derives it from Theorems 9 and 10 (p. 20).
