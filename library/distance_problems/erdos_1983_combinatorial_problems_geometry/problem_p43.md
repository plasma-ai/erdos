## Statement

**Problem** (p. 43). Erdős notes that it is easy to place $n$ points on a
circle with all distances integers, and asks whether one can find $n$
points in general position, which he glosses as "no three on a line, no
four on a circle", with all distances integers.

**Reported state** (p. 43). Harborth settled the case $n=5$; the general
case, even $n=6$, was not known. Erdős adds that it is probably difficult
because it belongs to Diophantine approximation.
