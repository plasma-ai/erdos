## Statement

Setting (p. 126). $W(n)$ is the least $W$ such that in any partition of
$\{1,2,\ldots,W\}$ into two classes some class contains an $n$-term
arithmetic progression.

**Conjecture** (p. 127, unnumbered, with a prize). For all
$n$, $W(n)\le 2^{2^{\cdot^{\cdot^{\cdot^{2}}}}}$, a tower of $n$ twos.

The paper places it after the bounds it reports (p. 126): Shelah's theorem
(J. Amer. Math. Soc. 1 (1988)) that $W(n)$ is bounded by a primitive
recursive function, displayed as a tower of towers of twos with $n$ layers,
and a best known lower bound growing "roughly like $n\cdot 2^n$".

## Read depth

Claims checked: the definition, the conjecture and the reported bounds were
