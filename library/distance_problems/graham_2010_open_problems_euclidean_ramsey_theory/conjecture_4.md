## Statement

**Conjecture 4** (p. 3, quoted). "(\$100). Every 4-point subset of a circle is
Ramsey."

The paper calls it weaker than [[distance_problems/graham_2010_open_problems_euclidean_ramsey_theory/conjecture_3|Conjecture 3]]; a subset of
a circle is spherical. Ramsey is meant in the sense of p. 1. Kříž's
[[distance_problems/graham_2010_open_problems_euclidean_ramsey_theory/theorem_2|Theorem 2]], as reported, covers the 4-point subsets of a
circle that are the vertices of a trapezoid.

After it (p. 3) the paper asks, for a Ramsey set $X$ and an integer $r$,
what can be said about the least dimension $N(X,r)$, and calls this a major
open question already for two-point sets.

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the statement was read on p. 3 of the
preprint.
