## Statement

**Lemma 1** (p. 152). Let $t>0$ and consider a coloring with no blue points
at distance $t$. If $\gamma_P(r)$ and $\gamma_Q(r)$ form a complementary pair
and $r\ge t/2$, then both circles are $t$-alternating.

The terms are defined on the
[[distance_problems/juhasz_1979_ramsey_type_theorems_plane/definitions|definitions]]
page. The equality case $r=t/2$, where the relevant pairs are antipodal, is
included.

**Source.** R. Juhász, Ramsey type theorems in the plane, J. Combin. Theory
Ser. A 27 (1979), 152–160; Lemma 1 on p. 152, its proof on pp. 152–153. The
copy read is identified on the
[[distance_problems/juhasz_1979_ramsey_type_theorems_plane/_index|source card]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the statement was read clause by clause on
the page images, and the proof was read.

## Proof pointer

Pages 152–153. Two points of one circle at distance $t$ cannot both be blue
by hypothesis; if both were red, their corresponding points on the other
circle, also $t$ apart, would both be blue.
