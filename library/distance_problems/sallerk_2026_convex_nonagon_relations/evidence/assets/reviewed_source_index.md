convex nonagon with maximum distance multiplicity three at every vertex.
The source-owned

exact checks. This is author-recorded evidence awaiting independent review, not
a solution of the four-neighbor question.

The coordinates realize the three relations in
[[library/discrete_geometry/erdos_1987_combinatorial_metric_problems_geometry/_index|Er87b]],
printed pp. 175–176, Fig. 5. That source prints no coordinates. The supplied
coordinates are not identified as Danzer's original choice, and the local
derivation does not reconstruct every part of the printed Reuleaux-triangle
construction. The existing Er87b PDF remains the sole canonical copy.

The post's degree-four, mirror-symmetry and minimum-size claims are separate
qualified reports; completion uniqueness and alternate-root nonconvexity are
review-side reports. None is certified by this finite witness check. In
particular, the six-point exclusion needed for the asserted lower bound
$n_3\geq7$ has not been inspected.
