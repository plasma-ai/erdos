## Statement

**Question** (p. 130, unnumbered, quoted). "At present I cannot decide
whether the number of highly composite numbers not exceeding $x$ is
greater than $(\log x)^k$ for every $k$."

Here $n$ is highly composite if $d(m)<d(n)$ for all $m<n$, with $d$ the
divisor function. The sentence follows the paper's announcement of the
lower bound $(\log x)^{1+c}$ for a certain $c$, proved through the
[[divisors/erdos_1944_highly_composite_numbers/theorem|Theorem of p. 131]];
the paper does not answer the question.

**Source.** P. Erdős, On highly composite numbers, J. London Math. Soc. 19
(1944), 130–133, p. 130. The edition read is named on the
[[divisors/erdos_1944_highly_composite_numbers/_index|source card]].

the print. Nothing here is independently reviewed.
