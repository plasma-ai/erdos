## Statement

**Theorem 5** (p. 309, quoted). "Let $A$ denote the set of integers $a$
such that $\Omega(n)-\Omega(n+1)=a$ holds for infinitely many integers
$n$. Then $A$ has positive lower density."

Here $\Omega(n)$ counts prime factors with multiplicity, and $A$ is a set
of integers of either sign. The paper presents Theorem 5 as the analogue of
[[divisors/hildebrand_1987_divisor_function_at_consecutive_integers/theorem_3|Theorem 3]]
for $\Omega$ (p. 308).

## Proof pointer

Not written out. The paper says (p. 308) that the analogues of Theorems 2
and 3 for $\Omega(n)$ can be established, by arguments technically simpler
than for $d(n)$. It does not name the route; by analogy with the deduction of
Theorem 3 from Theorem 2, it would be
[[divisors/hildebrand_1987_divisor_function_at_consecutive_integers/theorem_4|Theorem 4]]
followed by the argument of §5.
