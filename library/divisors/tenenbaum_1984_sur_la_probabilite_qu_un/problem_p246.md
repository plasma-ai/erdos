## Statement

**Open problem** (p. 246, end of §1). The paper closes its introduction
with an open problem it attributes to Erdős. Let $\epsilon(y)$ be the
natural density of the integers having at least one divisor in the
interval $[y,2y)$, and $\epsilon'(y)$ the natural density of those having
exactly one. The question, quoted: "A-t-on pour y infini
$\epsilon'(y)/\epsilon(y)=o(1)$?"

The paper poses the question and proves nothing about it.

## Proof pointer

None; the paper states the problem without a result either way.
