## Statement

**Lemma 3** (p. 328). "$\dim(K_{n,m})=4$ for $m,n\ge3$."

The paper presents Lemmas 1--4 as results of Erdős, Harary and Tutte [1]
"used repeatedly in the arguments that follow", given "for easy reference";
no proof is printed. For $n=m=3$ the lemma gives $\dim(K_{3,3})=4$, the
nine-edge witness in Theorem 6.

**Source.** Chaffee and Noble, *Dimension 4 and dimension 5 graphs with
minimum edge set*, Australas. J. Combin. 64 (2016), no. 2, 327--333; Lemma 3
on printed p. 328 = PDF p. 2 of the journal PDF, read on the page
image. The edition is identified in the
[[extremal_graph_theory/chaffee_2016_dimension_4_dimension_5_graphs_minimum/_index|source digest]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the statement was read on the page image;
it is quoted from [1], Erdős, Harary and Tutte, *On the dimension of a
graph*, Mathematika 12 (1965), 118--122, where the value is an unnumbered
sentence on printed p. 119, paged on
[[extremal_graph_theory/erdos_harary_tutte_1965_dimension_graph/complete_bipartite_graphs_p119|complete_bipartite_graphs_p119]];
that note prints no proof of it beyond Lenz's construction for the upper
bound $\dim K_{m,n}\le4$, so the lower bound is second-hand here.
