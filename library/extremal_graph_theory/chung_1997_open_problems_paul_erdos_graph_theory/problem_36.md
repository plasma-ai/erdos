## Statement

As printed on preprint p. 9 (page image and text layer):

"(36) *A problem on Turán numbers for an $n$-cube* (proposed by Erdős and
Simonovits [130], 1970)
Let $Q_k$ denote an [sic] $k$-cube on $2^k$ vertices.
Determine $t(n,Q_k)$. In particular, determine $t(n,Q_3)$.
Erdős and Simonovits [130] proved that

$$
t(n,Q_3)\le cn^{8/5}.
$$

No better lower bound than $cn^{3/2}$ is known."

Here $t(n,G)$ is the survey's notation for $\mathrm{ex}(n;G)$; the survey's
[130] is P. Erdős and M. Simonovits, Some extremal problems in graph theory,
Combinatorial theory and its applications, I (Proc. Colloq., Balatonfüred,
1969), 377--390, North-Holland, Amsterdam, 1970 (reference list, text layer).
The wording "Determine $t(n,Q_k)$" is the site's wording of Problem 576 up
to notation; the 1997 state of the bounds for $Q_3$ is the state in 2026.

**Source.** F. R. K. Chung, *Open problems of Paul Erdős in graph theory*,
J. Graph Theory 25 (1997), 3--36; Problem (36) on p. 9 of the
45-page author preprint (the journal pagination was not consulted), read in the
text layer and on the rendered page image. The artifact is identified in the
[[extremal_graph_theory/chung_1997_open_problems_paul_erdos_graph_theory/_index|source digest]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the problem and its two sentences were read
clause by clause on the page image; the reference entry [130] in the text
layer. The survey proves nothing.
