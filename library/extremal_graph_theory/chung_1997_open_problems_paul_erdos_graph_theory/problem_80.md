## Statement

As printed on preprint p. 21 (page image and text layer):

"(80) *A conjecture on a spanning cube in a random graph* (proposed by Erdős
and Bollobás, see [88])
A random graph on $n=2^d$ vertices with edge density $1/2$ contains an [sic]
$d$-cube.

Alon and Füredi [7] showed that this conjecture is true if the random graph
has edge density $>1/2$ for $n$ large enough."

The survey's [88] is "P. Erdős, Some recent combinatorial problems, Technical
Report, University of Bielefeld, Nov. 1990" and its [7] is "N. Alon and Z.
Füredi, Spanning subgraphs of random graphs, Graphs and Combinatorics 8
(1992), 91--94" (reference list, preprint pp. 37 and 31 respectively, text
layer). The conjecture is the statement of Problem 578 (the site's "almost
surely" is the survey's unstated probability qualifier), and [88] is the
site's source key [Er90c], the reference text of which the site gives as the
same Bielefeld technical report. The survey, written before Riordan's 2000
paper, records the partial result for edge density above $1/2$ and not the
solution.

**Source.** F. R. K. Chung, *Open problems of Paul Erdős in graph theory*,
J. Graph Theory 25 (1997), 3--36; Problem (80) on p. 21 of the
45-page author preprint (the journal pagination was not consulted), read in the
text layer and on the rendered page image. The artifact is identified in the
[[extremal_graph_theory/chung_1997_open_problems_paul_erdos_graph_theory/_index|source digest]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the problem and the sentence after it were
read clause by clause on the page image; the reference entries [88] and [7]
in the text layer. The survey proves nothing.
