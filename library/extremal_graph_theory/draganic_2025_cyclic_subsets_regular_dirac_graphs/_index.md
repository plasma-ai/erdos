## Results and proof coverage

A vertex subset is cyclic if it induces a graph with a Hamilton cycle. Writing
$p(G)=\operatorname{Cyc}(G)/2^{|V(G)|}$ turns the problem into Hamiltonicity of
a uniform random induced subgraph.

[[extremal_graph_theory/draganic_2025_cyclic_subsets_regular_dirac_graphs/theorem_2_2|Theorem 2.2]]
proves that every $(n+1)$-regular graph on $2n$ vertices has $p(G)\ge c$ for an
absolute $c>0$, resolving the Erdős–Faudree question. Its rewritten proof
includes the bidense case
[[extremal_graph_theory/draganic_2025_cyclic_subsets_regular_dirac_graphs/lemma_2_3|Lemma 2.3]],
the two-almost-cliques case
[[extremal_graph_theory/draganic_2025_cyclic_subsets_regular_dirac_graphs/lemma_2_4|Lemma 2.4]],
and the almost-bipartite case
[[extremal_graph_theory/draganic_2025_cyclic_subsets_regular_dirac_graphs/lemma_2_5|Lemma 2.5]],
with their supporting path, matching, and cover lemmas. The external
structural classification and probability tools are stated in
[[extremal_graph_theory/draganic_2025_cyclic_subsets_regular_dirac_graphs/external_inputs|external inputs]],
without recursive proofs.

[[extremal_graph_theory/draganic_2025_cyclic_subsets_regular_dirac_graphs/theorem_4_1|Theorem 4.1]]
improves the lower bound to $1/2-o(1)$. The proof uses
[[extremal_graph_theory/draganic_2025_cyclic_subsets_regular_dirac_graphs/lemma_4_2|Lemma 4.2]],
combining cover sizes, internal bounded-degree subgraphs, asymptotic linear
arboricity, and the calculus inequality in
[[extremal_graph_theory/draganic_2025_cyclic_subsets_regular_dirac_graphs/lemma_4_7|Lemma 4.7]].
The reconstruction makes the source's constant, parameter, and cut-transfer
bookkeeping explicit; these local clarifications are identified on the affected
pages.

[[extremal_graph_theory/draganic_2025_cyclic_subsets_regular_dirac_graphs/theorem_1_2|Theorem 1.2]]
states that, for sufficiently large $n$, the minimum is attained in the family
$\mathcal G_n$ obtained from $K_{n-1,n+1}$ by adding a $2$-factor in its larger
part. Its exact-proof page currently contains a detailed source proof map and
an unresolved conditional-exposure estimate in its small-cover cases and
missing quantitative failure bounds, **not a complete rewritten proof**.
Routine cut-transfer, matching, endpoint, and sign repairs are recorded
separately. These stronger-result reconstruction limits do not change the
original problem's proved status.

For every member of $\mathcal G_n$,
[[extremal_graph_theory/draganic_2025_cyclic_subsets_regular_dirac_graphs/lemma_5_1|Lemma 5.1]]
proves

$$
p(G)=\frac12+\frac{3}{2\sqrt{\pi n}}+O(n^{-3/2}).
$$

The theorem does not specify which $2$-factor minimizes the exponentially
small remainder.
[[extremal_graph_theory/draganic_2025_cyclic_subsets_regular_dirac_graphs/remarks_p14|remarks p14]]
preserves that question and other sampling and degree directions.

[[extremal_graph_theory/draganic_2025_cyclic_subsets_regular_dirac_graphs/remarks_p2|remarks p2]]
records the elementary obstructions when degree $n+1$ is lowered to $n$ or
regularity is replaced by minimum degree.
[[extremal_graph_theory/draganic_2025_cyclic_subsets_regular_dirac_graphs/proposition_1_3|Proposition 1.3]]
records the nonregular $N/2+\Omega(\sqrt N)$ minimum-degree proposition as a
source sketch, with its omitted details explicit.
[[extremal_graph_theory/draganic_2025_cyclic_subsets_regular_dirac_graphs/conjecture_6_1|Conjecture 6.1]]
records the clique-factor question and the later Sun–Wei–Yang large-order
resolution claim.

## Later literature and formalization

The 2026-09-05 search also found Liu–Niu–Wang–Yan's lower-degree staircase
bounds and Hunter–Liu–Milojević–Sudakov's tournament analog; precise
primary-source leads and their differing scopes are in
[[extremal_graph_theory/draganic_2025_cyclic_subsets_regular_dirac_graphs/remarks_p14|remarks p14]].
They were not found to replace the exact theorem for $(n+1)$-regular graphs
on $2n$ vertices. Their proofs remain separate compilation tasks. No materially
distinct accepted proof of the graph question was found in this bounded search
or the discussion thread (accessed 2026-09-05).

The Problem 622 page accessed records no formalized statement or
solution. A bounded GitHub/web search found no corresponding public Lean
artifact; no Lean build or formalization was performed.
