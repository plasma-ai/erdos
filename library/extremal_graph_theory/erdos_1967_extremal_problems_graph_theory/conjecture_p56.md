## Statement

Copy p. 3 (printed p. 56), page image, after Pósa's generalization of
Dirac's Hamiltonian theorem: "It has been conjectured that any graph with
$rm$ points, all of degree at least $m(r-1)$, contains $mK_r$. Its validity
when $r=2$ follows from Dirac's result [3] on hamiltonian cycles; Corrádi
and Hajnal [2] proved it when $r=3$."

Here $mK_r$ is the graph of $m$ vertex-disjoint copies of $K_r$, so the
conjecture is the site's statement of Problem 914: every graph with $rm$
vertices and minimum degree at least $m(r-1)$ contains $m$ vertex-disjoint
copies of $K_r$. The paper does not name the conjecture's author; the
footnote on copy p. 1 marks every result stated without a reference as
unpublished, and the site and Kierstead, Kostochka, Mydlarz and Szemerédi
(2010, p. 217) attribute the conjecture to Erdős. The references are [3] G. A.
Dirac, Some theorems on abstract graphs, Proc. London Math. Soc. (3) 2
(1952) 69--81, and [2] K. Corrádi and A. Hajnal, On the maximal number of
independent circuits in a graph, Acta Math. Acad. Sci. Hung. 14 (1963)
423--439 (copy p. 5).

**Source.** P. Erdős, *Extremal problems in graph theory*, A Seminar on Graph
Theory (1967), 54--59; copy p. 3 of the Rényi archive's re-typeset copy,
whose own pagination the printed p. 56 follows by the archive's page range.
The copy's text layer is unreadable; the passage was read on the rendered
page image. The edition read is identified in the
[[extremal_graph_theory/erdos_1967_extremal_problems_graph_theory/_index|source digest]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the two sentences were read clause by

a conjecture and credits two cases.

## Proof pointer

None in the paper. The conjecture is the Hajnal--Szemerédi theorem of 1970
in its clique form; the library states that theorem as
[[extremal_graph_theory/kierstead_2010_fast_algorithm_equitable_coloring/theorem_1|Theorem 1 of Kierstead, Kostochka, Mydlarz and Szemerédi (2010)]],
whose transfer to this form is written on the problem page.
