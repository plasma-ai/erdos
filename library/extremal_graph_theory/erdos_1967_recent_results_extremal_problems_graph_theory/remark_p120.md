## Statement

Here $\alpha(\mathcal G)$ is the exponent of the conjecture
[[extremal_graph_theory/erdos_1967_recent_results_extremal_problems_graph_theory/equation_7|(7)--(8)]],
and the discussion is framed by $\chi(\mathcal G)=2$ (p. 119). The remark
(p. 120): "It seems that $\alpha(\mathcal G)$ can take on only the values
$1+\frac1k$, $k=2,3,\ldots$ and $2-\frac1k$, $k=1,2,\ldots$"

On the same page it comes after the observation that
$\alpha(\mathcal G)=2-1/v^*(\mathcal G)$ does not always hold, because
$v^*(C_6)=2$ while $f(n;C_6)<cn^{4/3}$ (see
[[extremal_graph_theory/erdos_1967_recent_results_extremal_problems_graph_theory/equation_9|display (9)]]).
