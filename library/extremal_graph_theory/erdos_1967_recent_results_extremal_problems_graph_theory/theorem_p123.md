## Statement

Notation as on
[[extremal_graph_theory/erdos_1967_recent_results_extremal_problems_graph_theory/equation_2|display (2)]].
Stated as provable "By the same method" as the
[[extremal_graph_theory/erdos_1967_recent_results_extremal_problems_graph_theory/theorem_p118|Theorem]]:
every

$$
\mathcal G\Bigl(n;\frac{n^2}2\Bigl(1-\frac1{r-1}-\varepsilon\Bigr)\Bigr)
$$

which does not contain a $K_r(t,\ldots,t)$ contains, for
$n>n_0(r,t,\varepsilon)$, an $(r-1)$-chromatic subgraph with more than

$$
\frac{n^2}2\Bigl(1-\frac1{r-1}-c_r\varepsilon\Bigr)
$$

edges. Erdős adds that he has just learnt that Simonovits proved this
independently by a different method.

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the statement was read clause by clause on
