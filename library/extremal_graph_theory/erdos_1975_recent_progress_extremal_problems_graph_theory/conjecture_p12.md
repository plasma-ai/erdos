## Statement

The opening of Chapter 4, printed p. 12 (PDF p. 10 of the scan;
PDF p. $n$ is printed p. $n+2$), page image, as printed: "4. In this final
chapter I state some recent extremal problems and results on somewhat
unconventional problems. Let $G(rn)$ be an $r$-partite graph having $n$
vertices of each color. Bollobás, Szemerédi and I conjectured that if each
vertex has valency $\ge(r-\tfrac32)n$ then our graph contains a $K(r)$. We
know that $r-\tfrac32$ cannot be replaced by $r-\tfrac32-\varepsilon$ but we
cannot prove it even if $r-\tfrac32$ is replaced by $r-1-\varepsilon$. Our
paper on this and related questions will appear in Discrete Mathematics."

The threshold is printed four times as the stacked fraction $r-\frac32$; the
OCR text layer drops the numerator and reads "$(r-2)n$", which is not what the
page prints. $K(r)$ is the complete graph on $r$ vertices, here one vertex
from each color class. The conjecture is stated without an $o(1)$ term and
without a lower bound on $n$; the paper announced for Discrete Mathematics is
the Bollobás--Erdős--Szemerédi paper of 1975 (recorded as
[[extremal_graph_theory/bollobas_1975_complete_subgraphs_chromatic_graphs/_index|bollobas_1975_complete_subgraphs_chromatic_graphs]]).

**Source.** P. Erdős, *Some recent progress on extremal problems in graph
theory*, Congr. Numer. XIV (1975), 3--14; Chapter 4, printed p. 12 = PDF
p. 10 of the scan, read on the rendered page image. The artifact is
identified in the
[[extremal_graph_theory/erdos_1975_recent_progress_extremal_problems_graph_theory/_index|source digest]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the paragraph was read clause by clause on
the page image on 2026-09-18, with the fraction confirmed on a 300 dpi
render. A conjecture with two unproved remarks; nothing to prove in the
source.

## Proof pointer

None; a conjecture. The paper's own statement is that the constant
$r-\frac32$ cannot be lowered and that even $r-1-\varepsilon$ was out of
reach in 1975.
