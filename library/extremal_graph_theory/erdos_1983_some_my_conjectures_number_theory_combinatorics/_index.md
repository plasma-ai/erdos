# extremal_graph_theory/erdos_1983_some_my_conjectures_number_theory_combinatorics

[[extremal_graph_theory/_index|..]]

[[extremal_graph_theory/erdos_1983_some_my_conjectures_number_theory_combinatorics/conjecture_p15|conjecture_p15]]: Erdős's 1983 statement of two conjectures with Gallai: the edges of every
n-vertex graph are covered by at most n-1 circuits and edges, reported as
proved by Pyber, and decompose into at most cn edge-disjoint circuits and
edges for an absolute c, reported as open, with c >= 3/2 by an unnamed
example.

***

P. Erdős, *On some of my conjectures in number theory and combinatorics*,
in: Proceedings of the fourteenth Southeastern conference on combinatorics,
graph theory and computing (Boca Raton, Fla., 1983), Congr. Numer. **39**
(1983), 3--19; MR 734525. The site's reference key [Er83b] for Problem 184
names this paper; the Rényi archive's index lists it as `1983-15.pdf`. The
2022 Bucić--Montgomery paper on the cycle decomposition conjecture cites it
(its [17]) as the source of the $(\tfrac32-o(1))n$ lower bound.

The copy read for this card is the Rényi archive's OmniPage scan of the
typewritten proceedings text: seventeen pages, printed pp. 3--19 = PDF pp. 1--17
(printed p. $n$ is PDF p. $n-2$; the foot of p. 3 reads "CONGRESSUS NUMERANTIUM,
VOL. 39 (1983), pp. 3-20", and the text ends on p. 19 with the last problem),
with a text layer that locates passages and garbles the displays. Provenance:
retrieved from
<https://users.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1983-15.pdf> (HTTP 200, one request); 1,674,167
bytes. No notice is printed in the scan; the hosting archive's site footer

is for scientifics purposes only."); the proceedings volume has no publisher

## Contents

- Introduction (p. 3): to avoid repetition, the author says he will mainly
  discuss problems on which significant progress was made in the preceding
  year.
- Part I, number theory (pp. 3--9), three items: 1, the conjecture
  $\min d_{i+1}/d_i<2$ for the divisors of almost all $n$, its history
  through primitive sequences and the Erdős--Hall and Erdős--Tenenbaum
  results, and its proof by Maier and Tenenbaum (p. 4, spelled "Meier" in
  the print); 2 (p. 7), the consecutive divisors $1=d_1<\cdots<d_{\tau(n)}=n$
  and related questions; 3 (p. 9), a problem with Graham, Ruzsa and
  Szemerédi; then references 1--8.
- Part II, combinatorics (pp. 10--17), seven items: 1 (p. 10), the density
  of a sequence of $r$-graphs; 2 (p. 12), a question from the Poznań
  meeting on random graphs; 3, a result with Bollobás, Simonovits and
  Szemerédi; 4 (p. 13), an old conjecture with Hanani; 5 (pp. 14--15),
  $f(n;H)$, with the conjecture (1)
  $f(n;C_4)=\tfrac12n^{3/2}+\tfrac n4+o(n)$ ("We are very far from being
  able to prove (1)"), the Kővári--Sós--Turán bound
  $f(n;K(r,r))<c_rn^{2-1/r}$ and Frankl's $f(n;K(r;2^r))<cn^{2-1/r}$; 6
  (p. 15), quoted below; 7 (pp. 15--16), Ramsey--Turán type theorems with
  V. T. Sós; then references 1--11 (p. 17).
- Part III (pp. 18--19): a geometric problem partially solved by Elekes
  ($f(n)>cn^{3/2}$), and, to end the paper, an "unconventional problem in
  number theory" on sums of prime powers.

**The p. 15 item 6** (PDF p. 13, page image) states the two conjectures as
posed: "Gallai and I conjectured that the edges of every $G(n)$ can be
covered by at most $n-1$ circuits and edges of $G(n)$. We also conjectured
that there is an absolute constant $c$ so that the edges can be covered by
at most $cn$ edge disjoint circuits and edges of $G(n)$. An example of
$G(n)$ shows that $c\ge\frac32$." The item then reports that Pyber proved
the first conjecture a few months earlier, using a result of Lovász [9],
and calls the second open and perhaps in need of new ideas. (Reference [9]
is Lovász, *On coverings of graphs*, Theory of graphs, Proc. Colloq. Tihany
1966.)

## Compiled scope

The paper is compiled as a problem source. Its one result page is
[[extremal_graph_theory/erdos_1983_some_my_conjectures_number_theory_combinatorics/conjecture_p15|conjecture_p15]]:
item 6 of part II (p. 15), the two Erdős-Gallai conjectures with the
reported proof of the first and the unstated example for $c\ge\frac32$.
Item 6 gives no proof. The other items were located in the text
layer only and have no result pages here; whether they bear on other
problems is not assessed on this card.

**Bears on.** [[../wiki/problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0184/_index|#184]]: the site's
key [Er83b]. Item 6
([[extremal_graph_theory/erdos_1983_some_my_conjectures_number_theory_combinatorics/conjecture_p15|conjecture_p15]])
states the problem's conjecture (the edge-disjoint form
with an absolute constant $c$) beside the covering form with $n-1$ cycles
and edges, which it reports as proved by Pyber, and it is the source of the
lower bound $c\ge\frac32$: the sentence "An example of $G(n)$ shows that
$c\ge\frac32$" names no graph, so the construction Bucić and Montgomery
take it to refer to ($K_{2k+1,n-2k-1}$) is their reading, not the paper's
text.
