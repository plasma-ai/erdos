## Contents

The survey has five chapters numbered I--V, the last a list of fourteen
numbered problems and two closing paragraphs of "very recent problems", followed
by fifty-three references (printed pp. 347--350, PDF pp. 16--19). Displays are
numbered afresh in Chapters I and II and, in Chapter V, within each numbered
problem (problems 1 and 3 each print a display (1)); Chapters III and IV number
none.

- I. Turán type problems (pp. 333--336, PDF pp. 2--5): the Turán number
  $T(n;G)$; the Erdős--Simonovits conjectures (1) $T(n;G)<cn^{3/2}$ for a
  bipartite $G$ all of whose subgraphs have minimum degree $\le2$ and (2)
  $T(n;G)>n^{3/2+\epsilon}$ when some subgraph has all degrees $\ge3$,
  with a prize for a disproof and the guess that degree $r$ replaces
  the exponent by $2-\tfrac1r$ (pp. 333--334); Füredi's proof of (1) for the
  graphs $G(\binom k2+k+1;k^2)$, whose case $k=3$, the cube minus a vertex,
  Erdős proved easily; (3) $T(n;G)<cn^{8/5}$ for the three-dimensional cube (p.
  334); (4) $T(n;K(r,r))<cn^{2-1/r}$, whose exponent is "almost certainly best
  possible" but known to be so only for $r=2,3$ (p. 334); (5) the
  Erdős--Stone--Simonovits formula (pp. 334--335); the supersaturation
  conjecture for $C_4$; $f(n;G)$, the number of $G$-free graphs, with (6), the
  conjecture (7) $f(n,G)=2^{(1+o(1))T(n;G)}$, and Kleitman--Winston's (8) (p.
  335); $T(n;C_4)=(\tfrac12+o(1))n^{3/2}$, the conjecture (9)
  $T(p^2+p+1;C_4)=\tfrac12(p^3+p)+p^2+1$ proved by Füredi, and the conjecture
  (10) $T(n;C_4)=\tfrac12n^{3/2}+\tfrac n4+O(n^{1/2})$ (pp. 335--336); the
  $\{C_3,C_4\}$-free question with the constant $\tfrac1{2\sqrt2}$ (p. 336);
  remarks on $f(n;G)$ for nonbipartite $G$ and on counting maximal triangle-free
  graphs (p. 336).
- II. Ramsey numbers (pp. 337--340, PDF pp. 6--9): $r(u,v)$ and the
  diagonal $r(n)$; (1) $cn2^{n/2}<r(n)\le\binom{2n-1}{n-1}$, Rödl's and
  Thomason's (2) upper bounds, Frankl's constructive $r(n)>n^{c\log n}$,
  a prize for a constructive proof of (3) $r(n)>(1+c)^n$ (p. 337); prizes
  for a proof and a disproof of (4)
  $r(n)/(n2^{n/2})\to\infty$ (pp. 337--338); a prize for the existence
  of (5) $\lim r(n)^{1/n}=c$ and one for its value (p. 338); $r(3)=6$,
  $r(4)=18$, the evil-spirit joke about $r(5)$ and $r(6)$ (p. 338);
  Szekeres's (6), the 1961 bound (7) $r(3,n)<cn^2/(\log n)^2$, Graver and
  Yackel, and Ajtai--Komlós--Szemerédi's (8) $r(3,n)>cn^2/\log n$ (both
  inequality signs as printed, reversed) (pp. 338--339); $r(k,n)$ and
  Spencer's $r(4,n)>cn^{5/2}$ (p. 339); the Erdős--Sós conjectures (9), (10)
  and (11) on consecutive Ramsey numbers (pp. 339--340); the
  Fajtlowicz--Staton--Erdős induced regular subgraph questions (12) and (13)
  (p. 340).
- III. Ramsey--Turán type problems (p. 340, PDF p. 9): the
  Szemerédi--Bollobás--Erdős $K_4$ result at $\tfrac{n^2}8$ and the
  Erdős--Sós question for $K(2,2,2)$.
- IV. Chromatic numbers (pp. 341--343, PDF pp. 10--12): Dirac's critical
  graphs, $f_k(n)$, the minimum-degree question for 4-chromatic critical
  graphs, Toft's conjecture (p. 341); the Erdős--Faber--Lovász conjecture
  with Kahn's $(1+o(1))n$ and the Füredi--Erdős generalization
  (pp. 341--342); the Erdős--Hajnal--Szemerédi nearly bipartite conjecture
  with a prize, the odd-cycle reciprocal sums, the
  Gyárfás--Komlós--Szemerédi $\sum1/t_i>c\log k$, the Mihók--Erdős
  question on forbidden cycle lengths and cycles of length $2^i$
  (pp. 342--343); uncountable chromatic number and Taylor's problem
  (p. 343).
- V. Miscellaneous problems (pp. 343--347, PDF pp. 12--16): 1, $C_4$-free
  subgraphs of the $n$-cube (p. 343); 2, making a triangle-free graph
  bipartite by omitting $\tfrac{n^2}{25}$ edges (p. 343); 3, the degree
  sum $h(n)$ of a forced triangle (pp. 343--344); 4, the Bollobás--Erdős
  book of size $\tfrac n6$ and the Erdős--Faudree triangle conjecture
  (p. 344); 5, induced subgraphs of minimum degree 3 in $G(n;2n-1)$
  (p. 344); 6, local density and triangles (pp. 344--345); 7, the largest
  minimum degree $f(n)$ of a $C_4$-free graph (p. 345); 8, the Erdős--Sós
  tree conjecture (p. 345); 9, the size Ramsey splitting conjecture with
  Faudree's partial proof (p. 345); 10, minimum degree $(1-\epsilon)2^n$
  forcing the $n$-cube (pp. 345--346); 11, the Pyber--Tuza--Erdős totally
  multicolored $C_6$ and $K_4$ (p. 346); 12, the Ramsey number of the
  $n$-cube (p. 346); 13, the Erdős--Hajnal conjecture on induced-$H$-free
  graphs (p. 346); 14, nonisomorphic induced subgraphs of Ramsey graphs,
  with the Alon--Bollobás question and the Erdős--Sós $n^{3/2}$ (p. 346);
  the closing problems on cycles of different lengths in graphs of girth
  $\ge5$ and on graphs with many distinct degrees (pp. 346--347).

[[../wiki/problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0573/_index|#573]], the page cites

back more than 20 years: "Let $G(n)$ be a graph of $n$ vertices
