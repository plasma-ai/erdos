**Source.** Z. Füredi and D. Gerbner, Hypergraphs without exponents, J.
Combin. Theory Ser. A 184 (2021), Paper No. 105517,
doi:10.1016/j.jcta.2021.105517. Labels and pages are those of the arXiv
preprint arXiv:1906.06657v1 named on the
[[extremal_graph_theory/furedi_2021_hypergraphs_without_exponents/_index|source card]].

## Statement

**Conjecture** (p. 2, quoted). "We *conjecture* that examples with no
exponents should exist for $k=3$ and 4, too."

An example here is a single $k$-uniform hypergraph $H$ for which there is
no real $\alpha$ with $\mathrm{ex}_k(n,H)=\Theta(n^\alpha)$ (p. 2). The
abstract (p. 1) states the same conjecture for $k\in\{3,4\}$. For
$k\ge5$ such hypergraphs are given by
[[extremal_graph_theory/furedi_2021_hypergraphs_without_exponents/theorem_3_3|Theorem 3.3]], for instance
$Q_k(3)$. The paper recalls (p. 2) that the Ruzsa--Szemerédi
$(6,3)$-theorem gives a family of two 3-uniform hypergraphs with no
exponent, so the open case is that of a single forbidden hypergraph.
