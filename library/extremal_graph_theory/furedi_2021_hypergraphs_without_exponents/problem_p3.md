## Statement

**Problem** (p. 3, quoted). "Determine
$\limsup_{n\to\infty}\mathrm{ex}_k(n,Q_k(r))/n^{k-1}$ for
$4\leq k\leq 2r-2$."

Here $Q_k(r)$ is the hypergraph of Definition 3.2 (p. 2), stated on the
page of [[extremal_graph_theory/furedi_2021_hypergraphs_without_exponents/theorem_3_3|Theorem 3.3]]. For
$k\ge r\ge3$ the range $k\le2r-2$ is the range $r\ge(k/2)+1$, where
Theorem 3.3 gives $\mathrm{ex}_k(n,Q_k(r))=\Theta(n^{k-1})$, so the
problem asks for the constant.
