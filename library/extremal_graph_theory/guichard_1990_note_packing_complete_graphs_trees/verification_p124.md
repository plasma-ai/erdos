## Statement

The note states no numbered theorem. Its result is the two sentences of
p. 124 (page image): "Of the 106 trees on 10 vertices, all but one can be
removed from $H_{10}$ to leave one of the graphs in $\mathcal U_8$; the
exception, $T^*$, is shown in figure 1. Thus $H_{10}\notin\mathcal U_{10}$,
but we were able to show directly that all sequences of trees
$T_2,T_4,T_6,T_8,T^*$ could indeed be packed into $H_{10}$, proving the
Gyárfás--Lehel conjecture for $n=10$." and "Of the 235 trees on 11 vertices,
all but one can be removed from $H_{11}$ to leave one of the graphs in
$\mathcal U_9$; the exception, $T'$, is shown in figure 2. Thus
$H_{11}\notin\mathcal U_{11}$, but we were able to show directly that all
sequences of trees $T_3,T_5,T_7,T_9,T'$ could indeed be packed into $H_{11}$,
proving the Gyárfás--Lehel conjecture for $n=11$." The abstract (p. 123)
summarizes: "Using a computer, we have shown that the conjecture is true
through $n=11$ but also that an approach suggested by Fishburn is unlikely
to work in general."

Here $T_i$ is any tree on $i$ vertices, $H_n$ is Fishburn's half-complete
graph on $n$ vertices (the unique graph with degree sequence
$1,2,\ldots,\lfloor n/2\rfloor,\lfloor n/2\rfloor,\ldots,n-1$, so that
$H_{n-1}$ and $H_n$ pack into $K_n$), and $\mathcal U_n$ are Fishburn's
universally recursive families (p. 124). Packing the odd-order trees into
$H_{2k-1}$ and the even-order trees into $H_{2k}$ is Fishburn's route: the
two half-complete graphs pack into $K_n$, so the two packings together give
the packing of $T_2,\ldots,T_n$ into $K_n$. The count of trees, 106 on ten
vertices and 235 on eleven, is the number of unlabeled trees.

**Source.** D. R. Guichard and J. D. Massman, *A note on packing complete
graphs with trees*, J. Combin. Math. Combin. Comput. 8 (1990), 123--126;
printed p. 124 = PDF p. 2 of the retained publisher's copy, read on the page
image, with the abstract on p. 123 = PDF p. 1 and the figures on p. 125 =
PDF p. 3. The artifact is identified in the
[[extremal_graph_theory/guichard_1990_note_packing_complete_graphs_trees/_index|source digest]].

## Proof pointer

Pp. 124--125: the classes $\mathcal U_8$ and $\mathcal U_9$ were generated by
computer from $\mathcal U_6$ and $\mathcal U_7$ by attaching a star on $n+2$
vertices to each member in all admissible ways, the Lemma of p. 125 (at most
$\lceil(n+2)/2\rceil$ special vertices) pruning the candidates; membership
was tested by removing every tree on $n+2$ vertices. The exceptional trees
$T^*$ and $T'$ were handled "directly". The closing counts are
$|\mathcal U_8|=77$ and $|\mathcal U_9|=78$ (p. 126).
