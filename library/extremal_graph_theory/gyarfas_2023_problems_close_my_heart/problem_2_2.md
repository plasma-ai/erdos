**Source.** Theorem 2.1 and Problem 2.2, §2.1, p. 4, of András Gyárfás,
"Problems close to my heart," *European Journal of Combinatorics* **111**
(2023), 103695, doi:10.1016/j.ejc.2023.103695. Labels and pages are those of
the manuscript dated August 11, 2020, the edition identified on the
[[extremal_graph_theory/gyarfas_2023_problems_close_my_heart/_index|source card]].

## Statement

**Theorem 2.1** (p. 4; cited to Erdős and Gyárfás [7]). For every
2-coloring of the edges of $K_n$, the vertex set is covered by the vertices of
at most $2\sqrt n$ monochromatic paths, all of the same color.

**Problem 2.2** (p. 4; cited to [7]). Can the vertex set of every 2-colored
$K_n$ always be covered by at most $\sqrt n$ monochromatic paths of the same
color? The paper adds that this "would be best possible."

The paper notes (p. 4) that in both statements the covering paths need not be
even edge-disjoint. Without the same-color requirement, two monochromatic
paths always partition the vertex set, the footnote of Gerencsér and Gyárfás
[13] that the section starts from.

**Proof.** The paper gives none; Theorem 2.1 is the bound of [7], whose
corpus page is
[[ramsey_theory/erdos_1995_vertex_covering_monochromatic_paths/corollary_2|Corollary 2 of Erdős and Gyárfás (1995)]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: Theorem 2.1, Problem 2.2 and the remark on
edge-disjointness were read clause by clause on p. 4.

## Scope

A reported theorem and an open question as the paper states them; the
manuscript records no progress on Problem 2.2.
