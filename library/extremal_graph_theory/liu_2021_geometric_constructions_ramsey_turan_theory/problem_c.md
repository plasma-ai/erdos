## Statement

**Problem C** ([12, 14, 35, 36]). Is $\mathsf{RT}_2(n,K_{2,2,2},o(n))=o(n^2)$?

Here $\mathsf{RT}_2(n,H,m)$ is the maximum number of edges of an $n$-vertex
$H$-free graph with independence number at most $m$ (p. 1, with $p=2$). The
text before it (p. 5) lists Ramsey--Turán work on graphs other than cliques,
on hypergraphs, in the multicolour setting and on counting, and then calls
the octahedron question "A particular tantalising open problem". The paper's
[14] is the 1983 paper of Erdős, Hajnal, Sós and Szemerédi.

**Source.** H. Liu, C. Reiher, M. Sharifzadeh and K. Staden, *Geometric
constructions for Ramsey-Turán theory*, arXiv:2103.10423v2 (18 August 2025),
retained; Journal of the European Mathematical Society, vol. 28, no. 1,
79--112, doi:10.4171/jems/1712 (Crossref record read; the journal
text is not held). Problem C on p. 6 of the retained version and the text
before it on p. 5, read on the page images. The artifact is identified in
the
[[extremal_graph_theory/liu_2021_geometric_constructions_ramsey_turan_theory/_index|source digest]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the problem and its introduction were read
clause by clause on the page images. It states a question and proves
nothing; the paper's results concern cliques and do not treat $K_{2,2,2}$.
