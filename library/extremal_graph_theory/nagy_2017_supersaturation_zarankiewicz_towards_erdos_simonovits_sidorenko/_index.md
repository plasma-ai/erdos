# extremal_graph_theory/nagy_2017_supersaturation_zarankiewicz_towards_erdos_simonovits_sidorenko

[[extremal_graph_theory/_index|..]]

[[extremal_graph_theory/nagy_2017_supersaturation_zarankiewicz_towards_erdos_simonovits_sidorenko/theorem_1_10|theorem_1_10]]: Nagy's theorem that for a fixed positive integer k and m = (√k + o(1))n√n,
the least number of four-cycles in an m-edge subgraph of K_{n,n}, divided
by n^2, tends to k(k−1)/4, against k^2/4 for the random balanced bipartite
graph.

[[extremal_graph_theory/nagy_2017_supersaturation_zarankiewicz_towards_erdos_simonovits_sidorenko/theorem_1_9|theorem_1_9]]: Nagy's four-regime lower bound on the least number of four-cycles in a
subgraph of K_{n,n} with m = n(√n + 1/2) + ξ(n) edges, from Ω(n) for
ξ(n) = O(√n) up to the random-graph count (1/4)(m/n)^4 for ξ(n) much larger
than n√n.

[[extremal_graph_theory/nagy_2017_supersaturation_zarankiewicz_towards_erdos_simonovits_sidorenko/theorem_2_1|theorem_2_1]]: Nagy's lower bound on the number of copies of K_{a,b}, with the a-side in
one fixed class, in a subgraph of K_{n,n} with m edges, in terms of the
average degree m/n and truncated binomial coefficients.

[[extremal_graph_theory/nagy_2017_supersaturation_zarankiewicz_towards_erdos_simonovits_sidorenko/theorem_3_1|theorem_3_1]]: Nagy's theorem that for n = q^2+q+1 with q a prime power and
m ≤ z(n,n,2,2) + n, the least number of four-cycles in an m-edge subgraph
of K_{n,n} equals the improved theoretical lower bound; Proposition 3.7
shows that graphs containing the projective plane's incidence graph miss
that bound for z(n,n,2,2) + n < m ≤ √2 z(n,n,2,2).

[[extremal_graph_theory/nagy_2017_supersaturation_zarankiewicz_towards_erdos_simonovits_sidorenko/theorem_4_5|theorem_4_5]]: Nagy's theorem that for fixed positive integers t > 2 and k and
m = (√k + o(1))n√n, the least number of copies of K_{2,t} in an m-edge
subgraph of K_{n,n}, divided by n^2, tends to the binomial coefficient
k choose t.

***

Zoltán Lóránt Nagy, Supersaturation of $C_4$: from Zarankiewicz towards
Erdős-Simonovits-Sidorenko. arXiv preprint (2017). arXiv:1711.09282,
doi:10.48550/arXiv.1711.09282. The arXiv record names arXiv's non-exclusive
distribution license (arXiv:1711.09282), every other right reserved; it lists
only v1 (25 November 2017) and no journal reference (read 2026-10-07).
Published in Eur. J. Combin. 75 (2019), 19--31,
doi:10.1016/j.ejc.2018.07.015 (Crossref record read); the
journal text was not compared.

The arXiv identifier 1711.09282 identifies Nagy's Supersaturation of C_4: from
Zarankiewicz towards Erdos-Simonovits-Sidorenko. It is not a general
dominant-member result. Nagy studies supersaturation for K_{2,t} in subgraphs
of K_{n,n}, giving exact results for C_4 when n = q^2 + q + 1 for a prime
power q and the number of edges exceeds the Zarankiewicz number z(n,n,2,2) by
at most n (Theorem 3.1, p. 10), via finite-geometric constructions, and
asymptotically sharp counts of C_4 (Theorem 1.10, p. 5) and of K_{2,t} for
t > 2 (Theorem 4.5, p. 14) when the number of edges is
(sqrt(k) + o(1)) n sqrt(n) for a fixed positive integer k. Stability questions
are also treated, together with links to covering and packing block designs.
For problem 180, it serves only as a negative comparison: it counts copies of
C_4 and K_{2,t} above the Zarankiewicz number, and it bears neither on problem
180's question whether some member of a finite forbidden family has an
extremal number comparable to the family's nor on problem 575, the same
question for a bipartite member.

**Results.**

- [[extremal_graph_theory/nagy_2017_supersaturation_zarankiewicz_towards_erdos_simonovits_sidorenko/theorem_1_9|Theorem 1.9]]
  (p. 4): lower bounds on the least number of $C_4$ in an $m$-edge subgraph of
  $K_{n,n}$ for $m=n(\sqrt n+\frac12)+\xi(n)$, in four ranges of $\xi(n)$.
- [[extremal_graph_theory/nagy_2017_supersaturation_zarankiewicz_towards_erdos_simonovits_sidorenko/theorem_1_10|Theorem 1.10]]
  (p. 5): for fixed $k$ and $m=(\sqrt k+o(1))n\sqrt n$, that least number
  divided by $n^2$ tends to $k(k-1)/4$.
- [[extremal_graph_theory/nagy_2017_supersaturation_zarankiewicz_towards_erdos_simonovits_sidorenko/theorem_2_1|Theorem 2.1]]
  (p. 5): the lower bound on the number of $K_{a,b}$ with the $a$-side in a
  fixed class in an $m$-edge subgraph of $K_{n,n}$, behind the other
  results, with its improved form (p. 7).
- [[extremal_graph_theory/nagy_2017_supersaturation_zarankiewicz_towards_erdos_simonovits_sidorenko/theorem_3_1|Theorem 3.1]]
  (p. 10): for $n=q^2+q+1$, $q$ a prime power, and $m\le z(n,n,2,2)+n$, the
  least number of $C_4$ meets the improved lower bound; with Proposition 3.7
  (p. 11).
- [[extremal_graph_theory/nagy_2017_supersaturation_zarankiewicz_towards_erdos_simonovits_sidorenko/theorem_4_5|Theorem 4.5]]
  (p. 14): for fixed $t>2$ and $k$ and $m=(\sqrt k+o(1))n\sqrt n$, the least
  number of $K_{2,t}$ divided by $n^2$ tends to $\binom kt$.
