# extremal_graph_theory/narins_2017_graphs_without_proper_subgraphs_minimum_degree

[[extremal_graph_theory/_index|..]]

[[extremal_graph_theory/narins_2017_graphs_without_proper_subgraphs_minimum_degree/lemma_2_1|lemma_2_1]]: For an even 1-3 tree T, the graph G(T) obtained by joining two new adjacent
vertices to every leaf has a cycle of length 2k + 1 exactly when T has a
leaf-to-leaf path of length 2k - 2, with a matching rule for even cycles.

[[extremal_graph_theory/narins_2017_graphs_without_proper_subgraphs_minimum_degree/lemma_4_2|lemma_4_2]]: Every graph on n >= 2 vertices with at least 2n - 2 edges has an induced
subgraph of minimum degree 3, so a degree 3-critical graph is the only such
subgraph of itself.

[[extremal_graph_theory/narins_2017_graphs_without_proper_subgraphs_minimum_degree/problem_6_1|problem_6_1]]: The paper's Problem 6.1 asks whether some function C(n) tending to infinity
makes every degree 3-critical graph on n vertices contain cycles of all even
lengths from 4 to 2C(n).

[[extremal_graph_theory/narins_2017_graphs_without_proper_subgraphs_minimum_degree/proposition_5_1|proposition_5_1]]: Every graph with n >= 6 vertices, 2n - 2 edges and no proper induced
subgraph of minimum degree 3 contains a cycle of length 6.

[[extremal_graph_theory/narins_2017_graphs_without_proper_subgraphs_minimum_degree/theorem_1_2|theorem_1_2]]: There are arbitrarily large graphs with n vertices, 2n − 2 edges and no
proper induced subgraph of minimum degree 3 that contain no 23-cycle,
disproving the Erdős–Faudree–Gyárfás–Schelp conjecture that all short
cycles appear.

[[extremal_graph_theory/narins_2017_graphs_without_proper_subgraphs_minimum_degree/theorem_1_3|theorem_1_3]]: Every sufficiently large even 1-3 tree has leaf-to-leaf paths of all even
lengths from 0 to 18, while some infinite family of even 1-3 trees has no
leaf-to-leaf path of length 20.

[[extremal_graph_theory/narins_2017_graphs_without_proper_subgraphs_minimum_degree/theorem_1_4|theorem_1_4]]: Under the literal, non-induced reading of the 1988 definition the graphs
with 2n − 2 edges are wheels or a modified wheel family and contain cycles
of every length from 3 to n.

[[extremal_graph_theory/narins_2017_graphs_without_proper_subgraphs_minimum_degree/theorem_4_1|theorem_4_1]]: A graph on n vertices with 2n − 2 edges has no proper subgraph, induced or
not, of minimum degree 3 exactly when it is a wheel or is obtained by
identifying the two connectors of a graph H_i with those of a graph H_j.

The paper studies degree $3$-critical graphs: graphs on $n$ vertices with
$2n-2$ edges having no proper induced subgraph of minimum degree $3$ (the
term follows Bollobás and Brightwell, p. 2). Theorem 1.2 disproves the
conjecture of Erdős, Faudree, Gyárfás and Schelp that such graphs contain all
cycles of lengths $3,4,\ldots,C(n)$ with $C(n)$ tending to infinity, by
exhibiting an infinite family of degree $3$-critical graphs with no cycle of
length $23$. A historical remark (p. 3) records that the 1988 paper's
definition reads "no proper subgraph has minimum degree 3", that its
Examples 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6 have proper non-induced subgraphs of minimum degree
$3$, and that its results and proofs hold under the induced reading, so that it
is "plausible to assume" that the word "induced" belongs in the conjecture. The
construction rests on Theorem 1.3, a result of independent interest about
leaf-to-leaf path lengths in even $1$-$3$ trees: every sufficiently large such
tree has leaf-to-leaf paths of all even lengths up to $18$, but there is an
infinite family with no leaf-to-leaf path of length $20$; for a $1$-$3$ tree
$T$, $G(T)$, the tree with two adjacent vertices joined to all its leaves, is
degree $3$-critical, and for an even one its odd cycles correspond to
leaf-to-leaf paths (Lemma 2.1). Theorem 1.4 shows
that if "induced" is dropped from the definition, the graphs with $n$
vertices, $2n-2$ edges and no proper subgraph of minimum degree $3$ are
pancyclic, from a structure theorem (Theorem 4.1) identifying them as the
wheels and one modified wheel family. Section 5 (Proposition 5.1) verifies
that every degree $3$-critical graph on at least $6$ vertices contains a
$6$-cycle, so the smallest cycle length missing from some infinite family lies
between $7$ and $23$; Section 6 notes that the method can forbid any one odd
cycle length $m\ge23$ and asks about even cycles (Problem 6.1). For
Problem 815 this settles the conjecture in the negative and leaves the even
case open.

## Contents

- Conjecture 1.1 (p. 2, Erdős, Faudree, Gyárfás and Schelp): for some
  increasing function $C(n)$, each degree $3$-critical graph on $n$ vertices
  has a cycle of every length from $3$ to $C(n)$. The historical remark on
  the missing word "induced" (p. 3). The earlier results as the paper
  reports them (p. 2): on $n\ge5$ vertices, cycles of lengths $3$, $4$, $5$
  and of length at least $\lfloor\log_2n\rfloor$ but not necessarily more
  than $\sqrt n$ (Erdős, Faudree, Gyárfás, Schelp); the longest cycle at
  least $4\log_2n-o(\log n)$ and constructions with none longer than
  $4\log_2n+O(1)$ (Bollobás and Brightwell, Discrete Math. 75 (1989), 47--53,
  not held); Erdős's conjecture that $2n-1$ edges force a degree
  $3$-critical subgraph on at most $(1-\epsilon)n$ vertices (cf. the 1990
  paper with Faudree, Rousseau and Schelp).
- [[extremal_graph_theory/narins_2017_graphs_without_proper_subgraphs_minimum_degree/theorem_1_2|Theorem 1.2]] (p. 3): some infinite sequence $(G_n)_{n=1}^\infty$ of degree
  $3$-critical graphs has no member containing a cycle of length $23$.
- [[extremal_graph_theory/narins_2017_graphs_without_proper_subgraphs_minimum_degree/theorem_1_3|Theorem 1.3]] (p. 3): (i) there is $N_0$ such that every even $1$-$3$ tree
  with at least $N_0$ vertices has leaf-to-leaf paths of lengths
  $0,2,4,\ldots,18$; (ii) some infinite family $(T_n)_{n=1}^\infty$ of even
  $1$-$3$ trees has no member with a leaf-to-leaf path of length $20$.
- [[extremal_graph_theory/narins_2017_graphs_without_proper_subgraphs_minimum_degree/theorem_1_4|Theorem 1.4]] (p. 4): every graph on $n$ vertices with $2n-2$ edges in which
  no proper subgraph, induced or not, has minimum degree $3$ is pancyclic;
  from [[extremal_graph_theory/narins_2017_graphs_without_proper_subgraphs_minimum_degree/theorem_4_1|Theorem 4.1]] (p. 15), the family consists of all wheels and
  the graphs formed by identifying the two connectors of a copy of $H_i$ with
  those of a copy of $H_j$.
- The construction (p. 4): $G(T)$ adds to a tree $T$ two adjacent vertices
  joined to every leaf; for a $1$-$3$ tree it is degree $3$-critical; Lemma
  2.1 ([[extremal_graph_theory/narins_2017_graphs_without_proper_subgraphs_minimum_degree/lemma_2_1|page]]): for an even $1$-$3$ tree, $G(T)$ has a $C_{2k+1}$ if and only if $T$
  has a leaf-to-leaf path of length $2k-2$, and a $C_{2k}$ if and only if $T$
  has two vertex-disjoint leaf-to-leaf paths of total length $2k-4$ or one of
  length $2k-2$.
- [[extremal_graph_theory/narins_2017_graphs_without_proper_subgraphs_minimum_degree/lemma_4_2|Lemma 4.2]] (p. 15): a graph with $n\ge2$ vertices and at least $2n-2$
  edges has an induced subgraph of minimum degree $3$. Lemma 4.3
  (p. 16): an ordering of a degree $3$-critical graph with $d^+(x_1)=3$,
  $d^+(x_i)=2$ for $2\le i\le n-2$, $d^+(x_{n-1})=1$ and, for $n\ge7$,
  $d(x_n)\ge4$.
- [[extremal_graph_theory/narins_2017_graphs_without_proper_subgraphs_minimum_degree/proposition_5_1|Proposition 5.1]] (p. 19): every degree $3$-critical graph with $n\ge6$
  contains a $C_6$.
- Section 6 (p. 21): degree $3$-critical graphs with no $m$-cycle for any
  odd $m\ge23$; the least missing length lies between $7$ and $23$; [[extremal_graph_theory/narins_2017_graphs_without_proper_subgraphs_minimum_degree/problem_6_1|Problem 6.1]]
  (cycles of all lengths $4,6,\ldots,2C(n)$?); Conjecture 6.2 (at least
  $3\log_2n+O(1)$ distinct cycle lengths); Conjectures 6.3--6.4 on
  leaf-to-leaf path lengths in $1$-$3$ trees. Di Braccio, Katsamaktsis, Ma,
  Malekshahian and Zhao (Combinatorica 46 (2026), article 11; arXiv:2504.11656)
  prove Conjecture 6.2 up to a constant factor, prove a corrected form of
  Conjecture 6.3, disprove Conjecture 6.4 and restate Problem 6.1 as open
  (their Problem F); their arXiv v2 is held on its own card, the journal
  text is not.
- References (p. 22): [3] prints the 1988 paper's pages as "25(B):159--201";
  the paper's own pagination is 195--201.
