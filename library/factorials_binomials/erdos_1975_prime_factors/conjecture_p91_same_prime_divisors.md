## Statement

**Conjecture** (p. 91). The paper writes that "there is no doubt" that
there are infinitely many pairs $\binom{2m}{m}$, $\binom{2n}{n}$ with the
same set of prime divisors, gives the examples

$$
\binom{174}{87},\ \binom{176}{88}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\binom{1214}{607},\ \binom{1216}{608},
$$

and says the authors are "not at present able to prove this" (p. 91).

**Source.** P. Erdős, R. L. Graham, I. Z. Ruzsa and E. G. Straus, On the
prime factors of $\binom{2n}{n}$, Math. Comp. 29 (1975), no. 129, 83--92;
the conjecture on p. 91. The edition is identified on the
[[factorials_binomials/erdos_1975_prime_factors/_index|source card]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the statement and the two examples were
read on the page image. The examples were not recomputed here.
