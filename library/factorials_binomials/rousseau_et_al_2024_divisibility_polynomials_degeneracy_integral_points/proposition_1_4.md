## Statement

**Proposition 1.4** (p. 2). In the setting of
[[factorials_binomials/rousseau_et_al_2024_divisibility_polynomials_degeneracy_integral_points/theorem_1_3|Theorem 1.3]],
suppose the divisor $D_0+D_1+\cdots+D_r$ has simple normal crossing
singularities. Then the variety $X\setminus D$ of Theorem 1.3 is simply
connected.

The paper compares it with Corvaja and Zannier's Theorem 3, and says that
Theorem 1.3 yields simply connected varieties whose integral points are not
Zariski-dense, generalizing Corvaja and Zannier's construction to arbitrary
dimension (p. 2).

**Source.** Erwan Rousseau, Amos Turchet, Julie Tzu-Yueh Wang, Divisibility
of polynomials and degeneracy of integral points, Math. Ann. 388 (2024),
no. 2, 1969--1999; labels and pages are those of the preprint
arXiv:2106.11337v1, the edition identified on the
[[factorials_binomials/rousseau_et_al_2024_divisibility_polynomials_degeneracy_integral_points/_index|source card]].
Statement and proof on p. 2.

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the statement was read clause by clause on the
page image; the short proof was read for its structure only.

## Proof pointer

P. 2. The case $n=2$ is cited to the authors' earlier paper. For $n\ge3$, a
small loop around $\widetilde D_i$ contracts in $X\setminus D$ because the
exceptional divisor over $D_i\cap D_0$, minus $D$, has generic fibre
isomorphic to $\mathbb C$ over $D_i\cap D_0$.
