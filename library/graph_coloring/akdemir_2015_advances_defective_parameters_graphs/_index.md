# graph_coloring/akdemir_2015_advances_defective_parameters_graphs

[[graph_coloring/_index|..]]

***

A. Akdemir and T. Ekim, Advances on defective parameters in graphs, Discrete
Optimization 16 (2015), 62--69, doi:10.1016/j.disopt.2015.01.002, published
online 2015-02-08, in the issue of May 2015.

No copy of the paper and no abstract was read for this card; it rests on the
publisher's bibliographic record (Crossref: Discrete Optimization 16, pp.
62--69, issue of May 2015) and on the citations recorded on the problem's
claim page (Demirci, Ekim and Yildiz, arXiv:2107.12031, and the lean-proofs
informal-source list). As those citations report it, the paper writes c_0(m)
for the largest n such that every graph on n vertices can be partitioned into
at most m sets each inducing a complete or an empty graph, so that the
cochromatic number z(n) is at most m exactly when n <= c_0(m), and treats the
general defective version c_k(m). It proves c_0(4) = 12 by a computer-assisted
proof built on an efficient graph generation method, and also establishes
c_1(3) = 12 and c_2(2) = 10. For problem 758, c_0(4) = 12 gives z(12) <= 4 and
z(13) >= 5; with the lower bound z(12) >= 4 of Erdős and Gimbel this is
z(12) = 4, and with z(13) <= z(12) + 1 it is z(13) = 5.
