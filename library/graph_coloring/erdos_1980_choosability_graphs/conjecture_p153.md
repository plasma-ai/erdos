## Statement

**Context** (p. 153). Since every planar graph has a node of valence at most
$5$, the paper says it follows easily that every planar graph is
$6$-choosable.

**Conjecture** (p. 153, quoted). "Every planar graph is $5$-choosable."

**Conjecture** (p. 153, quoted). "There exists a planar graph which is not
$4$-choosable."

## Proof pointer

The paper proves neither conjecture, and for the $6$-choosability of planar
graphs it says only that it follows easily. It follows by removing a node of valence at most $5$, choosing for the rest by
induction, and then choosing a letter at the removed node that none of its
neighbours used.
