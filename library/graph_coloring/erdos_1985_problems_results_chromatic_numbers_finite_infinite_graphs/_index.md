# graph_coloring/erdos_1985_problems_results_chromatic_numbers_finite_infinite_graphs

[[graph_coloring/_index|..]]

[[graph_coloring/erdos_1985_problems_results_chromatic_numbers_finite_infinite_graphs/problem_p206|problem_p206]]: Erdős's 1985 restatement of the El-Zahar-Erdős problem, with the report
that the case of 3-chromatic subgraphs is proved for every excluded clique
order and that great difficulties appeared for 4-chromatic subgraphs.

***

P. Erdős, *Problems and results on chromatic numbers in finite and infinite
graphs*, Graph theory with applications to algorithms and computer science
(Kalamazoo, Mich., 1984), Wiley-Interscience, Wiley, New York (1985),
201--213; MR 87f:05068 (MR 812666 per the site's reference text).
The site's reference key Er85b (its reference text read). The
paper's first two sentences (p. 201): "During my long life I wrote many papers
of similar title. To avoid repetitions and to shorten the paper I will discuss
almost entirely recent problems and will not give proofs."

**Edition read.** The copy read for this card
is the Rényi archive's scan `1985-26.pdf`: 13 pages, an image scan of the
typescript offprint with an OCR text layer that garbles the Hebrew letters
and subscripts; the running heads print 201 to 213, so printed p. $n$ is
PDF p. $n-200$. Provenance: retrieved from

bytes. The scan prints "Copyright © 1985 by John Wiley & Sons, Inc." in the

Read status: claims checked for the El-Zahar passage (printed p. 206 = PDF
p. 6), read clause by clause on the page image. The other pages (pp.
201--205 and 207--213) were read on the page images for the contents below;
their problems are not compiled. The paper gives no proofs (p. 201), so
there is no proof to check.

## Contents

- Pp. 201--206: problems on the chromatic number of products of graphs (the
  Hedetniemi-type conjecture, "known (and easy) for $k\le3$ and Sauer and
  El-Zahar proved it for $k=4$ not long ago", p. 201), and transfinite problems
  on subgraphs of graphs of uncountable chromatic number (with Hajnal, Komjáth,
  Shelah, Taylor's conjecture), read for the contents only.
- P. 206 (page image), "Now I discuss some finite problems": the El-Zahar--Erdős
  problem, "Is it true that for every $k$ and $\ell$ there is an $n(k,\ell)$ so
  that if the chromatic number of $G$ is $\ge n(k,\ell)$ and $G$ contains no
  $K(\ell)$, then $G$ contains two vertex-disjoint $k$-chromatic subgraphs $G_1$
  and $G_2$ so that there is no edge between $G_1$ and $G_2$?", with "We proved
  this for $k=3$ and every $\ell$, but great difficulties appeared for $k=4$",
  Rödl's suggestion that the probability method might give a
  counterexample, and the "simplest unsolved problem" about two independent
  edges in a $5$-chromatic $K(4)$-free graph; paged at
  [[graph_coloring/erdos_1985_problems_results_chromatic_numbers_finite_infinite_graphs/problem_p206|problem_p206]].
- Pp. 206--212: further finite problems (the forcing relation
  $(n,e)\to(k,\ell)$ posed with Rothschild, the function $f(n;k,\ell)$ with
  Fan Chung, the cube, Sauer's quasiregularity conjecture, Toft's question and
  edge-critical graphs, Folkman-type graphs, the Hajnal--Erdős girth
  conjecture and its infinite version, and later problems on
  $k$-chromatic graphs), read on the page images for the contents only, not
  compiled; follow-ups for the pages that cite this key.
- Pp. 212--213: the reference list (items 1--16).

## Compiled scope

All thirteen pages were read on the page images; the El-Zahar passage (p.
206) alone is compiled, and nothing here is independently reviewed.
