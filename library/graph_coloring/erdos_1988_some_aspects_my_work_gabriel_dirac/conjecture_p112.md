## Statement

**Question** (p. 112, unnumbered). Is there an edge-critical four-chromatic
graph on $n$ vertices each vertex of which has degree $>c_1n$?

**Conjecture** (p. 112). Erdős conjectures, "with some trepidation", that such
a graph does not exist. The paper does not quantify $c_1$ or $n$ further.

Reported alongside (p. 112):

- The strongest known result, due to Simonovits [10] and Toft [15]: there is
  a four-chromatic edge-critical graph $G(n)$ on $n$ vertices each vertex
  of which has degree $>cn^{1/3}$.
- Dirac's six-chromatic edge-critical graph is regular of degree $n/2+2$.
- Erdős thinks a four-critical regular graph of degree $c_1n$ on $n$
  vertices unlikely but not impossible. He knows no regular edge-critical
  four-chromatic graph of degree $\ell$ for $\ell\ge6$, and expects such
  graphs to exist for every $\ell$.

## Proof pointer

The question and conjecture carry no proof. The reported results are
M. Simonovits, *On colour-critical graphs*, Studia Sci. Math. Hungar. **7**
(1972), 67--81, and B. Toft, *Two theorems on critical 4-chromatic graphs*,
Studia Sci. Math. Hungar. **7** (1972), 83--89, the paper's references [10]
and [15].

minimum degree linear in $n$. The paper conjectures the answer is no and
