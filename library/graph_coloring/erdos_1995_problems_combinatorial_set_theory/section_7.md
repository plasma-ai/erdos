## Statement

Section 7 is on printed p. 64. Let $G_1$ and $G_2$ be graphs of uncountable
chromatic number. Erdős asks whether there is a graph $G$ of chromatic number
$4$ that is a subgraph of both. Perhaps, he adds, there is a common subgraph
of infinite chromatic number, though this may be too optimistic.

**Source.** P. Erdős, *On some problems in combinatorial set theory*, Publ.
Inst. Math. (Beograd) (N.S.) 57(71) (1995), 61–65; Section 7, printed p. 64.
