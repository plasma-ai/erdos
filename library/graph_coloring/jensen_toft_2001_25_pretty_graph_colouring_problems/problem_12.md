## Statement

**Problem 12** (Dirac, 1957; Gallai, 1963; Ore, 1967; p. 168, quoted).
"What is the minimum number of edges of a $k$-critical graph on $n$
vertices? Is it $\lfloor 5n/3\rfloor$ for $k=4$?"

The paper does not define "$k$-critical", gives no bounds or
constructions, and proves nothing about the problem.

**Source.** T. R. Jensen and B. Toft, 25 Pretty graph colouring problems,
Discrete Math. 229 (2001), 167--169, doi:10.1016/S0012-365X(00)00206-5; the
edition read is named on the
[[graph_coloring/jensen_toft_2001_25_pretty_graph_colouring_problems/_index|source card]].
