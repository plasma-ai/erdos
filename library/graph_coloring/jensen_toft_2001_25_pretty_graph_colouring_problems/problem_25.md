## Statement

**Problem 25** (Erdős, 1985; p. 169, quoted). "Given two graphs of
uncountable chromatic numbers, is there a 4-chromatic graph which is
isomorphic to a subgraph of both?"

The paper poses the question only and proves nothing about it.

**Source.** T. R. Jensen and B. Toft, 25 Pretty graph colouring problems,
Discrete Math. 229 (2001), 167--169, doi:10.1016/S0012-365X(00)00206-5; the
edition read is named on the
[[graph_coloring/jensen_toft_2001_25_pretty_graph_colouring_problems/_index|source card]].

## Bears on

- [[../wiki/problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0062/_index|Problem 62]]:
  Problem 62 asks the same question for two graphs of chromatic number
  exactly $\aleph_1$, and also asks for a common subgraph of chromatic
  number $\aleph_0$. Graphs of chromatic number $\aleph_1$ have
  uncountable chromatic number, so a yes to Problem 25 gives a yes to
  Problem 62's $4$-chromatic question, and a no to that question gives a
  no to Problem 25. The converse implications are not immediate, and the
  paper settles neither problem.
