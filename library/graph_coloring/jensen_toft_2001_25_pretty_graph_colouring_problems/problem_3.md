## Statement

**Problem 3** (Hadwiger; Nelson, 1961; p. 167, quoted). "What is the
chromatic number $k$ of the graph whose vertices are all points in the
plane, with two vertices joined by an edge when they are of distance 1? It
is known that $4\leqslant k\leqslant 7$."

The bounds $4\le k\le 7$ are stated as known, with no proof and no
reference beyond the paper's one reference, Jensen and Toft's 1995 monograph
*Graph Coloring Problems*. The paper proves nothing about the problem.

**Source.** T. R. Jensen and B. Toft, 25 Pretty graph colouring problems,
Discrete Math. 229 (2001), 167--169, doi:10.1016/S0012-365X(00)00206-5; the
edition read is named on the
[[graph_coloring/jensen_toft_2001_25_pretty_graph_colouring_problems/_index|source card]].
