## Statement

**Problem 4** (Erdős, Faber and Lovász, 1972; p. 167, quoted). "If a simple
graph $G$ is the edge-disjoint union of $k$ complete $k$-graphs, is
$G$ then $k$-colourable?"

The paper writes "complete $k$-graph" for the complete graph on $k$
vertices, as in its Problems 5 and 10. It poses the question only and proves
nothing about it.

**Source.** T. R. Jensen and B. Toft, 25 Pretty graph colouring problems,
Discrete Math. 229 (2001), 167--169, doi:10.1016/S0012-365X(00)00206-5; the
edition read is named on the
[[graph_coloring/jensen_toft_2001_25_pretty_graph_colouring_problems/_index|source card]].

## Bears on

- [[../wiki/problems/graph_coloring/E0019/_index|Problem 19]]: such a graph
  contains a complete graph on $k$ vertices, so $\chi(G)\ge k$, and
  $G$ is $k$-colourable exactly when $\chi(G)=k$. With $n=k$,
  Problem 4 is therefore Problem 19's question. The paper poses it and
  settles nothing.
