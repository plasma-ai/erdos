The unnumbered Main theorem on p. 2 includes a uniform quantitative
conclusion for $G_n\sim G(n,1/2)$:

$$
\mathbb P\!\left(
\chi(G_n)-\zeta(G_n)\geq
\frac{(\log 2)^2}{4}\log\!\left(\frac{200}{153}\right)
\frac{n}{(\log n)^3}
\right)\longrightarrow 1.
$$

All logarithms are natural. The
[[library/graph_coloring/petkov_2026_full_sequence_chromatic_cochromatic_gap/main_theorem|uniform main theorem]]
records this exact conclusion and its proof pointer. It addresses the
full integer sequence in [[problems/graph_coloring/E0625|E625]], whereas
Heckel's earlier result applies only under a phase-dependent restriction.
A matching upper bound and the optimal constant remain open. The stronger
phase-dependent coefficient is a separate manuscript claim outside the
extracted result and the reported formal theorem's scope.
