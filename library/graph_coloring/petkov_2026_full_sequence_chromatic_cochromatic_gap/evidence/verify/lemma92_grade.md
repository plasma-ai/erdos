## Rederivation spot-checks

**Counting identity (event-restricted).** Faithful and correct. The REPORT
rederives `S_R(r)=T_R(r)·1{r>=2}` (both sides zero for `r<2`; equal for `r>=2`,
valid at `R=1,2`), hence `Phi_F = (prod_{E_0} T_R(r'_e))·1{F\M ⊆ H_res}`, so
summing over the even sets of the complete cell graph and applying (6.7) gives
`sum_F Phi_F = (prod_{E_0} T_R(r'_e))·2^{beta(M∪H_res)}`; on the event, cap gives
`T_R(r'_e)=g(r'_e)` by telescoping `1 + sum_{x=3}^{r}(g(x)-g(x-1)) = 1 + g(r) - g(2) = g(r)` (g(2)=1)
and no-return gives `g=1` on `M`, yielding
`(prod g)·2^{beta}=sum_F Phi_F` on `E`. I checked (6.5) and (6.7) on p. 23-24 and
the printed identity at the top of p. 42; all match. The REPORT re-proved (6.7)
itself from the F2 incidence-map kernel rather than merely quoting it. Its two
boundary counterexamples (no-return `U=7,R=3`, giving actual attachment 0; off-M
cap `U=6,R=3`, truncation `1+Δ3=4` vs `g(6)=2^14`) are arithmetically correct and
correctly show only that dropping the event breaks the identity, which the frozen
proof never does. "No concrete defect" is justified here.

**One-joint-demand expansion (Lemma 9.2 fixed-F bound).** Faithful and correct.
The REPORT expands `Phi_F` into monomials making one choice per cell (demand 2 at
a selected cell, or a single higher `x∈{3..R}`; 0 or a single higher `x` at an
unselected cell), so no cell ever carries two demands and no spurious
`theta^{2+x}` appears; each monomial's indicator is the joint exceedance
indicator, and (9.7)/(6.9) is applied once to the complete array with `c>=0` and
`1_E<=1`, then the deterministic per-cell weights sum by distributivity to `q_e`
(selected) and `1+lambda_e` (unselected). This matches the source proof on p. 42
and the specialization of Lemma 6.2 on p. 24 (`D=supp(x)`, residual degree lists,
infeasible demands empty). The increments check out: `Δ3=g(3)-g(2)=4-1=3` and
`g(x)/g(x-1)=2^{x-1}>1` for `x>=4`. I verified the REPORT's correlated-cell attack
on the `2x2`, all-degree-2, `m_0=4` system: `P(r'_12=2)=P(r'_21=2)=1/6` with
intersection `1/6` (not `1/36`), and the joint bound `theta^2/2·theta^2/2=e^4/4`
holds — establishing that a product-of-marginals bound would be false and the
single joint application is essential. "No concrete defect" is justified here.

**Summation to (9.8).** Faithful and correct. Multiplying the step-1 identity by
