## Statement

Setting as on
[[graph_coloring/scott_2018_survey_chi_boundedness/conjecture_3_1|3.1]]: a
graph $H$ is $\chi$-bounding if the ideal of $H$-free graphs is
$\chi$-bounded.

**3.5** (p. 6, quoted). "Trees that can be obtained from a tree of radius
two by subdividing once some edges incident with the root are
$\chi$-bounding."

The survey presents it as the authors' unification (its reference [112]) of
the Kierstead–Penrice theorem for trees of radius two, where no edge is
subdivided, and the Kierstead–Zhu theorem, where every edge at the root is
subdivided once (pp. 5–6, Figure 1).

## Proof pointer
