## Statement

Commuting subgroups and pure transversal coset partitions are as defined on
the
[[group_theory/akman_sissokho_2025_transversal_coset_partitions_groups/theorem_3|Theorem 3]]
and
[[group_theory/akman_sissokho_2025_transversal_coset_partitions_groups/theorem_2|Theorem 2]]
pages.

**Theorem 5** (p. 3). "For any group $G$ with four mutually commuting
distinct proper subgroups, one of which has index 2 in $G$, Conjecture 1
holds." By Conjecture 1 as stated on p. 2, this says: if $H_1,\ldots,H_4$ are
mutually commuting distinct proper subgroups of $G$ and some $[G:H_i]=2$,
there is no pure $\{H_1,\ldots,H_4\}$-transversal coset partition of $G$.

**Source.** F. Akman and P. A. Sissokho, *Transversal coset partitions of
groups*, Beitr. Algebra Geom. 66 (2025), no. 2, 417--441,
doi:10.1007/s13366-024-00748-9. Labels and pages are those of the authors'
manuscript identified on the
[[group_theory/akman_sissokho_2025_transversal_coset_partitions_groups/_index|source card]]:
Theorem 5 on p. 3, its proof in Section 5 on p. 12.

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the statement and its one-sentence proof were
read.

## Proof pointer

Section 5, p. 12. The paper's proof is one sentence: as in the proof of
[[group_theory/akman_sissokho_2025_transversal_coset_partitions_groups/theorem_4|Theorem 4]],
the case reduces to three mutually commuting subgroups, which
[[group_theory/akman_sissokho_2025_transversal_coset_partitions_groups/theorem_3|Theorem 3]]
covers. The reduction is that the three other cosets partition the missing
coset of the index-$2$ subgroup and, after translation, that subgroup itself.
