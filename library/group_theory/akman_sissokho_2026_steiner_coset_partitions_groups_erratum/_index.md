## Correction and scope

The erratum replaces one sentence in the 2025 article's abstract. As the
erratum quotes it, the original sentence credited the authors' earlier paper,
*Transversal coset partitions of groups* (Beitr. Algebra Geom. 66 (2025),
reference [1] of the article), with ruling out Steiner coset partitions for
every number $r\geq 2$ of mutually commuting subgroups $H_i$; the corrected
sentence limits this to $r\in\{2,3\}$. The held PDF of the 2025 article, also
a Cambridge Core download of 18 September 2026, prints that sentence with
$2\leq r\leq 7$ (p. 1359), matching neither version the erratum quotes; for
use in this corpus, the erratum's corrected range $r\in\{2,3\}$ controls.

The second correction is typographical. On page 12 of the article (printed
p. 1370), the malformed definition of $a_J$ is replaced by

$$
a_J\overset{\mathrm{def}}{=}a_1^{j_1}\cdots a_n^{j_n}\in G.
$$

The product defining $a_J$ is unchanged, and the held PDF of the article
already prints the corrected form.

## Consequences for the 2025 paper

The abstract cannot be used as an unconditional nonexistence result for
$r\geq 4$ mutually commuting subgroups. In particular, it supplies no such
claim for $4\leq r\leq 7$. Uses of the 2025 paper must instead cite its actual
result of the required scope: Proposition 2.2 for two distinct proper
subgroups, Proposition 2.3 for three distinct proper mutually commuting
subgroups, its hypothesis-dependent nonexistence results for larger $r$, or
Theorem 2 for the classified $r=4$ case.

The erratum does not alter those numbered results, their proofs, or the paper's
constructions. In particular, Theorem 2 still says that four distinct, proper,
mutually commuting subgroups admit no Steiner partition unless $G$ is an
extension of $C_2\times C_2\times C_2$, and that every such extension does
admit one. The conditional results for larger $r$ and the elementary-abelian
constructions also remain unaffected; the erratum leaves in place the printed
choice of $t$ in Proposition 4.12, whose sign slip the
[[group_theory/akman_sissokho_2025_steiner_coset_partitions_groups/_index|card for the 2025 article]]
records.
