## Statement

**Remark** (p. 137). Let $G_n$ be any group of order $n$, written
multiplicatively, and choose $a_1,\ldots,a_k$ in $G_n$ at random,
independently and uniformly. Let $V_k(b)$ be the number of
representations $b=a_{i_1}a_{i_2}\cdots a_{i_r}$ with
$1\le i_1<i_2<\cdots<i_r\le k$ and $0\le r\le k$, the empty product being
the identity. The paper states that then the statements of
[[group_theory/erdos_1965_probabilistic_methods_group_theory/theorem_1|Theorem 1]]
and
[[group_theory/erdos_1965_probabilistic_methods_group_theory/theorem_2|Theorem 2]]
are valid.

The paper adds that if all products of distinct elements
$a_{i_1}\cdots a_{i_r}$ are counted, without the condition
$i_1<\cdots<i_r$, the situation changes completely and the structure of
the group becomes relevant; it calls the restriction to increasing indices
unnatural and gives this as its reason for stating the theorems for
abelian groups only. It notes before this (p. 137) that the results for
abelian groups do not depend at all on the structure of $G_n$.

## Scope

The remark is stated without proof.
