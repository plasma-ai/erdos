## Statement

Notation as on the
[[group_theory/kriz_2013_maximal_cross_number_unique_factorization_sequences/conjecture_2|Conjecture 2]]
page.

**Corollary 7** (p. 3). For distinct primes $p,q$, either of which may be
$2$, and any $m,n\in\mathbb N$, $K_1(G)=K_1^*(G)$ holds for each of the
groups

$$
C_{p^m}\oplus C_p,\quad C_{p^m}\oplus C_q,\quad C_{p^m}\oplus C_q^2,\quad
C_{p^m}\oplus C_2^n,\quad C_{p^m}\oplus C_3^n.
$$

## Proof pointer

P. 12: the paper derives it from Proposition 3 (the lower bound
$K_1\ge K_1^*$), Theorem 5 (the values of $K_1$ on $C_{p^m}$, $C_p^2$,
$C_2^n$, $C_3^n$) and the upper bounds of
[[group_theory/kriz_2013_maximal_cross_number_unique_factorization_sequences/theorem_6|Theorem 6]],
together with the additivity of $K_1^*$ over direct sums.
