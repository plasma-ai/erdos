## Statement

Let $H$ be a subnormal subgroup of finite index in a group $G$. The least
positive integer $k$ admitting elements $a_1,\ldots,a_k\in G$ and subnormal
subgroups $G_1,\ldots,G_k$ of $G$ such that

$$
G=a_1G_1\mathbin{\dot\cup}\cdots\mathbin{\dot\cup}a_kG_k
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\bigcap_{i=1}^kG_i=H
$$

is $1+d(G,H)$.

Here the dotted union records the source's phrase "left coset decomposition":
the cosets form a partition of $G$.

**Proof pointer.** The source obtains the statement by combining Theorems 4
and 8'; Theorem 8' and its proof run across printed pp. 47--48. That proof was
not reconstructed or independently checked here.

**Bears on.** Qualified structural context for
[[../wiki/problems/covering_systems/E0274/_index|Problem 274]]. The theorem does not assert
that the cosets have different sizes.
