## Statement

"A generalization of the method which we used in the proof of (1) leads to
the following more general result: Let $a_1<\dots<a_k\le x$ be a sequence
of integers where no $a_i$ divides the product of $r$ other $a_j$'s. Then

$$
\Pi(x)+c_1^{(r)}x^{2/(r+1)}/(\log x)^2<\max k<\Pi(x)+c_2^{(r)}x^{2/(r+1)}/(\log x)^2. \tag{3}
$$
"

**Source.** P. Erdős, *Some applications of graph theory to number theory*,
The Many Facets of Graph Theory (Kalamazoo 1968), Springer (1969), 77--82;
display (3) on printed p. 78 (PDF p. 2), read on the page image.

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the statement was read clause by clause on
the page image. No proof is given; the text says only that the method of
(1) generalizes.

## Proof pointer

None in the paper ("A generalization of the method which we used in the
proof of (1)"). The site's discussion thread on Problem 793 (comment of 30
November 2025) records that Erdős gave no details, and cites later
published proofs for a range of $r$ (Chan, Győri and Sárközy, European J.
Combin. 31 (2010), 260--269; Chan, European J. Combin. 32 (2011),
443--447), which this library does not hold.

## Dependencies

The method of (1); nothing else stated.
