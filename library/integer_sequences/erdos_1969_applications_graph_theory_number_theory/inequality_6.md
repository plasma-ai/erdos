## Statement

"Let $a_1<\dots<a_k\le x$ be such that all products
$\prod_{i=1}^k a_i^{\varepsilon_i}$, $\varepsilon_i=0$ or $1$, are distinct.
Then [6]

$$
\max k<\Pi(x)+c_6x^{1/2}/\log x. \tag{6}
$$

The proof of (6) is not graph theoretical and will not be discussed here."
Reference [6] is Erdős, *Extremal problems in number theory II*, Mat.
Lapok 17 (1966), 135--155, the Hungarian paper whose Section I.11 sketches
the proof
([[number_theory/erdos_1966_szamelmeleti_megjegyzesek/section_1_11|result page]]).

**Source.** P. Erdős, *Some applications of graph theory to number theory*,
The Many Facets of Graph Theory (Kalamazoo 1968), Springer (1969), 77--82;
display (6) on printed p. 79 (PDF p. 3), read on the page image.
