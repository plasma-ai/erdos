## Statement

"Let $a_1<\dots<a_k\le n$; $b_1<\dots<b_q\le n$ be two sequences of
integers and assume that the products $a_ib_j$ are all distinct. Is it
true that $kq<c\,n^2/\log n$?" (printed pp. 81--82).

**Source.** P. Erdős, *Some applications of graph theory to number theory*,
The Many Facets of Graph Theory (Kalamazoo 1968), Springer (1969), 77--82;
the question begins at the foot of printed p. 81 and ends on p. 82 (PDF
pp. 5--6), read on the page images.

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the question was read clause by clause on
the page images. A question; no proof.
