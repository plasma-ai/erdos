## Statement

**Remark** (pp. 117--118, unnumbered). Let
$1\le a_1<a_2<\cdots<a_k\le N$ and $1<b_1<b_2<\cdots<b_l\le N$ (the
second chain begins with a strict inequality in the print) be two sequences
of integers such that all the sums $a_i+b_j$, $1\le i\le k$,
$1\le j\le l$, are squarefree. The paper states:

- their method gives $kl<N^{3/2+\varepsilon}$;
- they can show that $kl/N\to\infty$ is possible, and have no satisfactory
  upper bound for $kl$;
- there is an absolute constant $c$ such that $k>cN$, $l\to\infty$ is
  possible, and perhaps $l$ must then be less than $\log N$ or
  $(\log N)^c$.

The quantifier on $\varepsilon$ and the range of $N$ in the first
statement are not printed.

On p. 117 the paper also says that similar but slightly more complicated
methods give analogous results for $k$-th power free numbers; it states
none of them.

## Proof pointer

None: the paper gives no proof or construction for these statements.
