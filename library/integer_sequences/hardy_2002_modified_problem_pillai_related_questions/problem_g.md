## Statement

**Problem G** (p. 557). For a prime $p$, $f(p)$ is the least integer with
$f(p)!+1\equiv0\pmod p$; it exists and is at most $p-1$ by Wilson's
theorem. The authors write that they believe $f(p)=p-1$ for infinitely
many $p$, but that the number of such $p\le x$ is probably
$o(x/\log x)$. Below the problem the paper records that Erdős believed
$f(p)/p\to0$ for almost all $p$.

Problem G is not starred, so by Section 3's preamble it was raised in
discussion with Erdős.

Problem C on the same page is related: for $g(p)$, the number of $n<p$
with $n!+1\equiv0\pmod p$, it asks whether $\limsup g(p)=\infty$ and
suggests $g(p)\to\infty$ for almost all $p$.

## Proof pointer

An open problem; the paper proves nothing about it.
