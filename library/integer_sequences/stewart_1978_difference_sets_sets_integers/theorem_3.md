## Statement

Notation (p. 5-01). $\mathbb N_0$ is the set of non-negative integers; for
$A\subseteq\mathbb N_0$, $\mathcal D(A)$ is its ordinary-difference set and
$\mathcal D_0(A)$ its density-difference set, the $d\in\mathbb N_0$ with
$\overline d(A\cap(A-d))>0$; $\overline d$ and $\underline d$ are upper and
lower density (the print writes $d^-$ and $d_-$), as on
[[integer_sequences/stewart_1978_difference_sets_sets_integers/theorem_2|Theorem 2]].

**Theorem 3** (p. 5-03), with a pointer to Stewart and Tijdeman. Given a set
$A\subseteq\mathbb N_0$, there is a set $B\subseteq\mathbb N_0$ with
$\underline d(B)\ge\overline d(A)$ such that
$\mathcal D(B)\subseteq\mathcal D_0(A)$.

The survey presents it as a tool for translating results about one type of
difference set into results about another; it uses it on p. 5-05 in an
alternative proof of
[[integer_sequences/stewart_1978_difference_sets_sets_integers/theorem_7|Theorem 7]]
and on p. 5-08 to replace $\mathcal D(A)$ by $\mathcal D_0(A)$ in
[[integer_sequences/stewart_1978_difference_sets_sets_integers/theorem_8|Theorem 8]].

## Proof pointer

The survey gives no proof; it points to Stewart and Tijdeman, On
density-difference sets of sequences of integers (reference [15] of the
survey, then to appear).
