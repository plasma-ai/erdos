# Zeraoulia (2026): A conditional resolution of an Erdős–Graham–Ruzsa–Straus conjecture on reciprocal sums over primes

[[integer_sequences/_index|..]]

***

Rafik Zeraoulia, *A Conditional Resolution of an Erdős–Graham–Ruzsa–Straus
Conjecture on Reciprocal Sums over Primes*, Zenodo, preprint, version 1.0,
issued 11 August 2026, CC BY 4.0,
[doi:10.5281/zenodo.21882603](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21882603)
(concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21882602).

The record's file `EGRS_Conditional_Resolution.pdf` was not retrieved:
zenodo.org answered the record page, its API and the file download with
HTTP 403 ("unusual traffic") on 2026-09-27, so its size is not recorded and
no copy was read. The bibliographic data above are the
DataCite metadata of the DOI.

Scope, from the record's abstract and the erdosproblems.com proof-claims
thread: for $S(n)=\sum 1/p$ over the primes $p\le n$ with $n\bmod p>p/2$, the
sum of [[../wiki/problems/integer_sequences/E0726/_index|Problem 726]], the preprint claims
$S(n)=\tfrac12\log\log n+O(\log\log\log n)$ conditionally on a
"Reciprocal-Prime Equidistribution Hypothesis", an extension of the smooth
equidistribution estimate over primes, Proposition 1.12 of
[[factorials_binomials/matomaki_2022_singmaster_s_conjecture_interior_pascal_s/_index|Matomäki, Radziwiłł, Shao, Tao and Teräväinen]],
to the range $|N|\le\exp(P^c)$ for primes $p\asymp P$. The abstract states
that the hypothesis is unproved and that no unconditional resolution is
claimed. The preprint was posted on the site's proof-claims thread for Problem
726 on 11 August 2026 as a full proof, with the submitter's own note that the
result is conditional; a comment of 12 August 2026 on that thread classes it as
partial, and a third-party evidence record of 16 August 2026, linked from the
thread on 24 August 2026, lists no independent rerun, assessment or review. No
refereed publication and no independent review were found on 2026-09-27. A
conditional theorem does not resolve the problem, whatever the standing of the
argument.
