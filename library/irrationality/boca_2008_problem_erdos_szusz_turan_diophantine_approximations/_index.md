# irrationality/boca_2008_problem_erdos_szusz_turan_diophantine_approximations

[[irrationality/_index|..]]

***

Florin P. Boca, *A problem of Erdős, Szüsz and Turán concerning Diophantine
approximations*, International Journal of Number Theory **4** (2008), no. 4,
691–708.
[DOI:10.1142/S1793042108001626](https://doi.org/10.1142/S1793042108001626).

The abstract says the paper proves directly that the Lebesgue measure of
$S(N,A,c)$ (there the set itself; Problem 1001's $S(N,A,c)$ is its measure)
has a limit $f(A,c)$ as $N\to\infty$, and identifies that limit for all $A>0$
and $c>1$. By the abstract, this completes the evaluation asked for in
[[../wiki/problems/irrationality/E1001/_index|Problem 1001]], beyond the
existence result of Kesten and Sós and Kesten's explicit computation for
$Ac\le1$. This card records DOI metadata and the abstract; the full paper has
not been read here.
