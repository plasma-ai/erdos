# irrationality/cassels_1950_some_metrical_theorems_diophantine_approximation_i

[[irrationality/_index|..]]

***

J. W. S. Cassels, *Some metrical theorems in Diophantine approximation. I*,
Proceedings of the Cambridge Philosophical Society **46** (1950), 209–218.
[DOI:10.1017/S0305004100025676](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004100025676).

The maintained entry for [[../wiki/problems/irrationality/E1000/_index|Problem 1000]] cites
Cassels for introducing the quantity $\phi_A$ and obtaining a sequence with
zero lower limit of the associated averages. The problem asks for a full limit
of zero, which the entry attributes to Haight. This card records DOI metadata;
the Cassels paper has not been read here.

**Bears on.** [[../wiki/problems/irrationality/E1000/_index|Problem 1000: liminf precursor]].
