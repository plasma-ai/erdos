[reconstruction grade](reconstruction_grade.md) is the distinct grader's

that first adds the three reviewed pages, beside the review-time state and the
retained snapshots, so the grader's required change is applied. With both
filed, the
reconstruction is independently accepted compilation proof coverage in the
review's scope.

The [focused statement-fidelity record](statement_fidelity/_index.md) is an

images. Its verdict is refutation-failed for that frozen extraction, disclosed
proof outline and exact specialization; it awards no tier and does not extend
the reconstruction review above.
