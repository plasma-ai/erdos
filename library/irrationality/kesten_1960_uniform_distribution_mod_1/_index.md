# irrationality/kesten_1960_uniform_distribution_mod_1

[[irrationality/_index|..]]

***

Harry Kesten, *Uniform distribution mod 1*, Annals of Mathematics **71**
(1960), 445–471. [DOI:10.2307/1969938](https://doi.org/10.2307/1969938).

This is the historical paper cited for
[[../wiki/problems/irrationality/E1002/_index|Problem 1002]]. The available record supplies
the citation and DOI, but no full text or theorem statement has been checked
here. It does not by itself establish a limit law for the exact spatial
distribution asked for in the problem.
