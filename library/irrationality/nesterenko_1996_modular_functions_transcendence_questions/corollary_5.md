## Statement

**Corollary 5** (p. 68). Each of the sets

$$
\{\pi,\ e^{\pi},\ \Gamma(1/4)\},\qquad \{\pi,\ e^{\pi\sqrt3},\ \Gamma(1/3)\}
$$

consists of numbers algebraically independent over $\mathbb Q$. In
particular, the numbers $\pi$ and $e^{\pi}$ are algebraically independent
over $\mathbb Q$.

The paper notes (p. 68) that the algebraic independence of $\pi$ and
$e^{\pi}$ was an old folklore conjecture of transcendence theory, and that
the independence of $\pi,\Gamma(1/4)$ and of $\pi,\Gamma(1/3)$ was proved
earlier by G. V. Chudnovsky (1976). The companion Corollary 6 (p. 68) states
that $\pi$ and $e^{\pi\sqrt D}$ are algebraically independent over
$\mathbb Q$ for every natural number $D$.

**Source.** Yu. V. Nesterenko, *Modular functions and transcendence
questions*, Mat. Sb. 187 (1996), no. 9, 65--96 (Russian; English translation
Sb. Math. 187 (1996), no. 9, 1319--1348), Corollary 5 (Следствие 5), p. 68 of
the Russian original; see the
[[irrationality/nesterenko_1996_modular_functions_transcendence_questions/_index|source card]].
Labels and pages follow the Russian pagination.

## Proof pointer

The paper obtains it (p. 68) from Corollary 4 (p. 67), on Weierstrass
functions with algebraic invariants and complex multiplication, which it
derives from
[[irrationality/nesterenko_1996_modular_functions_transcendence_questions/theorem_1|Theorem 1]]
through Corollary 3. Corollary 4 is applied to the curves $y^2=4x^3-4x$
(complex multiplication by $\mathbb Q(i)$) and $y^2=4x^3-4$ (by
$\mathbb Q(\zeta)$, $\zeta=e^{2\pi i/3}$), whose real periods the paper
expresses through $\Gamma(1/4)$ and $\Gamma(1/3)$.
