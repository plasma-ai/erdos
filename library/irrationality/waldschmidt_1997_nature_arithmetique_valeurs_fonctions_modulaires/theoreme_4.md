## Statement

"Soit $q$ un élément de $\mathcal C$ satisfaisant $0<|q|<1$. Alors le degré
de transcendance sur $\mathbb Q$ du corps

$$
\mathbb Q\big(q,P(q),Q(q),R(q)\big)
$$

est supérieur ou égal à 3." Here $\mathcal C$ is either $\mathbb C$ or a
field $\mathbb C_p$ with $p$ prime (p. 106), so the statement covers complex
and $p$-adic $q$. That is, at least three of $q,P(q),Q(q),R(q)$ are
algebraically independent over $\mathbb Q$; for $\mathcal C=\mathbb C$ this
is Nesterenko's
[[irrationality/nesterenko_1996_modular_functions_transcendence_questions/theorem_1|Theorem 1]].
The exposé recalls Mahler's theorem that $P,Q,R$ are algebraically
independent over $\mathbb C(z)$ and gives the Ramanujan system as
$12\,DP/P=P-Q/P$, $3\,DQ/Q=P-R/Q$, $2\,DR/R=P-Q^2/R$ with $D=z\,d/dz$
(pp. 118--119), so that $D$ preserves $\mathbb Q[P,Q,R]$.

## Consequences listed by the exposé (pp. 119--122)

Bertrand's conjecture ($J(q),DJ(q),D^2J(q)$ algebraically independent for
algebraic $q$ with $0<|q|<1$; more generally, for $q\in\mathcal C$ with
$0<|q|<1$, $J(q)\ne0$ and $J(q)\ne1728$, at least three of
$q,J(q),DJ(q),D^2J(q)$ algebraically independent); Corollaire 1
($q,P(q),\Delta(q)$ algebraically independent for complex $q$ with
$0<|q|<1$ and $J(q)$ algebraic); Corollaire 2 (elliptic periods and
quasi-periods); Corollaire 3 (in particular $\pi$ and $e^{\pi}$
algebraically independent); Corollaire 4 (Jacobi theta series;
$\theta_3(q)$ transcendental for algebraic $q$ with $0<|q|<1$); Corollaire 5
(Lucas sequences). The card lists them with page numbers.

## Relation to Problem 250

For algebraic $q$ the theorem gives that $P(q),Q(q),R(q)$ are algebraically
independent (Nesterenko's
[[irrationality/nesterenko_1996_modular_functions_transcendence_questions/corollary_2|Corollary 2]]);
at $q=1/2$, $P(1/2)=1-24\sum_{n\ge1}\sigma(n)/2^n$ is transcendental and so
is the problem's number. The exposé does not state this specialization.
