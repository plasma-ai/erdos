## Statement

With $C(r)+1=\max g(n)$ over the integers $n$ with at most $r$ distinct
prime factors, $g$ being Jacobsthal's function (the page
[[number_theory/erdos_1965_recent_advances_current_problems_number_theory/display_29|display (29)]]
gives the definition): "Jacobsthal conjectured that

$$
C(r)<c_4r^2. \qquad (30)
$$

The exponent in (29) can be reduced by Selberg's improvement of Brun's
method, but (30) seems hopeless at present [21]." Reference [21] is
Erdős's own paper on the integers relatively prime to $n$ and on a
number-theoretic function considered by Jacobsthal (Math. Scand. 10, 1962),
not held here.

In the notation of Problem 970 the conjecture reads $h(k)\ll k^2$, the
site's displayed question.

**Source.** P. Erdős, *Some recent advances and current problems in number
theory*, Lectures on Modern Mathematics, Vol. III, Wiley (1965), 196--244;
printed p. 208 (PDF p. 14 of the Rényi archive's 50-page scan), read on the page
image.

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the sentence and the display were read
clause by clause on the page image. A conjecture; nothing to prove.
