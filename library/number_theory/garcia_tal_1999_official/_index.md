# number_theory/garcia_tal_1999_official

[[number_theory/_index|..]]

***

M. V. P. Garcia and F. A. Tal, *A note on the generalized 3n+1 problem*, Acta
Arith. 90 (1999) 245-250, DOI 10.4064/aa-90-3-245-250. Six pages, A4, 211,308
bytes, PDF 1.2 (the journal digitization, dvipdfm production).

Relevance: for the Hasse map $H$ of (1) (p. 245), with $m>d\ge2$,
$\gcd(d,m)=1$, $m<d^{d/(d-1)}$ and $R_d$ the system of residues modulo $d$
fixed there, Theorem 1 (p. 249) shows that a complete set of
representatives of the relation on the positive integers given by
$a\sim b$ iff $H^k(a)=H^k(b)$ for some $k\in\mathbb{N}$ has Banach density
zero, and Corollary 1 (p. 249) that every orbit $\mathcal{O}(n)$ under $H$
has Banach density zero. For $d=2$ the hypotheses force $m=3$, and with
$R_2=\{0,-1\}$ the map $H$ is the Collatz shortcut map of problem 1135
(p. 245). Statements read on the page images of pp. 245 and 249; proofs not
checked.
