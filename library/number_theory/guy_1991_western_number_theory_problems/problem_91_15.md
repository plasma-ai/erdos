## Statement

**Problem 91:15** (p. 15), attributed "(Paul Erdős & Ron Graham)", quoted
as printed: "Is it true that any coloring of the integers with $k$ colors
gives a monochromatic solution of $\sum\frac1{x_i}=1$, $x_1<x_2<\ldots$
(finite sum)? This is open even for $k=2$. If the answer is affirmative,
let $f(k)$ be the smallest integer for which every $k$-coloring of the
integers $1\le t\le f(k)$ contains a monochromatic solution. Determine or
estimate $f(k)$."

The print does not exclude the one-term solution $x_1=1$; the question has
content only when $1$ is excluded, as the problem's statement does by
requiring every denominator to be at least $2$.

**Source.** *Western Number Theory Problems, 1991-12-19 & 22*, edited by
Richard K. Guy (Department of Mathematics and Statistics, The University of
Calgary; dated 92-08-20), problem 91:15, printed p. 15. The edition is
identified in the
[[number_theory/guy_1991_western_number_theory_problems/_index|source digest]].
