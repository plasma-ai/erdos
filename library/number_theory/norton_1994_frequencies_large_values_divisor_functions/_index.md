# On the frequencies of large values of divisor functions

[[number_theory/_index|..]]

[[number_theory/norton_1994_frequencies_large_values_divisor_functions/theorem_1_11|theorem_1_11]]: Bounds integers with many divisors using an explicit one-sided exponential estimate.

***

Karl K. Norton, *On the frequencies of large values of divisor functions*,
*Acta Arithmetica* **68** (1994), no. 3, 219–244,
[DOI 10.4064/aa-68-3-219-244](https://doi.org/10.4064/aa-68-3-219-244).
The [canonical published PDF](norton_1994_frequencies_large_values_divisor_functions.pdf)
was acquired from the [journal archive](https://matwbn.icm.edu.pl/ksiazki/aa/aa68/aa6833.pdf)
on 2026-09-05. The file's text layer carries no copyright or license line; the
publisher's record (https://www.impan.pl/get/doi/10.4064/aa-68-3-219-244, read
2026-10-02) offers the PDF under the link "Pobierz zgodnie z CC-BY" ("Free
download under CC-BY license" on the English site), a Creative Commons
Attribution license whose version the record does not name; the site footer
"Copyright © 2026 by IMPAN. All rights reserved." speaks for the site, not the
article.

The [[number_theory/norton_1994_frequencies_large_values_divisor_functions/theorem_1_11|ordinary-divisor specialization of Theorem 1.11]]
is recorded as an exact external input. Its full proof is not yet compiled.
The source studies the generalized divisor function $d_z(n)$, with
$d_2(n)=\tau(n)$; it distinguishes strict and non-strict upper tail counts.

Read status: claims checked for Theorem 1.11 (printed p. 221) and the
definitions (1.1)--(1.4) and the $\log_k$ and $O$ conventions it uses
(pp. 219--220), read clause by clause on the page images; the proof was not
read.

The selected estimate is sufficient for
[[covering_systems/mcnew_2026_densities_covering_numbers_abundant_numbers/theorem_2_3|McNew–Setty's primitive-covering count]],
without relying on a broad two-sided asymptotic across all thresholds.
Other estimates and proofs in the paper remain to be compiled.
