## Statement

Setting (pp. 233--234). $F$ is a global field and $B$ a quaternion algebra
over $F$. $\Omega\subseteq\operatorname{Ram}B$ is the set of ramified
archimedean places, which are necessarily real; $\Omega=\emptyset$ when $F$ is
a function field. The group $F^\times_{>_\Omega0}$ (14.7.3) consists of the
$x\in F^\times$ with $v(x)>0$ for every $v\in\Omega$, that is, positive under
each real embedding in $\Omega$.

**Main Theorem 14.7.4** (Hasse--Schilling, p. 234).
$\operatorname{nrd}(B^\times)=F^\times_{>_\Omega0}$.

The inclusion $\operatorname{nrd}(B^\times)\le F^\times_{>_\Omega0}$ comes from
the local norm groups (14.7.1, p. 233): $\operatorname{nrd}(B_v^\times)$ is
$\mathbb R^\times_{>0}$ at a ramified real place and all of $F_v^\times$
otherwise.

**Source.** John Voight, "Quaternion algebras over global fields," Chapter 14
of *Quaternion Algebras*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 288, Springer, 2021,
pp. 217--240, doi:10.1007/978-3-030-56694-4_14. The statement is on p. 234 and
its proof on p. 235. The edition read is identified on the
[[number_theory/voight_2021_quaternion_algebras_over_global_fields/_index|source card]].

## Proof pointer

Page 235. Given $n\in F^\times_{>_\Omega0}$, it suffices by
[[number_theory/voight_2021_quaternion_algebras_over_global_fields/proposition_14_6_7|Proposition 14.6.7]]
to find a quadratic extension $K\supseteq F$ containing an element of norm $n$
with $K_v$ a field at every $v\in\operatorname{Ram}B$; such a $K$ embeds in
$B$, and the reduced norm restricts to the norm from $K$. Lemma 14.7.5
(p. 234) gives, at each ramified $v$ (using $v(n)>0$ at real ones), a separable
irreducible $x^2-t_vx+n$ over $F_v$. Corollary 14.7.8 (p. 235), a weak
approximation form of Lemma 14.7.6 (p. 234), gives one $t\in F$ with
$x^2-tx+n$ irreducible over every such $F_v$, and $K$ is generated by a root.

The theorem concerns reduced norms and bears on no Erdős problem directly. The
card's note on
