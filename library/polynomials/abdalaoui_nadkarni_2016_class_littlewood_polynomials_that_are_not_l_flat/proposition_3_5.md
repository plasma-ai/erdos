**Source.** Proposition 3.5, p. 10, of E. H. el Abdalaoui, with an appendix
jointly with M. G. Nadkarni, "A class of Littlewood polynomials that are not
$L^\alpha$-flat," arXiv:1606.05852v3 (10 May 2017). Pages are those of the

## Statement

The normalization (2.1), the frequency $\operatorname{fr}(-1)$ and
$L^\alpha$-flatness are as on the
[[polynomials/abdalaoui_nadkarni_2016_class_littlewood_polynomials_that_are_not_l_flat/theorem_2_1|Theorem 2.1 page]].

**Proposition 3.5** (p. 10). Let $(P_q)_{q\ge0}$ be a sequence of
$L^2$-normalized Littlewood polynomials that is $L^\alpha$-flat for some
$0<\alpha<2$. Then

$$
\tfrac14\le\operatorname{fr}(-1)\le\tfrac34 .
$$

It extends Proposition 3.3 (p. 8), the case $\alpha=1$, and is implied by
[[polynomials/abdalaoui_nadkarni_2016_class_littlewood_polynomials_that_are_not_l_flat/theorem_2_1|Theorem 2.1]],
which excludes the endpoints and covers every $\alpha\ge0$.

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the statement was read on the print.

## Proof pointer

Page 10. The paper writes out no proof. It introduces the proposition by
saying that it is a simple matter to strengthen Proposition 3.1 (p. 7) from
Lemma 3.4 (p. 8), the uniform integrability of the normalized Dirichlet
kernels in $L^\alpha$ for $0<\alpha<2$. The frequency bound for $L^1$-flat
sequences is Proposition 3.3 (p. 8, proved on pp. 9-10).
