## Statement

Notation (p. 125): $f$ is a monic polynomial (1), $E(f)$ the set where
$|f|<1$ and $D$ the open unit disk.

**Problem 11** (p. 142). "Let $\Lambda(f)$ denote the maximum of the lengths
$\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\ldots,\lambda_k$ of the boundaries of the components
$E_1,E_2,\ldots,E_k$ of $E(f)$. What is the infimum of $\Lambda(f)$, for all
$f$ whose zeros lie in $D$?"

**Source.** P. Erdős, F. Herzog, G. Piranian, *Metric properties of
polynomials*, J. Analyse Math. 6 (1958), 125--148, doi:10.1007/BF02790232;
Problem 11 on p. 142. The copy read is identified on the
[[polynomials/erdos_1958_metric_properties_polynomials/_index|source card]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the problem was read on the page image of
p. 142 on 2026-10-08, the disk symbol at high resolution: $D$ is printed
without the bar the paper uses for the closed disk. Nothing here is
independently reviewed.
