## Statement

Notation (p. 125): $f$ is a monic polynomial (1), $E$ the set where $|f|<1$
and $D$ the open unit disk.

**Problem 12** (p. 142). "For fixed degree $n$ of $f$, is the length of the
lemniscate $|f(z)|=1$ greatest in the case where $f(z)=z^n-1$? Is the length
at least $2\pi$, if $E$ is connected? What is the infimum of the length for
polynomials (1) whose zeros all lie in $D$?"

**Source.** P. Erdős, F. Herzog, G. Piranian, *Metric properties of
polynomials*, J. Analyse Math. 6 (1958), 125--148, doi:10.1007/BF02790232;
Problem 12 on p. 142. The copy read is identified on the
[[polynomials/erdos_1958_metric_properties_polynomials/_index|source card]].

page image of p. 142 on 2026-10-08. Nothing here is independently reviewed.
