## Statement

**Problem 16** (p. 145). "Let $\{z_\mu\}_{\mu=1}^m$ be a set of distinct
points, and let

$$
f(z)=\prod_{\mu=1}^m(z-z_\mu)^{k_\mu},
$$

where the $k_\mu$ are positive integers. Let $c$ be a constant small enough
so that the lemniscate $|f(z)|=c$ consists of $m$ distinct loops. Are all the
loops convex?"

The paper credits the question to H. Grunsky (private communication) and
calls it related to
[[polynomials/erdos_1958_metric_properties_polynomials/theorem_11|Theorem 11]]
(p. 145).

**Source.** P. Erdős, F. Herzog, G. Piranian, *Metric properties of
polynomials*, J. Analyse Math. 6 (1958), 125--148, doi:10.1007/BF02790232;
Problem 16 on p. 145. The copy read is identified on the
[[polynomials/erdos_1958_metric_properties_polynomials/_index|source card]].

page image of p. 145 on 2026-10-08. Nothing here is independently reviewed.
