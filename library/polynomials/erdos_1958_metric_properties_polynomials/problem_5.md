## Statement

Notation (p. 125): $f$ is a monic polynomial (1), $E$ the set where $|f|<1$
and $D$ the open unit disk.

**Problem 5** (p. 139). "If all the $z_\nu$ lie in $D$, does there exist a
path of length less than 2 which lies in $E$ and joins two of the $z_\nu$?"

Just before it the paper notes (p. 139) that the remarks opening Section 5
(p. 136, where Szegő's bound of $n-1$ components of $\bar E$ is cited) show
that when all zeros lie in $D$ some component of $E$ contains at least two
zeros.

**Source.** P. Erdős, F. Herzog, G. Piranian, *Metric properties of
polynomials*, J. Analyse Math. 6 (1958), 125--148, doi:10.1007/BF02790232;
Problem 5 on p. 139. The copy read is identified on the
[[polynomials/erdos_1958_metric_properties_polynomials/_index|source card]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the problem and the sentence before it were
read on the page image of p. 139 on 2026-10-08. Nothing here is independently
reviewed.
