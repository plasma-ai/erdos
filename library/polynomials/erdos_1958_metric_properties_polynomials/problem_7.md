## Statement

Notation (p. 125): $E$ is the set where $|f|<1$ and $D_r$ the open disk
$|z|<r$.

**Problem 7** (p. 142). "If all the zeros of $f$ lie in $D_r$ $(r<2)$, does
the set $E$ have a component with diameter greater than $2-r$?"

**Source.** P. Erdős, F. Herzog, G. Piranian, *Metric properties of
polynomials*, J. Analyse Math. 6 (1958), 125--148, doi:10.1007/BF02790232;
Problem 7 on p. 142. The copy read is identified on the
[[polynomials/erdos_1958_metric_properties_polynomials/_index|source card]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the problem was read on the page image of
p. 142 on 2026-10-08; the disk is printed without the bar the paper uses for
closed disks. Nothing here is independently reviewed.
