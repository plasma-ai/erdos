polynomials and some extremal problems," Mathematica (Cluj) 10 (33) (1968), 65-73; see the

## Statement

Notation and conditions (9), (10) as in
[[polynomials/erdos_1967_problems_results_convergence_divergence_properties_lagrange/theorem_1|Theorem 1]].

**Theorem 1'** (p. 72). For a point group $x_i^{(n)}$ the following are
equivalent.

- For every $c>0$ there is an $A(c)$ such that for all $y_i^{(n)}$ with
  $|y_i^{(n)}|\le1$, $i=1,\ldots,n$, $n=1,2,\ldots$, there is a polynomial
  $P_m(x)$ of degree $m<(1+c)n$ with $P_m(x_i^{(n)})=y_i^{(n)}$ for
  $i=1,\ldots,n$ and $\max_{-1\le x\le1}|P_m(x)|<A(c)$.
- Conditions (9) and (10) hold.

The paper says Theorems 1 and 2 follow from Theorems 1' and 2'.
