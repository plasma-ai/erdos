## Statement

The maps below act on pairs $(f,g)$ of Laurent polynomials (Definition 4.1,
p. 14), with $f^\ddagger(z)=z^{\operatorname{ord}f+\deg f}\,\overline{f(z)}$
the conjugate reverse (p. 13), where
$\overline{f(z)}=\sum_j\overline{f_j}z^{-j}$:

- $\mathrm{swap}(f,g)=(g,f)$;
- $\mathrm{crev}(f,g)=(f^\ddagger,g)$ and $\mathrm{crev}'(f,g)=(f,g^\ddagger)$;
- $\mathrm{srev}(f,g)=\bigl(z^{\operatorname{ord}f+\deg f}f(z^{-1}),\ z^{\operatorname{ord}g+\deg g}g(z^{-1})\bigr)$;
- $\mathrm{scal}_{u,v}(f,g)=(uf,vg)$;
- $\mathrm{subs}_{wz}(f,g)=(f(wz),g(wz))$.

**Definition 7.5** (p. 32). The *restricted Golay group* $\mathrm{RGol}$ is
the subgroup of the stationary Golay group $\mathrm{SGol}$ generated by
$\mathrm{swap}$, $\mathrm{crev}\circ\mathrm{crev}'$, $\mathrm{srev}$, all
$\mathrm{scal}_{u,v}$ with $u,v$ nonzero complex numbers and $|u|=|v|$, and
all $\mathrm{subs}_{wz}$ with $w$ a unimodular complex number.

$\mathrm{SGol}$ (Definition 4.10, p. 17) has the same generators except that
it contains $\mathrm{crev}$ and $\mathrm{crev}'$ separately. Remark 7.6
(p. 32) shows that $\mathrm{RGol}$ is a proper subgroup of $\mathrm{SGol}$:
$\mathrm{crev}\in\mathrm{SGol}\setminus\mathrm{RGol}$. Definitions 7.7 and
7.9 (pp. 32–33) set $\mathrm{RUGol}=\mathrm{RGol}\cap\mathrm{UGol}$ and
$\mathrm{RBGol}=\mathrm{RGol}\cap\mathrm{BGol}$.

## Role in the paper

Lemma 7.11 (p. 33) shows that after one simple Golay interleaving step
followed by an element of $\mathrm{RGol}$, the output pair $(f,g)$ has
$\operatorname{Re}(\overline z(f\overline g)^2)_0=0$, which removes the
correction term of Proposition 7.4 at later stages of
[[polynomials/katz_moore_2017_sequence_pairs_lowest_combined_autocorrelation_crosscorrelation/theorem_7_12|Theorem 7.12]].
The proof checks that each generator preserves parity-purity of $f\overline g$
(no terms of both even and odd degree).
