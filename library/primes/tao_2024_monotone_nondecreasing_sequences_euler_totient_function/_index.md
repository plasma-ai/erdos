# primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function

[[primes/_index|..]]

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/composite_barrier|composite_barrier]]: A composite placed after a prime while preserving its totient must lie
beyond an explicit square-root gap.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/corollary_1_2|corollary_1_2]]: Every weakly increasing totient subset has reciprocal sum at most log log x
plus an absolute constant.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/dedekind_fibre|dedekind_fibre]]: A finite prime-support induction handles the two-three cancellation and
proves the sharp Dedekind fiber bound.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/external_context|external_context]]: Separate the source’s historical bounds, conditional comparisons and
unperformed finite computations from the compiled proof chain.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/half_bound|half_bound]]: Bound the restricted monotone maximum with leading upper coefficient
one-half after excluding the two extremal families.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/lemma_1_5|lemma_1_5]]: Bound a weighted sum over smooth integers by its largest Rankin weight, with
a logarithmic factor.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/lemma_1_6|lemma_1_6]]: Derive a uniform interval prime count from the classical prime number
theorem with its exponential error.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/lemma_1_7|lemma_1_7]]: Control the reciprocal sum over a short or long prime interval uniformly in
both endpoints.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/lemma_2_1|lemma_2_1]]: Identify the unique prime support of a nonempty totient-ratio fiber and
compute its reciprocal mass.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/lemma_3_1|lemma_3_1]]: Cover the integers by a primary family, a negligible monotone family and
six controlled exceptional classes, which may overlap.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/notation|notation]]: Fix the finite maxima, smoothness conventions and precise classical prime
estimates used throughout the proof.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/proposition_1_4|proposition_1_4]]: Every positive rational totient ratio has reciprocal fiber mass at most one.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/proposition_3_2|proposition_3_2]]: Bound all six exceptional factorization classes by the error allowed in the
main theorem.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/proposition_3_3|proposition_3_3]]: Use local monotonicity and interval counting to make the secondary
factorization family negligible.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/proposition_3_4|proposition_3_4]]: Bound the primary family with the printed logarithmic error using exact
fiber mass and a uniform logarithmic moment.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/proposition_4_1|proposition_4_1]]: Infinitely many prime counterexamples to the stated Legendre interval force
an unbounded additive excess over the primes.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/proposition_4_5|proposition_4_5]]: A uniform shortage of prime-ceiling pairs forces an additive totient excess
of order at least x over log squared x.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/remark_2_2|remark_2_2]]: The reciprocal fiber bound is sharp only at ratios one and one-half, and
each totient ratio determines its prime support.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/remark_4_6|remark_4_6]]: For each positive threshold only finitely many sum-of-divisors ratio fibers
have reciprocal mass above it.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/remark_4_7|remark_4_7]]: The weakly increasing Dedekind totient maximum has the same prime asymptotic
and reciprocal bound.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/strict_transfer|strict_transfer]]: Transfer the weak maximum theorem to the strict asymptotic and density
questions in Problem 49.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/sum_of_divisors_analogue|sum_of_divisors_analogue]]: The same leading asymptotic and reciprocal-sum bound hold for weakly
increasing sums of divisors.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/sum_of_divisors_fibre|sum_of_divisors_fibre]]: Zhang’s powerful-number argument bounds each sum-of-divisors ratio fiber,
with equality only at ratio one.

[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/theorem_1_1|theorem_1_1]]: The largest weakly increasing totient subsequence has size asymptotic to the
number of primes.

For the weak maximum defined in [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/notation]],
[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/theorem_1_1|Theorem 1.1]] proves
$$
\pi(x)\le M(x)\le
\left(1+O\!\left(\frac{(\log\log x)^5}{\log x}\right)\right)\pi(x)
\qquad(x\ge10).
$$
[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/corollary_1_2|Corollary 1.2]] gives a reciprocal-sum bound
$\log\log x+O(1)$ for each such subset.
The [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/strict_transfer|strict transfer]] proves the asymptotic and
$o(N)$ clauses of [[../wiki/problems/primes/E0049/_index|Problem 49]], whose
statement uses strict totient inequalities. It does not assert that
primes are an exactly largest strict example for every $N$.

**The complete main chain.** The proof decomposes typical integers as
either $dp$ or $dps$. In the second family, local monotonicity constrains
the intermediate prime and forces a negligible contribution. In the
first, rational separation and the exact reciprocal mass of a
totient-ratio fiber supply the leading constant one.

- [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/lemma_1_5|Lemma 1.5]] proves the smooth-number Rankin bound.
- [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/lemma_1_6|Lemma 1.6]] and [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/lemma_1_7|Lemma 1.7]] give the precise
  prime counts and reciprocal windows.
- [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/lemma_2_1|Lemma 2.1]] proves [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/proposition_1_4|Proposition 1.4]];
  [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/remark_2_2|Remarks 2.2–2.3]] identify equality and prime support.
- [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/lemma_3_1|Lemma 3.1]] gives the full factorization decomposition.
- [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/proposition_3_2|Proposition 3.2]] bounds all six exceptional classes.
- [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/proposition_3_3|Proposition 3.3]] bounds the secondary family.
- [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/proposition_3_4|Proposition 3.4]] treats the primary family,
  including the logarithmic-moment expansion needed for its stated error.
- [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/theorem_1_1|Theorem 1.1]], [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/corollary_1_2|Corollary 1.2]] and
  [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/strict_transfer|the strict transfer]] complete the consequences.

These are ordinary proofs relative to the precise classical PNT and
Mertens inputs stated in [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/notation]]. The analytic proofs of those
external theorems are not included.

**Distinct additional arguments.** [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/proposition_4_1|Proposition 4.1]]
and its Oppermann variant insert bad prime squares. Their conclusion is
conditional on infinitely many prime-gap counterexamples.
[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/proposition_4_5|Proposition 4.5]] constructs an excess from powers of
two times primes, under its explicit uniform shortage of prime-ceiling
pairs. The [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/half_bound|restricted-family half bound]] and
[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/composite_barrier|composite barrier]] are complete local deductions.

The [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/sum_of_divisors_fibre|sum-of-divisors fibre proof]] uses Shengtong
Zhang's different powerful-number argument. It also yields
[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/remark_4_6|finiteness of the large fibres]], and the
[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/sum_of_divisors_analogue|full sigma analogue]] supplies the same
main asymptotic and reciprocal bound. In arXiv v1 the sharp sigma
inequality was still a missing input. Tao's dated
[blog discussion](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2023/09/06/monotone-non-decreasing-sequences-of-the-euler-totient-function/)
records Zhang's contribution in October 2023; v3 and the later versions
include it. The [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/dedekind_fibre|Dedekind fibre induction]] and
[[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/remark_4_7|Remark 4.7]] fully supply the finite cases and adaptations
left to the reader in the published source. Tao attributes this
Dedekind extension to an anonymous referee.

**Source precision.** The result pages preserve the relevant issues:
the reversed subtraction in the introductory motivation; the strict
endpoint in Lemma 3.1; the extra fiber logarithmic moment for the
stronger Proposition 3.4 error; the larger post-deletion remainder in
Proposition 4.5; and the $q>1$ range and finer mesh required by sigma
and psi. These are explicit compilation expansions, not an
author-issued erratum. The source's dyadic Vinogradov bound and its
hull-order direction are correct and are not treated as errors.

**Scope limits.** [[primes/tao_2024_monotone_nondecreasing_sequences_euler_totient_function/external_context]] separates historical constant
and decreasing maxima, the finer additive-64 conjecture, the unspecified
finite computation in Remark 4.3, and the exact external Selberg/RH and
Maynard comparisons. The latter are not main-proof inputs or new
complete proof claims here. Source-dated questions are not assertions of
current openness. This unit does not certify any formal proof, public
build or local kernel verification.
