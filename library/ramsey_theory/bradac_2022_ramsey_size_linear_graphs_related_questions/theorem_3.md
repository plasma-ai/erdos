title: A bipartite-target size bound for the one-edge subdivision of K_4
desc: |
  The Ramsey number of the one-edge subdivision K_4* against a bipartite
  no-isolate graph F is linear in the number of edges of F.
created: 2026-09-07T12:10:52Z

***

**Source.** Domagoj Bradač, Lior Gishboliner, and Benny Sudakov,
*On Ramsey Size-Linear Graphs and Related Questions*, Theorem 3 in the
published SIAM 2024 edition, printed p. 226 (physical p. 2). The same statement
is Theorem 3 on physical and printed p. 2 of arXiv:2202.10388v2 (10 March
2023).

**Statement.** Let $K_4^*$ be the graph obtained from $K_4$ by subdividing
one edge. For every bipartite graph $F$ with no isolated vertices,

$$
R(K_4^*,F)=O(e(F)).
$$

**Proof scope.** Exact statement and edition mapping only. The proof is in
the paper's section 3. It is not reconstructed or independently certified
here.

**Relation to E568.** The target graph $F$ must be bipartite. The result does
not give the all-no-isolate-target conclusion in
[[../wiki/problems/ramsey_theory/E0568/_index|Problem 568]], nor does it prove the problem's
tree-plus-clique criterion.

**Bears on.** [[../wiki/problems/ramsey_theory/E0567/_index|#567]]: the $H_5$ case of the
problem restricted to bipartite targets $H$, the site's second sentence on
this paper; [[../wiki/problems/ramsey_theory/E0568/_index|#568]].
