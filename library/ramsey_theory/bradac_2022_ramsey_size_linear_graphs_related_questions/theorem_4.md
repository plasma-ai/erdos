***

**Source.** Domagoj Bradač, Lior Gishboliner, and Benny Sudakov,
*On Ramsey Size-Linear Graphs and Related Questions*, Theorem 4 in the
published SIAM 2024 edition, printed p. 227 (physical p. 3). The same statement
is Theorem 4 on physical and printed p. 2 of arXiv:2202.10388v2 (10 March
2023).

**Statement.** Every subdivision of $K_4$ on at least six vertices is Ramsey
size-linear. Equivalently, if $H$ is any fixed such subdivision, then

$$
R(H,F)=O(e(F))
$$

for every graph $F$ with no isolated vertices.

**Proof scope.** Exact statement, the paper's definition of Ramsey
size-linearity, and edition mapping only. The proof is in section 4. It is not
reconstructed or independently certified here.

**Relation to E568.** This proves the all-target conclusion for a specific
family of fixed graphs $H$. It does not establish the implication asked in
[[../wiki/problems/ramsey_theory/E0568/_index|Problem 568]] for every graph satisfying the
two test-family hypotheses.

**Bears on.** [[../wiki/problems/ramsey_theory/E0567/_index|#567]]: the site's first
sentence on this paper; the five-vertex $K_4^*=H_5$ is exactly the excluded
subdivision, so the theorem does not decide the problem;
[[../wiki/problems/ramsey_theory/E0568/_index|#568]].
