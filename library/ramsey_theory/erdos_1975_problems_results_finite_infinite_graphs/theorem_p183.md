## Statement

**Theorem** (Erdős and Hajnal; p. 183, quoted). "Hajnal and I proved that
every graph which contains no infinite path has chromatic number
$\le\aleph_0$."

No restriction is placed on the cardinality of the graph.

**Source.** P. Erdős, *Problems and results on finite and infinite graphs*,
Recent advances in graph theory (Proc. Second Czechoslovak Sympos., Prague,
1974), Academia, Prague, 1975, pp. 183--192; Section I, p. 183. The
edition read is identified on the
[[ramsey_theory/erdos_1975_problems_results_finite_infinite_graphs/_index|source card]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the sentence was read on the printed page.
The paper gives no proof and no reference for it.
