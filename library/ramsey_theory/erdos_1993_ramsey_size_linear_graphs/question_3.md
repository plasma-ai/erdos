**Source.** Erdős, Faudree, Rousseau, and Schelp (1993), Question 3,
printed p. 398
(PDF, physical p. 11).

**Historical question.** Let $G$ be a fixed graph. Suppose there is a
constant $c$ such that, for every integer $n$ and every tree $T_n$ on $n$
vertices,

$$
R(G,T_n)\leq cn
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
R(G,K_n)\leq cn^2.
$$

Must $G$ be Ramsey size-linear, meaning that $R(G,H)=O_G(e(H))$ for every
graph $H$ without isolated vertices?

[[../wiki/problems/ramsey_theory/E0568/_index|Problem 568]]. The page transcribes the 1993
question only. It does not infer present-day openness from the age or wording
of that source.
