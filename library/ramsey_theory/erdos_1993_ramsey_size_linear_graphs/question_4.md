**Source.** Erdős, Faudree, Rousseau, and Schelp (1993), Question 4,
printed p. 398
(PDF, physical p. 11).

**Historical question.** Question 4 (p. 398) asks: "For which constants
$c$ is $r(C_{2k+1},H_n)\leq c(2k+1)n$, where $H_n$ is a graph of size $n$
without isolated vertices?" In the paper's terminology, size $n$ means
that $H_n$ has $n$ edges.

For fixed $k$, the substitution $c_k=c(2k+1)$ converts this to the coefficient
form in [[../wiki/problems/ramsey_theory/E0569/_index|Problem 569]]; conversely,
$c=c_k/(2k+1)$. This is only a harmless normalization of the coefficient.
The page records the 1993 question, not a solution or a claim about its
present-day status.
