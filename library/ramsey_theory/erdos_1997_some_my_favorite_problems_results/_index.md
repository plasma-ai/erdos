# ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results

[[ramsey_theory/_index|..]]

[[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/conjecture_p54|conjecture_p54]]: Erdős's 1997 statement of his conjecture that every finite Sidon sequence
extends to a Singer perfect difference set modulo p^2+p+1 for some prime
power p, his remark that it is perhaps too optimistic, and the weaker
conjecture on completing to a Sidon sequence with a_n < (1+ε) n^2; the
origin wording of Problem 707.

[[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/conjecture_p65|conjecture_p65]]: Erdős's 1997 statement that for n points forming a convex polygon, with
s_i the number of pairs at the i-th distance, he conjectured and Fishburn
proved that the sum of the squares of the s_i is below c n^3, with the
regular n-gon conjecture and the prize conjecture (5.3) for point sets
without convexity; the origin wording of Problem 94.

[[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/display_2_20|display_2_20]]: Erdős's 1997 dilation question: a sequence of reals above one with
|kα_i − α_j| ≥ 1 for all i ≠ j and all k generalizes a primitive
sequence; the printed question on the reciprocal sums, whose signs are
the reverse of the site's, and the prize offer; the origin wording
of Problem 143.

[[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/display_2_21|display_2_21]]: Erdős's 1997 prime-gap passage: his 1934 bound, Rankin's 1938 factor, the
conjecture that the Rankin bound holds for every constant c infinitely
often (the offer lowered) and the new offer for d_n > (log n)^{1+ε}; the
origin wording of Problem 4.

[[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/display_4_2|display_4_2]]: Erdős's 1997 statement of the Erdős–Szekeres bounds on the diagonal
Ramsey number, his offers of prizes for the existence and for
the value of the limit of f(n)^{1/n} with the guess c = 2,
and his offer for a constructive exponential lower bound; the
origin wording of Problems 77 and 78.

[[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/display_4_3|display_4_3]]: Erdős's 1997 statement of the Erdős–Hajnal–Rado bounds on the two-color
Ramsey number of the complete 3-uniform hypergraph, his belief that the
double-exponential upper bound is the truth, the Erdős–Hajnal unbalanced
triples result that seems to favor the lower bound, the criterion that
would make him doubt the upper bound, and Hajnal's four-color bound
r_3(n,n,n,n) > 2^{c 2^n}; the site's form of the bounds for Problem 564.

[[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/display_4_4|display_4_4]]: Erdős's 1997 statement of the Erdős–Graham question whether every
k-coloring of the integers at least 2 has a finite monochromatic set with
reciprocal sum one, with the threshold f(k), the every-positive-rational
conjecture, and the density forms: a reciprocal sum growing faster than
(log log n)^2 should force a unit subsum, and a reciprocal sum above
c log n is believed to; the origin wording of Problems 46 and 47.

[[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/display_4_5|display_4_5]]: Erdős's 1997 statement, attributed to Simonovits and himself, of the
degenerate Turán conjecture with the parameter shifted by one from the
site's, the remark that it is open even for r = 3, the companion
lower-bound conjecture for graphs with an induced subgraph of minimum
degree at least r, and the prize offers; the origin wording of
Problem 146.

[[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/problem_p51|problem_p51]]: Erdős's 1997 statement of the progression-free function r_k(n), the
bounds on r_3(n) he lists as current records, and his offers of prizes
for r_3(n) < n/(log n)^c for every c and for any
asymptotic formula for r_k(n); the origin wording of Problems 140 and 142.

[[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/problem_p62|problem_p62]]: Erdős's 1997 account of the off-diagonal Ramsey numbers: the bounds
c_1 n^2/log n < r_2(3,n) < c_2 n^2/log n credited to Ajtai, Komlós and
Szemerédi and to Kim, the wish for an asymptotic formula for r_2(3,n),
his former expectation r_2(4,n) > n^{3−ε} and
r_2(k,n) > n^{k−1}/(log n)^2 now doubted, Spencer's c n^{5/2} and the
printed upper bound r_2(ℓ,n) < c n^{ℓ−1}/log n; the origin wording of
Problems 165 and 166.

[[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/remark_p58|remark_p58]]: Erdős's 1997 sentence that he and Ricci proved the set of limit points of
the normalized prime gaps d_n/log n has positive measure, with the
expectation that the limit points are everywhere dense and the note on
Maier's partial results; the origin wording of Problem 5.

## Contents

The chapter has five sections: 1. Introduction (p. 47); 2. Number theory
(pp. 47--59); 3. Polynomials (pp. 59--60); 4. Combinatorics (pp. 60--64);
5. Geometry (pp. 65--66); references (pp. 66--67). The introduction tells
the Hilbert anecdote on $2^{\sqrt2}$ and notes that many of the problems
have come to carry cash prizes.

- § 2, pp. 47--49 (text layer): the distinct-subset-sums function $f(n)$
  with the conjecture (2.1) $f(n)<\log n/\log2+c$, the
  Erdős--Moser bound and the Conway--Guy example; covering systems (2.3)
  with the question whether the least modulus can be arbitrarily large,
  Choi's system with $n_1=20$, the odd-moduli question and
  Schinzel's question for a covering system with $n_i\nmid n_j$ for
  $i\ne j$, and the $2^k+p$ history (Romanoff, Crocker, Gallagher); the
  Erdős--Selfridge theorem on products of consecutive integers.
- § 2, pp. 50--52 (pp. 50--51 page images): the Tauberian theorem (2.4);
  van der Waerden numbers $W(k)$, Shelah's primitive recursive bound,
  $W(k)>2^{k/2}$ (Erdős--Rado), Berlekamp's $W(p+1)\ge p\cdot2^p$, Graham's
  tower offer. Then, quoted (p. 50): "let $r_k(n)$ be the smallest
  integer for which every sequence of integers $1\le a_1<a_2<\cdots<a_t\le
  n$ with $t\ge r_k(n)$ contains an arithmetic progression of $k$ terms";
  the Erdős--Turán conjecture $r_k(n)/n\to0$; p. 51: Salem--Spencer
  $r_3(n)>n^{1-c\log\log n}$ (so printed, for $n^{1-c/\log\log n}$),
  Behrend $r_3(n)>n\exp(-c\sqrt{\log n})$ ("the current record"), Roth
  $r_3(m)\le cn/\log\log n$ (so printed),
  Heath-Brown and Szemerédi $r_3(n)\le n/(\log n)^\alpha$, $\alpha\approx
  1/4$; the two offers paged on
  [[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/problem_p51|problem_p51]]

  formula for $r_k(n)$, which he calls probably unattackable at present) and

  prize, Furstenberg; the conjecture (2.5) that $\sum1/a_k=\infty$ forces

- § 2, pp. 52--55 (p. 54 page image): Sidon's two questions;
  $c_1\log n<f(n)<c_2\log n$ (2.7) by a random sequence, the Erdős--Turán

  $S(n)>n^{1/2}-n^{1/2-\epsilon}$ (2.13) from Singer, the conjectures
  (2.14) and (2.15) with a prize for "clearing up these two problems";

  (2.17), the conjecture (2.18), the Erdős--Sárközy conjecture

**Results.** All are statements without proof.

- [[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/problem_p51|Problem (p. 51)]]:
  the offer for $r_3(n)<n/(\log n)^c$ for every $c$ and the
  offer for an asymptotic formula for $r_k(n)$.
- [[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/conjecture_p54|Conjecture (p. 54)]]:
  every finite Sidon sequence completes to a Singer perfect difference set
  modulo $p^2+p+1$, $p=q^\alpha$; its weaker form and (2.16).
- [[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/display_2_20|Display (2.20)]]:
  the dilation condition $|k\alpha_i-\alpha_j|\ge1$ and the reciprocal-sum
  question, with a prize.
- [[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/display_2_21|Display (2.21)]]:
  $d_n>(\log n)^{1+\epsilon}$ infinitely often, with a prize, and the
  Erdős--Rankin conjecture with a smaller one.
- [[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/remark_p58|Remark (p. 58)]]:
  the Erdős--Ricci positive-measure theorem for the limit points of
  $d_n/\log n$ and the expectation that they are dense.
- [[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/display_4_2|Display (4.2)]]:
  $cn2^{n/2}<r_2(n,n)<\binom{2n-2}{n-1}$, the offers for
  $\lim f(n)^{1/n}$ and for a constructive $f(n)>(1+\epsilon)^n$.
- [[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/problem_p62|Problem (pp. 62--63)]]:
  the $r_2(3,n)$ bounds with the asymptotic-formula wish, and the
  retracted expectation on $r_2(4,n)$ and $r_2(k,n)$.
- [[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/display_4_3|Display (4.3)]]:
  $2^{cn^2}<r_3(n,n)<2^{2^n}$ and the evidence on each side.
- [[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/display_4_4|Display (4.4)]]:
  the monochromatic unit-fraction question and the reciprocal-sum
  thresholds.
- [[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/display_4_5|Display (4.5)]]:
  the degenerate Turán conjecture $T_n(H)<cn^{2-1/(r-1)}$ and its
  companion, with a prize for each.
- [[ramsey_theory/erdos_1997_some_my_favorite_problems_results/conjecture_p65|Conjecture (p. 65)]]:
  $\sum s_i^2<cn^3$ for convex polygons, stated as proved by Fishburn,
  with the regular $n$-gon conjecture and (5.3).
