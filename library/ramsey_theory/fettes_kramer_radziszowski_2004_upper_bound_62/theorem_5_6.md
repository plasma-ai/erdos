## Statement

There is no edge-coloring of $K_{62}$ with colors in
$\{1,2,3,4\}$ that has no monochromatic triangle. The palette need not
be used surjectively. Equivalently, $R_4(3)\leq62$, where $R_4(3)$ is
the least positive order forcing such a triangle in every four-coloring.
