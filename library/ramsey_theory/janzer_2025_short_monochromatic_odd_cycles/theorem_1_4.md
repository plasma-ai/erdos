***

**Source.** Oliver Janzer and Fredy Yip, *Short Monochromatic Odd Cycles*,
Theorem 1.4 in the
[published CUP 2026 alternate](janzer_2025_short_monochromatic_odd_cycles_cup_2026.pdf),
article and physical p. 2, DOI
[10.1017/S0305004125101801](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004125101801).
The same statement is Theorem 1.4 on physical and printed p. 2 of the
selected arXiv:2506.14910v1 PDF.

**Statement.** In every $k$-edge-coloring of $K_{2^k+1}$, there is a
monochromatic odd cycle of length

$$
O\!\left(k^{3/2}2^{k/2}\right).
$$

Equivalently, for the least forced length $L(k)$,

$$
L(k)=O\!\left(k^{3/2}2^{k/2}\right).
$$

**Proof scope.** Exact statement and edition mapping only. The proof is in
section 2 of the paper. It is not reconstructed or independently certified
here.

**Relation to E609.** The host graph is exactly $K_{2^k+1}$, so this is an
upper bound for [[../wiki/problems/ramsey_theory/E0609/_index|Problem 609]]. The result does
not match the known lower bound and does not determine the growth order of
$L(k)$.
