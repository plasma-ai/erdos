***

**Source.** Oliver Janzer and Fredy Yip, *Short Monochromatic Odd Cycles*,
Theorem 1.5 in the
[published CUP 2026 alternate](janzer_2025_short_monochromatic_odd_cycles_cup_2026.pdf),
article and physical p. 2, DOI
[10.1017/S0305004125101801](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004125101801).
The same statement is Theorem 1.5 on physical and printed p. 2 of the
selected arXiv:2506.14910v1 PDF.

**Statement.** Let $0<\delta\leq1$ and suppose that

$$
n=(1+\delta)2^k
$$

is an integer. Every $k$-edge-coloring of $K_n$ contains a monochromatic
odd cycle of length at most

$$
4k^{3/2}\delta^{-1/2}.
$$

**Proof scope.** Exact statement and edition mapping only. The proof is in
section 2 of the paper. It is not reconstructed or independently certified
here.

**Relation to E609.** This is a separately quantified near-threshold
generalization. Taking $\delta=2^{-k}$ gives $n=2^k+1$ and the order of the
bound in Theorem 1.4. It does not replace the exact-threshold formulation of
[[../wiki/problems/ramsey_theory/E0609/_index|Problem 609]].
