## Statement

Setting. A space-$s$ source is generated by a random walk on a branching
program of length $n$ and width $2^s$, each edge labelled with a transition
probability and a bit, the source being the concatenation of the $n$ bits
produced along the walk (Definition 7.10, p. 35, credited to the paper's
[65]). Extractor, error and explicit are as in Definition 1.1 (p. 1).

**Theorem 1.8** (p. 6). For every constant $\epsilon>0$ there is a constant
$c>1$ such that for every $s>0$ there is an explicit extractor
$\mathsf{SpExt}:\{0,1\}^n\to\{0,1\}$ with error $\epsilon$ for space-$s$
sources on $n$ bits with min-entropy $k\ge2s+c\log n$.

In the body the statement is Corollary 7.17 (p. 36), with the quantifiers in
another order: for every $s>0$ and every constant $\epsilon>0$ there is a
constant $c>1$ and an explicit $\mathsf{SpExt}$ for space-$s$ sources with
min-entropy $k\ge2s+c\log n$ and error $\epsilon$. As printed, the
introduction's $c$ depends only on $\epsilon$ while the body's may also
depend on $s$; the one-line derivation the paper gives (below) shows no
dependence of $c$ on $s$, but it was not reconstructed here.

## Proof pointer

P. 36. Lemma 7.16, from Chattopadhyay and Liao (the paper's [22]): a space-$s$
source with min-entropy at least $k_1+k_2+2s+2\log(n/\epsilon)$ is
$3\epsilon$-close to a convex combination of sources $X_1\circ X_2$ with
$X_1$ and $X_2$ independent, $H_\infty(X_1)\ge k_1$, $H_\infty(X_2)\ge k_2$
and $X_2$ a space-$s$ source. The paper then applies its sumset extractor
(Theorem 7.13, [[ramsey_theory/li_2023_two_source_extractors_asymptotically_optimal_entropy/theorem_1_7|Theorem 1.7]]).
