## Statement

**Question 5.2** (p. 12). Does every tree $T$ on $n$ vertices whose color
classes have sizes $n/3$ and $2n/3$ satisfy $r(T)\le1.4n+o(n)$?

The paper introduces it after Question 5.1 with the sentence "More
ambitiously, one can ask the following." (p. 12). With $n=3k$ the bound
reads $r(T)\le4.2k+o(k)$: the double stars show that the value $4k-1$ asked
about in Problem 549 is not the maximum over such trees, and the question
asks whether their $4.2k$ is.

**Source.** S. Norin, Y. R. Sun and Y. Zhao, *Asymptotics of Ramsey numbers
of double stars*, arXiv:1605.03612v1 (11 May 2016), p. 12, read on the page
image.

**Read depth.** Claims checked (the question read on the page image). A
question has no proof.
