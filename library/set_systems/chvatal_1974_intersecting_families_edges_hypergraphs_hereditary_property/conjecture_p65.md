## Statement

**Conjecture** (p. 65, unnumbered), as posed: "Let $F$ be a family of subsets
of a finite set $S$ such that $X\in F$, $Y\subset X\Rightarrow Y\in F$. Then
there is a $t\in S$ such that every intersecting subfamily $G$ of $F$
satisfies $|G|\le|\{X\in F: t\in X\}|$."

Here a family is intersecting when any two of its members, not necessarily
distinct, meet (p. 62). The hypothesis is closure under taking subsets, and
the finite ground set $S$ is part of it. The paper introduces the conjecture
as a possible "strengthening of our theorem" (p. 65): the
[[set_systems/chvatal_1974_intersecting_families_edges_hypergraphs_hereditary_property/theorem_p62|Theorem (p. 62)]]
gives the conclusion with $t=1$ under the stronger hypothesis of closure
under the left-shift order on $S=\{1,\dots,n\}$.

**Source.** V. Chvátal, *Intersecting families of edges in hypergraphs having
the hereditary property*, in: Hypergraph Seminar (Ohio State Univ., Columbus,
1972), Lecture Notes in Math. **411**, Springer, Berlin, 1974, pp. 61--66;
the Conjecture on p. 65. The edition is identified on the
[[set_systems/chvatal_1974_intersecting_families_edges_hypergraphs_hereditary_property/_index|source card]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the statement was read clause by clause on
the page image, and the quotation matches the print. A conjecture has no

## Proof pointer

None; the paper poses the statement as a conjecture and proves only the
case of the Theorem (p. 62).
