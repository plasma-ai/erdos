Peter Frankl and Vojtěch Rödl, *Forbidden intersections*, Transactions
of the American Mathematical Society **300** (1), March 1987, 259–286.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1987-0871675-6).

The copy read for this card is the 28-page published scan, retrieved
from [Frankl's author archive](https://www.renyi.hu/~pfrankl/1987-3.pdf)
on 2026-09-05. The [source record](source_record.json) gives that copy's
identity, publication metadata, and version scope. The title-page
received date, 24 October 1985, is distinct from the March 1987
publication date. No different
manuscript version or author-issued erratum is asserted. The file prints "©1987
American Mathematical Society" on its first page (printed p. 259) and, in every
page's footer, "License or copyright restrictions may apply to redistribution;
see http://www.ams.org/journal-terms-of-use", every other right reserved.

The principal forbidden-intersection theorem says that, for any fixed
positive buffer away from zero and half the ground-set size, forbidding
one intersection size forces a family below $(2-\epsilon)^n$.
The [[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/theorem_1_1|one-family proof]]
and the stronger
[[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/theorem_1_4|two-family product bound]]
use an iterative deletion of coordinates while tracking a widening
forbidden interval. The weighted proof in
[[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/theorem_1_5]]
is expanded beyond the source's outline, including both bias regimes.
The [[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/large_set_construction|large-set construction]]
provides the source's contrasting entropy lower bound.

The counting refinements pass through the
[[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/theorem_6_1|middle-layer argument]],
the [[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/proposition_7_2|containing-set averaging lemma]],
and [[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/theorem_1_14|general two-set count]]
to the [[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/theorem_1_15|matrix theorem]]
and [[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/theorem_1_16|full joint-array theorem]].
The last result controls every atom of the intersection of several
ordered partitions. It includes distinct families and compatible
marginals, and gives a positive exponential fraction of the exact
full-family count. This is the input used by the later simplex arguments;
pairwise intersection estimates alone would not supply it.

The separate Section 4 method is preserved in
[[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/theorem_1_7]],
followed by the [[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/theorem_1_9|weak delta-system induction]],
the [[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/theorem_1_11|asymptotic cube-orthogonality result]],
and the [[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/theorem_1_13|sphere bound]].
The [[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/theorem_1_10|coding proof]]
constructs the positive integral pattern matrix needed by the source
sketch. Section 10's
[[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/proposition_10_1|constant-intersection]],
[[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/theorem_10_2|parity]],
[[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/theorem_10_3|modular]],
and [[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/theorem_10_5|constant-distance]]
arguments are reconstructed, as is the
[[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/theorem_11_1|Galvin-family bound]].

The [[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/source_scope|coverage record]]
states the exact limits. In particular, the
[[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/numerical_corollaries_scope|advertised numerical constants]]
are not certified by the qualitative proofs, and the literal unrestricted
[[set_systems/frankl_1987_forbidden_intersections/theorem_9_1_scope|Theorem 9.1 metric claim]]
has a concrete obstruction. Theorem 1.11's proof is recorded in its
proved eventual range; the printed finite range is not silently included.
Theorem 1.18 is an announcement whose proof the source explicitly defers
to another paper. None of these qualifications limits the fully expanded
compatible joint-array theorem. Harper's isoperimetry and invariant
rotation measure remain the precisely identified external inputs.
