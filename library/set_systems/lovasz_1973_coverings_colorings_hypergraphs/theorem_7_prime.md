## Statement

Here $\nu(H)$ is the largest number of pairwise disjoint edges of $H$ and
$\tau(H)$ the least number of points meeting every edge (p. 3), so
$\nu(H)=1$ says that any two edges intersect.

**Theorem 7'** (p. 10). Let $H$ be an $r$-uniform hypergraph with more than
$r^r$ edges and $\nu(H)=1$. Then $\tau(H)\leq r-1$.

Equivalently, an $r$-uniform hypergraph in which any two edges meet and no
$r-1$ points meet every edge has at most $r^r$ edges.

**Source.** László Lovász, *Coverings and colorings of hypergraphs*,
Proceedings of the Fourth Southeastern Conference on Combinatorics, Graph
Theory, and Computing (Boca Raton, 1973), Congressus Numerantium VIII,
3--12; Theorem 7' on printed p. 10. The edition is recorded on the
[[set_systems/lovasz_1973_coverings_colorings_hypergraphs/_index|source card]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the statement was read against the print. The
paper gives no proof.
