# set_theory/abraham_1985_consistency_partition_theorems_continuous_colorings_structure

[[set_theory/_index|..]]

[[set_theory/abraham_1985_consistency_partition_theorems_continuous_colorings_structure/theorem_10_1|theorem_10_1]]: Abraham, Rubin and Shelah's characterization: for a finite poset with an
involution (L, <=, *), MA_aleph_1 together with the statement that the
homogeneous aleph_1-dense order types plus the empty set, under embeddability
and reversal, are isomorphic to (L, <=, *) is consistent iff (L, <=, *) is a
distributive lattice with an involution.

[[set_theory/abraham_1985_consistency_partition_theorems_continuous_colorings_structure/theorem_11_1|theorem_11_1]]: Shelah's ZFC theorem in Abraham, Rubin and Shelah: a c.c.c. forcing of power
aleph_2, with aleph_2 dense sets, such that any filter meeting them all yields
a set of reals of power aleph_1 with a two-color open coloring admitting no
countable homogeneous partition; hence MA + OCA implies 2^aleph_0 = aleph_2.

[[set_theory/abraham_1985_consistency_partition_theorems_continuous_colorings_structure/theorem_1_1|theorem_1_1]]: Abraham, Rubin and Shelah's theorem that SOCA is consistent with ZFC: every
second countable Hausdorff space of power aleph_1, colored in two colors with
one color class open in X x X, has an uncountable homogeneous subset.

[[set_theory/abraham_1985_consistency_partition_theorems_continuous_colorings_structure/theorem_3_1|theorem_3_1]]: Abraham, Rubin and Shelah's theorem that Martin's Axiom is consistent with
their open coloring axiom OCA (every finite symmetric open cover of D(X), X
second countable Hausdorff of power aleph_1, admits a partition of X into
countably many homogeneous sets), with SOCA, and with the existence of an
increasing set.

[[set_theory/abraham_1985_consistency_partition_theorems_continuous_colorings_structure/theorem_8_3|theorem_8_3]]: Abraham, Rubin and Shelah's theorem that MA_aleph_1 is consistent with the
existence, for every k > 0, of a k-entangled set of reals, from which they
derive that the Magidor-Malitz language is consistently not countably
compact.

[[set_theory/abraham_1985_consistency_partition_theorems_continuous_colorings_structure/theorem_9_2|theorem_9_2]]: Abraham, Rubin and Shelah's theorem that MA, the axiom BA1 that every two
K-shuffles are isomorphic, and 2^aleph_0 >= aleph_alpha are jointly
consistent; the paper draws from it the consistency of Baumgartner's axiom BA
with 2^aleph_0 > aleph_2.

***

Abraham, Uri and Rubin, Matatyahu and Shelah, Saharon, On the consistency of
some partition theorems for continuous colorings, and the structure of
{$\aleph_1$}-dense real order types. Ann. Pure Appl. Logic 29 (1985), 123--206.
The copy read for this card is the publisher's PDF from the Shelah archive
(shelah.logic.at/papers/153/); it prints "0168-0072/85/$3.30 © 1985, Elsevier
Science Publishers B.V. (North-Holland)" in its first-page footer (the text
layer renders the symbol as "0"), every other right reserved.

The paper develops techniques in forcing with the countable chain condition
(c.c.c.), chiefly Shelah's club method, the explicit contradiction method, the
preassignment of colors and Rubin's tail method, and applies them to sets of
reals of power aleph_1: partition axioms for open and semiopen colorings of
second countable spaces of power aleph_1, and the embeddability and
isomorphism relations on the class K of aleph_1-dense sets of reals. Section 1
proves SOCA, the semiopen coloring axiom, consistent (Theorem 1.1, p. 132).
Section 3 proves MA + OCA + SOCA + ISA consistent (Theorem 3.1, p. 141), where
OCA says that for every second countable Hausdorff space X of power aleph_1 and
every finite cover of D(X) = X x X minus the diagonal by symmetric open sets, X
splits into countably many pieces X_i each with D(X_i) inside one set of the
cover, and ISA says that an increasing set of reals exists. Section 5 replaces
CH in the ground model by an axiom A1 that survives c.c.c. forcings of power
below 2^{aleph_1}, so that finite support iterations of length 2^{aleph_1}
can raise the continuum beyond aleph_2. Section 8 proves MA_{aleph_1}
consistent with a k-entangled set for every k > 0, and deduces that the
Magidor-Malitz language is consistently not countably compact
(Theorem 8.3, p. 171). Section 9 builds a forcing that makes two members of K
isomorphic without CH and proves MA + BA1 + 2^{aleph_0} >= aleph_alpha
consistent (Theorem 9.2, p. 179), which gives Con(BA + 2^{aleph_0} > aleph_2)
for Baumgartner's axiom BA that every two aleph_1-dense sets of reals are
order-isomorphic. Section 10 characterizes the finite structures that the
homogeneous members of K can form under MA_{aleph_1} (Theorem 10.1, p. 187).
Section 11 proves in ZFC that MA + OCA implies 2^{aleph_0} = aleph_2
(Theorem 11.1, p. 203). The paper's summary also records that MA + OCA almost
determines the structure of K^H: with ISA it is as described in Section 6, and
with not-ISA, BA holds (p. 127).

The paper does not treat the quotient Boolean algebras of Erdős problem 1123;
the problem page lists it among its references, and it bears on the problem
only through Theorem 3.1, the consistency of MA with its axiom OCA. That axiom,
in which every set of the cover is open, is a different axiom from Todorčević's
Open Coloring Axiom.

Read status: claims checked for the results linked below, statements and
definitions read clause by clause on the page images of the print; the proofs
were followed for structure only. Nothing here is independently reviewed.

**Results.**

- [[set_theory/abraham_1985_consistency_partition_theorems_continuous_colorings_structure/theorem_1_1|Theorem 1.1]]
  (p. 132): SOCA is consistent with ZFC.
- [[set_theory/abraham_1985_consistency_partition_theorems_continuous_colorings_structure/theorem_3_1|Theorem 3.1]]
  (p. 141): MA + OCA + SOCA + ISA is consistent.
- [[set_theory/abraham_1985_consistency_partition_theorems_continuous_colorings_structure/theorem_8_3|Theorem 8.3]]
  (p. 171): MA_{aleph_1} together with a k-entangled set for every k > 0 is
  consistent; so the Magidor-Malitz language is consistently not countably
  compact.
- [[set_theory/abraham_1985_consistency_partition_theorems_continuous_colorings_structure/theorem_9_2|Theorem 9.2]]
  (p. 179): MA + BA1 + 2^{aleph_0} >= aleph_alpha is consistent, giving
  Con(BA + 2^{aleph_0} > aleph_2).
- [[set_theory/abraham_1985_consistency_partition_theorems_continuous_colorings_structure/theorem_10_1|Theorem 10.1]]
  (p. 187): for a finite poset with an involution (L, <=, *), MA_{aleph_1}
  with (K^H plus the empty set, modulo isomorphism, under embeddability and
  reversal) isomorphic to (L, <=, *) is consistent iff (L, <=, *) is a
  distributive lattice with an involution.
- [[set_theory/abraham_1985_consistency_partition_theorems_continuous_colorings_structure/theorem_11_1|Theorem 11.1]]
  (p. 203): a ZFC forcing construction showing MA + OCA implies
  2^{aleph_0} = aleph_2.
