**Identifiers.** James E. Baumgartner, Remarks on partition ordinals. In: Set
theory and its applications (Toronto, ON, 1987), Lecture Notes in Mathematics
1401, Springer, Berlin, 1989, pp. 5--17. DOI
<https://doi.org/10.1007/BFb0097328>; Zbl 0703.03027; MR 1031762.

**What was read.** The zbMATH Open record Zbl 0703.03027, read 2026-09-27
through the zbMATH Open API. Its review reports that §1 surveys partition
ordinals, ordinals $\alpha$ with $\alpha\to(\alpha,n)^2$ for all finite $n$;
that §2 treats Ramsey near-orderings; and that §3 shows that
$\mathrm{MA}(\aleph_1)$ implies that $\omega_1\omega$ and $\omega_1\omega^2$
are partition ordinals, in contrast with the Erdős--Hajnal theorem that the
continuum hypothesis implies $\alpha\not\to(\alpha,3)^2$ for both. The
publisher's chapter page for the DOI served no abstract or preview on the same
date.

**Corroboration.** The paper of Chen, Garti and Weinert
([[set_theory/chen_2018_cardinal_characteristics_continuum_partitions/_index|card]])
states in its introduction, after Fact 1.1, that Baumgartner proved in this
chapter that $\mathrm{ZFC}+\mathrm{MA}_{\aleph_1}$ implies
$\omega_1\omega\to(\omega_1\omega,n)^2$ for all natural numbers $n$. The
catalog page for Problem 1171 and Gao's deposit cite the chapter for the case
$n=3$ only.
