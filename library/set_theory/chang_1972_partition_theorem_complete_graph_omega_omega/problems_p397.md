## Statement

As printed on p. 397 (PDF p. 2 of the publisher's scan, page image), after
the remark that the proof of the theorem "is long and complicated": "The
methods used in the following sections will yield, when properly used,
other known (positive) results involving the $\to$ notation and countable
ordinals. We believe that it can also be used to settle some of the unknown
problems. Two representative problems are:

$\omega^\omega\to(\omega^\omega,4)^2$?

$\omega^{\omega^\alpha}\to(\omega^{\omega^\alpha},3)^2$ if
$\alpha<\omega_1$?"

The arrow notation is the paper's (p. 396): a red/blue coloring of the
pairs from the ordinal on the left has a red set of the finite size named
or a blue set of the order type named. The second problem asks, for each
countable $\alpha$, whether every red/blue coloring of the edges of
$K_{\omega^{\omega^\alpha}}$ has a red triangle or a blue complete graph of
the same order type; it is Problem 592's question for the exponents
$\beta=\omega^\alpha$ (the problem writes $\alpha=\omega^\beta$, so the
paper's $\alpha$ is the problem page's $\gamma$), and the paper's
[[set_theory/chang_1972_partition_theorem_complete_graph_omega_omega/theorem_p396|Theorem]]
is its case $\alpha=1$, the problem's $\beta=\omega$. The paper calls both
problems unknown and offers its methods for them; it does not attempt either.
Footnote 1 on the same page bears on the first: "E. C. Milner has written me
that he has obtained a proof of $\omega^\omega\to(\omega^\omega,m)^2$,
$m<\omega$, using the methods of this paper. He has also found a somewhat
simpler proof of the normal form lemma. Added in proof March 15, 1972: Jean
Larson has obtained a shorter proof of the author's and Milner's results.
This proof is a part of her Ph.D. thesis, Dartmouth College, 1972." Milner's
result contains the first problem at $m=4$; no proof of it is printed here,
and the footnote reports it from a letter.

**Source.** C. C. Chang, A Partition Theorem for the Complete Graph on
$\omega^\omega$, J. Combinatorial Theory (A) 12 (1972), 396--452; the two
problems and footnote 1 on printed p. 397 (PDF p. 2), read on the page
image. The artifact is identified in the
[[set_theory/chang_1972_partition_theorem_complete_graph_omega_omega/_index|source digest]].

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the paragraph, the two displayed problems
and footnote 1 were read clause by clause on the page image.
A posed problem has no proof to check. Nothing here is independently
reviewed.

## Proof pointer

None: posed problems. The first is answered, per footnote 1, by Milner's
$\omega^\omega\to(\omega^\omega,m)^2$ for $m<\omega$, with Larson's shorter
proof of the author's and Milner's results in her 1972 thesis; the
published form of Larson's proof is Problem 590's [La73], not held. The
second is the question of Problem 592, open on that page; Problem 592's
[Sc10],
[[set_theory/schipperus_2010_countable_partition_ordinals/_index|in the library]],
answers it for $\alpha$ the sum of one or two indecomposable ordinals
(yes) and for $\alpha$ the sum of four or more (no).
