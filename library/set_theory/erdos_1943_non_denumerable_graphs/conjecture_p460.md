## Statement

**Conjecture** (p. 460, unnumbered). Following the proof of
[[set_theory/erdos_1943_non_denumerable_graphs/theorem_2|Theorem 2]], the
paper says it seems likely that a stronger theorem also holds: if the
continuum hypothesis is false and each of the sets $M_1,M_2,\ldots$ consists
of rationally independent real numbers, then $\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}M_n$ has
inner measure $0$. It is stronger than the converse half of Theorem 2, which
says only that such a union is not the whole real line.

The paper gives no proof and no partial result toward it.
