## Statement

**Theorem** (p. 461, unnumbered). The continuum has power $\aleph_{x+1}$ if
and only if both of the following hold: the set $R$ of real numbers is the
union of $\aleph_x$ sets, each consisting of rationally independent numbers;
and $R$ is not the union of fewer than $\aleph_x$ such sets.

## Proof pointer

P. 461: the paper says only that the proof is the same as that of
Theorem 2. In that proof the role of Theorem 1 would fall to the
$\aleph_x$ remark stated without proof on p. 459 (see
[[set_theory/erdos_1943_non_denumerable_graphs/theorem_1|Theorem 1]]); the
paper does not say this.
