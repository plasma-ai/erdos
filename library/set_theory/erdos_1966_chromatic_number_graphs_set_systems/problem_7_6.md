## Statement

**Problem 7.6** (p. 77, open in the paper). "Is it true that for every
graph $\mathcal G$ of Chr $(\mathcal G)>\omega$ there exists an integer $j$
such that $\mathcal G$ contains odd circuits of length $2i+1$ for every
$i\geq j$?"

The authors add that the answer is affirmative under the stronger
assumption $\operatorname{Chr}(\mathcal G)>\omega_2$, and omit the proof
because they do not regard that result as final. They also note that a
positive answer would follow from the connectivity assertion recorded on
[[set_theory/erdos_1966_chromatic_number_graphs_set_systems/assertion_p77|the p. 77 assertion page]].
The problem is stated in contrast to
[[set_theory/erdos_1966_chromatic_number_graphs_set_systems/theorem_7_5|Theorem 7.5]],
which gives infinitely many odd circuit lengths already at chromatic number
$\omega$, and to
[[set_theory/erdos_1966_chromatic_number_graphs_set_systems/theorem_7_4|Theorem 7.4]],
which shows that short odd circuits can be avoided at every infinite
chromatic number.
