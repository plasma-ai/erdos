## Statement

The survey's entry on Problem 35 of the 1967 list (printed p. 278) makes two
points. The problem was badly stated: under the conditions of the problem a
free set of power $\aleph_\omega$ had already been proved, in Theorem 49 of the
paper's reference [10]. The real problem is whether there is a free set of
power $\aleph_{\omega+1}$.

The survey does not restate the conditions of Problem 35. Reference [10]
(p. 287) is P. Erdős, A. Hajnal and R. Rado, Partition relations for cardinal
numbers, Acta Math. Acad. Sci. Hungar. 16 (1965), 93--196, whose card is
[[ramsey_theory/erdos_1965_partition_relations_cardinal_numbers/_index|the 1965 partition-relations card]].

**Source.** P. Erdős and A. Hajnal, Unsolved and solved problems in set
theory, Proceedings of Symposia in Pure Mathematics 25 (1974), 269--287;
printed p. 278, reference [10] on p. 287 (PDF pp. 10 and 19 of the Rényi
archive scan identified on the
[[set_theory/erdos_1974_unsolved_solved_problems_set_theory/_index|source card]]).

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the entry and reference [10] were read clause by
clause on the page images. The survey gives no proof; Theorem 49 of [10] was not
checked here.

## Proof pointer

None in this survey; it points to Theorem 49 of [10] for the free set of power
$\aleph_\omega$.

## Dependencies

Theorem 49 of Erdős, Hajnal and Rado (1965), cited, not restated.

## Bears on

- [[../wiki/problems/set_theory/E1173/_index|Problem 1173]]: the problem asks,
  under GCH, for a free set of cardinality $\aleph_{\omega+1}$ for a set mapping
  on $\omega_{\omega+1}$ with values of size at most $\aleph_\omega$ and pairwise
  intersections below $\aleph_\omega$. The survey names a free set of power
  $\aleph_{\omega+1}$ as the real problem under the conditions of Problem 35,
  which it does not restate, and records a free set of power $\aleph_\omega$ as
  known. It gives no answer to the $\aleph_{\omega+1}$ question.
