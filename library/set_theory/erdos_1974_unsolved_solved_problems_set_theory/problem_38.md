## Statement

The entry on Problem 38 (printed p. 279) opens "S. Hechler [18] proved" and
continues with three statements, none proved in the survey.

1. **Theorem** (p. 279, quoted). "If C.H. is true then 38/A is false even if
   $f(x)$ is a sequence with the only limit point $x$ for every $x\in R$."
2. 38/A is independent from ZFC + $2^{\aleph_0}>\aleph_1$.
3. If MA is true, then 38/C is false even if $f(x)$ is of measure $0$ for
   $x\in R$.

The survey does not restate parts 38/A and 38/C of the 1967 list. The
attribution to Hechler is made once, at the head of the entry; the survey does
not say whether the second and third statements are also Hechler's. Its
reference [18] (p. 287) is S. Hechler, A note on some topological problems of
Erdős (preprint).

**Source.** P. Erdős and A. Hajnal, Unsolved and solved problems in set
theory, Proceedings of Symposia in Pure Mathematics 25 (1974), 269--287;
printed p. 279, reference [18] on p. 287 (PDF pp. 11 and 19 of the Rényi
archive scan identified on the
[[set_theory/erdos_1974_unsolved_solved_problems_set_theory/_index|source card]]).

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the entry and reference [18] were read clause by
clause on the page images. The survey gives no proof, and Hechler's preprint is
not held.
