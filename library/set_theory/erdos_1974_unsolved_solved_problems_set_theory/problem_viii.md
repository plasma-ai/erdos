## Statement

**Problem VIII** (printed p. 285). Is

$$
(\omega_2,\omega)\xrightarrow{0}(\omega_1+\omega,\omega_1\cdot\omega)^2
$$

provable in ZFC + G.C.H., or is the negative relation consistent with
ZFC + G.C.H.?

The relation $\xrightarrow{0}$ is that of Definition 2 (p. 284), recorded on
[[set_theory/erdos_1974_unsolved_solved_problems_set_theory/theorem_p284|theorem_p284]].
The survey motivates the problem on pp. 284--285: when $a>\omega_1$ the
independence results make the question harder, and since
$\omega_2\not\to(\omega_1+\omega)^2_2$ is consistent with G.C.H., it considers
problems with $c$ smaller than $a$; it calls Problem VIII the first such
problem that remains.

**Source.** P. Erdős and A. Hajnal, Unsolved and solved problems in set
theory, Proceedings of Symposia in Pure Mathematics 25 (1974), 269--287;
printed pp. 284--285 (PDF pp. 16--17 of the Rényi archive scan identified on
the [[set_theory/erdos_1974_unsolved_solved_problems_set_theory/_index|source card]]).

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the problem and its motivation were read clause
by clause on the page images. A question has no proof to check.
