## Statement

After reporting Chang's, Milner's and Larson's theorems
([[set_theory/erdos_1974_unsolved_solved_problems_set_theory/theorem_p270_chang|theorem_p270_chang]]),
the survey says that the following remains open (printed p. 270): is

$$
\omega^{\omega^\rho}\to(\omega^{\omega^\rho},n)^2
$$

true for $n<\omega$ and $0<\rho<\omega_1$?

It then reports a remark of Galvin, without proof: if
$\omega^\alpha\to(\omega^\alpha,3)^2$ and $2<\alpha<\omega_1$, then $\alpha$ is
of the form $\omega^\rho$.

The survey also asks, on the same page, whether there is an order type $\Phi$
with $\Phi\to(\Phi,3)^2$ but $\Phi\not\to(\Phi,4)^2$.

**Source.** P. Erdős and A. Hajnal, Unsolved and solved problems in set
theory, Proceedings of Symposia in Pure Mathematics 25 (1974), 269--287;
printed p. 270 (PDF p. 2 of the Rényi archive scan identified on the
[[set_theory/erdos_1974_unsolved_solved_problems_set_theory/_index|source card]]).

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the question and Galvin's remark were read
clause by clause on the page image. A question has no proof to check, and the
survey gives none for the remark.
