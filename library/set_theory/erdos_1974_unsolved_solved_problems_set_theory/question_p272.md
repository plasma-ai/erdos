## Statement

**Context** (printed p. 272). The survey reports, without proof, that Hajnal,
by a slight generalization of Prikry's consistency result, proved

$$
\mathrm{Con}(\mathrm{ZF})\Rightarrow\mathrm{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC}+\mathrm{G.C.H.}+\omega_2\not\to[\omega_1+\omega]^2_{\aleph_1})
$$

and

$$
\mathrm{Con}(\mathrm{ZF})\Rightarrow\mathrm{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC}+\mathrm{G.C.H.}+\omega_2\not\to(\omega_1+2)^2_{\aleph_0}).
$$

**Question** (p. 272, quoted). "Is $\omega_2\to(\omega_1+\omega)^2_2$ or even
$\omega_2\to(\xi)^2_2$ for $\xi<\omega_2$ consistent with G.C.H.?"

The survey adds that, by the independence results, the best possible theorem
that can still be true is

$$
\mathrm{G.C.H.}\Rightarrow\omega_2\to(\xi,(\omega_1+n)_k)^2\quad\text{for }\xi<\omega_2,\ n,k<\omega .
$$

**Related remarks** (p. 273). The authors say that they proved
$\omega_2\to(\omega_1+n)^2_2$ for $n<\omega$ several years earlier; the page
treats it as a consequence of G.C.H., describing a theorem of Shelah as a
generalization of "G.C.H. $\Rightarrow\omega_2\to(\omega_1+n)^2_2$". They ask
whether G.C.H. implies $\omega_2\to(\omega_1+2)^2_3$, and whether under G.C.H.
for every $\alpha<\omega_2$ there is $\xi<\omega_3$ with $\xi\to(\alpha)^2_2$.
They note that G.C.H. implies, for example,
$\omega_2\cdot2\to(\omega_1+\omega)^2_2$. No proofs are given.

**Source.** P. Erdős and A. Hajnal, Unsolved and solved problems in set
theory, Proceedings of Symposia in Pure Mathematics 25 (1974), 269--287;
printed pp. 272--273 (PDF pp. 4--5 of the Rényi archive scan identified on the
[[set_theory/erdos_1974_unsolved_solved_problems_set_theory/_index|source card]]).

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the question, Hajnal's two consistency
statements and the remarks on p. 273 were read clause by clause on the page
images. A question has no proof to check, and the survey proves none of the
reported results.
