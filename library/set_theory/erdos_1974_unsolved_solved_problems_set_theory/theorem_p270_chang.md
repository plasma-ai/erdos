## Statement

All three results are reported on printed p. 270, under Problem 7 of the
authors' 1967 list, with no proof in this paper.

**Theorem (Chang)** (p. 270, quoted). "$\omega^\omega\to(\omega^\omega,3)^2$
[3]", the paper's reference [3] being Chang's 1972 paper. The survey says that
Problem 7 "has been solved by" this theorem.

**Milner.** The survey reports that E. C. Milner simplified Chang's proof and
proved

$$
\omega^\omega\to(\omega^\omega,k)^2\quad\text{for }k<\omega ,
$$

and that Jean Larson then gave a relatively simple proof of this general
result. No reference is given for either.

**Theorem (J. Larson)** (p. 270). As printed:

- (a) $(\omega^*+\omega)^\omega\to((\omega^*+\omega)^\omega,k)^2$ for
  $k<\omega$;
- (b) if $a\to(a)^{<\aleph_0}_2$ and $\kappa=\Omega(a)$, then
  $\kappa^\omega\to(\kappa^\omega,n)^2$ for $n<\omega$.

The survey uses $\Omega(a)$ without defining it; it denotes the initial
ordinal of the cardinal $a$. The survey adds that it is not known whether (b) holds
for $\kappa>\omega$ under the weaker hypothesis $a\to(a)^2_2$.

**Source.** P. Erdős and A. Hajnal, Unsolved and solved problems in set
theory, Proceedings of Symposia in Pure Mathematics 25 (1974), 269--287;
printed p. 270 (PDF p. 2 of the Rényi archive scan identified on the
[[set_theory/erdos_1974_unsolved_solved_problems_set_theory/_index|source card]]).

**Read depth.** Claims checked: the three reports were read clause by clause on
the page image. The survey gives no proof, so there is none to check.
