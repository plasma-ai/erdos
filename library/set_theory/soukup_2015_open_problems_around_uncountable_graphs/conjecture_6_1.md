**Source.** Conjecture 6.1 (p. 2) and the remarks after it (p. 3), Section
6 ("Simultaneous chromatic number"), of Daniel T. Soukup, *Open problems
around uncountable graphs*, workshop handout, Independence Results in
Mathematics and Challenges in Iterated Forcing, University of East Anglia,
November 2015, as identified on the
[[set_theory/soukup_2015_open_problems_around_uncountable_graphs/_index|source card]].

## Statement

**Conjecture 6.1** (p. 2; credited to Erdős and Hajnal, quoted). "Suppose
that $(V,E)$ is an $\omega_1$-chromatic graph. Then there is a colouring
$c:E\to\omega_1$ such that for every $V=\bigcup_{i\in\omega}V_i$ there is
$i<\omega$ such that $F$ [sic] assumes all values on $V_i$." The $F$ is a
misprint for $c$: some class $V_i$ spans edges of every color.

**What the handout records** (p. 3): "Consistently yes [8] e.g. adding a
single Cohen real suffices." Its reference [8] is A. Hajnal and P.
Komjáth, Some remarks on the simultaneous chromatic number, Combinatorica
23 (2003), 89--104. The handout asks whether special classes of graphs,
such as Hajnal–Máté graphs or comparability graphs of posets, satisfy the
conjecture, and poses a tree version as its own Conjecture 6.2 (p. 3),
which it says holds for Suslin trees and, under MA, for non-special trees
of size $2^\omega$ with no uncountable chains.

**Read depth.** Claims checked: Conjectures 6.1 and 6.2 and the remarks
were read clause by clause on pp. 2--3 of the handout.

## Scope

A conjecture the handout records as consistent; the handout proves
nothing.

## Bears on

- [[../wiki/problems/set_theory/E1176/_index|Problem 1176]]: Conjecture 6.1
  is the problem's statement: an edge coloring with $\aleph_1$ colors of a
  graph of chromatic number $\aleph_1$ such that every countable vertex
  coloring has a class containing edges of every color. The handout records
  that the statement holds consistently, adding a single Cohen real
  sufficing, by Hajnal and Komjáth's paper; it does not say whether ZFC
  proves it.
