## Statement

Let $U_n\subset\mathbb A^3_{\mathbb Q}$ be the affine cubic surface

$$
U_n:\ 4u_1u_2u_3=n(u_1u_2+u_1u_3+u_2u_3)
$$

(the paper's (1.2), p. 2), with $n\in\mathbb N$.

**Theorem 1.6** (p. 3). $\operatorname{Br}U_n/\operatorname{Br}\mathbb Q\cong\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z$,
generated by the quaternion algebra $(-u_1/u_3,-u_2/u_3)$.

The paper notes (p. 3) that $U_1\cong U_n$ over $\mathbb Q$ for every
$n\in\mathbb N$ by rescaling, that $U_n$ is singular only at the origin, and
that the generating algebra is transcendental.

**Source.** Martin Bright and Daniel Loughran, Brauer--Manin obstruction for
Erdős--Straus surfaces, Bull. Lond. Math. Soc. 52 (2020), no. 4, 746--761,
read in the arXiv version (arXiv:1908.02526v2) identified on the
[[unit_fractions/bright_2020_brauer_manin_obstruction_erdos_straus/_index|source card]]:
the statement on p. 3, the proof in Section 2 (pp. 5--9).

**Read depth.** Statement only: the statement was read on the page image;
the proof in Section 2 was not checked.

## Proof pointer

Section 2, pp. 5--9 (outlined on pp. 3--4). The Brauer group of a
desingularisation is computed first (Corollary 2.7), and
[[unit_fractions/bright_2020_brauer_manin_obstruction_erdos_straus/theorem_1_7|Theorem 1.7]] shows that every element of it comes from the
singular surface (Corollary 2.9).
