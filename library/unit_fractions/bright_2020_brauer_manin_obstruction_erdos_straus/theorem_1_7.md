## Statement

**Theorem 1.7** (p. 4). Let $U$ be a normal surface over a field $k$ of
characteristic $0$ with rational singularities, and let
$f\colon\widetilde U\to U$ be a desingularisation. Then the induced map
$\operatorname{Br}U\to\operatorname{Br}\widetilde U$ is surjective.

Here, as the paper recalls (p. 4), a normal variety has only rational
singularities if some desingularisation has all higher direct images of its
structure sheaf trivial.

**Source.** Martin Bright and Daniel Loughran, Brauer--Manin obstruction for
Erdős--Straus surfaces, Bull. Lond. Math. Soc. 52 (2020), no. 4, 746--761,
read in the arXiv version (arXiv:1908.02526v2) identified on the
[[unit_fractions/bright_2020_brauer_manin_obstruction_erdos_straus/_index|source card]]:
the statement on p. 4, the proof in Section 2 (pp. 8--9).

**Read depth.** Statement only: the statement was read on the page image;
the proof was not checked.

## Proof pointer

Section 2, pp. 8--9, by computing the higher direct images $R^qf_*\mathbb G_m$.
Remark 2.10 (p. 9) notes the map need not be an isomorphism.

## Use in the paper

A general input to [[unit_fractions/bright_2020_brauer_manin_obstruction_erdos_straus/theorem_1_6|Theorem 1.6]]: applied to $U_n$, whose
only singularity is at the origin, it shows the Brauer group of the
desingularisation comes from $U_n$ itself (Corollary 2.9).
