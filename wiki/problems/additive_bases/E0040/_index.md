# Problem 40

[[problems/additive_bases/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** For what functions $g(N)\to \infty$ is it true that

$$
\lvert A\cap \{1,\ldots,N\}\rvert \gg \frac{N^{1/2}}{g(N)}
$$

implies $\limsup 1_A\ast 1_A(n)=\infty$?

**Status.** Open.
