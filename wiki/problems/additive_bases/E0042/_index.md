# Problem 42

[[problems/additive_bases/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_bases/E0042/claims/_index|claims/]]: The 2 claim pages of Problem 42, one per claimant's result; the problem's standing derives from them.

***

**Statement.** Let $M\geq 1$ and $N$ be sufficiently large in terms of $M$. Is
it true that for every Sidon set $A\subset \{1,\ldots,N\}$ there is another
Sidon set $B\subset \{1,\ldots,N\}$ of size $M$ such that
$(A-A)\cap(B-B)=\{0\}$?

**Formulation.** Read as the site words it, the question fails for the empty
Sidon set: then $A-A$ is empty, so no $B$ gives $(A-A)\cap(B-B)=\{0\}$. The site
and the sources read it for non-empty $A$, where the condition says that $A$ and
$B$ share no nonzero difference. The site's remarks call the case $M=1$ trivial,
which holds only for non-empty $A$. Tao noted in the thread on 2025-12-05 that
the problem's original form took $A$ maximal. Formal-conjectures quantifies over
maximal Sidon sets. Sedov's Lean asks only that the positive differences of $B$
avoid those of $A$. The write-ups of Sothanaphan, Barreto and Chojecki state the
theorem for non-empty $A$. The claim pages answer the question so read.

**Status.** The site labels the problem “SOLVED (LEAN)”: the question, read
for non-empty $A$ as the Formulation records, is answered yes, by a proof for
every $M$ posted 2026-04-27 and formalized 2026-05-10, so the problem stands
proved; the acceptance and the Lean qualifications are on the claim pages
below.

## Current assessment

The site's formulation of 2026-10-07 asks whether, for every $M$ and all $N$
large in terms of $M$, every Sidon set $A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ has a Sidon
set $B\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ of size $M$ with $(A-A)\cap(B-B)=\{0\}$. Read as
the site words it, it fails for the empty set; read for non-empty $A$, as the
Formulation records, it is answered yes:
[[problems/additive_bases/E0042/claims/2026_04_27_sandhu|Sandhu's claim page]]
records the proof, generated by GPT 5.5 Pro and posted on 2026-04-27, which the
site's curator accepted; a Lean 4 formalization of 2026-05-10 is linked from
that page; the corpus has not built it. Earlier partial progress, the cases
$M\leq3$, is on
[[problems/additive_bases/E0042/claims/2026_01_19_sedov|Sedov's claim page]].

Tao's remarks of 2025-12-05 in the thread: $A$ may be taken maximal (the
problem's original form); the requirement that $B$ be Sidon can be removed,
since a large set contains a Sidon subset of about square-root size; and a
positive answer with $\lvert A\rvert=f(N)$ gives a negative answer to the
first question of [[problems/additive_bases/E0043/_index|Problem 43]]. A
separate write-up that Barreto generated with GPT, an Overleaf document linked
from his thread post of 2026-04-29, claims an effective threshold
$N_0(M)\leq\exp(\exp(CM^2\log 2M))$ and, by a later edit of that post, a $B$
of size $\gg(\log\log N/\log\log\log N)^{1/2}$; the site's remarks record
that bound only as what the method seems able to prove. Two further write-ups
of the theorem produced with GPT, Barreto's note of 2026-04-29 and Chojecki's
draft note of 2026-04-30, are disclosed on Sandhu's claim page. The curator
sketched an alternative Fourier route with better bounds on 2026-04-30, not
written up in the thread. No refereed publication of the proof is known. The corpus holds no compiled or reviewed proof.
