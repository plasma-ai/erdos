# Problem 66

[[problems/additive_bases/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Is there $A\subseteq \mathbb{N}$ such that

$$
\lim_{n\to \infty}\frac{1_A\ast 1_A(n)}{\log n}
$$

exists and is $\neq 0$?

**Status.** Open.

**Source.** [erdosproblems.com/66](https://www.erdosproblems.com/66), accessed
