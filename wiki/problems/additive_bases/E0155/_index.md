# Problem 155

[[problems/additive_bases/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Let $F(N)$ be the size of the largest Sidon subset of
$\{1,\ldots,N\}$. Is it true that for every $k\geq 1$ we have

$$
F(N+k)\leq F(N)+1
$$

for all sufficiently large $N$?
