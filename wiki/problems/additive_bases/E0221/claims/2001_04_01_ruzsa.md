***

**Claim.** There is a set $A\subset\mathbb{N}$ with

$$
\lvert A\cap\{1,\ldots,N\}\rvert\sim\frac{N}{\log_2 N}
$$

as $N\to\infty$ such that every sufficiently large integer is $2^k+a$ with
$k\ge 0$ and $a\in A$. Such an $A$ is an exact additive complement of the
powers of two: since the integers up to $N$ use at most $\log_2 N+1$ powers of
two, every complement has at least $(1+o(1))N/\log_2 N$ elements up to $N$, so
the count is best possible. This sharpens the construction of
[[problems/additive_bases/E0221/claims/1972_06_01_ruzsa|Ruzsa 1972]], which
gives $\ll N/\log N$ with an unspecified constant, and it settles Problem 221
with the optimal constant. The library holds no copy of the paper; the
statement follows the site's remark.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki; the result is the paper's.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: I. Z. Ruzsa, Additive completion of lacunary
sequences, Combinatorica 21 (2001), no. 2, 279–291, published 2001-04-01.
Reviewed: the site's curator, T. F. Bloom, records this result at
erdosproblems.com as giving the sharpest possible count for the problem,
which is the site's acceptance. No Lean
formalization of the asymptotic statement is recorded; the formalization
linked from the 1972 page covers the $\ll N/\log N$ bound.
