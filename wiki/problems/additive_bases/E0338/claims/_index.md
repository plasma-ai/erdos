# problems/additive_bases/E0338/claims

[[problems/additive_bases/E0338/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_bases/E0338/claims/1957_04_01_kelly|1957_04_01_kelly]]: Kelly (1957) proves that every basis of order 2 in the classical sense has a
restricted order at most 4, and that an asymptotic basis of order 2 with
counting function at least Cx/log log x has one at most 3; refereed.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0338/claims/2005_03_01_hennecart|2005_03_01_hennecart]]: Hennecart (2005) proves that every asymptotic basis of order 2 has a
restricted order at most 4, and that 4 is attained, settling the order-2
case of the existence and boundedness questions; refereed.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0338/claims/2026_07_28_white|2026_07_28_white]]: A July 2026 working report classifies when an eventually periodic set has a
restricted order, shows it equals the order when every finite removal keeps
it, and gives a robust order-3 basis of restricted order 6; unreviewed.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0338/claims/2026_10_01_veljjanoski|2026_10_01_veljjanoski]]: A write-up of October 2026 states that a set of positive lower density delta
which stays a basis after removing any finite set has restricted order at
most 3 times the ceiling of 8 over delta squared, minus 2; it is unreviewed.
