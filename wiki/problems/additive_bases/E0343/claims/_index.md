# problems/additive_bases/E0343/claims

[[problems/additive_bases/E0343/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_bases/E0343/claims/1966_01_01_folkman|1966_01_01_folkman]]: Theorem 1.3 of Folkman (1966): a nondecreasing sequence of positive integers
with nth term at most M n^alpha for some M and alpha below 1 is subcomplete,
the case of Problem 343 with counting function of order above N; refereed.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0343/claims/2005_07_26_szemeredi_vu|2005_07_26_szemeredi_vu]]: Theorem 6.3 of Szemerédi and Vu (2006): there is an absolute constant C such
that a nondecreasing sequence of positive integers with at least CN terms up
to N for all large N is subcomplete, the corrected Statement of Problem 343 in
full.
