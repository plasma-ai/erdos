***

**Claim.** Let $\gamma=\sqrt\varphi\approx1.27202$, $\varphi$ the golden
ratio. For every $u>0$ there is $L\in\mathbb Z$ such that every integer
$z\ge L$ is $\sum_{i\in t}\lfloor\gamma^iu\rfloor$ for some finite
$t\subset\mathbb N$. Hence the catalog statement `erdos_354.parts.ii` of
formal-conjectures,

```lean
∃ γ ∈ Set.Ioo (1 : ℝ) 2, ∀ᵉ (α > 0) (β > 0),
  Irrational (α / β) → IsAddCompleteNatSeq' (FloorMultiples.interleave α β γ)
```

holds with `answer(True)`: the $\alpha$-sequence alone represents every large
integer, its representation maps to the even positions of the interleaving,
and the hypothesis that $\alpha/\beta$ is irrational is discarded. The result
page
[[../library/additive_bases/kitamura_2026_lean_proof_erdos_problem_354_ii/erdos_354_part_ii_solved|erdos_354_part_ii_solved]]
and the source card
[[../library/additive_bases/kitamura_2026_lean_proof_erdos_problem_354_ii/_index|kitamura_2026_lean_proof_erdos_problem_354_ii]]
record the theorem and the repository.

**Covers.** The second question of
[[problems/additive_bases/E0354/_index|Problem 354]], "What if $2$ is replaced
by some $\gamma\in(1,2)$?", under the reading "for some $\gamma$", which is
the catalog's existential form; it says nothing under the reading "for every
$\gamma$", which [[problems/additive_bases/E0354/claims/2026_09_20_geneson|Geneson's claim page]]
answers no, and nothing about base $2$. The author's README says the result
does not solve the first question and is stronger than the catalog's part (ii)
because the second sequence and the irrational ratio are not needed at this
base.

**Argument.** The even indices give $c_n=\lfloor u\varphi^n\rfloor$ with
$c_{n+2}=c_n+c_{n+1}+\delta_n$, $\delta_n\in\{0,1\}$, and $\delta_n=1$
infinitely often, since otherwise the fractional parts would satisfy the exact
Fibonacci recurrence from some point on, whose bounded nonnegative solutions
vanish, making two consecutive $u\varphi^n$ integers and $\varphi$ rational;
$D$ disjoint carry-one triples represent an interval of $D+1$ consecutive
integers by choosing in each triple either $c_n+c_{n+1}$ or $c_{n+2}$; the odd
indices, $\lfloor(u\gamma)\varphi^n\rfloor$, are eventually positive with each
term at most twice its predecessor, and adding them one at a time extends the
represented interval without gaps past every threshold.

**Claimant and postings.** Kenta Kitamura (GitHub `KitaKen1`), who gives
that name in the erdosproblems.com thread comment of 5 September 2026 announcing
the proof, published the repository the same day (the pinned revision) and
opened formal-conjectures PR #5286 to mark part (ii) solved. The README
states that the formalization was developed with assistance from ChatGPT and
OpenAI Codex, using GPT-6 (Astra), the author's own disclosure. The README
reports kernel checks under Lean 4.33.1 against a pinned formal-conjectures
commit and under Lean 4.34.0-rc2 with Mathlib alone, no `sorry`, `admit`,
custom axiom, `native_decide` or `unsafe`, and `#print axioms` giving
`propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`; this corpus has built none of
it.

a catalog contributor (GitHub `Deicyde`) on 2026-09-18 and was still open and

question is unfixed by the site and by both of Graham's and Erdős–Graham's

already follow from Hegyvári's 1989 paper, which nobody in the thread
verified.
