**Claim.** Let $\gamma$ be the Salem number with minimal polynomial
$x^{18}-x^{12}-x^{11}-x^{10}-x^9-x^8-x^7-x^6+1$, for which
$6/5<\gamma<13/10$. There are $\alpha,\beta>0$ with
$\beta/\alpha\ne r\gamma^k$ for all $r\in\mathbb Q$ and $k\in\mathbb Z$, so
that $\alpha/\beta$ is irrational and neither sequence is a tail of the other,
such that every term of $(\lfloor\alpha\gamma^n\rfloor)_{n\ge0}$ and
$(\lfloor\beta\gamma^n\rfloor)_{n\ge0}$ is even; hence the interleaved
sequence, repeated occurrences retained, is not complete. This is Corollary
12 (p. 12) of Jesse Geneson, *Deletion thresholds and exponential examples
for complete sequences*, arXiv:2609.25107v1, 20 September 2026, 14 pages; the
result page
[[../library/additive_bases/geneson_2026_deletion_thresholds_exponential_examples_complete_sequences/corollary_12|Corollary 12]]
states it and the source card
[[../library/additive_bases/geneson_2026_deletion_thresholds_exponential_examples_complete_sequences/_index|geneson_2026_deletion_thresholds_exponential_examples_complete_sequences]]
records the preprint.

**Covers.** The second question of
[[problems/additive_bases/E0354/_index|Problem 354]], "What if $2$ is replaced
by some $\gamma\in(1,2)$?", under the reading "for every $\gamma\in(1,2)$",
answered no at one base. The paper says that this answers the variable-base
extension of the two-sequence question negatively and does not resolve the
base-$2$ question; it says nothing under the reading "for some $\gamma$",
which [[problems/additive_bases/E0354/claims/2026_09_05_kitamura|Kitamura's claim page]]
addresses. The construction is existential and gives no explicit $\alpha$ or
$\beta$.

**Argument.** Theorem 9 (p. 11) gives arbitrarily large $t>0$ with
$\lfloor t\gamma^n\rfloor$ even for every $n\ge0$ at this base, through
Dubickas's theorem on the fractional parts of $t\gamma^n$ (the paper's Lemma
10) with a sign adjustment (Proposition 11); that theorem is also the paper's
counterexample to Graham's conjecture for bases below the golden ratio
(Problem 349). Corollary 12 chooses two such coefficients whose ratio is not a
rational multiple of a power of $\gamma$. The library card records the
two-page proof, which has not been independently reviewed.

**Claimant and posting.** Jesse Geneson, the preprint's sole author; its only
version on 2026-09-28 was v1 of 20 September 2026, with no journal reference
or DOI. The paper's closing declaration states that the proofs were found with
the assistance of Codex with GPT-6 Astra Ultra, that the author directed
separate writing and auditing teams, read and edited the proofs and requested
revisions, and that the author takes responsibility for the content; the
source's own disclosure.

**Standing.** Claimed: a preprint, not refereed, with no review, no site
mention and no catalog record found. The reading of the
second question is unfixed by the site and by the original wordings, so this
claim settles the question only under one of its two readings and leaves the
first question, base $2$, untouched.
