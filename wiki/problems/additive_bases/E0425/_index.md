# Problem 425

[[problems/additive_bases/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Let $F(n)$ be the maximum possible size of a subset
$A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ such that the products $ab$ are distinct for all
$a<b$. Is there a constant $c$ such that

$$
F(n)=\pi(n)+(c+o(1))n^{3/4}(\log n)^{-3/2}?
$$

If $A\subseteq \{1,\ldots,n\}$ is such that all products $a_1\cdots a_r$ are
distinct for $a_1<\cdots <a_r$ then is it true that

$$
\lvert A\rvert \leq \pi(n)+O(n^{\frac{r+1}{2r}})?
$$
