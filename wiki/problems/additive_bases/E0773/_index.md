# Problem 773

[[problems/additive_bases/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** What is the size of the largest Sidon subset
$A\subseteq\{1,2^2,\ldots,N^2\}$? Is it $N^{1-o(1)}$?

**Status.** Open.

**Source.** [erdosproblems.com/773](https://www.erdosproblems.com/773), accessed
2026-09-04. Cite as: T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problem #773,
https://www.erdosproblems.com/773.
