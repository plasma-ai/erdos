***

Write $S_r(N)$ for the largest size of a set $A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ in
which every integer has at most $r$ representations $a+a'$ with $a\le a'$,
and $D_r(N)$ for the largest size of a set $B\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ in
which every nonzero difference has at most $r$ ordered representations
$b-b'$. Ho proves (Theorem 1 of the write-up) that for every fixed
$r\geq 2$, with $s=\lfloor r/2\rfloor$,

$$
\limsup_{N\to\infty}\frac{D_r(N)}{\sqrt N}
\leq \sqrt r
< \frac{r+s}{\sqrt{r+2s}}
\leq \liminf_{N\to\infty}\frac{S_r(N)}{\sqrt N}.
$$

If the constants $c_r$ and $c_r'$ of the statement exist, then
$c_r'\leq\sqrt r<c_r$, so both questions are answered yes: $c_r\neq c_r'$
and $c_r'<c_r$ for every $r\geq 2$. The separation holds without assuming
that either limit exists.

The upper bound adapts the Erdős–Turán argument for Sidon sets: counting
pairs of elements of $B$ inside windows of length $H$ by Cauchy–Schwarz and
by the difference bound gives $|B|^2\leq rN+o(N)$ for
$H=\lfloor N^{2/3}\rfloor$. The lower bound is the finite $B_2[g]$
construction of Cilleruelo, Ruzsa and Trujillo (Theorem 2.1 of
[[../library/additive_bases/cilleruelo_2002_upper_lower_bounds_finite_b_h/_index|cilleruelo_2002_upper_lower_bounds_finite_b_h]],
J. Number Theory 97 (2002), 26–34), re-proved in the write-up: a Singer
cyclic Sidon set of size $q+1$ modulo $q^2+q+1$ is lifted along the pattern
$\{0,\ldots,r-1\}\cup\{r+s,\ldots,r+2s-1\}$, which has at most $r$ ordered
representations of every sum, and the prime number theorem supplies a prime
$q$ of the right size. For $r=2$ the bounds read $c_2'\leq\sqrt2$ and
$c_2\geq 3/2$.
