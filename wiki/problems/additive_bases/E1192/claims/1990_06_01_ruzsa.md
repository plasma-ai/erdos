***

**Claim.** Theorem 2 of Imre Z. Ruzsa, *A just basis*, Monatsh. Math. 109
(1990), no. 2, 145–151, gives a set $A$ of nonnegative integers such that
every nonnegative integer is a sum $a+a'$ with $a,a'\in A$ and
$\sum_{n\le N}\sigma(n)^2=O(N)$, where $\sigma(n)$ counts these
representations. The function $f_2(n)$ of
[[problems/additive_bases/E1192/_index|Problem 1192]] counts ordered pairs, so
$1\le f_2(n)\le2\sigma(n)$ whether $\sigma$ counts ordered or unordered
pairs, and $\sum_{n\le x}f_2(n)^2\le4\sum_{n\le x}\sigma(n)^2\ll x$. If $A$
contains $0$, the set $A+1$ lies in the positive integers and has the same
counts shifted by $2$, so it serves equally. Hence the answer is yes for
$r=2$. The source card is
[[../library/additive_bases/ruzsa_1990_just_basis/_index|ruzsa_1990_just_basis]].

**Covers.** The case $r=2$. The cases $r\ge3$ are open.

of that month. The site's remarks credit the result, but the site labels the
problem OPEN, so the remarks are not acceptance.
