***

**Claim.** The optimal constant $c$ of
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/_index|Problem 36]], the minimum
overlap constant, satisfies $c\ge0.38055470$. The claim was submitted to the
site's proof-claims tab on 2026-07-20 by Liam Price as a partial proof
credited to GPT Pro; its summary says only that the system improves the
lower bound to that value. The posting links a write-up hosted on Overleaf
and, in its notes, a repository described as a reproducible certificate
packet, both linked above, the repository at its commit of 2026-07-01, the
last before the posting. The value lies above the refereed lower bound
$0.379005$ of White ([[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims/2022_01_14_white|claim page]]) and the reported
$0.37912$ of Kim and Pilanci ([[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims/2026_06_30_kim_pilanci|claim page]]), and below the
upper bounds $0.380876$ of TTT-Discover, the site's record
([[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims/2026_01_22_yuksekgonul_et_al|claim page]]), and $0.38085906$ certified by Russell
([[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims/2026_07_12_russell|claim page]]); if correct it would narrow the gap between
the two sides to about $0.0003$. The write-up and the certificate packet are
linked above; this page does not describe the method.

**Submission note.** Posted to erdosproblems.com as a proof claim by Liam Price
(account Leeham) on 20 July 2026, giving "GPT Pro" as the AI used:

> GPT Pro improves the lower bound to 0.38055470. Notes: The reproducible
> certificate packet can be found here.

**Covers.** The lower bound alone: $c\ge0.38055470$. The claim does not
determine $c$ and says nothing about the upper bound.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki.

**Standing.** Claimed. The site's label is OPEN and its commentary, last edited
23 January 2026, gives White's $0.379005$ as the record lower bound and does
not mention this claim; the claim had no comments on its thread as of
2026-10-06. No outside review, refereed publication or formalization is known.
A later and slightly larger claimed bound by another submitter has
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims/2026_09_19_drynshock|its own claim page]].
