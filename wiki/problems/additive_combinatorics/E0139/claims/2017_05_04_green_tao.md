***

**Claim.** Theorem 1.1 of Green and Tao, *New bounds for Szemerédi's theorem,
III: a polylogarithmic bound for $r_4(N)$*, states that there is an absolute
constant $c>0$ with

$$
r_4(N)\ll N(\log N)^{-c},
$$

where $r_4(N)$ is the largest size of a subset of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ with no
non-trivial four-term arithmetic progression. Since $(\log N)^{-c}\to0$, the
bound gives $r_4(N)=o(N)$, the instance $k=4$ of
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0139/_index|Problem 139]], together with a
rate the problem does not ask for. The paper improves Gowers's bound
$N(\log\log N)^{-c}$ and the authors' earlier $N\exp(-c\sqrt{\log\log N})$,
and brings the four-term bound to the quality of the Heath-Brown–Szemerédi
bound for $r_3(N)$; its method replaces Roth's density increment by an energy
decrement over Bohr sets with a local inverse theorem for the $U^3$ norm. The
library card is
[[../library/additive_combinatorics/green_2017_new_bounds_szemeredi_s_theorem/_index|Green and Tao 2017]].

**Covers.** The instance $k=4$ of the statement, $r_4(N)=o(N)$, which
Szemerédi's accepted full claim already settles; the page records the bound's
rate, $N(\log N)^{-c}$, which no claim of this problem requires. Nothing about
any other $k$.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki; the theorem is the paper's own.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: B. Green and T. Tao, New bounds for Szemerédi's
theorem, III: a polylogarithmic bound for $r_4(N)$, Mathematika 63 (2017),
no. 3, 944–1040, the DOI linked above; the page name carries the date of the
first arXiv version, 2017-05-04. Not reviewed: the site's curator labels the
problem proved on Szemerédi's theorem and cites this paper in the commentary
only as the best known bound for $k=4$, which credits the bound and not a
settlement of the problem, so no `reviewed` evidence is listed. The proof is
not checked here.
