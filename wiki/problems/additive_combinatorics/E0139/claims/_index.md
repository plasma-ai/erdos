# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0139/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0139/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0139/claims/1975_01_01_szemeredi|1975_01_01_szemeredi]]: Szemerédi's 1975 theorem in Acta Arithmetica that r_k(N) = o(N) for every
k, accepted on the refereed publication and the site's credit, with the
unaudited Lean formalization of it in Boris Alexeev's repository linked.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0139/claims/2017_05_04_green_tao|2017_05_04_green_tao]]: Theorem 1.1 of Green and Tao (Mathematika 2017) bounds a subset of the first
N integers with no four-term progression by N over a power of log N, which
proves the k = 4 instance of Problem 139 with a rate; accepted as refereed.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0139/claims/2023_02_10_kelley_meka|2023_02_10_kelley_meka]]: Theorem 1.1 of Kelley and Meka (FOCS 2023) bounds a subset of the first N
integers with no three-term progression by N times 2 to the minus a power of
log N, the k = 3 instance of Problem 139 with a rate; accepted as reviewed.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0139/claims/2023_09_05_bloom_sisask|2023_09_05_bloom_sisask]]: Theorem 1 of Bloom and Sisask's 2023 note bounds a subset of the first N
integers with no three-term progression by exp(-c (log N)^(1/9)) N, the k = 3
instance of Problem 139 with a sharper rate; a preprint, so claimed.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0139/claims/2024_02_28_leng_sah_sawhney|2024_02_28_leng_sah_sawhney]]: Theorem 1.1 of Leng, Sah and Sawhney (2024) bounds a subset of the first N
integers with no k-term progression by N exp(-(log log N)^(c_k)) for every
k at least 5, those instances of Problem 139 with a rate; a preprint, claimed.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0139/claims/2026_09_23_openai|2026_09_23_openai]]: The OpenAI release's bound r_k(N) <= C_k N exp(-c_k (log N)^(eps_k)) for
each fixed k at least 3, which gives r_k(N) = o(N) at once; accepted as a
second route on a built Lean declaration of a weaker saving that suffices.
