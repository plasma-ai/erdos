[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0140/claims/2023_02_10_kelley_meka|2023_02_10_kelley_meka]]: Theorem 1.1 of Kelley and Meka (FOCS 2023) bounds a progression-free subset
of the first N integers by N over any fixed power of log N; accepted on the
site's credit and Bloom and Sisask's refereed exposition.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0140/claims/2026_09_23_openai|2026_09_23_openai]]: OpenAI's September 2026 Theorem 1.1 bounds r_k(N) by C_k N exp(-c_k (log
N)^(eps_k)) for k >= 3, at k = 3 below N over any power of log N; its Lean
proves a weaker bound a library lemma turns into the problem's statement.
