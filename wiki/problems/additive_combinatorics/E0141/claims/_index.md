[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0141/claims/1958_01_01_schinzel_sierpinski|1958_01_01_schinzel_sierpinski]]: Schinzel and Sierpiński's 1958 paper derives from Hypothesis H that for
every k there are infinitely many progressions of k consecutive primes; the
hypothesis is unproven, so the page derives nothing for the standing.

consecutive primes in arithmetic progression, which answers the problem yes
