# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0169/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0169/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0169/claims/1968_08_01_berlekamp|1968_08_01_berlekamp]]: Berlekamp's Galois-field two-coloring shows W(p+1) exceeds p times 2 to the
p for prime p, which with the trivial comparison to log W(k) gives f(k) at
least (log 2 over 2 minus o(1)) times k, the bound the site credits to him.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0169/claims/1977_02_01_gerver|1977_02_01_gerver]]: Gerver proved that the largest reciprocal sum of a set with no k-term
arithmetic progression is at least (1-o(1)) k log k, and that the finiteness
of every f(k) is equivalent to Erdős's reciprocal-sum conjecture.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0169/claims/1984_07_01_wroblewski|1984_07_01_wroblewski]]: Wróblewski constructed an explicit set of integers with no three-term
arithmetic progression and reciprocal sum above 3.00849, the record for f(3)
that the site credits.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0169/claims/2022_03_11_walker|2022_03_11_walker]]: Walker found digit-restricted Kempner sets with no four-term, respectively
ten-term, arithmetic progression and reciprocal sums 4.43975 and 14.056,
and showed Kempner sets suffice for f(k); an arXiv preprint, claimed.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0169/claims/2026_09_23_openai|2026_09_23_openai]]: Corollary 11.2 of the OpenAI release manuscript of 23 September 2026 claims
that every set with no k-term progression has reciprocal sum at most H_k =
Σ 2^{-m} r_k(2^m) < ∞, so f(k) is finite for each k ≥ 3; no numerical bound.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0169/claims/2026_09_26_kiichi|2026_09_26_kiichi]]: Two explicit finite sets, one free of three-term and one of four-term
progressions, with reciprocal sums beyond the records of Wróblewski (1984)
and Walker (2025); each stated as the claimants' own Lean theorem, claimed.
