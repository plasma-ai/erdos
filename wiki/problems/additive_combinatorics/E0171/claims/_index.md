# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0171/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0171/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0171/claims/1991_12_01_furstenberg_katznelson|1991_12_01_furstenberg_katznelson]]: Furstenberg and Katznelson's 1991 theorem that every dense enough subset
of a large cube of words over t letters contains a combinatorial line, the
problem's question; refereed and adopted by the site as the proof.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0171/claims/2009_10_20_polymath|2009_10_20_polymath]]: The Polymath project's 2009 combinatorial proof of the density Hales-Jewett
theorem, the first elementary one and the first with explicit bounds; in
the Annals of Mathematics in 2012, a second accepted proof of the theorem.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0171/claims/2012_09_22_dodos_kanellopoulos_tyros|2012_09_22_dodos_kanellopoulos_tyros]]: Dodos, Kanellopoulos and Tyros's 2012 combinatorial proof of the density
Hales-Jewett theorem, modeled on Polymath's but with the uniform measure
only; refereed, and the proof that Alexeev's lean-proofs file formalizes.
