# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0185/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0185/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0185/claims/1991_12_01_furstenberg_katznelson|1991_12_01_furstenberg_katznelson]]: Furstenberg and Katznelson's 1991 density Hales-Jewett theorem, whose
three-letter case gives that a subset of the ternary n-cube with no three
collinear points has size o(3^n); refereed, credited by the site.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0185/claims/2012_09_22_dodos_kanellopoulos_tyros|2012_09_22_dodos_kanellopoulos_tyros]]: Dodos, Kanellopoulos and Tyros's 2012 combinatorial proof of the density
Hales-Jewett theorem, whose ternary case gives f_3(n)=o(3^n) since a
combinatorial line is collinear; refereed; a Lean formalization exists,
not built here.
