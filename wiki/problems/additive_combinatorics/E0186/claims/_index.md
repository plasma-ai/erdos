# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0186/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0186/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0186/claims/1989_03_01_bosznay|1989_03_01_bosznay]]: Bosznay's 1989 theorem that the largest non-averaging subset of the first
N integers exceeds c N^{1/4} for large N, by the set i q^3 + i(i+1)/2 for
i = 1, ..., q-1; the lower half of the answer, refereed and site-credited.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0186/claims/2024_10_18_pham_zakharov|2024_10_18_pham_zakharov]]: Pham and Zakharov's theorem that every non-averaging subset of the first
N integers has at most N^{1/4+o(1)} elements, which with Bosznay's bound
gives F(N)=N^{1/4+o(1)}; in Geom. Funct. Anal. 2025, adopted by the site.
