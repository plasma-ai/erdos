# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0271/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0271/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0271/claims/1978_01_01_odlyzko_stanley|1978_01_01_odlyzko_stanley]]: The 1978 Bell Laboratories memorandum of Odlyzko and Stanley describing the
members of A(3^m) and A(2·3^m) by their ternary digits, with growth of order
k^{log_2 3}; stated without proof and unrefereed.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0271/claims/2014_08_08_rolnick|2014_08_08_rolnick]]: Rolnick's 2017 European J. Combin. paper proving that A(3^k) and A(2·3^k),
k at least 1, are independent Stanley sequences with the ternary descriptions
of Odlyzko and Stanley and growth of order n^{log_2 3}.
