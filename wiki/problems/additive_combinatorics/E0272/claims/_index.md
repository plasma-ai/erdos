# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0272/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0272/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0272/claims/1981_12_01_simonovits_sos|1981_12_01_simonovits_sos]]: The 1981 European J. Combin. paper of Simonovits and Sós bounding the
extremal family by (pi^2/24 + 1/2 + o(1)) N^2 and refuting the Erdős–Graham
guess that the progressions through a fixed element are extremal.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0272/claims/1999_07_01_szabo|1999_07_01_szabo]]: Szabó's 1999 European J. Combin. theorem that the extremal family has
N^2/2 + O(N^{5/3} (log N)^3) members, with a construction of binom(N,2) +
floor((N-1)/4) + 1 sets refuting the Simonovits–Sós conjecture.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0272/claims/2026_07_25_yang|2026_07_25_yang]]: Zhanfu Yang's 2026 preprint reporting t(N) for 3 <= N <= 12 by computation
and proving Szabó's lower bound exact for every family with a common
element; unrefereed, its computations not rerun by this corpus.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0272/claims/2026_09_09_jenw1n|2026_09_09_jenw1n]]: A Lean proof accepted by the bounty site Conjectures.io in September 2026
that the largest family with pairwise nonempty progression intersections
has N^2/2 + O(N) members, Szabó's linear-error question, not the exact value.
