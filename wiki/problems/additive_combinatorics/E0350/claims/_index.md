# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0350/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0350/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0350/claims/1974_04_01_benkoski_erdos|1974_04_01_benkoski_erdos]]: Theorem 1 of Benkoski and Erdős (Math. Comp. 1974), by an argument credited
to C. Ryavec, proves that a set of positive integers with distinct subset
sums has reciprocal sum below two, with the refinement 2 - 2^(1-n); accepted.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0350/claims/1977_09_01_hanson_steele_stenger|1977_09_01_hanson_steele_stenger]]: A two-page note (Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 1977) proving that a set with distinct
subset sums has sum of n^(-s) below 1/(1 - 2^(-s)) for every s > 0; s = 1 is
the problem's bound. Accepted on the publication and the site's credit.
