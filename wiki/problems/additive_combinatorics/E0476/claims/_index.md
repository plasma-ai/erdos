# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0476/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0476/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0476/claims/1994_03_01_dias_da_silva_hamidoune|1994_03_01_dias_da_silva_hamidoune]]: Theorem 4.1 of Dias da Silva and Hamidoune (Bull. London Math. Soc. 1994)
bounds the sums of m distinct elements of A in characteristic p below by
min(p, m|A| - m^2 + 1); m = 2 is the problem's bound. Accepted, refereed.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0476/claims/1995_03_01_alon_nathanson_ruzsa|1995_03_01_alon_nathanson_ruzsa]]: Theorem 2 of Alon, Nathanson and Ruzsa (Amer. Math. Monthly 1995) derives the
restricted-sumset bound min(p, 2k - 3) by the polynomial method; their 1996
paper reproves the general m-fold theorem. A second accepted proof, refereed.
