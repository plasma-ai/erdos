# Problem 749

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Let $\epsilon>0$. Does there exist $A\subseteq \mathbb{N}$ such
that the lower density of $A+A$ is at least $1-\epsilon$ and yet $1_A\ast 1_A(n)
\ll_\epsilon 1$ for all $n$?

**Status.** Open.
