***

**Claim.** The Theorem of I. Z. Ruzsa, *Sum-avoiding subsets*, Ramanujan
J. 9 (2005), no. 1--2, 77--82, display (1.1), printed p. 77:
"$\frac2{\log3}\log n-1<l(n)\ll e^{c\sqrt{\log n}}$ with arbitrary
$c>\sqrt{8\log2}$", where $\lambda(A)$ is the largest size of a subset
$S\subset A$ with $s+s'\notin A$ for distinct $s,s'\in S$ and
$l(n)=\min\{\lambda(A):A\subset\mathbb N,\ |A|=n\}$. A set of positive
integers is a set of reals, so in the notation of
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/_index|Problem 787]]

$$
g(n)\le l(n)\ll e^{c\sqrt{\log n}}\qquad(c>\sqrt{8\log2}),
$$

with no reduction step. The upper estimate (§ 2, pp. 78--79) comes from a
union of dilated lattice balls $U_r=\bigcup_{i<r}2^i(B_{r-i}+y)$ in
$\mathbb Z^d$, in which more than $2^d$ points of one layer contain two
whose sum lies in the next layer, projected to the positive integers in
base $m$; Sanders describes the construction as Behrend's adapted. The
lower half of the Theorem, $l(n)>\frac2{\log3}\log n-1$, is proved
(pp. 79--81) by a greedy selection and improves the constant of Klarner's
and Choi's logarithmic lower bounds. It bounds $l(n)$, over sets of positive
integers, and reaches $g(n)$ only through Choi's reduction of the problem
for real numbers to sets of integers (see
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/claims/1971_12_01_choi|Choi's page]]);
it is superseded by
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/claims/2018_04_10_sanders|Sanders's]]
$(\log n)^{1+c}$. Cited as [Ru05] on the problem page. Library home
[[../library/additive_combinatorics/ruzsa_2005_sum_avoiding_subsets/_index|ruzsa_2005_sum_avoiding_subsets]];
result page
[[../library/additive_combinatorics/ruzsa_2005_sum_avoiding_subsets/theorem|Theorem]].

**Covers.** The upper bound $g(n)\ll e^{c\sqrt{\log n}}$ for every
$c>\sqrt{8\log2}$, the site's $\exp(O(\sqrt{\log n}))$. Not covered: the
order of growth of $g(n)$.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki; the construction and the greedy
argument are self-contained.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: the paper is the publisher's version of record in
The Ramanujan Journal (received August 27, 2002, accepted December 23,
2002; Crossref record: issue of March 2005, with no day, so this page is
named by the first of the month). The site's curator, Thomas F. Bloom,
credits the upper bound to Ruzsa in the problem page's commentary (label
OPEN, page last edited 23 January 2026); the problem is not marked settled
there, so the credit is recorded here and is not listed as `reviewed`. The
Theorem is checked against printed p. 77, the proof of the upper estimate
was followed in full, and the proof of the lower estimate was read for its
structure; none of this is an independent review.
