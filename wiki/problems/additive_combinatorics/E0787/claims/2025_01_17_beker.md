***

**Claim.** Theorem 1.2 of A. Beker, *The Erdős--Moser sum-free set problem
via improved bounds for $k$-configurations*, arXiv:2501.10203, p. 3: "Let
$c\in(0,\frac1{68})$ be arbitrary. Then for any sufficiently large finite
set $A\subseteq\mathbb Z$, there exists a subset $B\subseteq A$ of size at
least $(\log|A|)^{1+c}$ such that $b_1+b_2\notin A$ for any distinct
$b_1,b_2\in B$." In the notation of
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/_index|Problem 787]],

$$
g(n)\ge(\log n)^{1+c}\qquad\text{for every }c<\tfrac1{68}\text{ and all large }n,
$$

which the paper states as $\phi(n)=\Omega((\log n)^{1+c})$ with $c=1/69$.
It is deduced from Theorem 1.1 (for $\alpha\in(0,1]$ and $k\ge2$, a set of
density $\alpha$ in $[N]$ with $N\ge\exp(Ck^{68}\log(2/\alpha)^{16})$, $C$
an absolute constant, contains a non-degenerate $k$-configuration), with
Sanders's Proposition 2.7 in explicit form. The paper claims no improvement
in the value of $c$ over Sanders's theorem and notes that the
$k$-configuration route is limited to $c<1$; its gain is an explicit
exponent, which the site renders as $(\log n)^{1+1/68+o(1)}$. Cited as
[Be25] on the problem page. Library home
[[../library/additive_combinatorics/beker_2025_erdos_moser_sum_free_set_problem/_index|beker_2025_erdos_moser_sum_free_set_problem]];
result page
[[../library/additive_combinatorics/beker_2025_erdos_moser_sum_free_set_problem/theorem_1_2|Theorem 1.2]].

**Covers.** The lower bound $g(n)\ge(\log n)^{1+c}$ for every $c<1/68$, an
explicit form of
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/claims/2018_04_10_sanders|Sanders's]]
bound. Not covered: the order of growth of $g(n)$.

**Depends on.**
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/claims/2018_04_10_sanders|Sanders's theorem]],
whose deduction of the bound from Proposition 2.7 the paper follows, and
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/claims/1971_12_01_choi|Choi's 1971 paper]]
for the reduction from real sets to integer sets (Beker's footnote 1).

**Standing.** Claimed. The arXiv v2 of 2 October 2026 (24 pages) is the
final version, which its arXiv comment says incorporates the referee's
comments, corrects an error in the proof of Lemma 2.2 of v1, and is to
appear in International Mathematics Research Notices; Theorems 1.1 and 1.2
are unchanged. No journal record existed on 2026-10-07, so `refereed` is
not listed until the publication appears. The site's curator, Thomas F.

OPEN, page last edited 23 January 2026); the problem is not marked settled
