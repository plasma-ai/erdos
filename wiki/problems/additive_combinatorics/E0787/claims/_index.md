# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/claims/1971_12_01_choi|1971_12_01_choi]]: Choi's 1971 bound g(n) << n^{2/5+o(1)} (Proc. London Math. Soc.), the first
power-saving upper bound for the Erdős–Moser function, with the reduction to
integer sets; refereed, known here through the papers that cite it.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/claims/2005_03_01_ruzsa|2005_03_01_ruzsa]]: The Theorem of Ruzsa (Ramanujan J. 2005): some n positive integers have no
sum-avoiding subset larger than a constant times exp(c sqrt(log n)), for
every c > sqrt(8 log 2); the best upper bound for g(n); refereed.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/claims/2018_04_10_sanders|2018_04_10_sanders]]: Theorem 1.2 of Sanders (Canad. J. Math. 2021): every finite set A of
integers has a subset of size at least (log |A|)^{1+c}, c > 0 absolute,
with no sum of two distinct elements in A; refereed.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/claims/2025_01_17_beker|2025_01_17_beker]]: Theorem 1.2 of Beker's preprint (accepted by Int. Math. Res. Not.): for every
c < 1/68, every large finite set A of integers has a subset of size at least
(log |A|)^{1+c} with no sum of two distinct elements in A; no journal
record on 2026-10-07.
