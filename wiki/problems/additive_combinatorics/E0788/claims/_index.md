[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0788/claims/1971_12_01_choi|1971_12_01_choi]]: Choi's 1971 bound f(n) << n^{3/4} (Proc. London Math. Soc.) for his interval

n^{1/2+o(1)}; refereed, known here through the papers that cite it.

with the greedy lower bound f(n) >= sqrt(n) of p. 172; refereed.

conjectured exponent one half, with a Lean development; unreviewed.
