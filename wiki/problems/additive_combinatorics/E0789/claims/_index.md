# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0789/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0789/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0789/claims/1962_01_01_erdos|1962_01_01_erdos]]: Theorem IV of Erdős (Mat. Lapok 1962): an admissible sequence has A(x) less
than C x^{5/6}, which gives h(n) < C n^{5/6}, the first upper bound for
Problem 789; refereed; the paper holds no (n log n)^{1/3} lower bound.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0789/claims/1966_01_01_straus|1966_01_01_straus]]: Straus's 1966 theorem (J. Math. Sci.) that an admissible subset of {1,...,N}
has at most a constant times N^{1/2} elements, which gives h(n) << n^{1/2};
refereed, known here through the 1991 reproof and the 1999 account.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0789/claims/1974_04_01_choi|1974_04_01_choi]]: Choi's estimate (1) (J. Number Theory 1974): any n nonzero integers contain
an admissible subset of size >> (n log n)^{1/3}, the best refereed lower
bound for h(n), refining Erdős's n^{1/3}; refereed.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0789/claims/2026_09_12_korsky|2026_09_12_korsky]]: AI-assisted full claim that every set of n integers has an admissible subset
of size at least c (n log log n / log n)^{1/2}, fixing h(n) up to logarithms
with Straus's bound; a file-sharing write-up with a third-party Lean proof.
