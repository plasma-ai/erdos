***

**Claim.** There are finite additive $2$-bases of $k$ positive elements
whose range, the largest $n$ with $\{0,\ldots,n\}\subseteq A+A$, is at least
$\frac87(\frac k2)^2+O(k)$ (equation (3), printed p. 118, derived from the
basis $B_2$ of p. 121 with the parameters of p. 123). In the notation of
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0791/_index|Problem 791]], with $n(k)$
the largest range of a $2$-basis of $k$ elements and
$g(n)=\min\{k:n(k)\ge n\}$, the construction gives
$n(k)\ge(\frac27-\varepsilon)k^2$ for all large $k$ (counting the zero
element changes $k$ by one), so for large $n$ the integer
$k=\lceil\sqrt{n/(2/7-\varepsilon)}\rceil$ has $n(k)\ge n$, hence
$g(n)\le k$ and $g(n)^2\le(\frac72+o(1))n$, so

$$
\limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{g(n)}{\sqrt n}\le\sqrt{\tfrac72}<2
$$

and the question whether $g(n)\sim2n^{1/2}$ has the answer no. Rohrbach's
conjecture $n_2(k)=k^2/4+O(k)$, of which the site's question is the
asymptotic form, is refuted with it. A. Mrose, *Untere Schranken für die
Reichweiten von Extremalbasen fester Ordnung*, Abh. Math. Sem. Univ. Hamburg
48 (1979), no. 1, 118--124, cited as [Mr79] on the problem page. Library
home
[[../library/additive_combinatorics/mrose_1979_untere_schranken_reichweiten_extremalbasen_fester_ordnung/_index|mrose_1979_untere_schranken_reichweiten_extremalbasen_fester_ordnung]];
result page
[[../library/additive_combinatorics/mrose_1979_untere_schranken_reichweiten_extremalbasen_fester_ordnung/equation_3|Equation (3)]].
Mrose's $k$ counts the positive elements, so his $\frac87(\frac k2)^2$ is
the $\liminf n(k)/k^2\ge2/7$ that Kohonen quotes for him. Hämmerer and
Hofmeister refuted the guess independently and earlier in print, with the
constant $5/18$
([[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0791/claims/1976_11_01_hammerer_hofmeister|their claim page]]);
Mrose's paper does not cite them. Kohonen's basis raises the constant to
$85/294$
([[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0791/claims/2016_06_15_kohonen|his claim page]]),
the best upper bound for the estimate.

**Covers.** The "in particular" question only: $g(n)\sim2n^{1/2}$ is false.
Not covered: the estimate of $g(n)$, which the problem page records as open
between $(2.181\ldots+o(1))n\le g(n)^2\le(3.458\ldots+o(1))n$.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: the paper is the publisher's version of record in the
Abhandlungen aus dem Mathematischen Seminar der Universität Hamburg, received 25
April 1975 and published in April 1979 (Crossref: published in print 1979-04,
online 1979-04-01), which dates this page. The site's curator, Thomas F. Bloom,
credits the disproof of $g(n)\sim2n^{1/2}$ to Mrose in the problem page's
commentary (label OPEN, page last edited 24 September 2025); the problem is not
marked settled there, so the credit is recorded here and is not listed as
`reviewed`. Kohonen's paper (p. 1) restates Mrose's bound as the previous
record, a citation and not a review. Equation (3), the basis $B_2$ and Satz 2
are checked at statement depth; the parameter optimization behind (3) is not
printed, and the proof is not reviewed in this corpus.
