**Claim.** In the notation of
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0817/_index|Problem 817]], with $T_m$ the
$m$th central trinomial coefficient, for every $n\ge1$

$$
g_3(n)\ \ge\ \frac{T_n-1}{2}+\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}T_j,
\qquad\text{hence}\qquad
g_3(n)\ \ge\ \Bigl(\frac{\sqrt3}{2\sqrt\pi}+o(1)\Bigr)\frac{3^n}{\sqrt n}
$$

(Theorem 1.1, p. 2); for every fixed $k\ge4$,
$g_k(n)\gg_k((k-1)/(k-2))^n\,n^{-\log_2((k-1)/(k-2))}$, so
$\liminf g_k(n)^{1/n}\ge(k-1)/(k-2)$ (Theorem 1.2, p. 2); and for every
$k\ge3$ and prime $p\ge3$, $g_k(n)<2p^{\rho_{p,k}(n)-1}$ with
$\rho_{p,k}(n)=\max(q-1,\lceil2n/q\rceil)$ and $q=\min\{p,k\}-1$, so
$\limsup g_k(n)^{1/n}\le\min_pp^{2/(\min\{p,k\}-1)}$ (Theorem 1.3, p. 3).
S. Korsky, *Arithmetic progression-free subset-sum sets*,
arXiv:2606.24139v1 (23 June 2026), cited as [Ko26] on the problem page.

The $k=3$ bound rests on Proposition 4.1 and Corollary 4.2 (p. 6): the
subset sums of $A$ avoid non-trivial three-term progressions exactly when
the $3^n$ sums $\sum\varepsilon_aa$ with $\varepsilon_a\in\{0,1,2\}$ are
distinct, so $g_3(n)$ is the least possible maximum of $n$ positive integers
with injective ternary coefficient sums, and the exact bandwidth of the
ternary grid gives the bound. Remark 4.6 (p. 8) tabulates $g_3(n)=1,3,8,22$
for $n\le4$ against the bound's values $1,3,8,21$ and notes
$g_3(n)\le3^{n-1}$, leaving a factor of order $\sqrt n$ between the bounds.
Theorem 1.2 proceeds by chain expansion and averaging over unused
generators, Theorem 1.3 by a carry-free base-$p$ digit construction, and
Corollary 1.4 (p. 3) places the logarithms of the lower and upper
exponential rates between $(1+o(1))/k$ and $(2+o(1))\log k/k$. The author
announced the preprint in the site's discussion thread on 23 June 2026,
noting that a second-moment argument already gives $\Omega(3^n/\sqrt n)$
with a worse constant.

**Covers.** The lower bound $g_3(n)\ge(\sqrt3/(2\sqrt\pi)+o(1))3^n/\sqrt n$,
which sharpens the refereed $g_3(n)\gg3^n/n$ of
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0817/claims/1992_01_01_erdos_sarkozy|Erdős and Sárközy]],
and the bounds $(k-1)/(k-2)\le\liminf g_k(n)^{1/n}$ and
$\limsup g_k(n)^{1/n}\le\min_pp^{2/(\min\{p,k\}-1)}$ for fixed $k\ge4$. Not
covered: the displayed question $g_3(n)\gg3^n$, which the pending
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0817/claims/2026_09_05_costa|claim of Costa]]
answers in the negative, and the order of $g_k(n)$ for any $k$, which stays
open; the paper itself says that removing the factor $\sqrt n$ would settle
the principal question of Erdős and Sárközy.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki; the ternary characterization is the
paper's own Proposition 4.1, and the bounds of Erdős and Sárközy are not
used in the proofs.
