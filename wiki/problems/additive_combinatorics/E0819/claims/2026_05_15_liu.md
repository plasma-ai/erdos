***

**Claim.** The function $f(N)$ of
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0819/_index|Problem 819]], the largest
number of sums in $[1,N]$ of a subset of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ with
$\lfloor N^{1/2}\rfloor$ elements, satisfies

$$
\liminf_{N\to\infty}\frac{f(N)}{N}\ge\frac{16\sqrt2-17}{12}\approx0.4690,
$$

the note's Theorem 3, against the lower bound $3/8=0.375$ of Erdős and
Freud recorded on the problem page and the trivial upper bound
$f(N)\le N/2+O(N^{1/2})$. The construction, as the note describes it: for
$N=4q^2$ with $q$ large, take an asymptotically maximum Sidon set
$S\subset[0,M]$ of size $q$, $M=(1+o(1))q^2$, obtained by thinning a
Bose--Chowla set, fix $u\in(0,1/4)$ and $\eta\in(0,u/2)$, and set
$A=(a+S)\cup(N/2+\lfloor uN/2\rfloor-b-S)$ with $a$ and $b$ independent and
uniform in $\{1,\ldots,\lfloor\eta N/2\rfloor\}$, so that both copies are
shifted at random (the note says the construction and proof strategy are
inspired by Pikhurko's Lemma 12); the overlap between the Sidon set and its
reflection is controlled by Pikhurko's uniformity lemma for asymptotically
maximum Sidon sets (Lemma 10 of the paper on the card
[[../library/additive_bases/pikhurko_2006_dense_edge_magic_graphs_thin_additive/_index|pikhurko_2006_dense_edge_magic_graphs_thin_additive]]),
the expected number of sums in $[1,N]$ is at least
$(N/2)F(u)-O(\eta N)-o(N)$ with $F(u)=1-2u^2-(1-2u)^3/12$ (Theorem 20; the
proof of Theorem 3 lets $\eta\to0$), maximized at $u^*=3/2-\sqrt2$, where
$F(u^*)=(16\sqrt2-17)/6\approx0.938$, so the constant is $F(u^*)/2$
(Corollary 25); an interpolation argument carries the bound from the
subsequence $N=4q^2$ to all large $N$. The note is dated 15 May 2026 and
hosted on the author's personal site; its acknowledgments say that the
proof was found with the assistance of OpenAI's GPT-5.5 together with the
Rethlas open-source agentic mathematics-research pipeline and that the
author verified every argument and takes responsibility for the paper. The
argument is not checked in this corpus.

**Covers.** The lower bound alone: $\liminf f(N)/N\ge(16\sqrt2-17)/12$,
above the refereed $\tfrac38$ of
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0819/claims/1991_01_01_erdos_freud|Erdős and Freud]].
The claim does not determine the asymptotic size of $f(N)/N$, which the
problem asks for (its order $\Theta(N)$ being known), and says nothing
about the upper bound $1/2+o(1)$ or about the quasi-Sidon question of
[[problems/additive_bases/E0840/_index|Problem 840]] to which Erdős and Freud
tie any improvement of that upper bound.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki; Pikhurko's lemma and the Bose--Chowla
construction are literature inputs.

thread on 2026-05-15 and is not on the proof-claims tab, which is empty;
