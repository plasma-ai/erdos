# problems/additive_combinatorics/E1179/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1179/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1179/claims/1965_12_01_erdos_renyi|1965_12_01_erdos_renyi]]: Theorem 1 of Erdős and Rényi (J. Analyse Math. 1965) equidistributes the
subset sums of about 2 log_2 N random elements of an abelian group of order
N, so g_eps(N) <= (2+o(1)) log_2 N + O_eps(1); refereed, superseded in 1976.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1179/claims/1976_01_01_erdos_hall|1976_01_01_erdos_hall]]: The Theorem of Erdős and Hall (Houston J. Math. 1976) equidistributes the
subset sums of almost all choices of (1+o(1)) log_2 N elements of an abelian
group of order N, so g_eps(N) = (1+o(1)) log_2 N; refereed, site credit.
