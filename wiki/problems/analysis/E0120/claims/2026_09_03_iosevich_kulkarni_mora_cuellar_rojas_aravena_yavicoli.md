***

**Claim.** Let $A\subseteq\mathbb{R}$ support a probability measure whose
Fourier–Stieltjes transform tends to zero at infinity, a Rajchman measure. The
preprint of A. Iosevich, N. Kulkarni, N. Mora Cuéllar, I. Rojas Aravena and A.
Yavicoli, *The Erdős similarity conjecture and Rajchman measures*,
arXiv:2609.04456, posted 2026-09-03 (the claim's date; 28 pages), asserts that
for every $\varepsilon\in(0,1)$ there is a closed, $1$-periodic, nowhere dense
set $E\subseteq\mathbb{R}$ with $m(E\cap I)\ge1-\varepsilon$ for every interval
$I$ of length $1$, while $E$ contains no affine copy of $A$; so every such $A$
satisfies the question of [[problems/analysis/E0120/_index|Problem 120]] in a
uniform large-set form. The abstract describes the proof as combining
equidistribution modulo one for large dilates of the measure with a multiscale
family of low-density periodic blockers, using no quantitative rate of Fourier
decay, and adds a refinement of a theorem of Ivašev-Musatov on the dimensions of
null compact Rajchman supports. Read depth: the arXiv record and abstract; the
proof was not read. The release's geometric manuscript
([[problems/analysis/E0120/claims/2026_10_05_openai|claim page]]) cites the
result in its history section.

**Covers.** The question, answered yes for every set $A$ that supports a
Rajchman measure, with an avoiding set of positive measure in every unit
interval. Sets supporting no Rajchman measure, among them every countable
set such as the geometric progressions, are not covered.
