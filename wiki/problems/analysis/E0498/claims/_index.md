# problems/analysis/E0498/claims

[[problems/analysis/E0498/_index|..]]

[[problems/analysis/E0498/claims/1945_12_01_erdos|1945_12_01_erdos]]: Proves that for real numbers of absolute value at least one, at most the
middle binomial coefficient of the signed sums lie in any open interval of
length two, Problem 498 for real coefficients; refereed.

[[problems/analysis/E0498/claims/1965_08_01_kleitman|1965_08_01_kleitman]]: Proves that at most the middle binomial coefficient of the signed sums of n
complex numbers of modulus above one lie in a closed unit disc, and so, by
finite scaling, of modulus at least one in an open unit disc; refereed.

[[problems/analysis/E0498/claims/1970_08_01_kleitman|1970_08_01_kleitman]]: Resolves Erdős's 1945 Hilbert-space conjecture: for vectors of norm at
least one, at most the middle binomial coefficient of the signed sums lie
in any open ball of radius one; refereed.
