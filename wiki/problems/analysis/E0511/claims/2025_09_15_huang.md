**Claim.** For every $c\in(0,4)$ and every $N$ there is a monic polynomial
$p$ of some degree $n$ such that $\{z:\lvert p(z)\rvert\le1\}$ has at least
$N$ connected components each of diameter at least $c$. This is Theorem 1.1
of L. Huang, *Many lemniscates with large diameter*, arXiv:2509.11597, first
posted 15 September 2025, with
[[../library/analysis/huang_2025_many_lemniscates_large_diameter/_index|a source card]]
in the library. The method (Sections 2.1--2.3) builds one explicit domain $\Omega$, bounded by
the image of the circle $\{\lvert z\rvert=4/c\}$ under a shifted Joukowski
map, which contains the segment $[0,c]$ and has logarithmic capacity exactly
$1$; places inside $\Omega$ a set $\Omega_N$ of $N$ pairwise disjoint Jordan
domains, each of diameter greater than $c$, separated from one another and
from the boundary of $\Omega$; and applies the Hilbert lemniscate theorem to
$\Omega_N$ with an $\varepsilon$-neighborhood that has $N$ separated
components and lies inside $\Omega$, obtaining a polynomial $q$ with
$\Omega_N\subset\{\lvert q\rvert\le1\}\subset\Omega$ after
normalization. Since
$\operatorname{Cap}(q^{-1}(\overline{\mathbb D}))\le\operatorname{Cap}(\Omega)=1$
and the capacity of $r^{-1}(\mathbb D)$ is $\lvert a_d\rvert^{-1/d}$ for a
polynomial $r$ of degree $d$ with leading coefficient $a_d$, the leading
coefficient of $q$ has modulus at least $1$, and $p(z)=q(z/w)$ with $w^d=a_d$
is monic with $p^{-1}(\overline{\mathbb D})=w\cdot q^{-1}(\overline{\mathbb D})$,
a rotation and dilation by $\lvert w\rvert\ge1$ that can only enlarge
diameters. The author notes that the restriction $c<4$ is optimal by Pólya's
projection bound. The theorem answers the question of
[[problems/analysis/E0511/_index|Problem 511]] in the negative, and the
author adds that the problem had already been solved by Pommerenke, whose
[[problems/analysis/E0511/claims/1961_01_01_pommerenke|claim page]] records
the 1961 theorem; the two constructions are independent. The paper states
its theorem for the closed sublevel set, while the problem is posed for the
open set $\{\lvert p\rvert<1\}$.

**Acceptance.** The site's curator, Thomas Bloom, labels the problem
disproved and credits this paper as an independent proof of the negative
answer, the `reviewed` evidence. The paper is an arXiv preprint and no
journal publication of it has been identified, so no `refereed` evidence is
listed.

**Depends on.** Nothing beyond the cited paper.
