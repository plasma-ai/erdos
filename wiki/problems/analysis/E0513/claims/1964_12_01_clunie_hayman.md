***

**Claim.** Let $B$ be the supremum, over transcendental entire functions
$f$, of $\liminf_{r\to\infty}\mu(r,f)/M(r,f)$, where $\mu(r,f)$ is the
maximum term of the power series of $f$ at radius $r$ and $M(r,f)$ its
maximum modulus there; $B$ is the value
[[problems/analysis/E0513/_index|Problem 513]] asks for. J. Clunie and
W. K. Hayman, *The maximum term of a power series*, J. Analyse Math. 12
(1964), 143--186, doi:10.1007/BF02807433, prove

$$
\frac47<B\le\frac2\pi-c
$$

for an absolute constant $c>0$: a transcendental entire function whose limit
inferior exceeds $4/7\approx0.57143$, and an upper bound for every
transcendental entire function that improves the bound $2/\pi$ obtained from an
argument of Clunie reported by Gray and Shah in 1963. The constant $c$ is left
unspecified in the site's account of the paper, which the statement above
follows.

**Covers.** The two bounds only: $B>4/7$ and $B\le2/\pi-c$ for some
absolute $c>0$. The value of $B$ is not determined, and the pending claim
pages of the folder assert larger lower bounds.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki: the argument is the paper's own.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: the paper appeared in the Journal d'Analyse
Mathématique, a refereed journal. The site's commentary credits the two
bounds to the paper, but the site labels the problem OPEN, so that credit
is not acceptance of the problem and the page lists no `reviewed`
evidence. Later lower bounds that supersede $4/7$ are
[[problems/analysis/E0513/claims/2026_02_12_he_tang|He and Tang's certified
lower bound]],
[[problems/analysis/E0513/claims/2026_02_27_sothanaphan|Sothanaphan's
certified parameter improvement]] and
[[problems/analysis/E0513/claims/2026_08_02_lystad|Lystad's certified lower
bound]], all pending; the upper bound stands.

**Dating.** The page is dated by the issue month in the publisher's record
(Crossref), December 1964; the day is a placeholder.
