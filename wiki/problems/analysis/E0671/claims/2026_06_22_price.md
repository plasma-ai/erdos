**Claim.** For a triangular array of nodes $a_i^n\in[-1,1]$, $1\le i\le n$,
let $\lambda_n(x)=\sum_{i\le n}\lvert p_i^n(x)\rvert$ be the Lebesgue function
of the $n$th row and $\mathcal{L}^nf$ the Lagrange interpolant of $f$ on that
row. The claim answers both questions of
[[problems/analysis/E0671/_index|Problem 671]] yes: there is a node system
with $\limsup_n\lambda_n(x)=\infty$ at every $x\in[-1,1]$ such that for every
continuous $f:[-1,1]\to\mathbb{R}$ some $x\in[-1,1]$ has
$\mathcal{L}^nf(x)\to f(x)$. Such a system answers the second question
directly and the first as well, since every point then has an unbounded
Lebesgue function and a convergence point exists for every $f$. The tab
entry's summary says only that the system named as GPT Pro resolves both
questions in the affirmative and that the Lean formalization was performed
by the system named as GPT-5.5 in Codex; the construction is described on
this page only as far as the later re-derivation on
[[problems/analysis/E0671/claims/2026_07_24_quietmethod|QuietMethod's claim
page]] attributes it to this claim: a coalescing-node construction in
stages. The claimant is Liam Price, who filed the claim; the result is
declared as the work of the AI systems named.
