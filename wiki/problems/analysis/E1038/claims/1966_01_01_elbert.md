***

Á. Elbert, *Über eine Vermutung von Erdős betreffs Polynome* I and II, Studia
Sci. Math. Hungar. 1 (1966), 119--128, and 3 (1968), 299--324, prove the
supremum half of the problem. For $f$ a nonconstant monic real polynomial with
all roots in $[-1,1]$ and $E_f=\{x\in\mathbb R:|f(x)|<1\}$, the measure of $E_f$
is at most $2\sqrt2$; Erdős, Herzog and Piranian [EHP58] had proved that bound
when every root is $\pm1$, where $(x^2-1)^m$ attains it, and conjectured it in
general. The two papers are not held in the library, and the statement is
recorded here as the later claimants report it: the manuscript of Darvas, Peng
and Tao cites the two papers as the proof of the supremum, in a series of
technical papers, and records that Erdős asked in 1976 for a more elementary
proof, which Terence Tao's note of December 2025 supplies
([[problems/analysis/E1038/claims/2025_12_21_tao|Tao's page]]); Budala's
Section 1.3 says that the sharp upper value was proved previously by Elbert.
The site's commentary lists $\sup=2\sqrt2$ among the known values without
naming the papers. No independent check of the proofs is recorded.

**Covers.** The supremum only: $\sup|E_f|=2\sqrt2$ over the class. The claim
says nothing about the infimum, which the three pending full claims of the
folder claim to determine.

**Refereed.** Studia Scientiarum Mathematicarum Hungarica, volume 1 (1966) and
volume 3 (1968), as the manuscripts of Darvas, Peng and Tao and of Budala cite
the two papers. No reviewer is named: the site's label is OPEN for the
infimum, and its commentary credits no one with the supremum.
