***

P. Erdős, F. Herzog and G. Piranian, *Metric properties of polynomials*, J.
Analyse Math. 6 (1958), 125-148, pose the problem as their Problem 4 (p. 135)
and note (pp. 135-136) that for a line segment or a circular disc the vanishing
follows from Chebyshev polynomials with their Theorem 8, and from their Theorem
4, respectively: such an $F$ of transfinite diameter at least $1$ has
$\mu(F)=0$. The paper's
[[../library/polynomials/erdos_1958_metric_properties_polynomials/_index|library
card]] records its theorems. Ghosh and Ramachandran's Theorem 4.1, on
[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/2026_04_03_ghosh_ramachandran|their page]],
gives a rate for the segment $[-2,2]$.

**Covers.** The second question for a line segment of length at least $4$ or a
closed disc of radius at least $1$; nothing about other sets or about the first
question.

**Refereed.** J. Analyse Math. 6 (1958), 125-148. The site's commentary credits
the result on a problem it labels OPEN, which is not acceptance.
