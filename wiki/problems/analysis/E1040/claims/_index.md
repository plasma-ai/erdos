# problems/analysis/E1040/claims

[[problems/analysis/E1040/_index|..]]

[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/1958_12_01_erdos_herzog_piranian|1958_12_01_erdos_herzog_piranian]]: A line segment or a closed disc of transfinite diameter at least one has
minimal lemniscate area zero; the problem's source derives the two cases from
Chebyshev polynomials and from zeros on a circle.

[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/2024_02_03_krishnapur_lundberg_ramachandran|2024_02_03_krishnapur_lundberg_ramachandran]]: A compact set of capacity above one whose equilibrium measure obeys a
power bound on discs has minimal lemniscate area zero (Corollary 1.6 of the
journal version of Inradius of random lemniscates).

[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/2025_03_24_krishnapur_lundberg_ramachandran|2025_03_24_krishnapur_lundberg_ramachandran]]: For the closure K of a bounded open set with C^2 boundary and capacity one,
the minimal area of {|p| < 1} over monic polynomials with zeros in K has
infimum zero over the degrees; preprint.

[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/2026_01_29_feng|2026_01_29_feng]]: Two countable compact sets of transfinite diameter zero, one with minimal
lemniscate area at least pi/4 and one with minimal area below any prescribed
bound, so the minimal area is not a function of the transfinite diameter.

[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/2026_04_03_ghosh_ramachandran|2026_04_03_ghosh_ramachandran]]: Compact sets of equal capacity in (0, 1) with different minimal areas, so
the minimal area is not determined by the capacity; also, for compact K of
capacity t > 1, the degree-n minimal area of {|p| < 1} decays like t^(-2n).

[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/2026_09_05_tzachristas|2026_09_05_tzachristas]]: Every compact set of capacity one admits monic polynomials with zeros in it
and unit lemniscates of arbitrarily small area; hence every closed infinite
set of transfinite diameter at least one has minimal area zero.

[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/2026_09_06_gessel|2026_09_06_gessel]]: For every closed infinite set of transfinite diameter at least one, the areas
of {|p| < 1} over monic polynomials with all roots in the set have infimum
zero; Lean checked by an outside verifier, not built here.

[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/2026_09_06_kitamura|2026_09_06_kitamura]]: An independent Lean 4 development proving that a closed infinite set whose
infimum of finite Fekete diameters is at least one has minimal lemniscate
area zero; the bridge to the classical transfinite diameter is not formalized.

[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/2026_09_06_shlummi|2026_09_06_shlummi]]: Every closed infinite set of transfinite diameter at least one admits monic
polynomials with all roots in it and unit lemniscates of arbitrarily small
area; author-run Lean, not built here.
