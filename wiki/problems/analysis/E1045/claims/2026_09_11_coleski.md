***

**Claim.** The repository *A Lean-verified proof of the six-point case of
Erdős #1045*, published on GitHub by the user coleski on 11 September 2026,
claims that for six points of the plane with pairwise distances at most $2$
the maximum of the ordered product of
[[problems/analysis/E1045/_index|Problem 1045]] is

$$
\max\prod_{0\le i<j\le5}|z_i-z_j|^2=64(2\sqrt3-2)^{18},
$$

attained at $(-1,0),(1,0),(0,\sqrt3),(0,\sqrt3-2),(\sqrt3-1,1),(1-\sqrt3,1)$,
the configuration Quanyu Tang conjectured optimal. It gives a
computer-assisted written proof (a diameter-triangle bound, a certified local
deformation to that case and exact interval covering certificates) and a Lean
development of the final implication and of attainment. The repository says
the proof was developed using Codex. Hu's manuscript on large $n$
([[problems/analysis/E1045/claims/2026_09_22_hu|its claim page]]) cites the
repository as its reference [10].

**Covers.** The maximum for $n=6$. The value exceeds the regular hexagon's
$6^6$, which
[[problems/analysis/E1045/claims/1967_04_01_danzer_pommerenke|Danzer and Pommerenke]]
had already shown is not optimal.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki.

**Standing.** A self-published repository with no journal publication or
outside review recorded; the repository says no submission to the site's
forum was made. This corpus has not built the Lean development, so it gives
no `formalized` evidence here. The site labels the problem OPEN.
