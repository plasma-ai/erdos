# problems/analysis/E1047/claims

[[problems/analysis/E1047/_index|..]]

[[problems/analysis/E1047/claims/1961_01_01_pommerenke|1961_01_01_pommerenke]]: Pommerenke's 1961 Theorem 14: for large p and a slightly above
(1 + 1/p) p^{1/(p+1)}, the set where |z^p (z - a)| <= 1 has two components,
one of them not convex, the negative answer to Grunsky's question; refereed.

[[problems/analysis/E1047/claims/1966_04_01_goodman|1966_04_01_goodman]]: Goodman's 1966 counterexamples to Grunsky's question for the open sublevel
set: (z^2 + 1)(z - 2)^2 and a quartic with four simple roots, each with a
nonconvex component at a critical level; refereed.

[[problems/analysis/E1047/claims/2026_01_21_alexeev|2026_01_21_alexeev]]: A Lean 4 proof, found by Aristotle and published by Boris Alexeev, that the
six roots of z^6 - z lie in six distinct components of the set where
|z^6 - z| <= 0.582, and the one containing 0 is not convex.
