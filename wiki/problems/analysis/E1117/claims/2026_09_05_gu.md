**Claim.** Let $f$ be a nonzero entire function that is not a monomial, and
write

$$
f(z)=z^m\bigl(c_0+c_kz^k+O(z^{k+1})\bigr),\qquad m\ge0,\ k\ge1,\ c_0c_k\ne0,
$$

so that $k$ is the gap between the exponents of the first two nonzero terms.
Theorem 1.1 of the manuscript *A negative answer to a question of Erdős on
maximum modulus points* asserts that there is a countable set
$E\subset(0,\infty)$ with $\nu(r)\le2k$ for every $r\notin E$, where
$\nu(r)$ counts the points of $|z|=r$ at which $|f|$ attains its maximum
modulus as on [[problems/analysis/E1117/_index|Problem 1117]]. Hence
$\liminf_{r\to\infty}\nu(r)\le2k<\infty$: no non-monomial entire function
has $\nu(r)\to\infty$, which is a negative answer to the problem's second
question. The manuscript says the factor $2$ is attained by an explicit
example (its Remark 5.1). The route: at radii where $\log M(r,f)$ is
differentiable in $\log r$, every maximum modulus point has the same real
value of $A=zf'/f$, so the points lie in one fiber of the correspondence
$A(z)=\overline{A(\overline w)}$ with a common value of $zw$; every
irreducible component of that correspondence is shown to contain points with
$|zw|$ arbitrarily small, by continuation of implicit functions (the Iversen
property, cited to Stoïlow) and growth in a direct tract (a lemma of Fuchs,
cited through Bergweiler, Rippon and Stallard); the map $(z,w)\mapsto(zw,A(z))$
is then finite and proper, and since $A-m$ has local degree $k$ at the
origin, a degree count near the coordinate axes bounds the generic fibers by
$2k$. This page states the manuscript's theorem and the route its
introduction describes; the proof is not verified in this corpus.

> The proof studies the logarithmic derivative A(z) = zf′(z)/f(z), giving a

**Covers.** The second question, whether $\liminf\nu(r)=\infty$ is possible:
the theorem answers it no. The first question, whether
$\limsup\nu(r)=\infty$ is possible, is outside this claim; its affirmative
answer by Herzog and Piranian (1968), which the manuscript cites, is on
[[problems/analysis/E1117/claims/1968_01_01_herzog_piranian|their page]].

September 2026 on the author's behalf, crediting Qiyuan Gu, with a Zenodo
record as the write-up. The version of the record that the tab links was

so the analytic core of the proof is not formalized; a bridge file states
the negative answer from those two named hypotheses. This corpus has not
built or kernel-checked the development, and the archive awards nothing
here.
