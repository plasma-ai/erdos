**Claim.** Both questions are answered. Let $f$ be a nonconstant entire
function and $E(c)=\{z:|f(z)|>c\}$. First, the minimal growth is the one
Hayman conjectured: if $E(c)$ has finite planar measure for some $c>0$,
then

$$
\int^\infty\frac{r\,dr}{\log\log M(r,f)}<\infty
$$

(Part 1, formula (6)), and the bound is best possible: for every continuous
positive nondecreasing $\Phi$ with $\int^\infty r\,dr/\Phi(r)<\infty$ there
is an entire $f$ with $\log\log M(r,f)=O(\Phi(r))$ and $|E(c)|<\infty$ for
every $c>0$ (Part 2, with a lemma credited to V. S. Boichuk). Second, the
answer to Erdős's question is no: finiteness of $|E(c)|$ for one $c$ does
not force finiteness of $|E(c')|$ for some $c'<c$. The paper says at the
outset (p. 512) that it inserts the word "some" into Erdős's question
because the answer is negative already in that form, and hence in the form
asking for every $c'<c$. Part 3 (p. 517) gives the shape of the examples:
with $T=\{c>0:|E(c)|<\infty\}$, the cases $T=\emptyset$ and $T=(0,\infty)$
are clear (the function of Part 2 has the latter), and for every $m>0$ there
are entire functions with $T=[m,\infty)$ and with $T=(m,\infty)$, built by
Keldysh approximation of $m(1-\tfrac14z^{-1/4})^2$, respectively
$m(1+\tfrac14z^{-1/4})^2$, outside a domain of finite area; the site's
commentary reports the same description. The proof of Part 1 applies the
inequality proved independently by Pfluger and by Arima with Carleman's
method, $\log^+\log^+M(er,f)\ge\pi\int_{A(r)}dt/l(t)-K$, with $l(t)$ the
longest arc of $|z|=t$ inside $E(c)$, and the Cauchy--Bunyakovsky inequality
on dyadic blocks. The statements are those the paper prints, as recorded
on the source card
[[../library/analysis/goldberg_1979_sets_which_modulus_entire_function_has/_index|goldberg_1979_sets_which_modulus_entire_function_has]];
the proofs are not verified in this corpus.

**Source.** A. A. Gol'dberg, Sets on which the modulus of an entire function
has a lower bound (Russian), Sibirsk. Mat. Zh. 20 (1979), no. 3, 512--518,
691; the publisher's record is the linked mathnet.ru page. The record dates
the article to the year alone, and the page is named by that date.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: the paper appeared in Sibirskii Matematicheskii
Zhurnal. Reviewed: the site's curator, T. F. Bloom, labels the problem
solved, credits Gol'dberg and Camera with independent proofs of Hayman's
conjecture, and credits Gol'dberg with the negative answer to the second
question; Hayman and Lingham's 2018 survey of Hayman's problems
([[../library/polynomials/hayman_lingham_2018_research_problems_function_theory/_index|library card]],
Update 2.40) records Gol'dberg's independent proof of the growth bound and
his example for the second part. Camera's thesis is on
[[problems/analysis/E1118/claims/1977_01_01_camera|Camera's page]]. Nothing
here is independently reviewed by this project.
