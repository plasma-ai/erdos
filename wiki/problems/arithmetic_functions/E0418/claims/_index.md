# problems/arithmetic_functions/E0418/claims

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0418/_index|..]]

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0418/claims/1994_04_11_browkin_schinzel|1994_04_11_browkin_schinzel]]: Proves that no number 2^k times 509203 with k at least 1 is of the form n
minus Euler's totient of n, so infinitely many positive integers are not of
that form; refereed in Colloquium Mathematicum and recorded by Guy.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0418/claims/1999_07_02_flammenkamp_luca|1999_07_02_flammenkamp_luca]]: Gives a sufficient condition on k for every 2^m k with m at least 1 to be a
noncototient and finds seven such k by computation, so infinitely many
integers are not of the form n minus phi(n); refereed.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0418/claims/2004_09_14_banks_luca|2004_09_14_banks_luca]]: Proves that 2p is a noncototient for almost all primes p, so at least
(1+o(1))x/(2 log x) integers up to x are not of the form n minus phi(n);
arXiv preprint only, the journal version omitting this theorem.
