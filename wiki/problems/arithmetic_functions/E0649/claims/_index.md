# problems/arithmetic_functions/E0649/claims

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0649/claims/2025_01_16_sampaio|2025_01_16_sampaio]]: No integer n has greatest prime factor 19 while n plus one is a power of 2,
so the statement fails for the pair 19 and 2; the site's remarks record the
observation and extend it to primes above 13 with 2 as a primitive root.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0649/claims/2025_01_16_tong|2025_01_16_tong]]: For every prime p there are infinitely many primes q such that no integer n
has greatest prime factor p while n plus one has greatest prime factor q;
the elementary disproof the site's remarks record and credit to Tong.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0649/claims/2026_02_07_alexeev|2026_02_07_alexeev]]: Infinitely many primes q such that no n at least 2 has P(n)P(n+1) equal to
2q: the 2020 Romanian Master of Mathematics argument, formalized in Lean by
ChatGPT, Aristotle and Boris Alexeev; accepted on Lean built here.
