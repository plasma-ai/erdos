# Problem 823

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/_index|..]]

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0823/claims/_index|claims/]]: The 1 claim page of Problem 823, one per claimant's result; the problem's standing derives from them.

***

**Statement.** Let $\alpha\geq 1$. Is there a sequence of integers $n_k,m_k$
such that $n_k/m_k\to \alpha$ and $\sigma(n_k)=\sigma(m_k)$ for all $k\geq 1$,
where $\sigma$ is the sum of divisors function?

**Status.** Proved. The settling result is recorded on the claim page
[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0823/claims/2015_11_18_pollack|Pollack 2015]].

**Source.** [erdosproblems.com/823](https://www.erdosproblems.com/823), accessed
2026-09-04. Cite as: T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problem #823,
https://www.erdosproblems.com/823.
