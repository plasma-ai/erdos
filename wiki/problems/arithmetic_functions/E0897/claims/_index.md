with slowly growing g whose normalized jumps tend to zero and whose ratios
stay bounded, so both answers are no; accepted by the site.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0897/claims/2025_12_26_archivara|2025_12_26_archivara]]: Archivara's 2025 manuscript, written by its research agent, constructs an
explicit additive function answering both questions no; read on the site's
thread as a rediscovery of Wirsing's construction, later formalized in Lean.
