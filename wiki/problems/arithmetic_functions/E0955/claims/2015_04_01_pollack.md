***

**Claim.** P. Pollack, *Some arithmetic properties of the sum of proper
divisors and the sum of prime divisors*, Illinois J. Math. 58 (2014), no. 1,
125--147, Theorem 1.11: for all $x\ge2$, the number of $n\le x$ for which
$s(n)$ is prime is $O(x/\log x)$
([[../library/arithmetic_functions/pollack_2014_arithmetic_properties_sum_proper_divisors_sum/_index|card]]).
So the preimage under $s$ of the primes, a set of density zero, has density
zero, which is the assertion of
[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0955/_index|Problem 955]] for that set. The
issue is dated Spring 2014; the publisher records online publication on
1 April 2015, so this page carries that date.

**Covers.** The instance $A$ = the primes; the general assertion stays open.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: Illinois Journal of Mathematics. The site's
commentary credits the result, but the site labels the problem OPEN, so no
curator acceptance is listed.
