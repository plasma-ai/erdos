***

**Claim.** P. Pollack, *Palindromic sums of proper divisors*, Integers 15A
(2015), article A13, Theorem 1: "Fix $g\geq2$. Let $k$ be an integer with
$k\geq2$. The upper density of those $n$ for which $s(n)$ is
$k$-nearly-palindromic is $O_g(1/\log k)$." Every base-$g$ palindrome is
$k$-nearly-palindromic for every $k$, so the $n$ with $s(n)$ a base-$g$
palindrome have density zero
([[../library/arithmetic_functions/pollack_2015_palindromic_sums_proper_divisors/_index|card]]),
the assertion of [[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0955/_index|Problem 955]]
for the palindromes, a set with about $x^{1/2}$ elements up to $x$. The paper
prints its received and accepted dates and the publication date 15 June
2015, the date this page carries.

**Covers.** The instance $A$ = the base-$g$ palindromes, for each $g\ge2$;
the general assertion stays open.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki.

**Acceptance.** Claimed. Volume 15A of Integers is the proceedings of
Integers 2013, the Erdős Centennial Conference, and no record of the volume's
refereeing is on file, so `refereed` is not listed. The site does not credit
the paper.
