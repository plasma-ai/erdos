***

**Claim.** R. de la Bretèche, *Plus grand facteur premier de valeurs de
polynômes aux entiers*, Acta Arith. 169 (2015), 221--250, proves that for
every even monic irreducible quartic $f\in\mathbb Z[X]$ whose Galois group is
isomorphic to the Klein group $V_4=\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\times\mathbb
Z/2\mathbb Z$ there is $c>0$ such that the integers $n\le X$ with
$P^+(f(n))>X^{1+c}$ have positive density. Hence $F_f(X)>X^{1+c}$ for all
large $X$, in the notation of
[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0976/_index|Problem 976]]. The result
extends Dartyge's 2015 theorem for $X^4-X^2+1$, as the introduction of
Dartyge and Maynard's paper on cyclic and dihedral quartics records.

**Covers.** The first question for even monic irreducible quartics with
Galois group $V_4$. It does not give $F_f(n)\gg n^4$.
