***

**Claim.** The unnumbered main theorem of Ivan Ermoshin, *The Largest Prime
Factor of an Irreducible Cubic Polynomial* (arXiv:2602.03642, v3 of
12 June 2026, p. 3), states that for every monic irreducible cubic
$f\in\mathbb Z[X]$ there is $c_f>0$ such that, as $x\to\infty$, a positive
proportion of the integers $m\in[x,2x]$ have a prime factor of $f(m)$
exceeding $x^{1+c_f}$; in particular
$P^+\!\left(\prod_{m\le x}f(m)\right)\gg_f x^{1+c_f}$, which is
$F_f(n)\gg_f n^{1+c_f}$ in the notation of
[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0976/_index|Problem 976]]. The statement is
recorded on
[[../library/arithmetic_functions/ermoshin_2026_largest_prime_factor_irreducible_cubic_polynomial/main_theorem|the theorem card]].

**Covers.** The first question for monic irreducible cubics. It gives no
exponent uniform in $f$ and does not give $F_f(n)\gg n^3$.
