# problems/arithmetic_functions/E0977/claims

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0977/_index|..]]

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0977/claims/1975_01_01_stewart|1975_01_01_stewart]]: Stewart's 1975 Acta Arithmetica paper proves that P(a^n-b^n)/n tends to
infinity along the exponents n with at most kappa log log n distinct prime
factors, for each kappa below 1/log 2; refereed.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0977/claims/2010_08_06_stewart|2010_08_06_stewart]]: Stewart's 2013 Acta Mathematica theorem bounds the greatest prime factor of
a^n-b^n below by n times a factor tending to infinity, so P(2^n-1)/n tends
to infinity; refereed, and accepted by the site.
