# problems/arithmetic_functions/E1064/claims

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E1064/claims/2001_07_01_grytczuk_luca_wojtowicz|2001_07_01_grytczuk_luca_wojtowicz]]: Grytczuk, Luca and Wójtowicz prove the totient of n minus its totient exceeds
the totient of n infinitely often, with a growing gap, and the reverse on a
set of lower density at least 0.54; refereed and credited by the site.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E1064/claims/2002_01_01_luca_pomerance|2002_01_01_luca_pomerance]]: Luca and Pomerance prove that the totient of n exceeds the totient of n minus
its totient for almost all n, by a margin of any order below n, and remark
without proof that the reverse holds infinitely often by any factor.
