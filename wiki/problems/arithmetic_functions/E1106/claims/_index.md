# problems/arithmetic_functions/E1106/claims

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E1106/_index|..]]

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E1106/claims/1987_12_01_schinzel_wirsing|1987_12_01_schinzel_wirsing]]: Schinzel and Wirsing bound from below the number of multiplicatively
independent partition numbers in a range, which gives F(n) >> log n.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E1106/claims/1990_01_01_schinzel|1990_01_01_schinzel]]: Schinzel's argument, printed by Erdős and Ivić as their Lemma 2, that the
number of distinct prime factors of p(1)...p(n) tends to infinity.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E1106/claims/2000_01_01_ono|2000_01_01_ono]]: Ono proves that every prime divides some value of the partition function, so
every prime eventually divides the product of the partition numbers.
