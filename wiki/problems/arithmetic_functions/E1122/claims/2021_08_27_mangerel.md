**Claim.** Let $g:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{R}$ be completely additive, let
$B_g(X)=(\sum_{p^k\le X}g(p^k)^2/p^k)^{1/2}$, and let

$$
F_g(\varepsilon)=\limsup_{X\to\infty}\frac{1}{B_g(X)^2}
\sum_{\substack{p\le X\\ |g(p)|>\varepsilon^{-1}B_g(X)}}\frac{g(p)^2}{p},
$$

the share of $B_g(X)^2$ carried by primes where $|g(p)|$ exceeds
$\varepsilon^{-1}B_g(X)$. If $F_g(\varepsilon)\to0$ as $\varepsilon\to0^+$
and the set $\mathcal{B}=\{n: g(n)<g(n-1)\}$ of decreases satisfies
$|\mathcal{B}\cap[1,X]|\ll X/(\log X)^{2+\delta}$ for some $\delta>0$, then
$g(n)=c\log n$ for all $n$ and some constant $c$. This is Corollary 1.7 of
Mangerel, *Additive functions in short intervals, gaps and a conjecture of
Erdős*, Ramanujan J. 59 (2022), no. 4, 1023–1090, digested on the card
[[../library/arithmetic_functions/mangerel_2022_additive_functions_short_intervals_gaps_conjecture/_index|Mangerel 2022]].
The paper derives it from its Theorem 1.8, that an additive function in the
class $\mathcal{A}_s$ (additive functions with $B_g(X)\to\infty$, whose
prime values dominate $B_g(X)^2$, and with $F_g(\varepsilon)\to0$) whose
decreases have density zero is close to $\lambda(X)\log$ at prime powers,
together
with a growth estimate $B_g(X)=(\log X)^{1+o(1)}$ proved in its Section
7.2; the main tools are the paper's short-interval averaging theorems for
additive functions, analogs of the Matomäki–Radziwiłł theorem.

**Covers.** The instances of
[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E1122/_index|Problem 1122]] in which $f$ is
completely additive with $\lim_{\varepsilon\to0^+}F_f(\varepsilon)=0$ and
the set $A$ of decreases satisfies
$|A\cap[1,X]|\ll X/(\log X)^{2+\delta}$ for some $\delta>0$: for these the
answer is yes. It leaves open additive functions that are not completely
additive, functions with $F_f(\varepsilon)\not\to0$, and the full hypothesis
$|A\cap[1,X]|=o(X)$; the site's commentary states the hypothesis as
additive, which overstates the corollary. Problem 1122 as a whole has the
pending full claim
[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E1122/claims/2026_09_08_gu|Gu 2026]], which
cites this corollary as prior work, and the earlier accepted partial claim
[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E1122/claims/1946_01_01_erdos|Erdős 1946]]
settles the instances with no decreases at all.
