***

**Claim.** With $f(N)$ the quantity of
[[problems/covering_systems/E0202/_index|Problem 202]] and
$L(N)=\exp(\sqrt{\log N\log\log N})$,

$$
NL(N)^{-\sqrt2-o(1)}<f(N)<NL(N)^{-1/6+o(1)}.
$$

The lower bound is a Chinese-remainder construction with ordered
prime-power factors, refining Erdős and Szemerédi's; the upper bound is
the Corollary to Theorem 1, which reduces arbitrary distinct moduli to the
squarefree case, where Theorem 1 gives the coefficient $1/2$. E. S. Croot
III, *On non-intersecting arithmetic progressions*, Acta Arith. 110 (2003),
no. 3, 233–238, first posted as arXiv:math/0208236 on 2002-08-30 and cited
as [Cr03b] on the problem page; the library's
[[../library/covering_systems/crootiii_2003_non_intersecting_arithmetic_progressions/lower_bound|lower bound]],
[[../library/covering_systems/crootiii_2003_non_intersecting_arithmetic_progressions/theorem_1|Theorem 1]]
and
[[../library/covering_systems/crootiii_2003_non_intersecting_arithmetic_progressions/corollary_to_theorem_1|Corollary]]
pages compile the proofs.

**Covers.** The bounds above, which improved Erdős and Szemerédi's
$N\exp(-(\log N)^{1/2+\epsilon})<f(N)<N(\log N)^{-c}$ on
[[problems/covering_systems/E0202/claims/1968_01_01_erdos_szemeredi|their claim page]].
Both sides were later sharpened: the upper coefficient by
[[problems/covering_systems/E0202/claims/2005_01_01_chen|Chen]] and both by
[[problems/covering_systems/E0202/claims/2013_01_01_de_la_breteche_ford_vandehey|de la Bretèche, Ford and Vandehey]].
The claim does not determine $f(N)$.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki; the theorem is the paper's own.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: Acta Arithmetica 110 (2003), no. 3, 233–238,
doi:10.4064/aa110-3-3. Not reviewed: the site labels the problem SOLVED
(LEAN) and credits the answer to Ho's result, not to this paper.
