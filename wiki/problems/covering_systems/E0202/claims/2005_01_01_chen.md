***

**Claim.** With $f(N)$ the quantity of
[[problems/covering_systems/E0202/_index|Problem 202]] and
$L(N)=\exp(\sqrt{\log N\log\log N})$,

$$
f(N)\le NL(N)^{-1/2+o(1)},
$$

with no restriction on the moduli. Croot had proved the coefficient $1/2$
for squarefree moduli and $1/6$ in general; Chen removes the squarefree
restriction by counting the high-power parts of the moduli, selecting a
common residue and rescaling. Y.-G. Chen, *On disjoint arithmetic
progressions*, Acta Arith. 118 (2005), no. 2, 143–148, cited as [Ch05] on
the problem page; the library's
[[../library/covering_systems/chen_2005_disjoint_arithmetic_progressions/theorem|theorem page]]
compiles the proof.

**Covers.** The upper bound above, which improved the coefficient $1/6$ of
[[problems/covering_systems/E0202/claims/2002_08_30_croot|Croot's claim page]]
and was itself improved to $\sqrt3/2$ by
[[problems/covering_systems/E0202/claims/2013_01_01_de_la_breteche_ford_vandehey|de la Bretèche, Ford and Vandehey]].
The paper gives no new lower bound and does not determine $f(N)$.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki; the theorem is the paper's own.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: Acta Arithmetica 118 (2005), no. 2, 143–148,
doi:10.4064/aa118-2-4. Not reviewed: the site labels the problem SOLVED
(LEAN) and credits the answer to Ho's result, not to this paper. The page is
named by the publication year; the journal record gives no day and no
preprint posting is recorded.
