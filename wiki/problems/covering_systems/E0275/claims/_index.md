[[problems/covering_systems/E0275/claims/1970_03_01_crittenden_vanden_eynden|1970_03_01_crittenden_vanden_eynden]]: The 1970 theorem of Crittenden and Vanden Eynden in Proc. Amer. Math. Soc.

conjecture of Erdős also proved by Selfridge; accepted as a refereed paper.

consecutive integers cover every integer; accepted on the refereed paper.
