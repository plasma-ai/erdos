# Problem 276

[[problems/covering_systems/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Is there an infinite Lucas sequence $a_0,a_1,\ldots$ where
$a_{n+2}=a_{n+1}+a_n$ for $n\geq 0$ such that all $a_k$ are composite, and yet
no integer has a common factor with every term of the sequence?

**Status.** Open.

**Source.** [erdosproblems.com/276](https://www.erdosproblems.com/276), accessed
2026-09-04. Cite as: T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problem #276,
https://www.erdosproblems.com/276.
