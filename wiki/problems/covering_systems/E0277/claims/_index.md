[[problems/covering_systems/E0277/claims/1979_06_01_haight|1979_06_01_haight]]: Haight (Mathematika 1979) proves that for every c some n has sigma(n) > cn
while its divisors above one are not the moduli of any covering system;
accepted on the refereed publication and the site's credit.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0277/claims/2005_07_18_filaseta_ford_konyagin_pomerance_yu|2005_07_18_filaseta_ford_konyagin_pomerance_yu]]: Theorem 1 of Filaseta, Ford, Konyagin, Pomerance and Yu (J. Amer. Math. Soc.
2007) gives infinitely many H with sigma(H)/H about sqrt(log log H) whose
divisors above one support no covering system; accepted as refereed.
