***

**Claim.** For fixed moduli $n_1, \ldots, n_r$ the density of the integers
lying in none of the classes $a_i \pmod{n_i}$ is largest when every $a_i = 0$,
so the minimum covered density is attained when all the residues agree: the
answer to the second question of
[[problems/covering_systems/E0278/_index|Problem 278]] is yes. The theorem is
Rogers', first published in H. Halberstam and K. F. Roth, *Sequences*, Vol. I
(Clarendon Press, Oxford, 1966), pp. 242--244, reissued by Springer in 1983
(Section 5.3), cited as [HaRo66] on the problem page. Filaseta, Ford,
Konyagin, Pomerance and Yu cite it from the 1966 edition as Rogers' theorem
([[../library/integer_sequences/filaseta_2007_sieving_large_integers_covering_systems_congruences/_index|library card]]),
and Schroeder's manuscript
([[problems/covering_systems/E0278/claims/2026_09_22_schroeder|claim page]])
treats the second question as settled by it. Simpson's inclusion-exclusion
bound of 1986, the result the site's commentary credits for the second
question, states the same extremal fact with the extremal value written out
([[problems/covering_systems/E0278/claims/1986_04_01_simpson|claim page]]); a
comment of 2026-03-03 on the problem's discussion thread reports the book's
earlier publication. The same theorem supplies one step of the elementary
proof of [[problems/covering_systems/E0281/_index|Problem 281]] recorded on
[[problems/covering_systems/E0281/claims/2026_01_18_koishichan|KoishiChan's claim page]],
which also records a proof of the theorem posted by Terence Tao on his blog
on 2026-01-19.

**Covers.** The second question alone: equal residues minimize the covered
density. The first question, the value of the maximum covered density, is not
addressed.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki: the theorem is the book's own.

**Standing.** Claimed: the book is not a refereed journal publication, and
the site's commentary on this problem credits Simpson, not Rogers, with
settling the second question, so no acceptance evidence is listed. The book
gives the year 1966 and no month, which names the page.
