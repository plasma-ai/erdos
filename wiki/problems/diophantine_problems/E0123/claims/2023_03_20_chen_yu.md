***

**Claim.** Y.-G. Chen and W.-X. Yu, *On $d$-complete sequences of integers, II*,
Acta Arith. 207 (2023), 161--181 (online 20 March 2023). As the paper's abstract
and its zbMATH summary (Zbl 1528.11016) state, the authors give a criterion for
the $d$-completeness of $\{p^aq^br^c\}$ for pairwise coprime $p,q,r\ge2$ and
apply it to prove that $\{3^a5^br^c\}$ is $d$-complete for $1<r\le14$ with
$(r,15)=1$, $\{2^a5^br^c\}$ for $1<r\le87$ with $(r,10)=1$, and $\{2^a7^br^c\}$
for $1<r\le33$ with $(r,14)=1$; they also answer how sparse a $d$-complete
sequence can be. For [[problems/diophantine_problems/E0123/_index|Problem 123]]
this settles every triple in those three ranges.

**Covers.** The triples $(2,5,r)$ for $1<r\le87$, $(2,7,r)$ for $1<r\le33$ and
$(3,5,r)$ for $1<r\le14$, each $r$ coprime to the other two bases. Not covered:
every other triple.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki; the result rests on the cited paper. It
extends
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0123/claims/1996_04_01_erdos_lewin|Erdős and Lewin's triples]]
and
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0123/claims/2016_02_03_ma_chen|Ma and Chen's]].

**Read depth.** The statement is taken from the paper's abstract on the
publisher's page and its zbMATH summary; the body of the paper, which the
library does not hold, was not read.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: Acta Arithmetica 207 (2023). The site's commentary
lists the result, but the site credits the settlement of the problem to Snyder's
proof, so the commentary is not `reviewed` evidence for this paper.
