***

**The claim.** Let $A$ be the set of integers whose base-$3$ digits are all $0$
or $1$ and $B$ the set of integers whose base-$4$ digits are all $0$ or $1$. The
file `APNOutputs/ErdosProblems/erdos_125.variants.positive_lower_density.lean`
of DeepMind's public repository `google-deepmind/alphaproof-nexus-results`
proves, as `target_theorem_0`, the formal-conjectures statement
`answer(False) ↔ 0 < (A + B).lowerDensity`: the lower density of $A+B$ is not
positive, which answers the question of
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0125/_index|Problem 125]] in the negative. The
statement is the one proved by
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0125/claims/2026_03_30_deepmind|DeepMind's accepted result of March 2026]];
the two are recorded apart because this file names no informal author, its proof
runs through different lemmas, and no source says that it is that result's
artifact.

**Argument, in outline.** The lemma names and statements indicate the following.
A gap lemma (`A_B_gap`) shows that no integer strictly between
$(3^k-1)/2+(4^m-1)/3$ and $\min(3^k,4^m)$ lies in $A+B$, since the elements of
$A$ below $3^k$ are at most $(3^k-1)/2$ and those of $B$ below $4^m$ at most
$(4^m-1)/3$. The irrationality of $\log 4/\log 3$ (`log_ratio_irrational`)
gives, by a Dirichlet-type approximation (`dirichlet_approx`), exponents with
$3^k\leq 4^m\leq(1+\epsilon)3^k$, so that the gap is a fixed fraction of the
scale. A scale step (`scale_step`) then shows that a bound
$\lvert(A+B)\cap[0,N)\rvert\leq CN$ at one scale yields the bound
$\tfrac{11}{12}CN'$ at a larger scale $N'$, and induction
(`density_multi_scale`) gives, for every $d$, some $N$ with
$\lvert(A+B)\cap[0,N)\rvert\leq(11/12)^dN$; hence (`density_tends_to_zero`) the
count falls below $\epsilon N$ for some $N$ at every $\epsilon>0$, which is the
negation of positive lower density. The proof has not been reconstructed in this
corpus.
