# Problem 137

[[problems/diophantine_problems/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** We say that $N$ is powerful if whenever $p\mid N$ we also have
$p^2\mid N$. Let $k\geq 3$. Can the product of any $k$ consecutive positive
integers ever be powerful?

**Status.** Open.

**Source.** [erdosproblems.com/137](https://www.erdosproblems.com/137), accessed
2026-09-04. Cite as: T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problem #137,
