***

**Claim.** Theorem 1.1 of J.-H. Fang and Y.-G. Chen, *A quantitative form of
the Erdős–Birch theorem*, Acta Arith. 178 (2017), no. 4, 301--311, published
online 10 May 2017, the date this page carries: for any coprime integers
$p,q>1$ there are positive integers $K$ and $B$ with
$$
\log_2\log_2K<q^{2p},\qquad \log_2\log_2\log_2B<q^{2p},
$$
such that every integer $n\geq B$ is a sum of distinct terms of
$\{p^aq^b: a\geq0,\ 0\leq b\leq K,\ a+b>0\}$. The statement and the
surrounding history are recorded on the library's
[[../library/diophantine_problems/fang_2017_quantitative_form_erdos_birch_theorem/_index|card]].

**Covers.** The set in Theorem 1.1 is a subset of $\{p^aq^b\}$, so the
theorem proves the statement of
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/_index|Problem 246]], in its corrected
Statement, which takes $a,b\geq2$, in a stronger form with an explicit
threshold $B$.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: Acta Arithmetica, volume 178. Reviewed: the site's
curator, Thomas Bloom, marks the problem PROVED and the commentary cites the
paper among the quantitative forms (problem page last edited 7 December
2025); the curator had no part in the result. The problem's settling result
is [[problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/claims/1959_10_01_birch|Birch's theorem]].
The proof was read but not independently verified.
