# problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/claims/1959_10_01_birch|1959_10_01_birch]]: Proves that for coprime a and b every large integer is a sum of distinct
numbers of the form a to the k times b to the l; refereed in 1959, credited
by the site's curator, and later formalized in Lean by others.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/claims/1960_01_01_cassels|1960_01_01_cassels]]: Cassels's 1960 Theorem I, a circle-method criterion under which every large
integer is a sum of distinct elements of a set, of which the completeness
of the numbers a to the k times b to the l is an immediate consequence.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/claims/2000_01_01_hegyvari|2000_01_01_hegyvari]]: Hegyvári (Acta Math. Hungar. 86 (2000)) proves that the numbers p^a q^b
with b at most an explicit K(p,q) already form a complete set, the first
effective form of Birch's theorem; refereed.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/claims/2015_07_08_bergelson_simmons|2015_07_08_bergelson_simmons]]: Bergelson and Simmons (Acta Arith. 177 (2017)) prove that the numbers
a^n b^k with at most 4a-4 distinct exponents k, including 0, form a
complete set, so K(a,b) is at most 4a-5; refereed.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/claims/2017_05_10_fang_chen|2017_05_10_fang_chen]]: Fang and Chen (Acta Arith. 178 (2017)) give explicit towers K and B such
that every integer at least B is a sum of distinct numbers p^a q^b with b
at most K; refereed.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/claims/2024_01_01_yu|2024_01_01_yu]]: Yu (Publ. Math. Debrecen 104 (2024)) proves that every large n is a sum of
distinct numbers p^a q^b, all greater than c n/(log n)^(1+eps); refereed.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/claims/2026_09_03_song_yue|2026_09_03_song_yue]]: Claims that for coprime p and q above one the numbers p to the a times q to
the b with b at most 2p minus 3 already form a complete set, a quantitative
strengthening of the Erdős–Birch theorem; posted in 2026 and unreviewed.
