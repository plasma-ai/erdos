# problems/diophantine_problems/E0363/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0363/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0363/claims/2003_01_01_skalba|2003_01_01_skalba]]: Disproves the finiteness question as worded, with the number of blocks free:
disjoint blocks of four consecutive positive integers have a square product
infinitely often; refereed in 2003.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0363/claims/2005_01_01_ulas|2005_01_01_ulas]]: Disproves the finiteness question by exhibiting infinitely many collections
of n disjoint blocks of four consecutive integers whose product is a square,
for n equal to four and every n at least six; refereed in 2005.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0363/claims/2007_01_01_bauer_bennett|2007_01_01_bauer_bennett]]: Disproves the finiteness question with infinitely many collections of n
disjoint blocks of four consecutive integers with square product for every
n at least three, completing the cases Ulas left; refereed in 2007.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0363/claims/2012_03_01_bennett_van_luijk|2012_03_01_bennett_van_luijk]]: Disproves the finiteness question with infinitely many collections of r
disjoint blocks of five consecutive integers with square product for every
r at least five; refereed in 2012.
