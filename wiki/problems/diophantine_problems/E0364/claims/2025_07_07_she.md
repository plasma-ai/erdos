***

**Claim.** Theorem 1 of J. She, Nonexistence of consecutive powerful
triplets around cubes with prime-square factors, Integers 25 (2025), Paper
No. A103: there are no consecutive powerful numbers $x^3-1=p^2a^3$, $x^3$,
$x^3+1=q^2b^3$ with $p,q$ prime and $a,b,x$ integers. Corollary 1 of the
paper: for primes $p,q$ and integers $x,a$ with $a\neq0$, the equation
$x^6-1=p^2q^2a^3$ has no solution. The proof is a case analysis on the
common factors of $x\mp1$ and $x^2\pm x+1$, using the $3$-adic valuation,
Thue equations and the integer points of the Mordell curve $y^2=x^3-432$
([[../library/diophantine_problems/she_2025_nonexistence_consecutive_powerful_triplets_around_cubes/_index|card]]).
The page is dated by the arXiv posting of 2025-07-07; the journal published
the paper on 2025-11-25.

**Covers.** Triples centered at a cube whose outer members are both a prime
squared times a cube, a partial no to the question of
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0364/_index|Problem 364]]. Triples of other
shapes, and those whose middle member is not a cube, are untouched.

**Acceptance.** Refereed in Integers (received 2025-07-09, accepted
2025-10-22): the `refereed` evidence. The site labels the problem
VERIFIABLE, an open label, so its commentary's mention of the paper is not
`reviewed` evidence.
