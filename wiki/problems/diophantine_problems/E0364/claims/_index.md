# problems/diophantine_problems/E0364/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0364/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0364/claims/2025_01_17_chan|2025_01_17_chan]]: Chan proves that no three consecutive powerful numbers have the form
x^3-1 = p^3 y^2, x^3, x^3+1 = q^3 z^2 with p, q prime; a partial no,
refereed in Integers (2025).

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0364/claims/2025_07_07_she|2025_07_07_she]]: She proves that no three consecutive powerful numbers have the form
x^3-1 = p^2 a^3, x^3, x^3+1 = q^2 b^3 with p, q prime; a partial no,
refereed in Integers (2025).

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0364/claims/2026_06_12_sayim|2026_06_12_sayim]]: Sayim claims that no triple of consecutive powerful numbers centered at a
cube has outer members of the mixed shapes p^2 a^3 and q^3 z^2, the two cases
Chan and She left open; a partial result that leaves the question open.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0364/claims/2026_08_24_ma|2026_08_24_ma]]: Ma claims that no three consecutive powerful numbers have the form
x^n-1 = q1^3 y^2, x^n, x^n+1 = q2^3 z^2 with q1, q2 prime and n at least 5
built from primes congruent to 5 mod 8; a partial no, unrefereed.
