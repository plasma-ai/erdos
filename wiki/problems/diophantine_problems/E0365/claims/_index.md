# problems/diophantine_problems/E0365/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0365/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0365/claims/1970_10_01_golomb|1970_10_01_golomb]]: Golomb's observation that 12167 = 23^3 and 12168 = 2^3 3^2 13^2 are
consecutive powerful numbers with neither a square, answering the first
question no; refereed in the American Mathematical Monthly.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0365/claims/1976_04_01_walker|1976_04_01_walker]]: Walker's 1976 theorem that the equation 7^3 x^2 = 3^3 y^2 + 1 has
infinitely many solutions, each giving two consecutive powerful numbers
with neither a square; refereed in the Fibonacci Quarterly.
