# Problem 388

[[problems/diophantine_problems/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Can one classify all solutions of

$$
\prod_{1\leq i\leq k_1}(m_1+i)=\prod_{1\leq j\leq k_2}(m_2+j)
$$

where $k_1,k_2>3$ and $m_1+k_1\leq m_2$? Are there only finitely many solutions?

**Status.** Open.

**Source.** [erdosproblems.com/388](https://www.erdosproblems.com/388), accessed
2026-09-04. Cite as: T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problem #388,
https://www.erdosproblems.com/388.
