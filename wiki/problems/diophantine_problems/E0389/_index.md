# Problem 389

[[problems/diophantine_problems/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Is it true that for every $n\geq 1$ there is a $k$ such that

$$
n(n+1)\cdots(n+k-1)\mid (n+k)\cdots (n+2k-1)?
$$
