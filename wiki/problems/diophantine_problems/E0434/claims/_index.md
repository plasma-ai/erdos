# problems/diophantine_problems/E0434/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0434/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0434/claims/2003_02_13_kiss|2003_02_13_kiss]]: Kiss proves that among coprime k-element subsets of the first n integers,
k at least 2, the set of the k largest leaves the most positive integers
without a representation, which answers Problem 434 affirmatively.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0434/claims/2026_02_24_joshuab|2026_02_24_joshuab]]: An AI-assisted Lean proof, posted to the site's forum, that the k largest
integers leave the most gaps for k between 2 and n, arguing interval by
interval from Dixmier's Theorem 2 rather than by Kiss's count.
