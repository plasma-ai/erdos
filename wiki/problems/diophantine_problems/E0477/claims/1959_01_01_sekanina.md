***

**Claim.** M. Sekanina, *Notes on the factorisation of infinite cyclic
groups* (in Russian), Czechoslovak Math. J. 9 (84) (1959), no. 4, 485-495.
Remark 1.7 (p. 490) proves that the set of squares $\{n^2:n\ge0\}$, the full
image of $f(X)=X^2$, is not a direct factor of $\mathbb Z$: there is no
$A\subseteq\mathbb Z$ such that every integer is uniquely $a+n^2$ with
$a\in A$ and $n\ge0$. The proof uses that $x^2-y^2=k$ is solvable exactly
when $k\not\equiv2\pmod 4$. The same page leaves the higher powers
$\{n^k:n\ge0\}$, $k>2$, unresolved, the question that the formal-conjectures
monomial variant of
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0477/_index|Problem 477]] records. Erdős and
Graham cite the paper in the problem itself. The journal and Crossref
records give no issue month, so the page carries 1 January 1959.

**Covers.** $f(X)=X^2$: the squares admit no tiling complement.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki; the claim is the cited paper's remark.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: Czechoslovak Mathematical Journal 9 (1959).
