# Problem 829

[[problems/diophantine_problems/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Let $A\subset\mathbb{N}$ be the set of cubes. Is it true that

$$
1_A\ast 1_A(n) \ll (\log n)^{O(1)}?
$$

**Status.** Open.
