# Problem 913

[[problems/diophantine_problems/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Are there infinitely many $n$ such that if

$$
n(n+1) = \prod_i p_i^{k_i}
$$

is the factorisation into distinct primes then all exponents $k_i$ are distinct?

**Status.** Open.
