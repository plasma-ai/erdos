# problems/diophantine_problems/E0936/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0936/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0936/claims/2016_11_03_cushing_pascoe|2016_11_03_cushing_pascoe]]: Cushing and Pascoe show that, assuming the abc conjecture, only finitely
many powerful numbers lie within a fixed distance of a factorial, which
answers the factorial half of Problem 936 conditionally.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0936/claims/2020_05_15_crowdmath|2020_05_15_crowdmath]]: CrowdMath shows that, assuming the abc conjecture, k^n + r is powerful only
finitely often for fixed coprime positive k and r, which covers 2^n + 1 and,
through (n!)^r + k, also n! + 1; the paper does not treat 2^n - 1.
