# Problem 938

[[problems/diophantine_problems/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Let $A=\{n_1<n_2<\cdots\}$ be the sequence of powerful numbers
(if $p\mid n$ then $p^2\mid n$).

Are there only finitely many three-term progressions of consecutive terms
$n_k,n_{k+1},n_{k+2}$?

**Status.** Open.

**Source.** [erdosproblems.com/938](https://www.erdosproblems.com/938), accessed
2026-09-04. Cite as: T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problem #938,
https://www.erdosproblems.com/938.
