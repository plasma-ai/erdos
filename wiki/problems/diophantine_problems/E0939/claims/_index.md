# problems/diophantine_problems/E0939/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0939/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0939/claims/1995_07_01_nitaj|1995_07_01_nitaj]]: Nitaj (Bull. London Math. Soc. 1995) proves that a + b = c has infinitely
many solutions in coprime 3-powerful numbers, answering the third question
yes with three explicit infinite families; refereed.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0939/claims/1998_01_01_cohn|1998_01_01_cohn]]: Cohn (Math. Comp. 1998) proves that a + b = c has infinitely many solutions
in pairwise coprime 3-powerful numbers none of which is a perfect cube,
answering the third question yes; refereed.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0939/claims/2024_04_05_walsh|2024_04_05_walsh]]: Walsh (Rad HAZU Mat. Znan. 2024) proves that if Y^2 = X^3 - 432p^2 has
positive rank for an odd prime p, then x^3 + y^3 = p^4 z^3 has infinitely
many pairwise coprime solutions; refereed, conditional on the rank.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0939/claims/2026_05_24_price|2026_05_24_price]]: A manuscript shared by Liam Price, with GPT-5.5 Pro on its author line,
proves infinitely many r-powerful sums of r-2 distinct jointly coprime
r-powerful numbers for every r at least 6; unrefereed.
