***

**Claim.** V. Y. Wang, *Sums of cubes and the Ratios Conjectures*,
arXiv:2108.03398v2 (19 April 2023), Theorem 1.3 (source card
[[../library/diophantine_problems/wang_2021_sums_cubes_ratios_conjectures/_index|wang_2021_sums_cubes_ratios_conjectures]]):
assume Conjectures 1.2, 1.4 and 1.5; then $N_F(X)\ll X^3$ for
$F=x_1^3+\cdots+x_6^3$, and, with $F_0=x^3+y^3+z^3$, "Let
$S\subseteq\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$. If $S$ has positive lower density in
$\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$, then so does $F_0(S^3)$." With
$S=\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$, the integers $x^3+y^3+z^3$ with $x,y,z\ge0$ have
positive lower density. Each of them other than $0$ is a sum of at most
three positive cubes, and every positive cube is $3$-powerful, so under the
three conjectures the integers that are sums of at most three $3$-powerful
numbers have positive lower density and do not have density $0$. That
answers the second question of
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0940/_index|Problem 940]] no at $r=3$,
conditionally. The first version of the preprint, posted on 7 August 2021
as *Approaching cubic Diophantine statistics via mean-value $L$-function
conjectures of Random Matrix Theory type*, already states in its abstract
that, under its hypotheses (1)--(4), a positive fraction of integers lie in
$\{x^3+y^3+z^3:x,y,z\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}\}$.

**Hypotheses.** As the second version numbers them: Conjecture 1.2 (HW2),
automorphy, and no zeros in $\operatorname{Re}(s)>1/2$, for the Hasse--Weil
$L$-functions of its list (1.7); Conjecture 1.4 (R2$'$), a log-free
second-moment bound of Ratios type, display (1.10); and Conjecture 1.5, a
square-free sieve conjecture for the discriminant polynomial. All three are
unproved, so the result is conditional and settles no case of the problem.

**Scope.** The result concerns $r=3$ and the density question only; it says
nothing about the first question or about any $r\ge4$. The
formal-conjectures statement
[`erdos_940`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/6fbb54f24ccc2e64dcfaffc28c58950e377110d2/FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/940.lean)
asks whether the representable set has density $0$ for every $r\ge3$ at
once, and its variant `erdos_940.variants.three_cubes` asks it for sums of
at most three nonnegative cubes; under the three conjectures both are
answered no.

**Acceptance.** None. The result is an arXiv preprint with no journal
version known, its hypotheses are unproved, and the site does not credit
it: its commentary (page last edited 3 November 2025) says that at $r=3$ it
is not even known whether the sums of at most three cubes have density $0$.
