# Problem 943

[[problems/diophantine_problems/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Let $A$ be the set of powerful numbers (if $p\mid n$ then
$p^2\mid n$). Is it true that

$$
1_A\ast 1_A(n)=n^{o(1)}
$$

for every $n$?
