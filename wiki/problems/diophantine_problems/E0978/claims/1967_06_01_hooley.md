***

**Claim.** Let $f\in\mathbb{Z}[x]$ be irreducible of degree $k\ge3$. Hooley
proved that the number of positive integers $n\le x$ with $f(n)$
$(k-1)$-power-free is asymptotic to $c_{f,k-1}\,x$, where

$$
c_{f,k-1}=\prod_p\left(1-\frac{\rho_f(p^{k-1})}{p^{k-1}}\right)
$$

and $\rho_f(q)$ counts the residues $a$ modulo $q$ with $f(a)\equiv0\pmod q$:
the asymptotic Ricci had proved at exponents $k$ and above, now at the
exponent $k-1$ at which
[[../library/diophantine_problems/erdos_1953_arithmetical_properties_polynomials/_index|Erdős 1953]]
had proved infinitude. This is what the first question of
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0978/_index|Problem 978]] asks for: the
$n\ge1$ with $f(n)$ $(k-1)$-power-free have positive natural density exactly
when $c_{f,k-1}>0$, that is, when no prime $p$ has $p^{k-1}$ dividing every
value of $f$. Under the question's hypotheses the constant is positive: an
irreducible element of $\mathbb{Z}[x]$ is primitive, the fixed divisor of a
primitive polynomial of degree $k$ divides $k!$, and $v_p(k!)\ge k-1$ holds
only for $p=2$ with $k$ a power of $2$, the case the statement excludes (and
Erdős's example $k!\bigl(\binom xk+1\bigr)$ shows that it occurs). The sign
of the leading coefficient plays no role, since power-freeness is a property
of $|f(n)|$.

**Covers.** The first question of Problem 978 (the part `k_minus_1_density`),
answered yes: for irreducible $f\in\mathbb{Z}[x]$ of degree $k\ge3$ with $k$
not a power of $2$, the set of $n\ge1$ with $f(n)$ $(k-1)$-power-free has
positive natural density $c_{f,k-1}$. Not covered: the second and third
questions, at exponent $k-2$, which
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0978/claims/2026_09_24_openai|OpenAI's density theorem]]
settles.

**Source.** C. Hooley, On the power free values of polynomials, Mathematika 14
(1967), 21--26, DOI 10.1112/S002557930000797X. The paper is not held in the
library. The statement above follows the site's remark, the survey in the
release manuscript carded as
[[../library/diophantine_problems/openai_2026_squarefree_values_quartics_power_free_values_polynomials/_index|OpenAI 2026]],
whose introduction cites Erdős 1953 for infinitude and Hooley 1967 for the
asymptotic at exponent $k-1$, and the introduction of Heath-Brown, Power-free
values of polynomials, Quart. J. Math. 64 (2013), 177--188 (arXiv:1103.2028v1),
which states the asymptotic $N_{f,k}(x)\sim C(f,k)\,x$ with the Euler-product
constant and records that Hooley obtained it for $k=d-1$, $d\ge3$. The paper
appeared in volume 14, issue 1, dated June 1967, so this page is dated the first
of June 1967.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: the paper appeared in Mathematika, a journal. The
curator of erdosproblems.com, T. F. Bloom, states in the problem's remark that
Hooley settled the first question, with a precise asymptotic for the number of
such $n\le x$; since the site labels the problem OPEN (page last edited 31 March
2026), that remark is commentary and is not counted as review. Nothing on this
page is independently reviewed by this project.
