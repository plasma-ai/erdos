***

**Claim.** N. A. Carella, *Squarefree Values Of Polynomials*, arXiv:2310.16952v1
(25 October 2023, math.GM). Its Theorems 1.1, 1.2 and 1.3 claim that each of the
quartics $T^4+1$, $T^4+2$ and $T^4-2T^2+2$ takes infinitely many squarefree
values, with an asymptotic formula $c_f x+O(x^{1/2+\varepsilon})$ for the
number of squarefree values at $x\le n\le2x$. Theorem 1.2 is the case $n^4+2$
that Problem 978 names, which the preprint describes as an open question.

**Covers.** The third question of
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0978/_index|Problem 978]], claimed yes. The
three quartics are also degree-$4$ instances of the second question, at
exponent $k-2=2$.

**Read depth.** The statements of Theorems 1.1--1.3 are read in the arXiv
version; the proofs are not checked here.

**Standing.** The preprint has no journal reference and no outside review is
recorded, so the claim stays claimed. The release manuscript carded as
[[../library/diophantine_problems/openai_2026_squarefree_values_quartics_power_free_values_polynomials/_index|OpenAI 2026]]
cites the preprint and does not use it as an input; the third question is
settled by
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0978/claims/2026_09_24_openai|OpenAI's density theorem]].
