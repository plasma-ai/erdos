# problems/diophantine_problems/E0979/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0979/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0979/claims/1937_04_01_erdos|1937_04_01_erdos]]: Erdős proves that infinitely many n have more than n^(c/(log log n)^2)
representations as a sum of two squares of primes, sharpened to
n^(c/log log n) in Part II, so limsup f_2(n) is infinite; refereed.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0979/claims/1965_01_01_erdos|1965_01_01_erdos]]: Erdős's 1965 statement that he could also prove limsup f_3(n) = infinity
for sums of three cubes of primes, in unpublished work that seemed to need
special properties of primes; no proof by him has appeared.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0979/claims/2026_08_17_kitamura|2026_08_17_kitamura]]: An independent Lean 4 proof that the number of representations of n as a
sum of three cubes of primes is unbounded, the case k = 3; registered as
the formal-conjectures proof of that variant, not built here.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0979/claims/2026_08_18_wang_zhang|2026_08_18_wang_zhang]]: A preprint proving unconditionally that some integers have arbitrarily many
representations as sums of three cubes of primes, the case k = 3, through
Hecke equidistribution for the Fermat cubic; not refereed.
