***

**Claim.** Let $A(x)$ count the integers $n\le x$ that are sums of two
squarefull numbers. There are constants $a,\beta,\gamma>0$ with

$$
A(x) > a\,\frac{x}{\sqrt{\log x}}
\exp\!\left(\beta\,\frac{\log\log x}{\log\log\log x}\right)
\qquad (x>\gamma),
$$

so $A(x)\sqrt{\log x}/x\to\infty$ and $A(x)$ is not asymptotic to
$c\,x/\sqrt{\log x}$ for any $c>0$. This disproves
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E1081/_index|Problem 1081]]. The result is
Theorem 1 of R. W. K. Odoni, *A problem of Erdős on sums of two squarefull
numbers*, Acta Arith. 39 (1981), 145–162; only its publication year is known,
so the page is dated to the start of 1981. The site's remark prints Odoni's
extra factor with the ratio inverted, as
$\exp(c\log\log\log x/\log\log x)$, which tends to $1$ and would not refute
the asymptotic; this page follows Theorem 1 of the paper. The source card is
[[../library/diophantine_problems/odoni_1981_problem_erdos_sums_two_squarefull_numbers/_index|odoni_1981_problem_erdos_sums_two_squarefull_numbers]].

**Method.** Every value $m^3x^2+n^3y^2$ with $x,y\geq1$ and $\gcd(m,n)=1$ is
a sum of two squarefull numbers, so a lower bound for the count of integers
these forms represent is a lower bound for $A(x)$; the reverse inclusion
fails ($16=8+8$ is a value of no such form), and the argument does not need
it. Odoni restricts to the forms with $m,n$ primes $p<q$ in a window
depending on $x$, where the genus theory of discriminant $-4p^3q^3$ is
tractable, proves a lower bound of order $x/\sqrt{\log x}$ for each such form
uniformly (Theorem 2), bounds the pairwise overlaps (Theorem 3), and combines
them by inclusion–exclusion.

**Later refinements.** Baker and Brüdern (Math. Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc.
1994) proved the upper bound $A(x)\ll x(\log x)^{\varepsilon-1/6}$ for every
$\varepsilon>0$. Blomer (J. Reine Angew. Math. 2004) proved
$x(\log x)^{-0.253}\ll A(x)\ll x(\log x)^{-1/6}\log\log x$, and his sequel
(J. London Math. Soc. 2005) proved $A(x)=x/(\log x)^{1-2^{-1/3}+o(1)}$.
Blomer and Granville (Duke Math. J. 2006, Corollary 2 on the card
[[../library/diophantine_problems/blomer_2006_estimates_representation_numbers_quadratic_forms/_index|blomer_2006_estimates_representation_numbers_quadratic_forms]])
pinned $A(x)$ to $x/(\log x)^{1-2^{-1/3}}$ up to powers of $\log\log x$,
with $1-2^{-1/3}\approx0.2063$. The lower bounds of Blomer (2004) and of
Blomer and Granville each refute the asymptotic on their own, and each has
its own claim page:
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E1081/claims/2004_01_30_blomer|Blomer 2004]]
and
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E1081/claims/2006_11_01_blomer_granville|Blomer and Granville 2006]].

**Acceptance.** The paper is refereed: Acta Arith. 39 (1981). The site's
curator, Thomas Bloom, marks Problem 1081 disproved and credits Odoni's paper
for the disproof.
