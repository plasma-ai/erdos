***

**Claim.** The Theorem and Corollary of W. M. Schmidt, Irregularities of
distribution. VI, Compositio Math. 24 (1972), 63–74 (p. 64). For a sequence
$\xi_1,\xi_2,\ldots$ in the unit interval let $Z(n,\alpha)$ count the $i\le n$
with $0\le\xi_i<\alpha$, let $D(n,\alpha)=\lvert Z(n,\alpha)-n\alpha\rvert$,
and for $\kappa\ge0$ let $S(\kappa)$ be the set of $\alpha$ with
$D(n,\alpha)\le\kappa$ for every $n$. If $d>4\kappa$, then the $d$-th derived
set of $S(\kappa)$ is empty. Hence each $S(\kappa)$ is at most countable and
nowhere dense, and $S(\infty)=\bigcup_\kappa S(\kappa)$, the set of anchors
$\alpha$ at which $D(n,\alpha)$ stays bounded in $n$, is at most countable.
So for every sequence all but countably many anchored intervals $[0,\alpha)$
have $\limsup_N\lvert D_N([0,\alpha))\rvert=\infty$, which answers
[[problems/discrepancy/E0255/_index|Problem 255]] yes and sharpens the
measure-zero statement of part I of the series
([[problems/discrepancy/E0255/claims/1968_01_01_schmidt|Schmidt 1968]]),
which already answers the question. The paper's closing example, a sequence
for which the $d$-th derived set of $S(d)$ is nonempty for every $d$, shows
that $4\kappa$ cannot be lowered to $\kappa-\varepsilon$. The source card is
[[../library/discrepancy/schmidt_1972_irregularities_distribution/_index|Schmidt 1972]].
