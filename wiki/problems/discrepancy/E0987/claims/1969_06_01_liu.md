***

**Claim.** The Theorem of M.-C. Liu, On a problem of Erdős, Proc. Amer.
Math. Soc. 21 (1969), 706--710 (p. 706): if a sequence $z_1,z_2,\ldots$ on
the unit circle takes only finitely many distinct values, then for every
$\delta>0$, $\limsup_{k\to\infty}A_k/k^{1-\delta}>1/5$, where
$A_k=\limsup_n\lvert\sum_{\nu\le n}z_\nu^k\rvert$ (Liu's $B_k$). With
$z_j=e(x_j)$ this answers the first question of
[[problems/discrepancy/E0987/_index|Problem 987]] yes for every sequence in
$(0,1)$ with finitely many distinct terms, with $A_k>k^{1-\delta}/5$ for
infinitely many $k$. The site's commentary adds that Clunie's MathSciNet
review of the paper (MR0245795) observes that $A_k$ is infinite for
infinitely many $k$ under the same hypothesis. formal-conjectures states
that observation as `erdos_987.variants.finite_distinct_points`, which the
contributor's fork, linked on the other claim pages, proves.

**Covers.** The first question for sequences with finitely many distinct
values. The question for every sequence is answered on
[[problems/discrepancy/E0987/claims/1965_03_01_erdos|Erdős's]] and
[[problems/discrepancy/E0987/claims/1967_01_01_clunie|Clunie's]] claim
pages.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki; the result rests on the cited paper
alone.

**Dating.** The paper appeared in volume 21, number 3, the June 1969 issue;
the day in the page name is a placeholder.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 21 (1969), no. 3,
706--710. The site's commentary credits Liu under its key [Li69], which its
reference record resolves to a different paper; the problem page's
reference [Liu69] records the publisher's citation.
