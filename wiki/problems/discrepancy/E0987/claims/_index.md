# problems/discrepancy/E0987/claims

[[problems/discrepancy/E0987/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrepancy/E0987/claims/1965_03_01_erdos|1965_03_01_erdos]]: Erdős's 1965 theorem that every sequence has $A_k>c\log k$ for infinitely
many $k$, so $\limsup_k A_k=\infty$; the first answer to the first question,
refereed in the Israel Journal of Mathematics and credited by the curator.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0987/claims/1967_01_01_clunie|1967_01_01_clunie]]: Clunie's 1967 theorem that every sequence has $A_k\gg k^{1/2}$ for
infinitely many $k$, so $\limsup_k A_k=\infty$; it answers the first
question and is refereed in the Journal of the London Mathematical Society.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0987/claims/1969_06_01_liu|1969_06_01_liu]]: Liu's 1969 theorem that a sequence taking finitely many distinct values has
$\limsup_kA_k/k^{1-\delta}>1/5$ for every $\delta>0$; the first question for
such sequences, refereed in Proc. Amer. Math. Soc.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0987/claims/2025_08_30_tao|2025_08_30_tao]]: Tao's 2025 forum proof that $\limsup_k A_k=\infty$ for every sequence, with
a Lean 4 formalization in his analysis repository, not built here; a reproof
of the first question's answer, credited by the site's curator.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0987/claims/2026_04_08_alexeev_putterman_sawhney_sellke_valiant|2026_04_08_alexeev_putterman_sawhney_sellke_valiant]]: Alexeev, Putterman, Sawhney, Sellke and Valiant's 2026 theorem, due to an
internal OpenAI model, that some sequence has $A_k\ll\sqrt{k\log 2k}=o(k)$
for all $k$, answering the second question; accepted by the site's curator.
