# problems/discrepancy/E0991/claims

[[problems/discrepancy/E0991/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrepancy/E0991/claims/2008_02_06_brauchart|2008_02_06_brauchart]]: Brauchart's bound of order n^{-1/4} on the spherical cap discrepancy of the
n-point maximizers of the product of distances on the sphere, so every cap
count deviates from its area share by O(n^{3/4}); refereed in Math. Comp.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0991/claims/2019_07_10_marzo_mas|2019_07_10_marzo_mas]]: Marzo and Mas bound the cap discrepancy of the Riesz s-energy minimizers on
the d-sphere by explicit negative powers of n; for d = 2, s = 0 the maximizers
of the product of distances have cap counts within O(n^{2/3}) of area share.
