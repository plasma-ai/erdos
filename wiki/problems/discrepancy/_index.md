# Discrepancy

[[problems/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrepancy/E0067/_index|E0067/]]: Asks whether every function on the
naturals taking values plus and minus one has unbounded discrepancy: for every
bound, some step and length give a partial sum along the multiples of the step
that exceeds it.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0161/_index|E0161/]]: Asks whether the smallest size
forcing balanced two-colorings of a complete uniform hypergraph varies
continuously with the density parameter or jumps.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0162/_index|E0162/]]: Asks whether the size threshold
beyond which some two-coloring of K_n balances every large induced subgraph
grows like c log n; corrected to "smallest", it is the question of Problem 563,
which is open.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0176/_index|E0176/]]: Bounds the least N forcing every
plus-minus-one sign pattern to have a k-term arithmetic progression with partial
sum of absolute value at least a given size.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0177/_index|E0177/]]: The smallest bound, as a function
of the common difference, on the largest partial sum along arithmetic
progressions of a single plus-minus-one sign function.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0178/_index|E0178/]]: Asks whether one plus-minus-one
function can keep the initial partial sums along each of infinitely many
prescribed integer sequences bounded.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0255/_index|E0255/]]: Asks whether every infinite
sequence in the unit interval has some subinterval whose counting discrepancy is
unbounded.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0987/_index|E0987/]]: Concerns the limiting sizes of the
exponential sums of an infinite sequence in the unit interval taken at integer
frequencies.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0988/_index|E0988/]]: Concerns how small the spherical
cap discrepancy of a finite set of points on the unit sphere can be made.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0989/_index|E0989/]]: Concerns how slowly the
discrepancy of an infinite plane sequence, measured against circles of radius r
and their area, can grow.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0991/_index|E0991/]]: Concerns the distribution of the
point sets on the unit sphere that maximize the product of all pairwise
distances.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0992/_index|E0992/]]: Asks whether, for any increasing
integer sequence, the discrepancy of its multiples of alpha stays near the
square root of N for almost every alpha.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0994/_index|E0994/]]: Asks whether, for almost every
alpha, the fractional parts of its integer multiples visit every measurable set
in the unit interval with frequency its measure.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0995/_index|E0995/]]: Estimates the growth of the sums
of a square integrable function at the fractional parts of alpha times a
lacunary integer sequence, for almost every alpha.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0997/_index|E0997/]]: Asks whether the fractional parts
of alpha times the primes fail to be well distributed for every alpha.

[[problems/discrepancy/E1028/_index|E1028/]]: Estimates the least possible
maximum, over subsets of the first n integers, of the sum of a plus or minus one
valued function over the pairs inside that subset.

coloring must become along arithmetic progressions, dilates and set systems, and
irregularities of distribution of sequences and of graph colorings.
