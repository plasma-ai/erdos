**Claim.** Every red-blue coloring of the plane has two red points at
distance $1$ or six blue points $x,x+v,\ldots,x+5v$ with $|v|=1$, and some
red-blue coloring of the plane has no two red points at distance $1$ and no
blue progression of $60$ points with unit step. So the least avoiding length
$K_*$ of [[problems/discrete_geometry/E0188/_index|Problem 188]] satisfies
$7\le K_*\le60$. The publication is the deposit *Erdős Problem #188:
$7\le k\le60$, with code and certificates*, author Carson Crocker, a note
with its programs and certificates under CC BY 4.0, first deposited on
Zenodo on 7 October 2026. The second version of 9 October adds the note as
a separate PDF, and the fifth, of 15 October, makes text corrections that its
description says change no theorem, bound, certificate or program; the
first, second and fifth `preprint` links are those three versions.

**Covers.** Both bounds, and not the exact value of $K_*$. The lower bound
$7$ lies above the refereed bound $6$ of
[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0188/claims/2017_03_31_tsaturian|Tsaturian]],
and the upper bound $60$ below the pending bound $6330$ of
[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0188/claims/2026_06_15_currier_mody_xie_zhang|Currier, Mody, Xie and Zhang]].

**Proof.** As the deposit describes it, the upper bound is a periodic
coloring with $90$ red cells of several sizes per period, given by exact
rational data in the cell framework of Currier, Mody, Xie and Zhang; exact
rational arithmetic excludes a red unit pair, and two separately written
interval-arithmetic programs exclude a blue $60$-term unit progression. A
third verifier finds a blue $59$-term unit progression, so $60$ is exact for
this coloring. The lower bound follows the method of
[[../library/discrete_geometry/arman_2017_equally_spaced_collinear_points_euclidean_ramsey/_index|Arman and Tsaturian]],
with Tsaturian's planar argument as the model, and is finished by four SAT
certificates in DRAT and LRAT form, whose LRAT proofs also pass the verified
checker cake_lpr. The deposit credits Gabriel Currier with suggesting red
cells of several sizes, and states that the search, the programs and the
checks were produced with Claude Code, with ChatGPT as a reviewer.

**Discussion.** Carson Crocker described the result in an issue on this
repository on 17 October 2026 and said it would be submitted to
erdosproblems.ai; no submission there is known. In the same issue, on 18
October 2026, Alexander Richard Towell, with OpenAI Codex assistance, reported
separate exact checks of the two halves of the upper bound, the absence of a
red unit pair and the cover of every $60$-term unit progression, and found no
defect. Those checks were not blind and do not cover the lower bound.

**Standing.** Claimed. Neither the note nor its certificates were checked
here, no outside review is recorded, and erdosproblems.com does not mention
the result.
