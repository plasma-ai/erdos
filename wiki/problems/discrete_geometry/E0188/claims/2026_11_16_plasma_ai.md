***

**Claim.** Every red-blue coloring of the whole Euclidean plane, with no
regularity assumed, has two red points at distance $1$ or seven blue points
$x,x+v,\ldots,x+6v$ with $|v|=1$. There is a red-blue coloring of the plane
with no two red points at distance $1$ and no blue progression of $42$ points
with unit step, in any location or direction. So the least avoiding length
$K_*$ of [[problems/discrete_geometry/E0188/_index|Problem 188]] satisfies
$8\le K_*\le42$.

**Covers.** Both bounds, and not the exact value of $K_*$. The lower bound
$8$ lies above the refereed bound $6$ of
[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0188/claims/2017_03_31_tsaturian|Tsaturian]]
and the pending bound $7$ of
[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0188/claims/2026_10_07_crocker|Carson Crocker]];
the upper bound $42$ lies below the pending bound $6330$ of
[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0188/claims/2026_06_15_currier_mody_xie_zhang|Currier, Mody, Xie and Zhang]]
and Crocker's pending $60$. Crocker's bounds and these were proved by different
arguments, and the formalization repository's README states that its bounds were
obtained independently of Crocker's work.

**Proof.** The lower bound restricts a counterexample to the algebraic plane
and averages over a translation- and rotation-invariant measure on its
labelings. A fourteen-point pattern inequality, checked over all $210$
admissible patterns, bounds the red-red correlation; the spectral measure of
the red indicator is radial on the ordinary characters; and a nonnegative
Bessel kernel $F(t)=191+\sum_n C_nJ_0(\sqrt n\,t)$, certified on $[0,81]$ by
rational polynomial approximation and beyond by an energy bound, gives the
contradiction. The spectral rigidity theorem and the invariant mean come from
OpenAI's manuscript
[[../library/discrete_geometry/openai_2026_euclidean_plane_not_five_colorable/_index|*The Euclidean plane is not five-colorable*]]
(OpenAI Math Release, September 23, 2026) and its Lean formalization. The
upper bound is an explicit red set: the closed disks of finite families of
rational records on a triangular lattice of scale $21/100$, repeated with
period $59$ and closed under rotation by $\pi/3$. Exact finite checks show
that the red set has no unit pair, and a covering certificate, which
subdivides a fundamental cell of starting points and a sector of directions
into closed cells, shows that every $42$-point unit progression meets a red
disk; no start or direction is sampled.

**Formalization.** The repository's `Challenge.lean` states both bounds against
the formal-conjectures definition of the admissible set, copied verbatim from
formal-conjectures at its commit of 2026-08-10, where the step of a progression
is any complex number of norm one, and `Solution.lean` proves them. The
upper-bound certificate is carried by 1518 generated modules, and the repository
includes the programs that generate them from its data and that data from the
construction's orbit tables. The repository's `formalization.yaml`, read
2026-10-29, records that the full development builds on Lean `v4.35.0-rc4`, that
both theorems depend only on `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`, and
that both programs reproduce the committed files byte for byte. It does not say
who ran these checks or when. It also names Jacob Parish as the formalization's
author and records the AI systems that produced it.

**Standing.** Claimed. By the repository's records the full development builds
and Comparator has not been run against `Challenge.lean`; no review of the
statements or of the argument is recorded, and the Lean proof is external, not
in this repository's Lean closure, so it confers no tier.

whose spectral rigidity theorem and invariant mean the proof uses in their Lean
form, from OpenAI's formalization. Nothing in this wiki.
