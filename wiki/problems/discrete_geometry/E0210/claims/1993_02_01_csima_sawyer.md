***

Csima and Sawyer prove that $f(n)\ge 6n/13$ for every $n\ge 8$: $n$ points in
the real plane, not all on one line, determine at least $6n/13$ ordinary lines,
lines through exactly two of the points. The paper is written in the dual
language of ordinary points in an arrangement of lines. This improves the
constant $3/7$ of
[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0210/claims/1958_01_01_kelly_moser|Kelly and Moser]]
for [[problems/discrete_geometry/E0210/_index|Problem 210]]; the exception
$n=7$ is the configuration where $3n/7$ is attained.

**Covers.** A linear lower bound with the constant $6/13$ for every $n\ge 8$.
It does not reach the sharp constant $1/2$, which
[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0210/claims/2012_08_23_green_tao|Green and Tao]]
later prove for large $n$.

The result is refereed: J. Csima and E. T. Sawyer, There exist $6n/13$ ordinary
points, Discrete Comput. Geom. 9 (1993), no. 2, 187-202. The site's page
credits this paper with the bound $6n/13$ for $n\ge 8$; its label, proved,
rests on the later result of Green and Tao, so the acceptance here stands on
the refereed publication. The paper is not held.
