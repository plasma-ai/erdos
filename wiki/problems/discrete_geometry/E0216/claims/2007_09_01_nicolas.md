***

**Claim.** There is an integer $N$ such that every set of $N$ points in the
plane in general position contains six points in convex position with no point
of the set inside their convex hull. In the notation of
[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0216/_index|Problem 216]], $g(6)$ exists.

**Covers.** The existence half of the question at $k=6$: $g(6)$ exists. The
proof gives no small bound on its value;
[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0216/claims/2007_09_11_gerken|Gerken]] proved the
same theorem independently, with the bound $g(6)\le 1717$, and
[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0216/claims/2024_03_01_heule_scheucher|Heule and Scheucher]]
later determined $g(6)=30$. Nothing is claimed about $k\ge7$, where $g(k)$
does not exist by
[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0216/claims/1983_12_01_horton|Horton's result]].

**Acceptance.** The paper is refereed: C. M. Nicolás, The empty hexagon
theorem, Discrete & Computational Geometry 38 (2007), no. 2, 389–397. The
site's remarks credit the existence of $g(6)$ to this paper and to Gerken's
independently; the site's label, disproved, rests on Horton's result, so that
remark is not acceptance of this partial claim.
