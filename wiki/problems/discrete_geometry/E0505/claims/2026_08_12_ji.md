Yibo Ji, *An AI Generated Counterexample to Borsuk Problem in Dimension
63*, arXiv:2608.12561v1, submitted 12 August 2026, 15 pages; the PDF's own
title line reads "An AI-Generated Counterexample to Borsuk's Problem in
Dimension 63". Theorem 6.2 claims a set $X$ of 321 points in
$\mathbb R^{63}$ of diameter $\sqrt8$, with squared distances 6 or 8 between
the original points and $8-2t$ or 8 from the added point, whose
smaller-diameter subsets have at most five points, so that at least 65 parts
are needed where the question allows $n+1=64$; rescaling by $1/\sqrt8$ gives
the unit-diameter form. The abstract says that ChatGPT using GPT-5.6 Sol
generated the example and the proof; Ji reports checking the result
personally, takes responsibility for that check and claims no originality,
and a first-page footnote says Ji's name appears as author only for
submission purposes. Appendix A prints a verification script. The corpus
records the statement at
[[../library/discrete_geometry/ji_2026_borsuk_dimension_63_claim/theorem_6_2|Theorem 6.2]]
of the source card.

**Withdrawal.** Version 2 of 14 August 2026 withdraws the submission. Its
comment says the submitter had found the counterexample already posted at
Grinsztajn's repository and on Nicholas Konz's page; it identifies no
mathematical error, and the record offers no v2 text. The claim therefore
stands as withdrawn, not refuted. The same construction, with its priority,
is recorded on
[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0505/claims/2026_05_27_grinsztajn|Grinsztajn's claim page]].
No run of the script or review of the proof is recorded.
