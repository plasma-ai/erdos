***

**Claim.** Let $C_k$ be the connective constant of
[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0528/_index|Problem 528]]. S. E. Alm, *Upper
bounds for the connective constant of self-avoiding walks*, gives a method
that bounds the connective constant of a lattice above by the largest
eigenvalue of a matrix indexed by short self-avoiding walks, for a class of
lattices that includes every lattice studied in connection with self-avoiding
walks, and applies it numerically. For the square lattice it proves

$$
C_2\le2.696,
$$

the bound as the paper's abstract states it (printed there as $\mu<2.696$);
the abstract also states $\mu<4.278$ for the triangular lattice and
$\mu<4.756$ for the simple cubic lattice, the latter being $C_3<4.756$ in the
problem's notation. Together with
[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0528/claims/1993_08_07_conway_guttmann|Conway
and Guttmann's lower bound]] this places $C_2$ in $[2.62,2.696]$.

**Covers.** The upper bound $C_2\le2.696$, and the upper bound $C_3<4.756$
that the same paper states. Not covered: any lower bound, any $k\ge4$, and
the value of $C_k$ for any $k$, which is not determined.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki; the claim rests on the cited paper.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: S. E. Alm, Upper bounds for the connective constant
of self-avoiding walks, Combin. Probab. Comput. 2 (1993), no. 2, 115--136. The
site's commentary records the square-lattice bound, but the site labels the
problem OPEN, so that remark is not acceptance of the problem and the page
lists no `reviewed` evidence. The proof is not compiled in this corpus.

**Dating.** The page is dated by the issue month in the publisher's record,
June 1993; the day is a placeholder.
