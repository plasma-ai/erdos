[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0529/claims/1987_12_01_slade|1987_12_01_slade]]: Slade's 1987 theorem gives, for every sufficiently large dimension, a
mean-square displacement asymptotic to Dn for the uniform n-step
self-avoiding walk, so d_k(n) << n^{1/2} for all large k; refereed.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0529/claims/1991_10_01_hara_slade|1991_10_01_hara_slade]]: Hara and Slade's theorem gives, for every k at least 5, mean-square
displacement Dn(1 + O(n^{-eps})) for the uniform n-step self-avoiding walk
on Z^k, so d_k(n) << n^{1/2}; announced 1991, proved in two 1992 papers.
