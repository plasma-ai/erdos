# Problem 588

[[problems/discrete_geometry/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Let $f_k(n)$ be minimal such that if $n$ points in $\mathbb{R}^2$
have no $k+1$ points on a line then there must be at most $f_k(n)$ many lines
containing at least $k$ points. Is it true that

$$
f_k(n)=o(n^2)
$$

for $k\geq 4$?

**Status.** Open.

**Source.** [erdosproblems.com/588](https://www.erdosproblems.com/588), accessed
2026-09-04. Cite as: T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problem #588,
https://www.erdosproblems.com/588.
