# problems/discrete_geometry/E0634/claims

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0634/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0634/claims/1991_08_01_snover_waiveris_williams|1991_08_01_snover_waiveris_williams]]: Snover, Waiveris and Williams classify the cuttings of a triangle into N
congruent copies similar to itself, so the values n^2 + m^2 and 3n^2 occur;
a refereed paper in Discrete Mathematics.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0634/claims/2009_01_01_soifer|2009_01_01_soifer]]: Soifer shows that some triangle can be cut into 2n^2, 3n^2, 6n^2 and
n^2 + m^2 congruent triangles, the constructions the site's remarks credit
and the later manuscripts call classical; a book chapter.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0634/claims/2018_11_23_beeson|2018_11_23_beeson]]: Beeson proves that no triangle can be cut into seven congruent triangles,
nor into eleven; the result the site's remarks credit through his talk
slides; a preprint without a journal record.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0634/claims/2025_12_27_zhang|2025_12_27_zhang]]: Zhang proves that for an integer-sided tile with sides a, b and
c = sqrt(a^2 + ab + b^2) an equilateral triangle can be cut into m^2 ab
congruent copies for every m above an explicit threshold; a preprint.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0634/claims/2026_06_27_bonfioli|2026_06_27_bonfioli]]: A manuscript excluding every prime 7 mod 12, 19 among them, determining
every n up to 80 by exact search, and leaving the primes 11 mod 12 open
under an unproved hypothesis; disputes several published exclusions.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0634/claims/2026_07_17_george|2026_07_17_george]]: A write-up posted to the site's proof-claims thread asserting that no
triangle can be cut into 19 or 46 congruent triangles, nor into p of them
for any prime p > 3 with p congruent to 3 mod 4; unreviewed.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0634/claims/2026_07_24_harries|2026_07_24_harries]]: A manuscript posted to the site's proof-claims thread asserting that no
triangle is tiled by p congruent triangles for a prime p > 3 congruent to
3 mod 4, so the prime counts are 2, 3 and the primes 1 mod 4; unreviewed.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0634/claims/2026_07_26_beeson|2026_07_26_beeson]]: A preprint asserting that a triangle cut into N > 3 congruent triangles,
with N prime, is a reptiling, so that the prime counts are exactly 2, 3 and
the primes 1 mod 4; one of three contemporaneous prime proofs; unreviewed.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0634/claims/2026_07_27_harries|2026_07_27_harries]]: A manuscript giving exact certificates that 88 and 189 occur, so that 21n^2
occurs exactly for n at least 2, and a computer-assisted, independently
certified exclusion of 33; unreviewed.
