***

**Claim.** K. B. Chilakamarri, *A 4-chromatic unit-distance graph with no
triangles*, Geombinatorics 4 (1995), no. 3, 64–76, constructs an infinite
family of unit distance graphs in the plane with girth $4$ and chromatic
number $4$, the smallest on $47$ vertices, as the site's commentary on
[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0705/_index|Problem 705]] reports. If its point
sets have no unit distance besides the graphs' edges, no $k\le4$ makes every
finite unit distance graph of girth at least $k$ $3$-colorable.

**Covers.** No $k\le4$ works. Nothing is settled for $k\ge5$; Wormald's graph
of girth $5$ settles $k=5$
([[problems/discrete_geometry/E0705/claims/1979_08_01_wormald|Wormald 1979]]),
and O'Donnell's dissertation every $k$
([[problems/discrete_geometry/E0705/claims/1999_10_01_odonnell|O'Donnell 1999]]).

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki.

**Standing.** Claimed. The paper appeared in Geombinatorics, whose archive
lists it in the issue of January 1995 and holds no copy online. The problem
asks about the faithful graph of a point set, with an edge exactly when two
points are at distance $1$, and whether this paper's point sets carry no
unit distance besides the graphs' edges is not established from the paper,
so `refereed` is not listed. The curator's label credits O'Donnell's
dissertation, not this paper.

**Dating.** The journal's archive dates the issue January 1995; the day is a
placeholder.
