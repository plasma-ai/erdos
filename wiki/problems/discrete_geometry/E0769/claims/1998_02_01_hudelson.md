***

**Claim.** M. Hudelson, *Dissecting $d$-cubes into smaller $d$-cubes*, J.
Combin. Theory Ser. A 81 (1998), no. 2, 190--200, proves that
$c(d)=O((2d)^{d-1})$, where $c(d)$ is the least integer such that the unit
$d$-cube splits into $k$ homothetic cubes for every $k\ge c(d)$, and that
$c(d)<6^d$ whenever $\gcd(2^d-1,3^d-1)=1$; more generally
$c(d)=O((2k)^d)$ whenever $\gcd(2^d-1,k^d-1)=1$, and the paper derives
specific bounds for $d\le5$. The paper's abstract states these results, and
the zbMATH review of the paper (Zbl 0891.05018) states the general bound and
reports the paper's bound $c(4)\le809$. These are upper bounds on the quantity
that [[problems/discrete_geometry/E0769/_index|Problem 769]] asks to bound; the
general bound improves by a factor of order $n$ the bound
$(2^n-2)((n+1)^n-2)-1$ that Burgess and Erdős proved, which has order
$2^nn^n$.

**Covers.** The upper bound $c(n)\ll(2n)^{n-1}$ for every $n$, the bound
$c(n)<6^n$ under the stated gcd condition, and the bound $c(4)\le809$, as part
of the request for good bounds. Not covered: the order of $c(n)$ and the
question whether $c(n)\gg n^n$, which these upper bounds do not decide: the
bound $6^n$ would answer it no if infinitely many $n$ met the gcd condition,
which Erdős conjectured and which is not known.

and no `reviewed` is listed.
