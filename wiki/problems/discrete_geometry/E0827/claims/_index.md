[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0827/claims/2014_02_25_martinez_roldan_pensado|2014_02_25_martinez_roldan_pensado]]: Martínez and Roldán-Pensado repair Erdős's 1978 argument and prove that n_k
exists with n_k = O(k^9), and that n_4 is at most 9 and n_5 at most 37;

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0827/claims/2015_05_19_martinez_sandoval_raggi_roldan_pensado|2015_05_19_martinez_sandoval_raggi_roldan_pensado]]: Martínez-Sandoval, Raggi and Roldán-Pensado derive from a sunflower

concyclic; an arXiv manuscript.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0827/claims/2026_09_22_sallerk|2026_09_22_sallerk]]: A computer-assisted proof that n_4 = 7 under Erdős's general position, using
SAT search and ideal saturation in Singular, with lower bounds for n_5, n_6
and n_7; posted on the site's thread with a dated write-up.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0827/claims/2026_09_24_kiichi|2026_09_24_kiichi]]: A proof that n_4 = 7 under Erdős's general position by a classification of

on the site's thread with a paper dated 2026-10-03.
