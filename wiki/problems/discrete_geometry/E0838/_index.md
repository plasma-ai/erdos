# Problem 838

[[problems/discrete_geometry/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Let $f(n)$ be maximal such that any $n$ points in $\mathbb{R}^2$,
with no three on a line, determine at least $f(n)$ different convex subsets.
Estimate $f(n)$ - in particular, does there exist a constant $c$ such that

$$
\lim \frac{\log f(n)}{(\log n)^2}=c?
$$

**Status.** Open.

**Source.** [erdosproblems.com/838](https://www.erdosproblems.com/838), accessed
