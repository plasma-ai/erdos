# Discrete and Convex Geometry

[[problems/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0101/_index|E0101/]]: Asks whether n plane points
with no five on a line determine only a negligible fraction of n squared lines
containing exactly four points.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0102/_index|E0102/]]: Estimates the largest number
of collinear points forced when n plane points admit a constant times n squared
lines with more than three points each.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0104/_index|E0104/]]: Asks whether n points in the
plane lie on only a negligible fraction of n squared distinct unit circles
containing three or more of them.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0105/_index|E0105/]]: Asks whether disjoint plane
sets of n and n minus 3 points, the first not all collinear, admit a line
meeting two points of the first and none of the second.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0106/_index|E0106/]]: Asks whether the largest
total side length of interior-disjoint squares packed in the unit square equals
k when there are k squared plus one squares.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0107/_index|E0107/]]: Asks whether two to the
power n minus 2, plus one, points in the plane with no three collinear are
always enough to force a convex n-gon.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0173/_index|E0173/]]: Asks whether every
two-coloring of the plane contains a monochromatic congruent copy of every
triangle, with at most one exception.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0174/_index|E0174/]]: Characterizes the finite
sets of points that admit a monochromatic copy in every finite coloring of a
high enough dimensional Euclidean space; answered in 2026 by OpenAI's algebraic
criterion, which refutes Graham's conjecture.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0188/_index|E0188/]]: The least number of terms k
such that the plane can be two-colored avoiding red points at unit distance and
blue unit-spaced progressions of k terms; Erdős and Graham's question, with the
step left free, has no finite answer.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0189/_index|E0189/]]: Asks whether every finite
coloring of the plane has a color class containing the vertices of a rectangle
of every possible area.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0193/_index|E0193/]]: Asks whether an infinite
walk in the integer lattice of dimension three with steps from a finite set must
contain three collinear points.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0209/_index|E0209/]]: Asks whether at least four
non-parallel lines with no four meeting at a point must form a triangle whose
corners each lie on only two lines.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0210/_index|E0210/]]: Asks whether the least
number of lines through exactly two of n points, not all collinear, grows
without bound, and how fast.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0211/_index|E0211/]]: Asks whether n points in the
plane with at most n minus k on any line always determine at least a constant
times k times n lines through two or more points.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0215/_index|E0215/]]: Asks whether some planar set
has the property that every translated and rotated copy of it contains exactly
one integer lattice point.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0216/_index|E0216/]]: Asks whether enough points
in general position in the plane always contain the vertices of an empty convex
k-gon, and asks for an estimate of how many are needed.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0224/_index|E0224/]]: Asks whether any two to the
d plus one points in d-dimensional space must include three that form an obtuse
angle.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0352/_index|E0352/]]: Asks whether some positive
constant makes every planar measurable set of at least that measure contain the
vertices of a triangle of area one.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0353/_index|E0353/]]: Asks whether a planar
measurable set of infinite measure must contain the vertices of an isosceles
trapezoid of area one, or of other prescribed shapes.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0504/_index|E0504/]]: Determines the largest angle
that is guaranteed to appear among some three points of every set of n points in
the plane.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0505/_index|E0505/]]: Asks whether every set of
diameter one in n-dimensional space splits into at most n plus one pieces of
smaller diameter.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0506/_index|E0506/]]: Determines the least number
of circles determined by n points of the plane, not all on one circle or one
line (Elliott's reading); known for n > 393 since Purdy and Smith's correction;
a 2026 claim of every value is pending.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0507/_index|E0507/]]: Estimates the smallest area
such that every set of n points in the unit disk contains three points forming a
triangle of at most that area.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0508/_index|E0508/]]: Determines the fewest colors
needed to color the plane so that no two points at distance one share a color.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0526/_index|E0526/]]: Characterizes which
sequences of arc lengths tending to zero with infinite sum make random
independent arcs cover the whole unit circle with probability one.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0528/_index|E0528/]]: The value of the limiting
growth rate per step of the number of self-avoiding walks of n steps from the
origin in the k-dimensional integer lattice.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0529/_index|E0529/]]: Asks whether the expected
end-to-end distance of an n-step self-avoiding walk is of larger order than the
square root of n in the plane, and at most of that order in every dimension at
least three.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0588/_index|E0588/]]: Asks whether, for k at least
four, n points in the plane with no k plus one collinear have only a negligible
fraction of n squared lines through k points.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0589/_index|E0589/]]: Estimates the largest subset
with no three points on a line that can always be found inside n points in the
plane having no four points on a line.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0606/_index|E0606/]]: Asks which values can occur
as the number of distinct lines determined by n distinct points in the plane;
determined for all sufficiently large n; the small cases are open.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0607/_index|E0607/]]: Asks whether the number of
distinct sets of line sizes determined by n points in the plane is at most
exp(O(sqrt n)).

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0633/_index|E0633/]]: Characterizes the triangles
that can only be cut into a square number of congruent triangles.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0634/_index|E0634/]]: Determines all n for which
some triangle can be cut into n congruent triangles.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0651/_index|E0651/]]: Asks whether the number of
points in general position in k-dimensional space needed to guarantee n of them
in convex position grows exponentially in n.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0669/_index|E0669/]]: Bounds how many lines can
pass through at least k, or through exactly k, of n given points in the plane.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0704/_index|E0704/]]: Estimates the chromatic
number of the unit distance graph in n-dimensional space, whose edges join
points at distance exactly one.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0705/_index|E0705/]]: Asks whether some girth
bound forces every finite unit distance graph in the plane to be 3-colorable.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0733/_index|E0733/]]: Bounds by exp(O(n^{1/2}))
the number of nondecreasing sequences that can be the point counts of lines,
each through at least two of n points in the plane.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0735/_index|E0735/]]: Determines when n points in
the plane can be given positive weights so that every line through at least two
of them has the same total weight.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0755/_index|E0755/]]: Asks whether n points in
six-dimensional space span at most about one twenty-seventh of n cubed unit
equilateral triangles.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0769/_index|E0769/]]: Bounds the least k beyond
which the n-dimensional unit cube splits into k homothetic cubes, in particular
whether it grows at least like n to the power n.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0798/_index|E0798/]]: The fewest points in the n
by n grid of integers whose pairwise connecting lines cover every point of that
grid.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0827/_index|E0827/]]: Estimates how many points in
general position in the plane force k of them whose triples all determine
circles of distinct radii.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0831/_index|E0831/]]: Estimates the least number
of distinct radii among circles through three of n points in the plane, with no
three collinear and no four concyclic.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0838/_index|E0838/]]: Estimates the least number
of distinct convex subsets determined by n points in the plane with no three
collinear, in particular whether a certain limit exists.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0846/_index|E0846/]]: Asks what follows for an
infinite plane set in which every n of its points contain at least a fixed
proportion with no three collinear.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0898/_index|E0898/]]: Asks whether the sum of
distances from an interior point to a triangle's vertices is at least twice the
sum of its distances to the three sides.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0960/_index|E0960/]]: Determines how many ordinary
lines a set of n planar points with no k on a line must have to force r of the
points to span only ordinary lines.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1069/_index|E1069/]]: Bounds the lines containing
at least k of n plane points by n squared over k cubed for k up to root n; fails
as worded at k = 1, and Szemerédi and Trotter proved it for k from 2 to root n.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1070/_index|E1070/]]: Estimates the largest
guaranteed number of points, among any n points in the plane, with no two at
distance one, and whether it is at least n over four.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1071/_index|E1071/]]: Asks whether a finite family
of pairwise disjoint unit segments in the unit square can be maximal, so that no
further unit segment can be added.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1088/_index|E1088/]]: Estimates how many points in
d dimensions force n of them with all pairwise distances distinct, and whether
that count is subexponential in d.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1090/_index|E1090/]]: Asks whether, for each k at
least 3, some finite planar set has every two-coloring giving a line whose at
least k points of the set share one color.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1121/_index|E1121/]]: Asks whether circles in the
plane that no disjoint line separates can always be covered by a single circle
whose radius is the sum of their radii.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1124/_index|E1124/]]: Asks whether a square and a
circle of the same area can be cut into finitely many congruent pieces.
