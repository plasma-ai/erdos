***

**Claim.** The answer to [[problems/distance_problems/E0090/_index|Problem 90]]
is no, with the exponent $1.0152$. Proposition 2 of Michael T. M. Emmerich,
*Optimizing explicit unit-distance lower-bound certificates*, arXiv:2606.03419
(p. 17 of v5, 9 June 2026; already stated in v1 of 2 June 2026), states that,
assuming Sawin's explicit criterion is applied exactly as in Sawin's paper,
the report's Tailored Integer Evolution Strategy certificate supports
$u(n)>n^{1.0152}$ for arbitrarily large $n$, where $u(n)$ is the largest
number of unordered unit-distance pairs among $n$ planar points. The
certificate keeps Sawin's prime set $T=\{3,5,\ldots,43\}$ and changes the
prime set $S_{\mathbb Q}$, the multiplicities $k$ and the parameter
$R=6672416/100000$, all recorded on the
[[../library/discrete_geometry/emmerich_2026_optimizing_explicit_unit_distance_lower_bound_certificates/proposition_2|Proposition 2]]
page; its exponent gain is $\delta=0.0152616\ldots$. A fixed positive gain
along an unbounded sequence of $n$ exceeds $C/\log\log n$ for every fixed $C$
once $n$ is large, so the bound the problem asks about fails. The report is
carded at
[[../library/discrete_geometry/emmerich_2026_optimizing_explicit_unit_distance_lower_bound_certificates/_index|emmerich_2026_optimizing_explicit_unit_distance_lower_bound_certificates]].
Its abstract and Section 8 also report an Emmerich--Cordella certificate for
an extended prime set, $\#T=67$, with $u(n)>n^{1.031}$, deposited on Zenodo on
6 June 2026, whose data the report does not print. The report's declaration
says that OpenAI ChatGPT 5.5 was used as an auxiliary tool for programming,
code review, debugging and cross-checking, and not to generate proofs or
certificates.

**Depends on.**
[[problems/distance_problems/E0090/claims/2026_05_20_sawin|Sawin's claim page]]

12.

**Acceptance.** None. No journal record is known. The report's Remark 2
treats its sharper decimals as candidates until they are checked with
interval arithmetic and reviewed by an expert in the number-theoretic
construction, and the corpus has not replayed the certificate. The claim is
therefore claimed.
