# Problem 91

[[problems/distance_problems/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Let $n$ be a sufficiently large integer. Suppose $A\subset
\mathbb{R}^2$ has $\lvert A\rvert=n$ and minimises the number of distinct
distances between points in $A$. Prove that there are at least two (and probably
many) such $A$ which are non-similar.

**Status.** Open.
