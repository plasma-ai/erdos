***

**Claim.** The statement of
[[problems/distance_problems/E0093/_index|Problem 93]] is true. E. Altman,
*On a problem of P. Erdős*, Amer. Math. Monthly 70 (1963), no. 2, 148--157,
proves the Theorem of its p. 149, whose heading names Erdős as the proposer:
the vertices of any convex $n$-gon in the plane determine at least
$\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ distinct distances. The bound is attained, by the regular
$(2N+1)$-gon for odd $n$ and by a regular $(2N+1)$-gon with one vertex
removed for even $n$ (the Remark, p. 157). The proof takes, among the
diagonals of maximum length, one that cuts off the fewest consecutive sides,
$x$ of them; it splits the polygon into an $(x+1)$-gon in which that diagonal
is strictly the longest segment and an $(n-x+1)$-gon in which it is of
maximum length.
Lemma 2 (p. 151) gives at least $n-1$ distinct distances in a convex $n$-gon
with a strictly longest side and at least $n-2$ with a side of maximum
length, by repeated use of Lemma 1 (p. 149), which produces, from a diagonal
of a polygon whose side $A_1A_n$ is longest, a strictly shorter diagonal
sharing a vertex with it. The two parts supply at least $x$ and $n-x-1$
distances of the original polygon, and fewer than $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ in all
would force an impossible integer $x$. The theorem is paged at
[[../library/distance_problems/altman_1963_problem_p_erdos/theorem_p149|Theorem, p. 149]]
of the source card
[[../library/distance_problems/altman_1963_problem_p_erdos/_index|altman_1963_problem_p_erdos]],
which records the proof coverage.

**Acceptance.** The result is refereed: it appeared in the American
Mathematical Monthly. The site's curator, Thomas Bloom, marks the problem
proved and credits Altman with the solution (problem page last edited 19
October 2025). The corpus's proof coverage of the paper is local and is not
counted as acceptance evidence here.

**Formalization.** Boris Alexeev's `lean-proofs` repository holds, at the
pinned commit linked above, a Lean 4 file whose header declares it a
formalization of a solution to Problem 93 with Altman as the informal author
and, as formal authors, Gemini 3.0 Flash and Pro, Claude Opus 4.5 and 4.6,
the Numina Lean Agent, Aristotle and the site's forum user JoshuaB, who
announced it on the discussion thread on 17 February 2026 as a formalization
of Altman's paper; the site's label carries a Lean marker. Its theorem
`Erdos93.altman_erdos` takes a finite set `s` of `n ≥ 3` points in a real
inner product space of dimension two that is convex independent and
concludes that the set of distances between distinct points of `s` has at
least `n / 2` elements, natural-number division giving the floor; the cases
$n\le2$ are trivial. The file prints the axioms of that theorem as
`propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. This corpus has not built
the file, audited its axioms or reviewed its statement, so the file is a link
here and not `formalized` evidence.
