***

**Claim.** The statement of
[[problems/distance_problems/E0094/_index|Problem 94]] is true. On 15 January
2026 the forum account Dingding, signing for the SpringSense Innovation
Institute, posted that besides its formalization of Lefmann and Thiele's
argument, made with ChatGPT 5.2 Thinking and Codex, it had used Seed-Prover
to generate the sketches and fill in the detailed proofs; the file names no
informal author, so it is an independent proof. Its proof bounds the sum
$S(P)$ of the squared distance multiplicities of a finite planar set $P$ in
convex position with no three points collinear by $\tfrac34|P|^2(|P|-1)$,
through the Cauchy--Schwarz inequality and the count of isosceles triangles,
but its exported theorem `erdos94` states only that some constant $C\ge0$,
chosen after the set, has $S(P)\le C|P|^3$, which holds for every finite set.
