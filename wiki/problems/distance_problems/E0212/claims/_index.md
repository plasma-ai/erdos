# problems/distance_problems/E0212/claims

[[problems/distance_problems/E0212/_index|..]]

[[problems/distance_problems/E0212/claims/2014_12_20_tao|2014_12_20_tao]]: Tao's blog post of 20 December 2014 shows that the Bombieri-Lang conjecture
for surfaces over the rationals implies that no dense subset of the plane
has all pairwise distances rational; the hypothesis is unproven.

[[problems/distance_problems/E0212/claims/2014_12_31_shaffaf|2014_12_31_shaffaf]]: Shaffaf's refereed paper proves that the Bombieri-Lang conjecture implies
that no dense subset of the plane has all pairwise distances rational; the
hypothesis is unproven, so the page derives nothing for the standing.
