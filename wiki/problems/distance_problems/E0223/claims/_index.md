# problems/distance_problems/E0223/claims

[[problems/distance_problems/E0223/_index|..]]

[[problems/distance_problems/E0223/claims/1934_01_01_hopf_pannwitz|1934_01_01_hopf_pannwitz]]: Among $n$ points of the plane with diameter one, the distance one occurs at
most $n$ times, and $n$ is attained for $n\ge3$, so $f_2(n)=n$.

[[problems/distance_problems/E0223/claims/1956_01_01_grunbaum|1956_01_01_grunbaum]]: Among $n\ge4$ points of three-dimensional space with diameter one, the
distance one occurs at most $2n-2$ times, so $f_3(n)=2n-2$ for $n\ge4$.

[[problems/distance_problems/E0223/claims/1956_09_01_heppes|1956_09_01_heppes]]: Among $n\ge4$ points of three-dimensional space with diameter one, the
distance one occurs at most $2n-2$ times, so $f_3(n)=2n-2$ for $n\ge4$.

[[problems/distance_problems/E0223/claims/1957_01_01_straszewicz|1957_01_01_straszewicz]]: Among $n\ge4$ points of three-dimensional space with diameter one, the
distance one occurs at most $2n-2$ times, so $f_3(n)=2n-2$ for $n\ge4$.

[[problems/distance_problems/E0223/claims/1960_01_01_erdos|1960_01_01_erdos]]: For $d\ge4$ and $p=\lfloor d/2\rfloor$, the maximum number of diameters
among $n$ points of diameter one is $(\frac{p-1}{2p}+o(1))n^2$.

[[problems/distance_problems/E0223/claims/2007_07_02_swanepoel|2007_07_02_swanepoel]]: For $d\ge4$ and all $n$ large in terms of $d$, the $n$-point sets of
diameter one with the most pairs at distance one are Lenz configurations,
which gives the exact value of $f_d(n)$.
