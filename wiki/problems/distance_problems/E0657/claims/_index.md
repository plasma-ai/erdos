# problems/distance_problems/E0657/claims

[[problems/distance_problems/E0657/_index|..]]

[[problems/distance_problems/E0657/claims/2008_12_01_dumitrescu|2008_12_01_dumitrescu]]: Dumitrescu's 2008 note proves the problem's assertion for collinear sets:
n points on a line with no isosceles triple determine at least (log n)^c n
distinct distances, and some determine at most n 2^{O(sqrt(log n))}; refereed.

[[problems/distance_problems/E0657/claims/2025_08_19_hunter|2025_08_19_hunter]]: An observation credited to Zach Hunter in the site's commentary, with
details by Alfaiz and Tang in the thread, that collinear sets with no
isosceles triple determine at least 2^{c (log n)^{1/9}} n distances; pending.
