# problems/distance_problems/E0956/claims

[[problems/distance_problems/E0956/_index|..]]

[[problems/distance_problems/E0956/claims/1990_09_01_erdos_pach|1990_09_01_erdos_pach]]: Among n pairwise disjoint translates of one compact convex planar set, at
most O(n^(4/3)) pairs are at distance exactly one.

[[problems/distance_problems/E0956/claims/2006_03_31_valtr|2006_03_31_valtr]]: An Oberwolfach abstract of 2005 announces h(n) = Theta(n^(4/3)) for disjoint
translates of a compact convex set, also with centrally symmetric
translates, outlining the construction only in the talk.

[[problems/distance_problems/E0956/claims/2026_04_27_chojecki|2026_04_27_chojecki]]: A small parabolic-cap body gives n disjoint translates with at least c n^(4/3)
unit distances, so with Erdős and Pach h(n) = Theta(n^(4/3)) and any c < 1/3
answers the question.
