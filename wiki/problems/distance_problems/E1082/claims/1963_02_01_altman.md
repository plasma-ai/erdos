***

**Claim.** E. Altman, *On a problem of P. Erdős*, Amer. Math. Monthly 70
(1963), no. 2, 148–157, proves that the vertices of every convex $n$-gon in
the plane determine at least $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ distinct distances. Points
in strictly convex position have no three on a line, so the first question of
[[problems/distance_problems/E1082/_index|Problem 1082]] holds for every such
set. The theorem is the whole of
[[problems/distance_problems/E0093/_index|Problem 93]], of which the first
question is a stronger form.

**Covers.** The first question for sets in convex position, for every $n$.
Nothing for other sets. Nothing on the second question, whose convex case is
the open [[problems/distance_problems/E0982/_index|Problem 982]].

**Depends on.**
[[problems/distance_problems/E0093/claims/1963_02_01_altman|Altman's claim page on Problem 93]].

**Acceptance.** Refereed: the paper appeared in the American Mathematical
Monthly. Not reviewed: the site labels Problem 1082 FALSIFIABLE and says only
that it is a stronger form of Problem 93, so there is no curator credit for
this case; the curator's acceptance of the theorem as the solution of
Problem 93 is recorded on that problem's claim page.
