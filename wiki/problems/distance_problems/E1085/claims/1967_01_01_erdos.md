***

**Claim.** Let $d=2p\ge4$ be even and let $t_p(n)$ be the number of edges of
the complete $p$-partite Turán graph on $n$ vertices, so that
$t_p(n)=\frac{p-1}{2p}n^2+O(n)$. Theorem 1 of the paper states that for all
$n>n_0(d)$

$$
t_p(n)+n-p\le f_d(n)\le t_p(n)+n,
$$

with equality $f_d(n)=t_p(n)+n$ whenever $2d$ divides $n$, in the notation of
[[problems/distance_problems/E1085/_index|Problem 1085]]. This determines
$f_d(n)$ for every even $d\ge4$ up to an additive constant, and exactly along
the multiples of $2d$.

**Covers.** The estimate of $f_d(n)$ for every even $d\ge4$ and all large
$n$, to within the additive constant $p$. The exact value for every $n$ is
not claimed; Brass's result for $d=4$ and Swanepoel's for even $d\ge6$, on
their own claim pages in this folder, supply it. Nothing is claimed for odd
$d$ or for $d\le3$.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki.

**The argument.** The lower bound places the points on $p$ mutually
orthogonal circles of radius $1/\sqrt2$, Lenz's construction, with the
$n/p$ points on each circle taken as the vertices of $n/(4p)$ squares, which
adds the $n$ unit distances inside the circles to the $t_p(n)$ between them.
The upper bound uses a lemma of Erdős and Simonovits, that a graph on $n$
vertices with $t_p(n)+n+1$ edges contains the complete $(p+1)$-partite graph
$K_{p+1}(1,3,\dots,3)$, together with the geometric fact that $p$ mutually
orthogonal planes cannot all be at unit distance from a further point in
$\mathbb R^{2p}$. The library's
[[../library/distance_problems/erdos_1967_applications_graph_theory_geometry/_index|card
for the paper]] records the theorem and the lemma.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: P. Erdős, On some applications of graph theory to
geometry, Canadian Journal of Mathematics 19 (1967), 968–971. Not reviewed:
the site's remarks say that this paper determined $f_d(n)$ up to $O(1)$ for
all even $d\ge4$, but the site labels the problem OPEN, so the remark is not
an acceptance of the problem or of a part.
