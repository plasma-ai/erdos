# problems/divisors/E0026/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0026/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0026/claims/1951_01_01_davenport_erdos|1951_01_01_davenport_erdos]]: Davenport and Erdős showed that the multiples of a sequence with convergent
reciprocal sum have a density, the limit over its finite stages; that limit
is below one, so every shift of such a set fails; the site credits this.

[[problems/divisors/E0026/claims/1995_01_01_ruzsa|1995_01_01_ruzsa]]: An explicit infinite sequence, built by the Chinese remainder theorem, each
of whose shifted copies misses the divisors of a whole arithmetic
progression; reported by Erdős in 1995 and formalized in Lean by others.

[[problems/divisors/E0026/claims/2026_04_06_deepmind|2026_04_06_deepmind]]: A Lean proof by the DeepMind prover agent of an increasing sequence with
divergent reciprocal sum none of whose shifts has multiples of lower
density above one half; it refutes Tenenbaum's variant and the question.
