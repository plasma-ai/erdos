# problems/divisors/E0144/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0144/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0144/claims/1964_01_01_erdos|1964_01_01_erdos]]: In 1964 Erdős announced a proof that almost all integers have two divisors
with ratio below one plus a negative power of log n, which contains the
problem; the claim was withdrawn in his 1979 paper with Hall.

[[problems/divisors/E0144/claims/1984_02_01_maier_tenenbaum|1984_02_01_maier_tenenbaum]]: Proves that for almost all n the closest pair of divisors of n has
logarithmic ratio below a negative power of log n, so the integers with
divisors d_1 < d_2 < 2 d_1 have density one.
