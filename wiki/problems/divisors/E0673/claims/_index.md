# problems/divisors/E0673/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0673/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0673/claims/1982_01_01_erdos|1982_01_01_erdos]]: Erdős, recalling the problem in a 1982 survey, states that the divergence
of G(n) for almost all n is trivial; the site records Tao's two-line proof
through the bound G(n) at least tau(n/m)/m for a divisor m > 1.

[[problems/divisors/E0673/claims/1983_01_01_erdos_tenenbaum|1983_01_01_erdos_tenenbaum]]: Erdős and Tenenbaum prove that the sum of G(n) for n up to x is (1 + o(1))
x log x and that G(n) is at least tau(n)/(2P(n)) for the least prime factor
P(n) of n, which gives G(n) tending to infinity for almost all n.
