***

**Claim.** A. Bremner, *On a problem of Erdős related to common factor
differences*, Int. J. Number Theory 15 (2019), no. 5, 1059--1068, proves that
there are infinitely many sets of four integers with four common factor
differences, as the paper's abstract states. This answers
[[problems/divisors/E0885/_index|Problem 885]] yes for $k=4$. The abstract
presents the result as an extension of the ideas of Jiménez-Urroz's paper
([[problems/divisors/E0885/claims/1999_09_01_jimenez_urroz|claim page]]),
which settles $k=3$. The page is dated by the paper's online publication.

**Covers.** The instance $k=4$.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: International Journal of Number Theory. The site
labels the problem OPEN, so its commentary crediting the paper is not
acceptance.
