# problems/divisors/E0885/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0885/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0885/claims/1997_01_01_erdos_rosenfeld|1997_01_01_erdos_rosenfeld]]: Erdős and Rosenfeld prove that any number of integers can share two factor
differences and print two triples sharing four, which settles k = 2 and k = 3.

[[problems/divisors/E0885/claims/1999_09_01_jimenez_urroz|1999_09_01_jimenez_urroz]]: Jiménez-Urroz proves that for every k there are k positive integers whose
factor difference sets share at least three values, which settles k = 3.

[[problems/divisors/E0885/claims/2019_05_28_bremner|2019_05_28_bremner]]: Bremner proves that there are infinitely many sets of four integers whose
factor difference sets share four values, which settles k = 4.

[[problems/divisors/E0885/claims/2026_09_23_sanexxxx777|2026_09_23_sanexxxx777]]: A Lean proof merged into formal-conjectures proves the k = 4 case with four

built it.
