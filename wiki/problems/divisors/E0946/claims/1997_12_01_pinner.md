***

**Claim.** C. G. Pinner, *Repeated values of the divisor function*, Quart. J.
Math. Oxford Ser. (2) 48 (1997), no. 192, 499--502, proves that for every
positive integer $B$ there are infinitely many $n$ with $\tau(n)=\tau(n+B)$;
at $B=1$ this answers [[problems/divisors/E0946/_index|Problem 946]] yes. The
zbMATH review (Zbl 0890.11028) states the theorem in this form and says that
the extension of Heath-Brown's method needs further care with the prime
divisors of $B$. The issue is dated December 1997 and no day is given, so
this page carries the first of that month.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: the Quarterly Journal of Mathematics. The site's
PROVED label credits Heath-Brown, so the site's credit is not counted as
review of this paper.
