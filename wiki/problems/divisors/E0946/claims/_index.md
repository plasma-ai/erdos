# problems/divisors/E0946/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0946/claims/1984_06_01_heath_brown|1984_06_01_heath_brown]]: Heath-Brown proves that tau(n) equals tau(n plus 1) for infinitely many n,
at least a constant times x over the seventh power of log x of them up to
x, answering the question of Erdős and Mirsky affirmatively.

[[problems/divisors/E0946/claims/1987_10_01_hildebrand|1987_10_01_hildebrand]]: Hildebrand proves that tau(n) equals tau(n plus 1) for at least a constant
times x over (log log x) cubed integers n up to x, answering the question
again with a far larger count.

[[problems/divisors/E0946/claims/1997_12_01_pinner|1997_12_01_pinner]]: Pinner proves that for every positive integer B there are infinitely many n
with tau(n) equal to tau(n plus B), which at B equal to one answers the
question again.
