# problems/divisors/E1099/claims

[[problems/divisors/E1099/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E1099/claims/1984_10_01_vose|1984_10_01_vose]]: Vose constructs an infinite sequence of integers along which the sum of the
alpha-th powers of the consecutive divisor ratios minus one stays bounded
for every alpha above one, so the limit inferior is bounded.

[[problems/divisors/E1099/claims/1987_01_01_tenenbaum|1987_01_01_tenenbaum]]: Tenenbaum's Théorème 1 bounds the sum of the alpha-th powers of the
consecutive divisor ratios minus one uniformly along the factorials, the
least common multiples and the primorials, settling Erdős's question again.
