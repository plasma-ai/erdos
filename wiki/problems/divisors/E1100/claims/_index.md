# problems/divisors/E1100/claims

[[problems/divisors/E1100/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E1100/claims/1989_03_01_erdos_tenenbaum|1989_03_01_erdos_tenenbaum]]: For almost all n the count of coprime consecutive divisor pairs exceeds a
power of log n, so its ratio to omega(n) tends to infinity, and its maximum
up to x is at least exp of order log x over (log log x) squared.

[[problems/divisors/E1100/claims/2026_07_31_korsky|2026_07_31_korsky]]: The maximum of the coprime-consecutive-divisor count up to x is
exp(Theta(log x / log log x)), with an explicit lower constant, answering
Erdős's maximal-order questions in the negative; prepared with GPT-5.6 Pro.

[[problems/divisors/E1100/claims/2026_08_04_ross|2026_08_04_ross]]: For squarefree n with k prime factors the maximum number of coprime
consecutive divisor pairs is at least c phi^k/root k; the base phi already
follows, with F_{k+1}, from Chevyrev, Searles and Slinko (2013).
