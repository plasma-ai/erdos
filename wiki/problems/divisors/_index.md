# Divisors and Multiples

[[problems/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0018/_index|E0018/]]: Asks how few distinct divisors of a
practical number represent every smaller integer, in particular for factorials;
h(n!) < n^{o(1)} is proved (Conjectures.io, 2026), the (log log m)^{O(1)} part
claimed, the rest open.

[[problems/divisors/E0026/_index|E0026/]]: Asks whether every infinite set of
natural numbers has a shift k for which almost all integers have a divisor that
is a member plus k.

[[problems/divisors/E0056/_index|E0056/]]: Asks whether the multiples of the
first k primes form the largest subset of the first N integers with no k plus
one pairwise relatively prime elements.

[[problems/divisors/E0143/_index|E0143/]]: Asks whether a countable set of reals
above one where every integer multiple of an element is at distance one or more
from another must be sparse.

[[problems/divisors/E0144/_index|E0144/]]: Asks whether almost every integer has
two divisors with the larger less than twice the smaller, so that the density of
such integers exists and equals one.

[[problems/divisors/E0164/_index|E0164/]]: Asks whether the sum of one over n
times the logarithm of n over a set with no member dividing another is largest
when the set is the primes.

[[problems/divisors/E0381/_index|E0381/]]: Asks whether the number of highly
composite numbers up to x grows faster than any fixed power of the logarithm of
x.

[[problems/divisors/E0444/_index|E0444/]]: Asks whether, for every k, an
infinite set of integers has some integer below x with more divisors from the
set than any power of the set's reciprocal sum.

[[problems/divisors/E0446/_index|E0446/]]: The growth rate of the density of
integers having a divisor strictly between n and twice n.

[[problems/divisors/E0448/_index|E0448/]]: Asks whether, for almost all n, the
number of dyadic ranges containing a divisor of n is an arbitrarily small
fraction of the total number of divisors.

[[problems/divisors/E0449/_index|E0449/]]: Asks whether, for almost all n, the
number of pairs of divisors within a factor of two of each other is an
arbitrarily small fraction of the divisor count.

[[problems/divisors/E0450/_index|E0450/]]: How long an interval must be so that
at most a small fraction of its integers have a divisor strictly between n and
twice n.

[[problems/divisors/E0468/_index|E0468/]]: Studies the set of partial sums of
the increasing divisors of n above one.

[[problems/divisors/E0469/_index|E0469/]]: Asks whether the reciprocal sum
converges over integers that are sums of distinct proper divisors of themselves
while no proper divisor has that property.

[[problems/divisors/E0470/_index|E0470/]]: Studies weird numbers, those whose
divisor sum is at least twice the number yet which are not the sum of any set of
their own divisors.

[[problems/divisors/E0486/_index|E0486/]]: Asks whether the set of integers
avoiding a prescribed residue class pattern modulo each member of a given set of
moduli always has a logarithmic density.

[[problems/divisors/E0534/_index|E0534/]]: The largest subset of the integers up
to N that contains N itself and in which every two distinct elements share a
common factor greater than one.

[[problems/divisors/E0673/_index|E0673/]]: Asks whether the sum of the ratios of
consecutive divisors of n tends to infinity for almost all n, and seeks an
asymptotic formula for its average.

[[problems/divisors/E0692/_index|E0692/]]: Asks whether the density of integers
with exactly one divisor in an interval from n to m is unimodular in m;
disproved by Cambie, who also shows many local maxima.

[[problems/divisors/E0693/_index|E0693/]]: Bounds the largest gap between
consecutive integers above n having a divisor between n and twice n, asking if
it is at most a power of the logarithm of n.

[[problems/divisors/E0696/_index|E0696/]]: The longest chain of primes dividing
n, and the longest chain of divisors of n, in which each term is congruent to 1
modulo the previous term.

[[problems/divisors/E0697/_index|E0697/]]: Asks whether some threshold exponent
splits the density of integers with a divisor above 1 congruent to 1 modulo m
into a limit of zero below and one above.

[[problems/divisors/E0844/_index|E0844/]]: Bounds the largest set of integers up
to N in which the product of any two members is never squarefree.

[[problems/divisors/E0858/_index|E0858/]]: Estimates the largest reciprocal sum,
over the logarithm of N, of a set of integers up to N in which no member equals
another member times a factor whose prime factors all exceed the smaller member.

[[problems/divisors/E0859/_index|E0859/]]: Asks whether the density of the
integers n for which a given t is a sum of distinct divisors of n is asymptotic
to a constant over a power of log t; false by a Lean disproof the bounty site
Conjectures.io certified in 2026.

[[problems/divisors/E0872/_index|E0872/]]: How long the game in which two
players alternately add integers up to n to a shared set free of divisibility
between members can be guaranteed to last, and whether it lasts at least εn or
(1-ε)n/2 moves.

[[problems/divisors/E0882/_index|E0882/]]: The size of the largest subset of one
to n whose nonempty subset sums form a set in which no element divides another.

[[problems/divisors/E0884/_index|E0884/]]: Asks whether the sum of one over all
differences of divisors of n is bounded by a constant times one plus the sum of
one over consecutive divisor gaps.

[[problems/divisors/E0885/_index|E0885/]]: Asks whether, for every k, there are
k integers whose sets of differences of complementary factor pairs share at
least k common values.

[[problems/divisors/E0886/_index|E0886/]]: Asks whether, for each fixed positive
epsilon, every large n has only boundedly many divisors just above the square
root of n.

[[problems/divisors/E0887/_index|E0887/]]: Asks whether there is an absolute
bound on the number of divisors of a large n lying within a constant times the
fourth root of n above its square root.

[[problems/divisors/E0892/_index|E0892/]]: Asks for a necessary and sufficient
condition on an increasing sequence for a primitive sequence, no term dividing
another, to grow no faster than it.

[[problems/divisors/E0893/_index|E0893/]]: Asks whether the ratio of the summed
divisor counts of two to the power k minus one, over k up to twice n and up to
n, tends to a limit.

[[problems/divisors/E0945/_index|E0945/]]: Estimates the longest run of
consecutive integers below x with all divisor counts distinct, and whether short
intervals must repeat a divisor count.

[[problems/divisors/E0946/_index|E0946/]]: Asks whether there are infinitely
many n for which n and n plus 1 have the same number of divisors.

[[problems/divisors/E0964/_index|E0964/]]: Asks whether the ratios of the number
of divisors of n plus one to the number of divisors of n are dense in the
positive reals.

[[problems/divisors/E1054/_index|E1054/]]: Studies the least m such that n is
the sum of the k smallest divisors of m for some k, and how that function
behaves.

[[problems/divisors/E1099/_index|E1099/]]: Asks whether the sum of the ratios of
consecutive divisors of n minus one, each raised to a power alpha above one, has
bounded limit inferior over all n.

[[problems/divisors/E1100/_index|E1100/]]: Concerns the number of consecutive
pairs of divisors of n that are coprime.

[[problems/divisors/E1196/_index|E1196/]]: Asks whether the sum of one over a
times log a over a primitive set of integers all at least x is at most one plus
a quantity tending to zero.

[[problems/divisors/E1217/_index|E1217/]]: Asks whether a sequence of positive
lower logarithmic density contains a divisibility chain whose upper growth rate
against log log x is at least the weighted sum's; answered yes in 2026 by
Alexeev and seven coauthors.
