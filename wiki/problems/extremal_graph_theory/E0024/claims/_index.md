[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0024/claims/2011_02_04_grzesik|2011_02_04_grzesik]]: Theorem 3 of Grzesik (J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 2012) bounds the number of
five-cycles in a triangle-free graph on n vertices by (n/5)^5, which at
n = 5m is the catalog's bound m^5; accepted on the refereed publication.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0024/claims/2011_02_08_hatami_hladky_kral_norine_razborov|2011_02_08_hatami_hladky_kral_norine_razborov]]: Corollary 3.3 of Hatami, Hladký, Král', Norine and Razborov (J. Combin.
Theory Ser. A 2013) bounds the pentagons of a triangle-free graph on n
vertices by (n/5)^5, with equality only for the balanced blow-up of C_5.
