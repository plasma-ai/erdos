# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0059/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0059/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0059/claims/1986_12_01_erdos_frankl_rodl|1986_12_01_erdos_frankl_rodl]]: Theorem 1.6 of Erdős, Frankl and Rödl (Graphs Combin. 1986) counts the
G-free graphs on n vertices as 2^{(1+o(1)) ex(n;G)} whenever G has
chromatic number at least 3; accepted on the refereed publication.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0059/claims/2013_09_11_morris_saxton|2013_09_11_morris_saxton]]: Proposition 1.4 of Morris and Saxton (Adv. Math. 2016) gives a constant
c > 0 and infinitely many n with at least 2^{(1+c) ex(n;C_6)} C_6-free
graphs on n vertices, so the question fails for G = C_6; accepted.
