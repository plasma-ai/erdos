# Problem 62

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** If $G_1,G_2$ are two graphs with chromatic number $\aleph_1$ then
must there exist a graph $G$ whose chromatic number is $4$ (or even $\aleph_0$)
which is a subgraph of both $G_1$ and $G_2$?

**Status.** Open.

**Source.** [erdosproblems.com/62](https://www.erdosproblems.com/62), accessed
2026-09-04. Cite as: T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problem #62,
https://www.erdosproblems.com/62.

**References.**

- [EHS74] Erdős, P. and Hajnal, A. and Shelah, S., On some general properties of
  chromatic numbers. Topics in topology (Proc. Colloq., Keszthely, 1972) (1974),
  243-255.
- [Er87] Erdős, P., Some problems on finite and infinite graphs. Logic and
  combinatorics (Arcata, Calif., 1985) (1987), 223-228.
