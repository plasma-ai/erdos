**Claim.** Every Cayley graph of minimum degree at least $3$ on a generalized
quaternion group, a dihedral group, a semidihedral group or a group of order
$p^3$ contains a cycle whose length is a power of $2$. The result is Mohammad
Hossein Ghaffari and Zohreh Mostaghim, *Erdős--Gyárfás conjecture for some
families of Cayley graphs*, Aequationes Math. **92** (2018), no. 1, 1--6,
published online 2017-11-21 (the claim's date). Read depth: the
Crossref record; the families are those the thread comment of 6 December
2025, which the site's remark on
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0064/_index|Problem 64]] points to, lists
for the paper, and the proof was not read.

**Covers.** The statement of Problem 64 for Cayley graphs on generalized
quaternion, dihedral and semidihedral groups and on groups of order $p^3$.

while labeling the problem FALSIFIABLE, which is commentary on an open
problem and not reviewed evidence.
