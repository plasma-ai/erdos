***

**Claim.** Every graph with minimum degree at least $3$ and no induced path on
ten vertices contains a cycle of length $4$ or $8$, so the conjecture of
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0064/_index|Problem 64]] holds for
$P_{10}$-free graphs, which include the $P_8$-free graphs of Gao and Shan
([[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0064/claims/2021_09_03_gao_shan|claim page]]).
The result is Zhiquan Hu and Changlong Shen, *The Erdős-Gyárfás conjecture
holds for $P_{10}$-free graphs*, Discrete Math. **347** (2024), no. 12, Paper
No. 114175,
[doi:10.1016/j.disc.2024.114175](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2024.114175),
whose Crossref record dates the issue to December 2024 without a day, first
posted as *Erdős--Gyárfás conjecture for $P_{10}$-free graphs*,
arXiv:2308.05675, on 2023-08-10 (the claim's date; v2 of 2023-08-12). Read
depth (2026-10-07): the arXiv record and abstract and the journal's Crossref
record; the proof was not read. The thread comment of 6 December 2025 that
the site's remark points to lists the paper among the families where the
conjecture is confirmed.

**Covers.** The statement of Problem 64 for $P_{10}$-free graphs, where the
cycle found has length $2^2$ or $2^3$.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: the paper is a publication in Discrete
Mathematics. The site's curator cites the family list that names the paper
while labeling the problem FALSIFIABLE, which is commentary on an open problem
and not reviewed evidence.
