***

**Claim.** Every graph with minimum degree at least $3$ and no induced path on
thirteen vertices contains a cycle whose length is a power of $2$, so the
conjecture of [[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0064/_index|Problem 64]] holds
for $P_{13}$-free graphs, which include the $P_{10}$-free graphs of Hu and
Shen
([[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0064/claims/2023_08_10_hu_shen|claim page]])
and the $P_8$-free graphs of Gao and Shan
([[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0064/claims/2021_09_03_gao_shan|claim page]]).
The result is Anand Shripad Hegde, R. B. Sandeep and P. Shashank,
*Erdős-Gyárfás conjecture on graphs without long induced paths*,
arXiv:2410.22842, posted 2024-10-30 (the claim's date; v2 of 2025-02-11, six
pages), whose abstract says the proof is obtained with the aid of a computer
search. Read depth: the arXiv record and abstract; the proof was
not read. Duran Ballester's manuscript
([[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0064/claims/2026_08_19_duran_ballester|claim page]])
cites the theorem; the site's remark and the thread's family list of
6 December 2025 do not name it.

**Covers.** The statement of Problem 64 for $P_{13}$-free graphs.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki.

**Standing.** Claimed: an arXiv preprint with no refereed version,
formalization or outside review known to this corpus, and a computer search
that is author-reported and has not been replayed here. The site labels the
problem FALSIFIABLE and its commentary does not credit the paper.
