***

**Claim.** Every graph of diameter $2$ with minimum degree at least $3$
contains a cycle of length $4$ or $8$, so the conjecture of
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0064/_index|Problem 64]] holds for graphs
of diameter $2$. The result is Avery Carr, *Cycles of length 4 or 8 in graphs
with diameter 2 and minimum degree at least 3*, arXiv:2508.19302, posted
2025-08-25 (the claim's date; v4 of 2026-01-30, twelve pages), whose arXiv
comment reports acceptance for publication in the Bulletin of the Institute
of Combinatorics and its Applications. Read depth: the arXiv
record and abstract; the proof was not read. The thread comment of 6 December
2025 that the site's remark points to lists the paper among the families
where the conjecture is confirmed, and Temeller's claim
([[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0064/claims/2026_10_02_temeller|claim page]])
extends the theorem to diameter $3$ at minimum degree $4$.

**Covers.** The statement of Problem 64 for graphs of diameter $2$, where
the cycle found has length $2^2$ or $2^3$.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki.

**Standing.** Claimed: the acceptance is author-reported on the arXiv record,
and no published version with volume and pages is known to this corpus, so
no refereed evidence is listed. The site's curator cites the family list that
names the paper while labeling the problem FALSIFIABLE, which is commentary
on an open problem and not acceptance.
