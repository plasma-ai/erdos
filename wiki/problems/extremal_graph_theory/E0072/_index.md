# Problem 72

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0072/claims/_index|claims/]]: The 2 claim pages of Problem 72, one per claimant's result; the problem's standing derives from them.

***

**Statement.** Is there a set $A\subset \mathbb{N}$ of density $0$ and a
constant $c>0$ such that every graph on sufficiently many vertices with average
degree $\geq c$ contains a cycle whose length is in $A$?

**Status.** Proved. The claim pages are
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0072/claims/2005_03_21_verstraete|Verstraëte]]
and
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0072/claims/2020_10_29_liu_montgomery|Liu and Montgomery]],
both accepted on refereed publication and the site's credit; the frontmatter
standing is derived from them.

**Source.** [erdosproblems.com/72](https://www.erdosproblems.com/72), accessed
2026-09-04. Cite as: T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problem #72,
https://www.erdosproblems.com/72.
