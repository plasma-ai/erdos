# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0081/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0081/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0081/claims/2026_06_17_agbanwa|2026_06_17_agbanwa]]: A Zenodo note proves cp(G) at most (2n-1)²/24 + 2 for every chordal graph on
n vertices having a maximum clique whose edge deletion lowers the clique
number by at most one, excluding the extremal split graphs; unreviewed.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0081/claims/2026_08_23_traverso|2026_08_23_traverso]]: Paper III of Traverso's series asserts that every split graph on n vertices
has a clique partition into n²/6 + O(n) cliques, the sharp coefficient, with
a Lean 4 freeze the author reports as unconditional; the split case only.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0081/claims/2026_09_08_morluto_luo_huang_lee|2026_09_08_morluto_luo_huang_lee]]: A manuscript and Lean 4 development in the N0zoM1z0/erdos-81 repository
assert that for all large n the maximum clique partition number of a chordal
graph is floor(n(n+1)/6), so n²/6 + O(n); the Lean assumes three theorems.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0081/claims/2026_09_15_okechukwu|2026_09_15_okechukwu]]: An arXiv preprint determines the eventual maximum clique partition number of
graphs with rooted simplicial defect s; the case s = 0 is the chordal graphs,
giving cp(G) at most n²/6 + n/6 + O(1); announced on the thread; unreviewed.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0081/claims/2026_09_22_traverso|2026_09_22_traverso]]: Paper IV of Traverso's series asserts that every chordal graph on n vertices
has a partition into at most floor(n(n+1)/6) + b cliques of order at most
four, hence n²/6 + O(n), with a Lean 4 proof the author reports unconditional.
