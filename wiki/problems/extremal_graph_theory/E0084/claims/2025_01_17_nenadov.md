***

**Claim.** The number of distinct cycle sets of graphs on $n$ vertices is at
most $2^{n-n^{1/2-o(1)}}$. This is the main theorem of R. Nenadov, *Improved
bound on the number of cycle sets*, arXiv:2501.09904, posted 17 January 2025
(the claim's date), revised 22 September 2025, and published in Combinatorial
Theory **6** (2026), no. 1, on 2026-04-20; the corpus's
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/nenadov_2025_improved_bound_number_cycle_sets/_index|card]]
records it as Theorem 1.1 (p. 2) of the v2 preprint, in the form
$2^{n-\Omega(\sqrt n/\log^{3/2}n)}$. In the notation of
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0084/_index|Problem 84]],
$f(n)\le2^{n-n^{1/2-o(1)}}$, which sharpens Verstraëte's $o(2^{n-n^c})$ with
$c\ge0.1$
([[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0084/claims/2004_09_01_verstraete|claim page]]).
The abstract describes the method as Verstraëte's reduction to counting the
cycle sets of Hamiltonian graphs with many chords or large maximum degree,
with near-optimal container lemmas as the new ingredient.

**Covers.** The first assertion, $f(n)=o(2^n)$, already settled by
Verstraëte's accepted claim, with a larger saving in the exponent. The second
assertion, $f(n)/2^{n/2}\to\infty$, is not addressed.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki.

**Acceptance.** The paper is a refereed publication in Combinatorial Theory,
volume 6, issue 1 (2026), published online 2026-04-20, which is the
`refereed` evidence. The site's remark names the improvement, but the site
labels the problem OPEN, the second assertion being unsettled, so no
`reviewed` evidence is listed. This corpus has not checked the proof.
