***

**Claim.** There exist a fixed connected bipartite $2$-degenerate graph $H$
and constants $c,\varepsilon>0$ with $\mathrm{ex}(n,H)\ge c\,n^{3/2+\varepsilon}$
for all sufficiently large $n$. This is Theorem 1.2 of Chapter 10 of OpenAI,
*Ten Advances in Mathematics and Theoretical Computer Science*, technical
report announced 1 August 2026 (the claim's date), PDF revised 6 August 2026;
the corpus states it on its
[[../library/ramsey_theory/openai_2026_ten_advances_mathematics_theoretical_computer_science/chapter_10/theorem_1_2|result page]]
under the report's
[[../library/ramsey_theory/openai_2026_ten_advances_mathematics_theoretical_computer_science/_index|card]].
The chapter remarks (p. 237) that Janzer's construction disproved only the
reverse implication of the equivalence of
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0113/_index|Problem 113]] and that this
theorem refutes the forward one. The site labels Problem 146 disproved by
the same theorem, which is recorded on
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0146/claims/2026_08_01_openai|that problem's claim page]].

The forward implication of the equivalence, that every $2$-degenerate
bipartite graph $H$ has $\mathrm{ex}(n,H)=O(n^{3/2})$, is false: the theorem's
$H$ is $2$-degenerate and bipartite, and $c\,n^{3/2+\varepsilon}$ exceeds
every $O(n^{3/2})$ bound for large $n$. The theorem therefore disproves the
equivalence the problem asserts on its own. The reverse implication is not
addressed; its disproof is
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0113/claims/2021_09_13_janzer|Janzer's accepted claim]],
which already settles the problem.

theorem in labeling Problem 146 DISPROVED (LEAN) and crediting the connected

edited 31 August 2026), as recorded on the Problem 146 claim page; the forward
implication of this problem follows from it in one line. The site's thread
for Problem 113 carries a comment of 1 August 2026 pointing to the chapter's
remark, but the site's page (last edited 19 October 2025) credits the

were produced by an internal OpenAI model and are at different stages of
