# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0128/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0128/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0128/claims/1995_03_01_krivelevich|1995_03_01_krivelevich]]: Krivelevich's Theorem 3 (J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 1995): every regular
triangle-free graph of degree at least 2n/5 has n/2 vertices spanning at most
n²/50 edges, the blown-up C_5 alone meeting the bound; refereed.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0128/claims/2006_01_05_keevash_sudakov|2006_01_05_keevash_sudakov]]: Keevash and Sudakov (J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 2006) prove that a
triangle-free graph with at most n²/12 edges, or with at least n²/5 edges,
has n/2 vertices spanning at most n²/50 edges; refereed.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0128/claims/2013_11_22_norin_yepremyan|2013_11_22_norin_yepremyan]]: Norin and Yepremyan (J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 2015) prove the sparse-halves
conjecture for triangle-free graphs of minimum degree at least 5n/14, with at
least (1/5 - γ)n² edges, or close to the Petersen graph; refereed.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0128/claims/2021_04_19_razborov|2021_04_19_razborov]]: Razborov (Mat. Sb. 2022) proves the sparse-halves conjecture for triangle-free
graphs with no induced 2K_2, girth at least 5, independence number at least
2n/5, low density or strong regularity, and the bound 27n²/1024; refereed.
