# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0133/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0133/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0133/claims/1984_01_01_hanson_seyffarth|1984_01_01_hanson_seyffarth]]: Cayley graphs of cyclic groups on symmetric complete sum-free sets give
triangle-free graphs of diameter two whose degree is of order root n along
an infinite sequence of n, so f(n) over root n does not tend to infinity.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0133/claims/1994_01_01_furedi_seress|1994_01_01_furedi_seress]]: Theorem 6.1 of Füredi and Seress gives, for every large n, a triangle-free
graph of diameter two on n vertices with maximum degree at most two over
root three times root n plus a lower-order term, so f(n) has order root n.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0133/claims/2017_03_12_haviv_levy|2017_03_12_haviv_levy]]: Theorem 1.5 of Haviv and Levy gives a symmetric complete sum-free subset of
every large cyclic group of size O(root n), whose Cayley graph is a
triangle-free graph of diameter two of degree O(root n); f(n) has order root n.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0133/claims/2026_09_15_korsky|2026_09_15_korsky]]: A proof claim posted on the site's proof-claim tab asserts that f(n) is
(1+o(1)) root n, the sharp constant Alon conjectured, by a fibered polarity
graph; unreviewed, with the proof in an external file.
