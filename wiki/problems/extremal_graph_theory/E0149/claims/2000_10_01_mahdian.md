***

**Claim.** For every $\varepsilon>0$ there is $\Delta_0$ such that every
graph $G$ with no cycle of length four and maximum degree
$\Delta\ge\Delta_0$ has

$$
\mathrm{sq}(G)\le(2+\varepsilon)\frac{\Delta^2}{\log\Delta},
$$

a bound sharp up to the constant factor. The result is Mahdian's, in his
M.Sc. thesis *The strong chromatic index of graphs* (University of Toronto,
2000; the site's thread links the University of Toronto repository copy,
linked above) and in M. Mahdian, *The strong chromatic index of
$C_4$-free graphs*, Random Structures Algorithms **17** (2000), no. 3--4,
357--375 (the Crossref record dates the issue October 2000, whose nominal
first day is this page's date). The site's commentary credits the
thesis. For $\Delta$ large enough that
$(2+\varepsilon)\Delta^2/\log\Delta\le\frac54\Delta^2$, the bound of
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0149/_index|Problem 149]] holds for these
graphs.

**Covers.** Every $C_4$-free graph whose maximum degree is at least the
threshold $\Delta_0$ of the theorem, which the statement does not make
explicit. It says nothing about graphs containing a four-cycle, among them
the blown-up five-cycles that make the conjectured constant sharp, nor about
$C_4$-free graphs of small maximum degree.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: Random Structures Algorithms 17 (2000), no.
3--4, 357--375. The statement recorded above is the one the thesis gives in
its University of Toronto repository abstract: $(2+o(1))\Delta^2/\ln\Delta$
for $C_4$-free graphs, which the abstract says implies the conjecture for
$C_4$-free graphs of large maximum degree. The zbMATH review of the journal
paper (Zbl 0961.05022) describes the same result: an asymptotically better
bound for graphs without a four-cycle, best possible up to a constant factor.
Cames van Batenburg, Kang and Pirot state it as their Theorem 4
([[../library/extremal_graph_theory/camesvanbatenburg_2020_strong_cliques_forbidden_cycles/_index|source
card]]): $\chi'_2(G)\le(2+\varepsilon)\Delta^2/\log\Delta$ for $C_4$-free
graphs and large $\Delta$, "sharp up to the multiplicative constant factor".
The site labels the problem OPEN, so the curator's commentary crediting the
thesis is not listed as review.
