# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0150/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0150/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0150/claims/2008_03_09_fomin_villanger|2008_03_09_fomin_villanger]]: Theorem 1 of Fomin and Villanger proves that an n-vertex graph has
O(1.6181^n) minimal separators, so the limit is at most the golden ratio;
accepted on the refereed paper in Combinatorica 32 (2012).

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0150/claims/2008_07_02_fomin_kratsch_todinca_villanger|2008_07_02_fomin_kratsch_todinca_villanger]]: Fomin, Kratsch, Todinca and Villanger prove that an n-vertex graph has
O(1.7087^n) minimal separators, the first proof that the limit is below 2;
accepted on the refereed paper in SIAM J. Comput. 38 (2008).

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0150/claims/2015_03_04_gaspers_mackenzie|2015_03_04_gaspers_mackenzie]]: Theorem 1 of Gaspers and Mackenzie proves by a short measure argument that
an n-vertex graph has O(rho^n n) minimal separators, rho the golden ratio;
accepted on the refereed paper in J. Graph Theory 87 (2018).

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0150/claims/2024_09_04_bradac|2024_09_04_bradac]]: Bradač's note proves that the n-th root of the maximum number of minimal
cuts converges to a limit at most 2 to the binary entropy of one third,
below 1.8899; refereed in J. Graph Theory 108 (2025), credited by the site.
