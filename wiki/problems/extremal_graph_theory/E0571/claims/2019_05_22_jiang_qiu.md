***

**Claim.** For $k\in\{3,4\}$ and every $s\ge2$ the rational
$\alpha=1+\frac1k-\frac1{sk}$, that is, $\frac43-\frac1{3s}$ and
$\frac54-\frac1{4s}$, is a Turán exponent. Writing $K^k_{s,t}$ for the graph
obtained from $K_{s,t}$ by replacing each edge with a path of length $k$,
Theorem 1.2 proves $\mathrm{ex}(n,K^k_{s,t})=O(n^{1+1/k-1/(sk)})$ for
$k\in\{3,4\}$ and all $s,t\ge2$, the cases $k=3,4$ of a conjecture of Conlon,
Janzer and Lee; the matching lower bound, for t large in terms of s and k, is
Bukh and Conlon's random algebraic construction. The statements are recorded
on the library's
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/jiang_2020_turan_numbers_bipartite_subdivisions/_index|source card]].

**Covers.** The instances $\alpha=\frac43-\frac1{3s}$ and
$\alpha=\frac54-\frac1{4s}$ for $s\ge2$, each realized by a single bipartite
graph. The statement for every rational $\alpha\in[1,2)$ is settled by the
accepted claim page
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0571/claims/2026_09_03_adamczewski|Adamczewski 2026]].

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki; the result is the paper's own theorem.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: T. Jiang and Y. Qiu, *Turán numbers of bipartite
subdivisions*, SIAM J. Discrete Math. 34 (2020), no. 1, 556--570,
doi:10.1137/19M1265442, a refereed journal. First posting: arXiv:1905.08994, v1
