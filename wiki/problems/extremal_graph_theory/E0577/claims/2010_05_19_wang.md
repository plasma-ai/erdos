**The claim.** Hong Wang, *Proof of the Erdős--Faudree conjecture on
quadrilaterals*, Graphs and Combinatorics 26 (2010), no. 6, 833--877,
doi:10.1007/s00373-010-0948-3; received 12 September 2006, revised 16 April
2010, published online 19 May 2010 (this page's date) and in print in
November 2010. The edition read is the publisher's version of record, carded
at
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/wang_2010_proof_erdos_faudree_conjecture_quadrilaterals/_index|its library home]].
Theorem B (p. 834, paged at
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/wang_2010_proof_erdos_faudree_conjecture_quadrilaterals/theorem_b|theorem_b]])
states that a graph of order $4k$ with minimum degree at least $2k$
contains $k$ disjoint cycles of length $4$, where disjoint means having no
common vertex (p. 833); this is the statement of the problem for every
positive integer $k$, with no further hypothesis, so the claim is full. The
$k$ four-cycles use all $4k$ vertices, so the conclusion is a spanning
subgraph of $k$ disjoint copies of $C_4$. The proof (pp. 835--877) argues by
contradiction from a chain of a triangle and $k-1$ disjoint four-cycles
chosen to maximize the number of chords of the four-cycles: a two-page
sketch derives Theorem B from Claims 2.5--2.7 by counting the edges from
the leftover vertex and the triangle into the four-cycles, and the
remaining 42 pages prove Claims 2.1--2.7 through 22 lemmas. The paper's
introduction attributes the conjecture to Erdős's 1990 Bielefeld report,
the site's [Er90c], which is not held, and records the earlier partial
results of Randerath, Schiermeyer and Wang (1999) and Wang (2004).

**Acceptance.** Refereed: Graphs and Combinatorics is a refereed journal,
and the paper is its version of record. Reviewed: the site's curator, Thomas
Bloom, labels the problem PROVED and, in the commentary of
erdosproblems.com/577 (accessed 2026-10-07; empty discussion thread and
proof-claim tab), credits the proof to this paper; Bloom took no part in the
paper, and the community database records the problem as proved. Theorem B,
the sketch and the derivation of Theorem B from Claims 2.5--2.7 were read;
the proofs of the claims were read for structure only, and no case analysis
was checked. No independent review of the proof is recorded in this
repository and none is claimed; no second paper attesting the theorem is on
record.

**Formalization, not evidence.** A public Lean 4 development in Boris
Alexeev's lean-proofs repository, `Erdos577.lean` with its supporting modules
under `Erdos577/` (linked above at a pinned revision; the file entered the
repository on 2026-08-28), says in its docstring that its proof follows
Theorem B of this paper, and its theorems `erdos_faudree` and `erdos_577`
state Theorem B for every $k$, with $k=0$ and $k=1$ handled explicitly. The
corpus has not built or audited the development, so it gives no `formalized`
evidence and the evidence stays `reviewed` and `refereed`.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki: the proof is the paper's.
