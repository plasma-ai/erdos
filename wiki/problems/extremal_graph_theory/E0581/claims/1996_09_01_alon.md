**Claim.** Let $f(m)$ be the largest $k$ such that every triangle-free graph
with $m$ edges contains a bipartite subgraph with at least $k$ edges, the
function of [[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0581/_index|Problem 581]]. There
are absolute constants $c',C'>0$ such that for every $m>1$

$$
\frac m2+c'm^{4/5}\le f(m)\le\frac m2+C'm^{4/5},
$$

so $f(m)=m/2+\Theta(m^{4/5})$ and the exponent $4/5$ cannot be improved. The
lower bound holds for every triangle-free graph with $m$ edges; the upper
bound is witnessed by explicit triangle-free graphs. The claim value is
`answered`: the question asks to determine $f(m)$, and the result is neither a
proof nor a disproof but a determination of the order of the surplus over
$m/2$, which the site's estimate vocabulary calls resolved and this corpus
reads the same way; the exact value of $f(m)$, the best constants and the
small values are not known from any source on record (Alon writes on p. 8
that determining the minimum precisely "seems more difficult").

**The result.** Theorem 1.2 of N. Alon, *Bipartite subgraphs*, Combinatorica
16 (1996), no. 3, 301--311, doi:10.1007/BF01261315, with the sharpness half
proved as Proposition 3.2; the result pages
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/alon_1996_bipartite_subgraphs/theorem_1_2|theorem_1_2]]
and
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/alon_1996_bipartite_subgraphs/proposition_3_2|proposition_3_2]]
state both in the corpus's words with the proof pointers, and the
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/alon_1996_bipartite_subgraphs/_index|source card]]
identifies the edition, the author's final version. In the paper's
notation $f(G)$ is the largest number of edges of a bipartite subgraph of
$G$, and the theorem says that every triangle-free graph with $e>1$ edges has
$f(G)\ge e/2+c'e^{4/5}$, while for every $e$ some triangle-free graph with
$e$ edges has $f(G)\le e/2+C'e^{4/5}$; with $e=m$ these are the two
inequalities above. The lower bound improves Shearer's exponent $3/4$ (and
his independent, earlier $4/5-\epsilon$, recorded in the paper's note added
in proof); the upper bound comes from the eigenvalue bound of Lemma 3.1
applied to Alon's explicit triangle-free regular graphs, extended to every
$e$ by disjoint copies and isolated edges. The constants are not made
explicit. Read depth, as the problem page records: claims checked for
Theorem 1.2 and Proposition 3.2; the proof of the lower
bound read for structure and not checked; nothing independently reviewed
here.
