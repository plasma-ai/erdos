**Claim.** G. Fan, *Path decompositions and Gallai's conjecture*, J. Combin.
Theory Ser. B 93 (2005), 117--125, published online on 11 November 2004 (the
claim's date). Its Main theorem (p. 124): a graph on $n$ vertices, not
necessarily connected, whose vertices of even degree induce an
$\alpha$-graph decomposes into $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ paths. An $\alpha$-graph
is one built from the empty graph by adding isolated vertices and vertices
joined to the independent sets of $\alpha$-pairs (Definitions 2.1--2.2,
pp. 118--119); every $\alpha$-graph is triangle-free, and forests are
$\alpha$-graphs. Its Corollary (p. 125): the same holds when each block of
the even-degree subgraph is a triangle-free graph of maximum degree at most
$3$, since such graphs are $\alpha$-graphs (Proposition 2.6). The paper shows
(p. 118) that the triangle-free condition cannot be dropped. The theorems are
recorded on the
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/fan_2005_path_decompositions_gallai_s_conjecture/main_theorem|Main theorem page]]
and the
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/fan_2005_path_decompositions_gallai_s_conjecture/corollary|Corollary page]]
of the
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/fan_2005_path_decompositions_gallai_s_conjecture/_index|source card]].

**Covers.** The statement of
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0583/_index|Problem 583]] for connected
graphs whose even-degree subgraph is an $\alpha$-graph, including those in
which each block of the even-degree subgraph is triangle-free with maximum
degree at most $3$, with $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ paths.
