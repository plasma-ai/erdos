***

**Claim.** Theorem 1.3 of M. Bonamy and T. J. Perrett, *Gallai's path
decomposition conjecture for graphs of small maximum degree*, Discrete Math.
342 (2019), no. 5, 1293--1299, first posted as arXiv:1609.06257v1 on
20 September 2016 (the claim's date): every connected graph on $n$ vertices
with maximum degree at most $5$ admits a path decomposition into
$\lceil n/2\rceil$ paths. The theorem is recorded, from the arXiv v1, on the
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/bonamy_2019_gallai_s_path_decomposition_conjecture_graphs/theorem_1_3|theorem page]]
of the
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/bonamy_2019_gallai_s_path_decomposition_conjecture_graphs/_index|source card]];
the journal text was not compared with it.

**Covers.** The statement of
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0583/_index|Problem 583]] for connected
graphs of maximum degree at most $5$.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: the paper is a publication in Discrete Mathematics.
The site's curator credits the result while labeling the problem FALSIFIABLE,
which is commentary on an open problem and not reviewed evidence.
