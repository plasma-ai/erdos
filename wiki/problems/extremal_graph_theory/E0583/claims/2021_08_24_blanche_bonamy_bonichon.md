***

**Claim.** A. Blanché, M. Bonamy and N. Bonichon first posted the result as
the extended abstract *Gallai's path decomposition for planar graphs*,
Extended Abstracts EuroComb 2021 (Trends in Mathematics), 758--764, online on
24 August 2021 (the claim's date), whose abstract states that every connected
planar graph except $K_3$ and $K_5^-$ ($K_5$ minus one edge) decomposes into
$\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ paths. The full paper, *Gallai's path decomposition in
planar graphs*, arXiv:2110.08870 (v1 17 October 2021; v2 21 June 2022,
95 pp.), states it as Theorem 1.1, every connected planar graph on $n$
vertices decomposes into $\lceil n/2\rceil$ paths, and Theorem 1.2,
$\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ paths except for $K_3$ and $K_5^-$, which still meet
$\lceil n/2\rceil$. The theorems are recorded, from the arXiv v2, on the
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/blanche_2021_gallai_s_path_decomposition_planar_graphs/theorem_1_1|Theorem 1.1 page]]
and the
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/blanche_2021_gallai_s_path_decomposition_planar_graphs/theorem_1_2|Theorem 1.2 page]]
of the
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/blanche_2021_gallai_s_path_decomposition_planar_graphs/_index|source card]].

**Covers.** The statement of
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0583/_index|Problem 583]] for connected
planar graphs.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki.
