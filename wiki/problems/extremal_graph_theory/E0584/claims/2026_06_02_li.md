**Claim.** E. Li, *On the Duke--Erdős--Rödl problem at the one-third
threshold*, arXiv:2606.06522v1 (2 June 2026, the claim's date), 20 pp.,
announced by its author on the site's discussion thread on 10 June 2026. As
its abstract states, for an $n$-vertex graph $G$ with $e(G)\ge n^2/k$ and
$k\le n^{1/3}$, $G$ contains a subgraph $H_8$ with $\Omega(n^2/k^2)$ edges in
which every two distinct edges lie together on a cycle of length at most $8$
contained in $H_8$, and a subgraph $H_6$ with $\Omega(n^2/k^3)$ edges in which
every two distinct edges lie together on a cycle of length at most $6$
contained in $H_6$. In the density $\rho=e(G)/n^2$ these are cores of
$\Omega(\rho^2n^2)$ and $\Omega(\rho^3n^2)$ edges for every
$\rho\ge n^{-1/3}$. The $H_8$ result is the second clause of
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0584/_index|Problem 584]] for
$\delta\ge n^{-1/3}$, extending Fox and Sudakov's range $\delta>n^{-1/5}$.

The preprint states three further results that settle nothing under the
reading the problem page adopts. The $H_6$ result lacks the condition that
adjacent edges lie on a $C_4$, so it is not the first clause; without that
condition it re-proves, for $\rho\ge n^{-1/3}$, what Theorem 1 of Duke, Erdős
and Rödl (1984) gives for every density above $n^{-1/2}$. Under the
ambient-witness convention, with the witnessing cycles taken in $G$, it
claims that every graph with at least $n^2/k$ edges and $k=o(n^{1/2})$ has
$\Omega(n^2/k^3)$ selected edges whose pairs lie on cycles of $G$ of length at
most $6$, with adjacent pairs on $C_4$'s of $G$; the problem page reads the
cycles as lying inside $H_1$, as the sources do, so this result does not bear
on the first clause, though under an ambient reading it would, if correct,
combine with Fox and Sudakov's theorem to answer the sparse question yes.
Finally, with the cycles inside the subgraph and adjacent pairs on a $C_4$
inside it, it claims that for every fixed $\beta\in[1/3,1/2)$ there are
bipartite graphs with $\Theta_\beta(n^{2-\beta})$ edges in which every such
subgraph has only $O_\beta(\rho^3n^2/(\log n)^2)$ edges, built from random
cyclic shift-lifts of $K_{q,q}$; that obstruction is confined to
$1/3\le\beta<1/2$ and excludes no smaller $c$.

**Covers.** The second clause ($H_2$) of Problem 584 for $\delta\ge n^{-1/3}$,
that is $\delta=n^{-c}$ for every $0<c\le1/3$. Nothing for the first clause.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki.

**Standing.** Claimed: an unrefereed preprint, described from its arXiv
abstract; the site's thread gives it no review and the site labels the
problem OPEN. The claim is partial and its range of the second clause
contains the one
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0584/claims/2007_06_13_fox_sudakov|Fox and Sudakov]]
proved, so the problem's standing is unchanged by it.
