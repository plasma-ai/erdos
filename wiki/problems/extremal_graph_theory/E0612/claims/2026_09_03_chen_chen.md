***

**Claim.** Hangdi Chen and Yaojun Chen, *Counterexamples to two conjectures
on the diameter of clique-free graphs*, state in their abstract a
construction that disproves the amended conjecture of Czabarka, Singgih and
Székely, including its $k$-colorable version, for every $k\ge7$ and
sufficiently large minimum degree, and, when $k=2r\ge8$ and $3r-1\mid\delta$,
part (ii) of the statement of
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0612/_index|Problem 612]]: connected
$K_{2r+1}$-free graphs of minimum degree $\delta$ whose diameter exceeds
$\frac{3r-1}{r}\frac n\delta+O(1)$, for every $r\ge4$ and every sufficiently
large $\delta$ divisible by $3r-1$.

**Covers.** Part (ii), in full if the claim is accepted: part (ii) is asked
for every $r$, so a refutation for every $r\ge4$ disproves it. Part (ii) is
the question that the refereed counterexamples to part (i) leave open; since
part (i) is already disproved, accepting this claim would make the problem
disproved. It does not bear on part (i).

**Standing.** A preprint (arXiv v1 of 3 September 2026, the only version
found), unrefereed; the preprint is not held, and this page cites its
abstract; no outside review was found. A thread comment of 4 September 2026
reports the result; the site marks comments as unverified. The claim stays
claimed, and part (ii) stays open until a refutation of it is accepted.
