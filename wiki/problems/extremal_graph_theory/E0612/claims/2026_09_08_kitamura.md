***

**Claim.** Kenta Kitamura announces, in thread comments of 7 and 8 September
2026 and the repository `KitaKen1/erdos-612-lean`, Lean 4 files for the
fixed-clique cases of [[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0612/_index|Problem
612]] left open by the counterexamples to part (i). The thread comment and the
repository's first commit of 7 September, like Kitamura's issue-828 comment
of that day, carry only the proved cases ($r=2$, $k=3,4$) and list the $K_7$
case as open; the $r=3$ refutation, on which this claim's value rests, was
added to the repository and announced in the thread on 8 September 2026.
Part (ii) at $r=3$ ($K_7$-free graphs, bound $\frac83\frac n\delta+O(1)$ for
$8\mid\delta$) is refuted: the repository's README describes a $71$-layer
periodic $K_7$-free family of minimum degree $800$, order $n=21296p+960$ and
diameter at least $71p+1$ for every positive integer $p$; the conjectured
main term at $\delta=800$ is
$\frac83\cdot\frac{21296p+960}{800}=\frac{5324}{75}p+\frac{16}5$, so the
diameter exceeds it by at least $\frac p{75}-\frac{11}5$, unbounded in $p$, and
no additive constant restores the inequality (the theorem
`erdos_612.variants.original_k7`, stated as `answer(False)`). Since $8\mid800$,
the same family refutes the amended conjecture of Czabarka, Singgih and Székely
at $k=6$ (`amended_k6`). The files also prove part (ii) at $r=2$ in the form
$2d(D+1)+4\le5n$ for every connected $K_5$-free finite graph, without the
divisibility hypothesis $5\mid d$ (`original_k5`), prove the amended conjecture
for $k=3$ (as $3d(D+1)+6\le7n$ for connected $K_4$-free graphs) and $k=4$, and
refute it for $k=5$; the proved cases use a strong reading of the $O(1)$ term,
with constant $0$, which implies the stated $O(1)$ form. The comments report
that the files contain no `sorry`, that `#print axioms` lists only `propext`,
`Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`, and that the formalization and proofs were
prepared, in the words of the README's AI disclosure, with assistance from
OpenAI Codex, Astra, and ChatGPT under Kitamura's direction; the amended
conjecture is not the problem.

**Covers.** Part (ii). The $r=3$ refutation is one false instance of part
(ii), which is asked for every $r$ and every admissible $\delta$, so if
accepted it disproves part (ii) in full, as would the page of
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0612/claims/2026_09_03_chen_chen|Chen and
Chen]]; since part (i) is already disproved, the problem would then be
disproved. The proof at $r=2$ is a partial result on part (ii) and settles
nothing by itself. It does not bear on part (i).

**Standing.** Claimed. The corpus has not built these Lean files, so they
give no `formalized` evidence, and no statement-fidelity audit of them exists
in this corpus; the site marks comments as unverified. A comment of 8
September 2026 by one of the authors of the
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0612/claims/2025_02_12_cambie_jooken|2025 counterexample]]
says the same conclusions for $r=2$ (true) and $r=3$ (false) were reached
independently, without a posted argument. Kitamura's comment of 7 September
2026 on formal-conjectures issue 828 (opened by another user in October 2025)
links a draft statement and its strong and weak formalizations of the
$O(1)$ term; no file `ErdosProblems/612.lean` was found there. If the
announcements hold, part (ii) is true exactly for $r\in\{1,2\}$, and so
disproved. Part (ii) stays open until a refutation of it is accepted.
