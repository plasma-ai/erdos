**Claim.** Noga Alon's Theorem 3.2 in *Problems and Results in Extremal
Combinatorics--V* (Bolyai Society Mathematical Studies 32, Springer, 2026,
13--29; article pp. 8--10): if a triangle-free graph $G$ on $n$ vertices has
maximum degree at most $c(n)\sqrt n$, where
$2(\log n)^{1/3}n^{-1/6}\le c(n)\le\frac1{10}$ and $n$ is large, then adding
at most $2.5c(n)n^2$ edges gives a triangle-free graph of diameter at most
two. For a sequence of triangle-free $n$-vertex graphs with maximum degree
$d_n=o(\sqrt n)$, the choice
$c(n)=\max\{d_n/\sqrt n,\,2(\log n)^{1/3}n^{-1/6}\}$ tends to zero and meets
the hypotheses eventually, so $h_2(G_n)=o(n^2)$. This answers
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0618/_index|Problem 618]] affirmatively
as its corrected Statement asks it, with $h_2$ in the question, the function
Erdős, Gyárfás and Ruszinkó call $h$, defined through diameter at most two;
for $n\ge3$ the site's "diameter $2$" gives the same function, since a
triangle-free completion cannot have diameter one. The theorem, its parameter
transfer and its proof outline are on the result page
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/alon_2026_problems_results_extremal_combinatorics_v/theorem_3_2|theorem_3_2]].
The result first appeared in the author's 2024 note on
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0134/_index|Problem 134]], where it is
Theorem 1.2 (the two author versions carry the embedded dates 1 and 2 July
2024, the earliest dates known for the posting); the chapter gives the same
theorem and proof as Theorem 3.2, and the same theorem is recorded on
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0134/claims/2024_07_01_alon|Problem 134's claim page]].

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki; the argument is self-contained, and
the problem page's account rests on this claim.
