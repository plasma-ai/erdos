# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0714/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0714/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0714/claims/1954_01_01_kovari_sos_turan|1954_01_01_kovari_sos_turan]]: Kővári, Sós and Turán prove that the least number of ones in an n by n 0-1
matrix forcing a 2 by 2 minor of ones is asymptotic to n^{3/2}, which gives
ex(n;K_{2,2}) >> n^{3/2}, the case r = 2; refereed in Colloq. Math. 3 (1954).

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0714/claims/1966_02_01_erdos_renyi_sos|1966_02_01_erdos_renyi_sos]]: Erdős, Rényi and Sós prove that the largest number of edges of a graph on n
vertices with no four-cycle is asymptotic to n^{3/2}/2, so ex(n;K_{2,2}) >>
n^{3/2}, the case r = 2; refereed in Studia Sci. Math. Hungar. 1 (1966).

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0714/claims/1966_08_01_brown|1966_08_01_brown]]: Brown's sphere graphs over GF(p) have no K_{3,3} and about n^{5/3}/2 edges,
and his Section 3 gives the C_4 asymptotic, so ex(n;K_{r,r}) >> n^{2-1/r}
holds for r = 3 and r = 2; a refereed paper in Canad. Math. Bull. 9 (1966).
