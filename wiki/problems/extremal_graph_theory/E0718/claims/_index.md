# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0718/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0718/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0718/claims/1996_03_01_komlos_szemeredi|1996_03_01_komlos_szemeredi]]: Komlós and Szemerédi prove, by refining their earlier method, that a
constant times r^2 n edges on n vertices force a subdivision of the complete
graph on r vertices, an independent second proof answering Problem 718.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0718/claims/1996_09_01_bollobas_thomason|1996_09_01_bollobas_thomason]]: Bollobás and Thomason prove that every graph on n vertices with at least
256 r^2 n edges contains a subdivision of the complete graph on r vertices,
which answers Problem 718 affirmatively with the constant 256.
