**Claim.** For $n=10$ and $n=11$, every family of trees $T_2,\ldots,T_n$
with $|T_k|=k$ packs into $K_n$. This is the computer verification of
David R. Guichard and John D. Massman, *A note on packing complete graphs with
trees*, J. Combin. Math. Combin. Comput. **8** (1990), 123--126 (no DOI; the
volume carries a year and no month, so this page is dated to 1990 with a
nominal day). The note generates Fishburn's universally recursive families
$\mathcal U_8$ and $\mathcal U_9$ by computer and shows directly that the
one exceptional tree in each case still packs, "proving the Gyárfás--Lehel
conjecture for $n=10$" and "for $n=11$" (p. 124); its abstract adds that
an approach suggested by Fishburn is unlikely to work in general. The corpus
holds the publisher's copy on its
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/guichard_1990_note_packing_complete_graphs_trees/_index|card]]
and pages the verification at
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/guichard_1990_note_packing_complete_graphs_trees/verification_p124|verification_p124]].
The note is not a site key; a thread comment of 27 August 2026 named it.

**Covers.** The instances of [[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0743/_index|Problem 743]]
with $n=10$ and $n=11$, every family of trees included; for them the
answer is yes. With
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0743/claims/1983_09_01_fishburn|Fishburn's verification]]
for $n\le9$, every $n\le11$ is settled, and $n=12$ is the first order
no published exhaustive check covers.

**Depends on.**
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0743/claims/1983_09_01_fishburn|Fishburn's verification]]:
the note follows Fishburn's route, splitting $K_n$ into his half-complete
graphs $H_{n-1}$ and $H_n$ (p. 124); for $n=10$ the odd-order trees
$T_3,\ldots,T_9$ pack into $H_9$ by Fishburn's $H_9\in\mathcal U_9$, and the
families $\mathcal U_8$ and $\mathcal U_9$ are generated by computer from his
hand-made $\mathcal U_6$ and $\mathcal U_7$ (pp. 124--125); the note's own
computation packs the even-order trees into $H_{10}$ and the odd-order trees
into $H_{11}$.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: the Journal of Combinatorial Mathematics and
Combinatorial Computing, volume 8 (zbMATH record read). The
site's commentary does not name the note, and the site labels the problem
FALSIFIABLE, an open problem, so no `reviewed` evidence exists. The
computation itself has not been repeated, and no independent review is
supplied.
