***

**Claim.** Corollary 2 of D. Aldous, *Brownian excursions, critical random
graphs and the multiplicative coalescent*, Ann. Probab. **25** (1997), no. 2,
812--854. Fix a real $t$ and let $C_n^t(j)$ be the number of vertices of the
$j$-th largest component of the random graph on $n$ vertices in which each
edge is present independently with probability $n^{-1}+tn^{-4/3}$. Then the
sequence $(n^{-2/3}C_n^t(j))_{j\ge1}$ converges in distribution, in $\ell^2$,
to the ordered sequence of excursion lengths, above its running minimum, of
the Brownian motion with parabolic drift $W(s)+ts-s^2/2$. At $t=0$ the edge
probability is $1/n$, the parameter of
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0745/_index|Problem 745]]. The limit's
second excursion length is finite and positive almost surely, so
$n^{-2/3}L_2$ converges in law to an almost surely positive, finite limit and
$L_2=\Theta_{\mathbb P}(n^{2/3})$, where $L_2$ is the number of vertices of
the second largest component. This describes the size the problem asks for,
so the claim's value is solved. T. Łuczak, B. Pittel and J. C. Wierman, *The
structure of a random graph at the point of the phase transition*, Trans.
Amer. Math. Soc. **341** (1994), 721--748, had described the components of
order $n^{2/3}$ in the same window.

The statement follows the zbMATH review of the paper (Zbl 0877.60010, by
J. Franchi), which gives the main result in this form together with the
surplus of each component, and Theorem 1 of L. Addario-Berry, N. Broutin and
C. Goldschmidt, *The continuum limit of critical random graphs*
([arXiv:0903.4730](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.4730)), which restates it
with the drift $W(t)+t\lambda-t^2/2$ and cites it as Corollary 2 of the
paper. The paper is not held, and the page rests on these two statements.
