***

**Claim.** Let each edge of a graph on $n$ vertices be present
independently with probability $\lambda/n$ for a fixed $\lambda>0$, and
let $L_2$ be the number of vertices of its second largest component. At
$\lambda=1$, the parameter of
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0745/_index|Problem 745]],
$L_2=\Theta_{\mathbb P}(n^{2/3})$: for every $\varepsilon>0$ there are
$0<c<C$ and $N$ such that $cn^{2/3}\le L_2\le Cn^{2/3}$ with probability
at least $1-\varepsilon$ for all $n\ge N$. For every fixed $\lambda\ne1$,
$L_2$ has logarithmic order with high probability, and
$L_2/\log n\to(\lambda-1-\log\lambda)^{-1}$ in probability. This is a
description of the size of the second largest component at $p=1/n$, which
is what the problem asks for, so the claim's value is solved; it also says
that Erdős's expectation of an order about $\log n$ holds for fixed
$\lambda\ne1$ and fails at $\lambda=1$.

**Claimant.** Boris Alexeev posted the result on the site's discussion
thread on 25 August 2026, writing that they had formalized it, that the
source [KSS80] the site credits assumes $\lambda>1$ strictly, and that the
problem should perhaps say disproved. The Lean development in their
repository of formalized Erdős problems, linked above at a pinned commit,
was committed to the repository's main branch on 26 August 2026 (UTC) and
strengthened on 27 August 2026. The file
`src/latest/ErdosProblems/Erdos745.lean`, at the pinned commit, has no
authors block and names no informal author and no AI system;
its note `ErdosProblems/Erdos745.md` calls it a formalized proof of Erdős
Problem 745 for Mathlib v4.33.0. Its theorems are `erdos745_supercritical`
(the upper bound $A\log n$ with high probability for $1<\lambda$ and
$A>1/(\lambda-1-\log\lambda)$, which its docstring calls the corrected KSS
logarithmic upper bound), `erdos745_critical` (the $n^{2/3}$ order at
$\lambda=1$), `erdos745_noncritical_asymptotic` (the limit of
$L_2/\log n$ for fixed $\lambda\ne1$) and `erdos745`, the conjunction
of the supercritical and critical statements; the module folder
`Erdos745/` holds thirteen files (Model, Components, Moments, PairRatio,
EdgeLaw, TreeComponents, Prufer, TreeCounting, TreeMoments, CriticalLower,
CriticalUpper, MacroscopicUniqueness, Noncritical). The file has no
`#print axioms` line and contains no `sorry`. It names no claimant whose
result it formalizes, so it is an independent proof with its own page, not
a link on the page of [KSS80].

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki.

**Standing.** Claimed: this corpus has not built, audited or kernel-checked
the development, so no evidence is listed; at the search recorded on the
problem page, the site's label PROVED, which credits [KSS80], was unchanged,
and neither the site's page nor the community database cited the development;
the claim is not refereed. The order $n^{2/3}$ at $\lambda=1$ agrees with
Erdős and Rényi's 1960 statement for the largest component at $N\sim n/2$ and
with the refereed limit law of
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0745/claims/1997_04_01_aldous|Aldous 1997]],
the accepted full claim on which the problem's standing rests. This page would
become accepted if the development were built and audited.
