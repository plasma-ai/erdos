**Claim.** Let $G(n,M)$ be uniform on the labeled simple graphs with $n$
vertices and $M$ edges, let $L_i$ be the number of vertices of its $i$-th
largest component, and for an edge sequence $M_n$ put $\lambda_n=2M_n/n$ and
$a(x)=x-1-\log x$. The development's principal theorem is a five-regime
atlas of the sparse evolution. Fixed subcritical and supercritical: for
$\lambda_n\to\lambda\in(0,\infty)\setminus\{1\}$,
$L_2-(\log n-\tfrac52\log\log n)/a(\lambda_n)=O_{\mathbb P}(1)$, with an
explicit lattice limit law along subsequences, and for $\lambda>1$ the
largest component has $n(1-x(\lambda_n)/\lambda_n)+O_{\mathbb P}(\sqrt n)$
vertices, where $x(\lambda)\in(0,1)$ solves $xe^{-x}=\lambda e^{-\lambda}$,
and is with high probability the only component of order at least
$n^{2/3}$. Barely subcritical and supercritical: for
$\lambda_n=1\mp\varepsilon_n$ with $\varepsilon_n\to0$ and
$n\varepsilon_n^3\to\infty$, $L_2$ has an exact-rate threshold law with
scale $1/a(\lambda_n)$ in place of the quadratic normalization, and in the
supercritical case the giant component has
$2n\varepsilon_n+O(n\varepsilon_n^2)+O_{\mathbb P}(\sqrt{n/\varepsilon_n})$
vertices, with one absolute constant in the deterministic error term.
Critical: for every fixed real $\lambda$ and $(2M_n-n)/n^{2/3}\to\lambda$,
the vector $n^{-2/3}(L_1,\ldots,L_k)$ converges in distribution, for each
fixed $k$, to the ranked excursion lengths of $B(t)+\lambda t-t^2/2$ above
its running minimum, Aldous's limit; the second length is finite and
positive almost surely, so $L_2$ has order $n^{2/3}$ in probability in the
critical window. The development states this for the uniform model; the
problem [[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0745/_index|Problem 745]] fixes
the binomial model with edge probability $1/n$, whose edge count is
concentrated at $n/2$ within $O_{\mathbb P}(\sqrt n)$, inside the critical
regime with $\lambda=0$, and Erdős's 1981 paper poses the question for the
uniform model with $k$ edges as $k$ varies. The passage from the uniform
model to the binomial one is not part of the development. The result
describes the size of the second largest component at the asked parameter
and throughout the sparse evolution, which is what the problem asks for, so
the claim's value is solved; it agrees with Boris Alexeev's development on
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0745/claims/2026_08_25_alexeev|the claim page]]
that Erdős's expectation of an order about $\log n$ holds away from the
critical point and fails at it.

finite-rank convergence at continuity points rather than the full $\ell^2$
limit; its `formalization.yaml` reports no `sorry` in the proof closure
and the axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`, with the
