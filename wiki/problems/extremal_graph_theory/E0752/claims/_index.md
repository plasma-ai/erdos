# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0752/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0752/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0752/claims/1999_04_01_erdos_faudree_rousseau_schelp|1999_04_01_erdos_faudree_rousseau_schelp]]: Erdős, Faudree, Rousseau and Schelp prove the case s = 2 of Problem 752, that
a graph of minimum degree k and girth at least five has order k^2 distinct
cycle lengths; Discrete Math. 200 (1999), known through later citations.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0752/claims/2007_07_14_sudakov_verstraete|2007_07_14_sudakov_verstraete]]: Sudakov and Verstraëte prove that a graph of average degree d and girth g has
order d^floor((g-1)/2) consecutive even cycle lengths, which gives the order
k^s distinct cycle lengths Problem 752 asks for; Combinatorica 28 (2008).
