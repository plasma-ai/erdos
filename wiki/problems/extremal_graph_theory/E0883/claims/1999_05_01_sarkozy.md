***

**Claim.** The second question of
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0883/_index|Problem 883]] has a positive
answer: for every $\ell\ge1$ there is $n_0(\ell)$ such that for $n\ge n_0(\ell)$
and every $A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}$ with
$|A|>\lfloor n/2\rfloor+\lfloor n/3\rfloor-\lfloor n/6\rfloor$, the coprime
graph $G(A)$ contains a complete tripartite graph $K(1,\ell,\ell)$ on $2\ell+1$
vertices. This follows from Theorem 1 of G. N. Sárközy, *Complete tripartite
subgraphs in the coprime graph of integers*, Discrete Math. 202 (1999), no. 1-3,
227--238: there are constants $c,n_0$ such that if $n\ge n_0$ and $|A|>f(n,2)$,
where $f(n,2)$ counts the integers up to $n$ divisible by $2$ or by $3$, that
is, $f(n,2)=\lfloor n/2\rfloor+\lfloor n/3\rfloor-\lfloor n/6\rfloor$, then
$K(1,\ell,\ell)\subseteq G(A)$ for $\ell=\lfloor c\log n/\log\log\log n\rfloor$.
Since this $\ell$ tends to infinity with $n$, every fixed $\ell$ is reached for
all large $n$. The paper poses the result as the answer to the question Erdős
raised in his last problem collection after the odd-cycle theorem of Erdős and
Sárközy
([[../library/extremal_graph_theory/erdos_1997_cycles_coprime_graph_integers/_index|card]]),
and deduces Theorem 1 from two cases split by the number of members of $A$
congruent to $1$ or $5$ modulo $6$: Theorem 2 treats the case where that number
is at most $c_1n$ and gives $\ell$ of order $\log n/\log\log\log n$, and Theorem
3 the case where it is at least $\varepsilon n$ and gives $\ell$ of order
$\log n$. The author remarks that the class of size $1$ cannot be enlarged,
since for $A$ the set of integers up to $n$ divisible by $2$ or $3$ together
with $5$ every complete tripartite subgraph of $G(A)$ has a class of one vertex,
and asks for the largest possible $\ell$. The library card is
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/sarkozy_1999_complete_tripartite_subgraphs_coprime_graph_integers/_index|source card]].
The site's commentary credits the paper with the weaker bound
$\ell\gg\log n/\log\log n$.

**Covers.** The second question, the part `tripartite`, for every fixed
$\ell$, with the explicit growth $\ell=\lfloor c\log n/\log\log\log n\rfloor$.
Not covered: the first question, on odd cycles, which is the subject of the
pending claims of
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0883/claims/2026_07_27_della_pietra|Della Pietra]]
and
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0883/claims/2026_10_05_pan|Pan]]; and the
best possible order of $\ell$, which the paper leaves open.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki; the argument is the paper's own.
