***

**Claim.** Two results of C. S. Edwards on the degree sum of a clique. First,
for $2\le r\le8$ and $n\ge r^2$, every graph with $n$ vertices and
$m\ge t_r(n)$ edges has a clique on $r$ vertices whose degree sum is at least
$2rm/n$. Second, for every $r\ge2$, a graph with $n$ vertices and
$m>(r-1)n^2/2r$ edges has such a clique; since $(r-1)n^2/2r\ge t_r(n)$, this
is the conclusion under a stronger hypothesis on $m$, and for $r=3$ it is a
triangle of degree sum at least $6m/n$ when $m>n^2/3$. The papers are C. S.
Edwards, *Complete subgraphs with largest sum of vertex degrees*,
Combinatorics (Proc. Fifth Hungarian Colloq., Keszthely, 1976), Vol. I,
Colloq. Math. Soc. János Bolyai 18, North-Holland (1978), 293--306, and C. S.
Edwards, *The largest vertex degree sum for a triangle in a graph*, Bull.
London Math. Soc. 9 (1977), no. 2, 203--208, DOI 10.1112/blms/9.2.203. Both
are unread; the statements above are those of the introduction of Bollobás and
Nikiforov's paper (p. 2;
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/bollobas_2005_sum_degrees_cliques/_index|card]]):
"Edwards [3], [4] proved (2) under the weaker condition $m>(r-1)n^2/2r$; he
also proved that the conjecture holds for $2\le r\le8$ and $n\ge r^2$", where
(2) is the conjectured bound and "weaker" is the paper's word for the stronger
condition. The site's commentary credits the first result to the 1978 paper.
Erdős and Laskar's note (Congr. Numer. 48 (1985), p. 82;
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/erdos_1985_note_size_chordal_subgraph/_index|card]])
attests the 1977 paper only in a weaker form, a triangle of degree sum at
least $2n$ when $m\ge n^2/3$. Both results prove parts of the corrected
Statement of [[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0904/_index|Problem 904]].

**Covers.** (a) $2\le r\le8$ with $n\ge r^2$ and every $m\ge t_r(n)$; (b)
every $r\ge2$ and $n\ge r$ with $m>(r-1)n^2/2r$, which leaves out the edge
counts $t_r(n)\le m\le(r-1)n^2/2r$. The whole statement is the accepted full
claim
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0904/claims/2004_10_08_bollobas_nikiforov|Bollobás--Nikiforov]].

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki; the results rest on the cited papers
alone.

**Acceptance.** Reviewed: the site's curator, T. F. Bloom, credits Edwards
[Ed78] with the range $2\le r\le8$, $n\ge r^2$ in the problem's commentary on
a page labeled PROVED (LEAN), and Bollobás and Nikiforov's refereed paper
credits both results in its introduction. The range (a) rests on the Bolyai
proceedings volume, which no record shows was refereed, so `refereed` is not
listed although the 1977 paper appeared in the Bulletin of the London
Mathematical Society.

**Dating.** The page is dated by the year of the Bolyai volume, 1978; the
month and day are placeholders. The colloquium met in 1976, and the triangle
case appeared in 1977.
