# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0904/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0904/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0904/claims/1978_01_01_edwards|1978_01_01_edwards]]: Edwards (Bolyai 18, 1978; Bull. London Math. Soc. 1977) proves the conjecture
for r from 2 to 8 with n at least r^2, and for every r when m exceeds
(r-1)n^2/2r; the results as Bollobás and Nikiforov state them.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0904/claims/1992_09_01_faudree|1992_09_01_faudree]]: Faudree (J. Graph Theory 1992) proves the Bollobás–Erdős conjecture for every
r at least 2 when n exceeds r^2(r-1)/4; the paper is unread, the range as
Bollobás and Nikiforov and the site's commentary state it.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0904/claims/2004_10_08_bollobas_nikiforov|2004_10_08_bollobas_nikiforov]]: Theorem 2 of Bollobás and Nikiforov (Electron. J. Combin. 2005) proves the
Bollobás–Erdős conjecture for n at least r, an r-clique of degree sum at
least 2rm/n; accepted on the refereed publication and the site's credit.
