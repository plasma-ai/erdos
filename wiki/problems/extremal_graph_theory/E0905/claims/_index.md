# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0905/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0905/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0905/claims/1977_01_01_edwards|1977_01_01_edwards]]: Edwards's unpublished 1977 manuscript proves the n/6 book bound, credited by
the site and by Bollobás and Nikiforov as an independent proof and by Erdős as
a proof of the conjecture; accepted on the site's curator's credit, the proof
itself never published.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0905/claims/1979_10_01_khadzhiivanov_nikiforov|1979_10_01_khadzhiivanov_nikiforov]]: Khadzhiivanov and Nikiforov (C. R. Acad. Bulgare Sci. 1979) prove that every
graph with n vertices and more than n^2/4 edges has an edge on at least n/6
triangles; accepted on the 1988 reproof, Fox and Loh, and the site's credit.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0905/claims/2004_05_05_bollobas_nikiforov|2004_05_05_bollobas_nikiforov]]: Corollary 3 of Bollobás and Nikiforov, Books in graphs (European J. Combin.
2005), gives every graph with n vertices and more than n^2/4 edges a book of
size greater than n/6; accepted on the refereed publication.
