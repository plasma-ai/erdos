# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0914/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0914/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0914/claims/1952_01_01_dirac|1952_01_01_dirac]]: Dirac (Proc. London Math. Soc. 1952) proves that a graph on n >= 3 vertices
with minimum degree at least n/2 has a Hamiltonian cycle, which gives the
case r = 2 through a perfect matching; refereed, site-credited.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0914/claims/1963_09_01_corradi_hajnal|1963_09_01_corradi_hajnal]]: Corrádi and Hajnal (Acta Math. Acad. Sci. Hungar. 1963) prove that a graph
on at least 3k vertices with minimum degree at least 2k has k disjoint
cycles, which at 3m vertices gives the case r = 3; refereed, site-credited.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0914/claims/1970_01_01_hajnal_szemeredi|1970_01_01_hajnal_szemeredi]]: Hajnal and Szemerédi (1970) prove Erdős's conjecture that every graph with
rm vertices and minimum degree at least m(r−1) has m disjoint copies of K_r;
accepted on the site's credit and Kierstead and Kostochka's refereed reproof.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0914/claims/2008_03_01_kierstead_kostochka|2008_03_01_kierstead_kostochka]]: Kierstead and Kostochka (Combin. Probab. Comput. 2008) give a short proof of
the Hajnal–Szemerédi theorem, whose clique form is Problem 914; refereed,
credited by the site, and formalized in an unbuilt external Lean file.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0914/claims/2010_03_01_kierstead_kostochka_mydlarz_szemeredi|2010_03_01_kierstead_kostochka_mydlarz_szemeredi]]: Theorem 1 of Kierstead, Kostochka, Mydlarz and Szemerédi (Combinatorica 2010)
restates the Hajnal–Szemerédi theorem and proves it algorithmically; the
refereed text behind the problem's standing.
