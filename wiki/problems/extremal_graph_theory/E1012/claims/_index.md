# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1012/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1012/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1012/claims/1961_12_01_ore|1961_12_01_ore]]: Ore (Ann. Mat. Pura Appl. 1961), Theorem 4.3: a graph on n vertices with at
least binom(n-1,2) + 2 edges has a Hamilton circuit, sharp by K_{n-1} with
a pendant edge; this gives f(0) = 1; refereed.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1012/claims/1971_09_01_bondy|1971_09_01_bondy]]: Bondy (Discrete Math. 1971), Theorem 2: a graph of order n and size at least
(n^2 - 5n + 14)/2 = binom(n-2,2) + 4 has a cycle of length n - 1, sharp for
n > 4; this gives f(1) = 1; refereed.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1012/claims/1972_05_01_woodall|1972_05_01_woodall]]: Woodall's Corollary 11.1 (Proc. London Math. Soc. 1972) gives, on n at
least 2k + 3 vertices with the problem's edge count, a circuit of every
length from 3 to n minus k: f(k) is at most 2k + 3, the count sharp there.
