**Claim.** There is an explicit continuous function $B$ on $[0,1/2]$ such that
$f(n,k)=n^2B(k/n^2)+o(n^2)$ uniformly in $k$: for every $\eta>0$ there is
$N$ such that

$$
\left|\frac{f(n,k)}{n^2}-B\!\left(\frac k{n^2}\right)\right|<\eta
\qquad\text{for all } n\ge N \text{ and all } 0\le k\le\binom n2.
$$

Here $f(n,k)$ is the least $m$ such that every graph on $n$ vertices with
exactly $k$ edges has its edge set partitioned into at most $m$ cliques, each
with at least two vertices. The function $B$ is defined as follows. Put
$\varepsilon=1/2-d$. For $a>0$ let $j=\max(1,\lceil(1-a)/a\rceil)$ and

$$
H(a)=\frac{2(j+1)a-(2j+1)a^2-1}{j(j+1)},\qquad H(0)=0 .
$$

The mixed curve uses
$\mu(s)=(2+16s+33s^2-15s^4)/(2(s^2+8s+2)^2)$ and
$M(s)=(6+46s+85s^2-16s^3-16s^4)/(6(s^2+8s+2)^2)$; let $\sigma_d$ be the
root of $29s^3-16s^2-14s-2$ in $[1,11/10]$, $\varepsilon_d=\mu(\sigma_d)$,
$\sigma_0=(3+\sqrt{21})/6$ and $\varepsilon_*=(51-9\sqrt{21})/100$. Then
$W(\varepsilon)=H(\sqrt{2\varepsilon})$ for $\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_*$,
$W(\varepsilon)=M(\sigma)$ with $\sigma\in[\sigma_d,\sigma_0]$ and
$\mu(\sigma)=\varepsilon$ for $\varepsilon_*<\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_d$,
and $W(\varepsilon)=\varepsilon$ for $\varepsilon>\varepsilon_d$. The
two-head curve uses $\tau(z)=z^2(13z^2-16z+5)/(16(z^2+z-1)^2)$ and
$T(z)=\tau(z)+(3z-2)^2(1-z)(3z-1)/(24(z^2+z-1)^2)$; let $z_c$ be the root
of $-95z^4+18z^3+193z^2-168z+40$ in $[3/4,4/5]$ and
$\varepsilon_c=\tau(z_c)$. Then $E_2(0)=0$,
$E_2(\varepsilon)=H(\sqrt{2\varepsilon})$ for
$0<\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_c$, and $E_2(\varepsilon)=T(z)$ with
$z\in[2/3,z_c]$ and $\tau(z)=\varepsilon$ for $\varepsilon>\varepsilon_c$.
Finally

$$
B(d)=\begin{cases}
d, & d\le 1/4,\\
\max\bigl(W(1/2-d),\,E_2(1/2-d)\bigr), & 1/4<d<1/2,\\
0, & d\ge 1/2.
\end{cases}
$$

The roots and the inverse values above are unique in their intervals. So, for
$k>n^2/4$, the bound $[n^2/4]$ of Erdős, Goodman and Pósa is replaced by
$n^2B(k/n^2)+o(n^2)$, and the leading term is exact.

**Submission note.** The Palomar registry's description of entry
PALOMAR-2026-11-11-000003, registered on 11 November 2026 by a maintainer of the
repository, whose `formalization.yaml` names Ryan Pettry, Jacob Parish and
Andrew Turner as its responsible maintainers:

> A Lean proof that f(n, k), the least number such that the edges of every
> graph with n vertices and k edges can be partitioned into at most f(n, k)
> cliques, satisfies f(n, k) = n² B(k/n²) + o(n²) uniformly in 0 ≤ k ≤ n(n −
> 1)/2, for an explicit continuous piecewise-algebraic function B on [0, 1/2].
> B(d) = d for d ≤ 1/4; above 1/4 it is the larger of two curves built from
> explicit dual certificates on split, mixed and two-head graphs. This
> provides an asymptotic resolution of Erdős Problem 1017, as posed by Erdős
> and as stated by Bloom, who asks for an estimate of f(n, k) for k > n²/4.
> The formula is new to our knowledge. The proof passes through the fractional
> relaxation and proves again, in Lean, the case of the fractional-to-integral
> transfer of Balogh, He, Krueger, Nguyen and Wigal that it needs.

**Covers.** The whole Statement of
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1017/_index|Problem 1017]]: an asymptotic
formula for $f(n,k)$, to within $o(n^2)$, at every edge density, which is an
estimate of $f(n,k)$ for $k>n^2/4$. It gives no rate of convergence, no exact
value of $f(n,k)$ and no description of the extremal graphs. Where
$k-n^2/4=o(n^2)$, Győri's bounds recorded on the problem page are more
precise, and as $k/n^2\to1/2$, where $B\to0$, the formula gives only
$f(n,k)=o(n^2)$.

**Argument and formalization.** Blow-ups of split graphs, mixed graphs and
two-head graphs (complete multipartite graphs with two large parts and all
other parts of size one) carry explicit feasible solutions of the dual of the
fractional clique partition problem, which give the lower bound. The integer
transfer from fractional to integral partitions, the case used here of
Theorem 1.10 of Balogh, He, Krueger, Nguyen and Wigal, is proved again in Lean.
The upper bound excludes, case by case, every possible location of a positive
maximum of the difference between the fractional extremum and $B$, using limit
models of extremal graphs and kernel-checked certificates. The separate Lean
project [plasma-ai/erdos-1017](https://github.com/plasma-ai/erdos-1017/tree/cf808826d555289332becdf4fd86de962ca00dfa)
supplies `Erdos1017.uniform_asymptotic`; its exact version, statement
conventions, authorship and checking status are recorded on the
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/plasma_ai_2026_clique_partitions_graphs_given_size/_index|formalization source card]].
The repository also holds a prose blueprint and a companion paper, neither
refereed.
