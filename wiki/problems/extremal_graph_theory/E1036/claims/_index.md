# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1036/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1036/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1036/claims/1989_05_01_erdos_hajnal|1989_05_01_erdos_hajnal]]: Theorem 2 of Erdős and Hajnal (Discrete Math. 75 (1989)): a graph on n
vertices with no K_{c log n, c log n} in it or its complement has at least
2^{n/4k} non-isomorphic induced subgraphs for k > 2c log 2 and n large.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1036/claims/1997_07_15_shelah|1997_07_15_shelah]]: Theorem 1.3 of Shelah (J. Combin. Theory Ser. A 82 (1998)) proves that a
graph on n vertices, n large, with no clique or independent set on c_1 log n
vertices has at least 2^{c_2 n} non-isomorphic induced subgraphs.
