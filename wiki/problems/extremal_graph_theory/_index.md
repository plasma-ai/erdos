# Extremal and Structural Graph Theory

[[problems/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0022/_index|E0022/]]: Asks whether some graph
on n vertices has at least n squared over 8 edges, no complete subgraph on 4
vertices, and no large independent set.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0023/_index|E0023/]]: Asks whether every
triangle-free graph on 5n vertices can be made bipartite by deleting at most n
squared edges.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0024/_index|E0024/]]: Asks whether every
triangle-free graph on 5n vertices contains at most n to the fifth power many
5-cycles.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0059/_index|E0059/]]: Asks whether the number
of graphs on n vertices containing no copy of a fixed graph is at most two
raised to nearly the extremal number of edges.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0060/_index|E0060/]]: Asks whether every graph
on n vertices with more edges than the extremal number for four-cycles contains
at least about the square root of n four-cycles.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0061/_index|E0061/]]: Asks whether every graph
on n vertices with no induced copy of a fixed graph has a clique or an
independent set of size at least a fixed power of n.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0062/_index|E0062/]]: Asks whether two graphs
of chromatic number aleph-one must share a common subgraph of chromatic number
four, or even of countably infinite chromatic number.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0064/_index|E0064/]]: Asks whether every
finite graph with minimum degree at least three contains a cycle whose length is
a power of two with exponent at least two.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0065/_index|E0065/]]: Asks whether the
reciprocals of the distinct cycle lengths of a graph with n vertices and kn
edges sum to at least a constant times log k, and whether a complete bipartite
graph minimizes that sum.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0071/_index|E0071/]]: Asks whether each
infinite arithmetic progression with even numbers has a degree bound forcing
every graph of that average degree to have such a cycle length.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0072/_index|E0072/]]: Asks whether some set of
integers of density zero meets the cycle lengths of every large graph whose
average degree is at least a fixed constant.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0073/_index|E0073/]]: Asks whether a graph
whose every n-vertex subgraph has an independent set of at least (n-k)/2
vertices becomes bipartite after deleting a number of vertices bounded in terms
of k.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0081/_index|E0081/]]: Asks whether the edges
of any chordal graph on n vertices can be partitioned into about n squared over
6 cliques.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0082/_index|E0082/]]: Asks whether the largest
regular induced subgraph guaranteed in every graph on n vertices has size
growing faster than the logarithm of n.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0084/_index|E0084/]]: Asks whether the number
f(n) of cycle sets of graphs on n vertices is o(2^n), and whether f(n)/2^(n/2)
tends to infinity.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0086/_index|E0086/]]: Asks whether every
subgraph of the n-dimensional hypercube with slightly more than half of its
edges must contain a four-cycle.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0113/_index|E0113/]]: Asks whether a bipartite
graph has Turan number O(n^(3/2)) exactly when it has no induced subgraph of
minimum degree at least three.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0127/_index|E0127/]]: Asks whether the excess
over a known bound in the number of edges of a largest bipartite subgraph of a
graph with m edges is unbounded along some sequence of m.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0128/_index|E0128/]]: Asks whether a graph on
n vertices whose every induced subgraph on at least half the vertices has more
than n squared over fifty edges has a triangle.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0133/_index|E0133/]]: Determines the growth of
the largest degree forced in every triangle-free graph on n vertices of diameter
two, in particular whether it beats root n.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0134/_index|E0134/]]: Asks whether a
triangle-free graph on n vertices with maximum degree below n to the power one
half minus epsilon can reach diameter two by adding few edges.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0136/_index|E0136/]]: Determines the least
number of edge colors of the complete graph on n vertices such that every four
vertices span at least five colors.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0146/_index|E0146/]]: Asks whether a bipartite
r-degenerate graph has extremal number at most n to the power two minus one over
r; disproved at r equal to two by Theorem 1.2 of Chapter 10 of OpenAI's 2026
report, credited by the site's curator.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0147/_index|E0147/]]: Asks whether every
bipartite graph of minimum degree r has extremal number at least order n to the
power two minus one over r minus one, plus a positive gain.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0149/_index|E0149/]]: Asks whether the strong
chromatic index of any graph, the least number of induced matchings partitioning
its edges, is at most five quarters of the squared maximum degree; open, with
the refereed record at 1.772 times it.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0150/_index|E0150/]]: Asks whether the n-th
root of the maximum number of minimal disconnecting vertex sets of a graph on n
vertices tends to a limit below two; proved, the limit lying between 1.4457 and
the golden ratio by refereed papers.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0151/_index|E0151/]]: Asks whether every graph
on n vertices has a clique transversal of at most n minus the triangle-free
independence bound H(n) vertices (Erdős–Gallai); open; best bounds n − √(2n) +
√2 (explicit) and n − c√(n log n) (asymptotic).

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0167/_index|E0167/]]: Asks whether a graph
with at most k edge-disjoint triangles can be made triangle-free by deleting at
most 2k edges (Tuza's conjecture); open, the site's label falsifiable, with
Haxell's 66/23 the best refereed constant.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0180/_index|E0180/]]: Asks whether every
finite family of forbidden graphs contains one member whose own extremal edge
count is comparable to that of the whole family.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0182/_index|E0182/]]: The maximum number of
edges on n vertices with no k-regular subgraph, and whether it is barely more
than linear in n, for every k at least 3.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0184/_index|E0184/]]: Asks whether every graph
on n vertices decomposes into at most a constant times n edge-disjoint cycles
and single edges, the Erdős-Gallai cycle decomposition conjecture; answered yes
by the OpenAI release's 2026 theorem.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0426/_index|E0426/]]: Asks whether some graph
on n vertices has as many as two to the number of vertex pairs divided by n
factorial distinct subgraphs occurring in exactly one way.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0500/_index|E0500/]]: Asks for the largest
number of triples on n vertices with no four vertices carrying all four of their
triples, Turán's tetrahedron problem; the density lies between Turán's 5/9 and a
flag-algebra bound of 0.5615.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0533/_index|E0533/]]: Asks whether a graph on
n vertices with no complete subgraph on five vertices and a positive edge
density has a triangle-free set of linearly many vertices.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0548/_index|E0548/]]: Records the proved
Erdős–Sós tree edge bound and the precise relation between its sharp threshold
and the site's statement.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0571/_index|E0571/]]: Every rational exponent
in [1,2) is realized by the Turán number of a finite bipartite graph; records
the 2026 proof, accepted on Lean built here, and its exposition qualifications.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0572/_index|E0572/]]: Asks whether, for every
k at least three, some graph on n vertices with no cycle of length two k has at
least a constant times n to the power one plus one over k edges; known for k
equal to 3 and 5, open for every other k.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0573/_index|E0573/]]: Asks whether the most
edges a graph on n vertices can have with no triangle and no four-cycle is
asymptotic to n over two to the power three halves; the ratio is known only to
lie between one and the square root of two.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0574/_index|E0574/]]: Asks whether the most
edges on n vertices avoiding cycles of length two k minus one and two k is
asymptotic to n over two to the power one plus one over k, for k above one;
disproved at k equal to 3 and 5.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0575/_index|E0575/]]: Asks whether the
extremal number of a finite family with a bipartite member is within a constant
factor of some bipartite member's; false as written for two forests, and false
for cyclic bipartite families by OpenAI's 2026 report.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0576/_index|E0576/]]: Determines how many
edges a graph on n vertices can have without containing the k-dimensional
hypercube graph; for the cube the order lies between n to the three halves and n
to the eight fifths, refuting Erdős's first guess.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0577/_index|E0577/]]: Asks whether every graph
on four k vertices with minimum degree at least two k contains k vertex-disjoint
four-cycles; the Erdős-Faudree conjecture, proved by Wang in 2010 as Theorem B
of a refereed paper.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0578/_index|E0578/]]: Asks whether a random
graph on two to the d vertices with each edge included with probability one half
almost surely contains a d-dimensional hypercube; proved by Riordan for every
fixed edge probability above one quarter.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0579/_index|E0579/]]: Asks whether a large
dense graph on n vertices with no complete tripartite subgraph having two
vertices per class must have a linear independent set; false at edge density
3/2048 by a Lean construction Conjectures.io certified.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0580/_index|E0580/]]: Asks whether every graph
on n vertices in which at least half the vertices have degree at least n over
two contains every tree on at most n over two vertices; proved by Zhao for large
n, with finitely many orders unchecked.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0581/_index|E0581/]]: Determines the largest
number of edges of a bipartite subgraph that every triangle-free graph with m
edges must contain; known to the order m/2 + Theta(m^{4/5}) by Alon, the
exponent sharp and the exact value open.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0583/_index|E0583/]]: Asks whether every
connected graph on n vertices can be split into at most n over two rounded up
edge-disjoint paths; Gallai's path decomposition conjecture, proved for several
classes and open in general.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0584/_index|E0584/]]: Asks whether every graph
on n vertices with edge density delta, allowed to shrink as a power of n,
contains dense subgraphs in which any two edges lie together on a short cycle.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0585/_index|E0585/]]: Asks for the most edges
a graph on n vertices can have without two edge-disjoint cycles on exactly the
same vertex set; known to lie between n log log n and n times a power of log n.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0600/_index|E0600/]]: Asks whether the least
edge count forcing an edge in r triangles, when every edge lies in a triangle,
has differences going to infinity and ratios going to one.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0608/_index|E0608/]]: Asks whether every graph
on n vertices with more than a quarter of n squared edges has at least two
ninths of n squared edges lying on five-cycles.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0610/_index|E0610/]]: Bounds the clique
transversal number of an n-vertex graph; proved, with the answer n − Θ(√(n log
n)), by the Joret–Micek–Reed–Smid clique-coloring bound and Kim's triangle-free
graphs, under the site's PROVED (LEAN) label.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0611/_index|E0611/]]: Asks whether cliques of
linear size force a sublinear clique transversal and which clique size k_c(n)
forces a transversal below (1 − c)n; open, with k_c(n) ≥ n^{c'/log log n}
(infinitely many n) and τ ≤ n − √(kn) from 1992.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0612/_index|E0612/]]: Records the two-part
Erdős–Pach–Pollack–Tuza diameter bound for connected graphs with no K_{2r} or
K_{2r+1}: part (i) refuted in a refereed paper; part (ii) proved at r = 1, with
pending claims that refute it.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0614/_index|E0614/]]: Determines the fewest
edges of a graph on n vertices in which every set of k plus two vertices induces
a subgraph of maximum degree at least k.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0616/_index|E0616/]]: Asks for the best bound
t on the covering number of an r-uniform hypergraph, r at least three, in which
every subhypergraph on at most 3r - 3 vertices has covering number at most one.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0617/_index|E0617/]]: Asks whether r-coloring
the edges of a complete graph on r squared plus one vertices forces r plus one
vertices whose induced edges miss a color.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0618/_index|E0618/]]: Records Alon's
subquadratic triangle-free diameter-two completion theorem, the catalog's
notation and diameter qualifications, and the earlier bounded-degree results.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0619/_index|E0619/]]: Asks whether every
connected triangle-free graph can be augmented to diameter at most four, still
triangle-free, using fewer than (1-c)n edges.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0620/_index|E0620/]]: Asks how large a
triangle-free induced subgraph every K_4-free graph on n vertices must contain;
the Erdős–Rogers problem, known to within a logarithmic factor in the refereed
record and claimed to be sqrt(n log n) by a 2026 preprint.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0621/_index|E0621/]]: Compares the largest set
of edges meeting each triangle at most once with the fewest edges meeting every
triangle, in a graph on n vertices.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0622/_index|E0622/]]: Every regular graph of
degree n plus one on two n vertices has a positive proportion of cyclic vertex
subsets; the limiting constant is one half.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0641/_index|E0641/]]: Asks whether a high
enough chromatic number forces a graph to contain k edge-disjoint cycles on the
same vertex set, for every k.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0642/_index|E0642/]]: Asks whether a graph on
n vertices in which every cycle has more vertices than chords can have at most a
constant times n edges.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0666/_index|E0666/]]: Asks whether every
subgraph of the n-dimensional hypercube with a positive fraction of its edges
contains a six-cycle, once n is large enough.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0712/_index|E0712/]]: Asks for the Turán
density of the complete r-uniform hypergraph on k vertices for any k > r > 2;
Turán's 1941 theorem settles r = 2 and no pair with r > 2 is known, with Erdős's
two prize offers standing.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0713/_index|E0713/]]: Asks whether every
bipartite graph with at least two edges has extremal number asymptotic to a
constant times a power of n in [1, 2), and whether that power is rational;
Erdős's 1967 exponent shapes were disproved in 1970.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0714/_index|E0714/]]: Asks whether the largest
graph on n vertices with no complete bipartite subgraph with r vertices per side
has roughly n to the power 2 minus one over r edges.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0715/_index|E0715/]]: Asks whether every
4-regular graph contains a 3-regular subgraph, and whether some degree r forces
one in every r-regular graph; both answered yes by Tashkinov in 1982, for degree
4 and for every degree at least 3.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0717/_index|E0717/]]: Asks whether the
chromatic number of an n-vertex graph is at most a constant times n^{1/2}/log n
times the order of its largest clique subdivision; the Erdős–Fajtlowicz
conjecture, proved by Fox, Lee and Sudakov in 2013.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0718/_index|E0718/]]: Asks whether a constant
times r squared times n edges on n vertices always force a subdivision of the
complete graph on r vertices; the conjecture of Erdős, Hajnal and Mader, proved
by Bollobás–Thomason and Komlós–Szemerédi.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0742/_index|E0742/]]: Asks whether a graph on
n vertices of diameter two in which deleting any edge raises the diameter has at
most n squared over four edges; proved by Füredi for all large n, with one
unreviewed proof claim for every n.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0743/_index|E0743/]]: Asks whether the
complete graph on n vertices can always be split into edge-disjoint copies of
given trees with 2, 3, up to n vertices; the tree packing conjecture of Gyárfás,
open, with one arXiv proof claim withdrawn.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0745/_index|E0745/]]: Asks for the size of the
second largest component of the random graph on n vertices with edge probability
one over n; the site credits Komlós, Sulyok and Szemerédi's supercritical log n
bound; Aldous (1997) gives n^(2/3).

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0746/_index|E0746/]]: Asks whether the uniform
random graph on n vertices with slightly more than half of n times log n edges
is almost surely Hamiltonian; the Erdős-Rényi conjecture, settled by Korshunov
and by Komlós and Szemerédi.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0752/_index|E0752/]]: Asks whether a graph
with minimum degree k and no cycle of length at most twice s must have at least
a constant times k to the power s distinct cycle lengths.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0765/_index|E0765/]]: Asks for an asymptotic
formula for the largest number of edges of a graph on n vertices containing no
cycle of length four.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0766/_index|E0766/]]: Asks for estimates of
the least Turán number over all graphs with k vertices and l edges in the range
k < l ≤ k²/4, and whether it is strictly monotone in l; open, with asymptotics
known at the pairs (5,6) and (6,9) only.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0767/_index|E0767/]]: Asks whether the most
edges on n vertices with no cycle carrying k chords at one cycle vertex is
(k+1)n minus (k+1) squared for large n; proved for n at least 3k+3 by Jiang
(2004), with a 2026 preprint claiming the threshold.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0777/_index|E0777/]]: Asks how many comparable
pairs a family of m subsets of one to n can have for m near 2^(n/2); three
questions answered yes, no, yes by Alon and Frankl and by Alon, Das, Glebov and
Sudakov.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0778/_index|E0778/]]: Asks whether the first
player can win the game of alternately coloring edges of a complete graph so
that her largest monochromatic clique beats her opponent's.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0794/_index|E0794/]]: Asks whether every
three-uniform hypergraph on 3n vertices with more than n cubed edges has four
vertices spanning three edges; false as written by a 28-edge example, while the
Turán density of K_4 minus an edge stays open.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0802/_index|E0802/]]: Asks whether every
K_r-free graph on n vertices with average degree t has an independent set of
order n log t over t; true for r = 3 since 1980, and for every r at least 4 by a
Lean-checked theorem of the 2026 OpenAI release.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0803/_index|E0803/]]: Asks whether every graph
with n log n edges has an almost-regular subgraph on m vertices with much more
than m log m edges; false by Alon's 2008 random bipartite construction, with
Janzer and Sudakov's bound the best positive one.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0804/_index|E0804/]]: Estimates the largest
independent set forced in a graph on n vertices where every induced subgraph on
m vertices has an independent set of size log n.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0805/_index|E0805/]]: Characterizes the
functions g for which some graph on n vertices has every induced subgraph on g
of n vertices with a clique and independent set of size log n.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0807/_index|E0807/]]: Asks whether the random
graph with edge probability one half almost surely needs exactly n minus its
independence number complete bipartite graphs to partition its edges; disproved
by Alon, then by Alon, Bohman and Huang.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0813/_index|E0813/]]: Estimates the largest
clique forced in an n-vertex graph where every seven vertices span a triangle:
the exponent lies between 5/12 (Bucić and Sudakov) and 1/2 (Erdős and Hajnal),
and whether either end can be moved is open.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0814/_index|E0814/]]: Asks whether a graph
with one edge more than the count forcing a subgraph of minimum degree k has
such an induced subgraph on a constant fraction fewer vertices; proved by
Sauermann for k at least 3, with k = 2 elementary.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0815/_index|E0815/]]: Asks whether a graph
with n vertices, 2n - 2 edges and no proper induced subgraph of minimum degree 3
has a cycle of each fixed length k for large n; false at k = 23 (Narins,
Pokrovskiy, Szabó), true for k up to 6, even k open.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0816/_index|E0816/]]: Asks whether a graph
with 2n + 1 vertices and n^2 + n + 1 edges has two equal-degree vertices joined
by a path of length 3; corrected to n at least 2, since n = 1 (the triangle)
fails; proved for n at least 600 by Chen and Ma and claimed for every n at least
2 by Liu and Zeng.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0883/_index|E0883/]]: Asks whether a subset of
one to n above the triangle threshold of the coprime graph forces all odd cycles
up to n/3 + 1 and, for large n, complete (1, l, l) tripartite subgraphs; the
second was settled by Sárközy in 1999.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0900/_index|E0900/]]: Asks whether the uniform
random graph with n vertices and cn edges, c above one half, almost surely has a
path of length f(c)n with f tending to 0 at one half and to 1 at infinity;
proved by Ajtai, Komlós and Szemerédi, 1981.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0902/_index|E0902/]]: Estimates the least
order of a tournament in which every n vertices have a common dominator; open,
with Erdős's 1963 upper bound and the Szekeres and Szekeres lower bound of 1965
a factor of order n apart, exact only to n = 3.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0904/_index|E0904/]]: Asks whether, for n at
least r, a graph with n vertices and at least the Turán number of edges for r
plus one has an r-clique of degree sum at least 2rm/n; proved by Bollobás and
Nikiforov in 2005, while the site's wording, with no range, fails below r
vertices.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0905/_index|E0905/]]: Asks whether every graph
on n vertices with more than n^2/4 edges has an edge on at least n/6 triangles;
proved by Khadzhiivanov and Nikiforov in 1979, by Edwards (unpublished) and by
Bollobás and Nikiforov in 2005.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0914/_index|E0914/]]: Asks whether every graph
on rm vertices with minimum degree at least m(r−1) has m vertex-disjoint copies
of K_r; Erdős's conjecture, proved by Hajnal and Szemerédi in 1970 and reproved
in refereed papers of 2008 and 2010.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0915/_index|E0915/]]: Asks whether a graph
with 1+n(m−1) vertices and 1+n·C(m,2) edges has two vertices joined by m
disjoint paths; false for m at least 5 if the paths are vertex-disjoint, true
for every m if edge-disjoint; the site labels it solved.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0916/_index|E0916/]]: Asks whether, for n at
least 4, every graph with n vertices and 2n−2 edges has a cycle and a further
vertex adjacent to three of its vertices; proved by Thomassen in 1974, while the
site's wording, with no range, fails at n = 1.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0926/_index|E0926/]]: Bounds by n to the power
three halves the edges of an n-vertex graph avoiding a fixed graph made of a
vertex joined to k others whose pairs are linked.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0927/_index|E0927/]]: Asks whether the largest
number of distinct clique sizes in a graph on n vertices is n minus log_2 n
minus the iterated-logarithm count, up to O(1); disproved by Spencer, whose
construction removes the iterated term.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0934/_index|E0934/]]: The least number of
edges forcing a graph of maximum degree at most d to have two edges at distance
at least t; open, exact for t = 1, t = 2 and h_3(3) = 23, between 0.629^t d^t
and 3d^t/2 + 1 in general, with 2026 preprints at t = 3.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0993/_index|E0993/]]: Asks whether the
sequence counting the independent sets of each size in a tree or forest is
unimodal.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1006/_index|E1006/]]: Asks whether every graph
of girth at least five has an acyclic orientation that stays acyclic after any
one edge is reversed; false, by Nešetřil and Rödl's large-girth graphs with a
monotone cycle under every vertex ordering.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1007/_index|E1007/]]: The smallest number of
edges of a graph of dimension four, the least Euclidean dimension in which it
embeds with every edge a unit segment; nine, attained only by K_{3,3} up to
isolated vertices, by House and Chaffee-Noble.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1008/_index|E1008/]]: Asks whether every graph
with m edges has a four-cycle-free subgraph with at least a constant times
m^{2/3} edges; true (Conlon, Fox and Sudakov), while Bollobás and Erdős's first
form with m^{3/4} fails by Folkman's example.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1009/_index|E1009/]]: Asks whether, for each
positive c, a graph on n vertices with the Turán number plus k edges, k below
cn, has at least k minus f(c) edge-disjoint triangles; proved by Győri (1988), a
paper known only by attestation.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1010/_index|E1010/]]: Asks whether every graph
on n vertices with the Turán number plus t edges, t below half of n, has at
least t times the floor of half of n triangles; the Erdős-Rademacher conjecture,
proved in full by Lovász and Simonovits.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1011/_index|E1011/]]: Determines the least
number of edges forcing a triangle in a graph on n vertices whose chromatic
number is at least r; known exactly for r up to three, for r equal to four and n
large, and open in general.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1012/_index|E1012/]]: Determines or estimates
how large n must be, in terms of k, for a given edge count to force a cycle
through all but k of the n vertices; Woodall's 1972 Corollary 11.1 gives every n
at least 2k + 3 and covers the smaller n too.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1016/_index|E1016/]]: Estimates the fewest
edges beyond n for an n-vertex graph to have cycles of every length from three
to n; the excess is at least log_2(n-1) - 1, Bondy claimed log_2 n plus an
iterated logarithm, and a 2026 proof claim is pending.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1017/_index|E1017/]]: Estimates the number of
edge-disjoint complete graphs needed to partition the edges of a graph on n
vertices with more than n squared over 4 edges; open; Győri and Keszegh settle
the K_4-free case up to about n squared / 16.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1018/_index|E1018/]]: Asks whether every large
graph with at least n to the power one plus epsilon edges has a non-planar
subgraph of bounded size; answered yes by Kostochka and Pyber in 1988 through a
bounded subdivided K_5.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1019/_index|E1019/]]: Asks whether every graph
on n vertices with the Turán number plus half of n edges contains a saturated
planar subgraph on more than three vertices; proved by Simonovits in his thesis,
attested by Erdős and the site.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1021/_index|E1021/]]: Asks whether, for every
k at least three, the extremal number of the bipartite graph joining each pair
among k vertices to its own vertex beats n to the 1.5.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1031/_index|E1031/]]: Asks whether a graph on
n vertices with no empty or complete subgraph of size ten times the logarithm of
n has an induced non-trivial (neither empty nor complete) regular subgraph of
logarithmic size.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1033/_index|E1033/]]: Estimates the largest
degree sum forced on a triangle in a graph on n vertices with more than n²/4
edges, and asks whether it is at least about 1.464 n; open, between Fan's 21n/16
and a construction's 2(√3 − 1)n + O(1).

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1034/_index|E1034/]]: Asks whether a graph on
n vertices with more than n²/4 edges has a triangle to which nearly half of all
vertices are joined twice; the Erdős–Faudree conjecture, disproved by Ma and
Tang's construction with constant 2 − √(5/2).

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1035/_index|E1035/]]: Asks whether some
positive c makes minimum degree above one minus c times two to the n force an
n-dimensional hypercube in a graph on two to the n vertices.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1036/_index|E1036/]]: Asks whether a graph on
n vertices with no empty or complete subgraph of logarithmic size has
exponentially many pairwise non-isomorphic induced subgraphs.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1037/_index|E1037/]]: Asks whether a graph on
n vertices with each degree repeated at most twice and more than half of n
distinct degrees has a large empty or complete subgraph.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1066/_index|E1066/]]: Concerns the graph on n
plane points that are pairwise at least distance one apart, with edges joining
the pairs exactly distance one apart.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1077/_index|E1077/]]: Asks whether every graph
with n^{1+α} edges has an almost-regular subgraph on more than n^{1−α} vertices
with εm^{1+α} edges; false as written, with n^α the right size on the site's
account of a 2025 preprint.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1078/_index|E1078/]]: Asks whether an
r-partite graph with n vertices per part and minimum degree about (r − 3/2)n
must contain a complete graph on r vertices; proved by Haxell, with the exact
threshold from Haxell and Szabó by complementation.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1079/_index|E1079/]]: Asks whether a graph
with the Turán number of edges has a linear-degree vertex whose neighborhood has
the Turán number of edges for r − 1; proved by Bollobás and Thomason in 1981,
strengthened by Bondy in 1983.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1080/_index|E1080/]]: Asks whether a bipartite
graph on n vertices with a part of size about n^(2/3) and at least cn edges must
contain a six-cycle; disproved by the superlinear 6-cycle-free constructions
credited by the site, papers not held.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1111/_index|E1111/]]: Asks whether bounded
clique number and large chromatic number force two anticomplete vertex sets both
of large chromatic number; the El-Zahar-Erdős problem, open beyond the case of
chromatic number three.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1155/_index|E1155/]]: Asks for the typical
structure of the graph left by repeated uniform random triangle removal from K_n
and whether its edge count has order n^{3/2}; the sharp constant is formally
verified, and the typical structure is open.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1157/_index|E1157/]]: Asks for the most edges
an r-graph on n vertices can have with no k vertices spanning s edges, the
Brown-Erdős-Sós problem; the conjecture's s = 3 case and large-uniformity linear
form are proved; 3-graphs with k = s + 3, s ≥ 4, open.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1158/_index|E1158/]]: Asks whether Erdős's
1964 upper exponent for the Turán number of the complete t-partite t-uniform
hypergraph with r vertices per class is attained up to o(1); known only for t =
2 and r at most 3.
