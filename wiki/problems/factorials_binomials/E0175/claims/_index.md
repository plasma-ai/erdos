# problems/factorials_binomials/E0175/claims

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0175/_index|..]]

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0175/claims/1985_02_01_sarkozy|1985_02_01_sarkozy]]: The central binomial coefficient is not squarefree for every sufficiently
large n, proved by exponential sums in the Journal of Number Theory (1985)
without an explicit threshold.

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0175/claims/1995_01_01_velammal|1995_01_01_velammal]]: The central binomial coefficient is never squarefree for n greater than 4,
proved with Vaughan's identity and exponent pairs in the Hardy-Ramanujan
Journal (1995), independently of Granville and Ramaré.

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0175/claims/1996_06_01_granville_ramare|1996_06_01_granville_ramare]]: The central binomial coefficient is never squarefree for n greater than 4,
proved with explicit exponential-sum bounds in Mathematika (1996); the
argument has a third-party Lean formalization.
