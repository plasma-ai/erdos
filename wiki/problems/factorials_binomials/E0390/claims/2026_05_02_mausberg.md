***

**Claim.** For $f(n)$ the least $m$ such that $n!=a_1\cdots a_k$ with
$n<a_1<\cdots<a_k=m$, as in
[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0390/_index|Problem 390]], Samuel Mausberg's
note "A Thirteen-Layer Lower Bound for Erdős Problem #390" (dated 2026-05-02,
four pages, posted in the problem's discussion thread) proves as its Theorem 1
that

$$
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{f(n)-2n}{n/\log n}\geq C_0,\qquad
C_0=\frac{\sum_{r=1}^{13}1/((r+1)(2r+1))}{\sum_{p\leq23}1/(p-1)}
=\frac{4029639598}{25970038185}=0.15516\ldots
$$

The proof works in the complement form: $f(n)\le M$ holds exactly when
$M!/(n!)^2$ is a product of distinct integers in $(n,M]$. For $M=2n+h$ with
$h=O(n/\log n)$, every prime $P$ in one of the thirteen layers
$M/(2r+2)<P\le M/(2r+1)$, $n/(r+1)<P\le n/r$ ($1\le r\le13$) divides the
quotient exactly once, so it sits in exactly one factor $Pq$, and the cofactor
$q$ lies in $[r+1,2r+1]\subset[2,27]$ and so carries a prime at most $23$;
the layers hold $(\sum_{r\le13}1/((r+1)(2r+1))+o(1))\,n/\log n$ primes by
the prime number theorem, while the total valuation of the quotient at the
primes up to $23$ is $h\sum_{p\le23}1/(p-1)+O(\log n)$, and comparing the
two gives $h\ge(C_0-o(1))\,n/\log n$. The constant improves the lower-bound
constant of Erdős, Guy and Selfridge, which could be taken arbitrarily close
to $1/9$ ([EGS82], carded at
[[../library/factorials_binomials/erdos_1982_another_property_239_related_questions/_index|Erdős, Guy and Selfridge 1982]]),
and quantifies an obstruction Tao had described in the same thread. The
digest and result list are on the library card
[[../library/factorials_binomials/mausberg_2026_thirteen_layer_lower_bound_erdos_problem/_index|Mausberg 2026]].

**Covers.** The lower bound only: $\liminf(f(n)-2n)\log n/n\ge C_0$, so a
constant $c$ with $f(n)-2n\sim cn/\log n$, if one exists, is at least $C_0$.
The note's Remark 1 says that its bookkeeping is a necessary condition only,
and the note claims no upper bound and no asymptotic formula; whether the
constant exists, and its value, are the subject of the pending full claim
[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0390/claims/2026_07_18_wang|Wang 2026]],
whose lower bound rests on this note.
