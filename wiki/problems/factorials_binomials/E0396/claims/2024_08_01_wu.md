***

**Claim.** For $k=5$ there is an $n$ with
$\prod_{0\le i\le 5}(n-i)\mid\binom{2n}{n}$, as
[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0396/_index|Problem 396]] asks: the least
such $n$ is $3648841$, the term $a(5)$ of the OEIS entry A375077, which its
extension line credits to Chai Wah Wu on 1 August 2024. A witness is checked
through Kummer's theorem, as
[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0396/claims/2024_07_29_stephan|Stephan's page]]
explains.

**Covers.** The instance $k=5$ of the question, with the answer yes by an
explicit witness.
