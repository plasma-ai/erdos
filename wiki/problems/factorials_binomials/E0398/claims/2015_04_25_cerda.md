***

**Claim.** The statement of
[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0398/_index|Problem 398]], that $n=4,5,7$
are the only solutions of $n!=x^2-1$. S. Cerdá, *The Brocard-Ramanujan's
Diophantine Equation $n!+1=m^2$*, arXiv:1504.06694, first posted 25 April
2015, presents, as its abstract says, a method to find the solutions of the
equation, and the comment on its arXiv record says that the problem was
solved by modular congruence techniques. The page is dated by the first
posting.

**Standing.** Withdrawn. The arXiv record shows five versions: versions 1 and
2 of April 2015; version 3 of 30 April 2015, withdrawn; version 4 of 18
February 2016, a new version of the paper, whose comment records that the
earlier one had been withdrawn because a reviewer found a logical fallacy;
and version 5 of 2 March 2016, again withdrawn, with the comment that the
solution contains a mistake. No version was published, the manuscript was
never posted to the site, and no later claim by the author is known here.
The problem's standing takes nothing from this page.
