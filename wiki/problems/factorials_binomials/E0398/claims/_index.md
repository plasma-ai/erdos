# problems/factorials_binomials/E0398/claims

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0398/_index|..]]

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0398/claims/2015_04_25_cerda|2015_04_25_cerda]]: An arXiv manuscript of 2015 presenting a method to find all solutions of
n! + 1 = m^2 by modular congruences; withdrawn by its author in 2015 and
again in 2016, the last time noting a mistake in the solution.

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0398/claims/2017_02_14_sabihi|2017_02_14_sabihi]]: A ResearchGate manuscript claiming a short solution of n! = x^2 - 1, that
n = 4, 5, 7 are the only solutions, and a generalization; rejected after a
concrete error was exhibited in the site's discussion thread.

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0398/claims/2025_08_15_naciri|2025_08_15_naciri]]: Naciri proves that the only solutions of n! + 1 = x^2 with x+1 or x-1 7-free
are n = 4, 5, 7, and that n = 4 is the only one with x+1 or x-1 a prime
power (Integers 25 (2025), #A71).

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0398/claims/2026_02_05_maiti|2026_02_05_maiti]]: An arXiv manuscript claims, as Theorem 2.15 of its second version, that the
Brocard-Ramanujan equation has no solution beyond n = 4, 5, 7, from a
comparison of growth rates; unrefereed and unreviewed.
