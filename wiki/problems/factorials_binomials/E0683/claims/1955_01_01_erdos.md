***

**Claim.** Theorem 1 of [Er55d], printed p. 124, with $f(k)$ the least
integer such that the product of $f(k)$ consecutive integers, each greater
than $k$, always contains a prime greater than $k$: "There is a constant
$c_1>1$ so that $f(k)\le c_1\frac{k}{\log k}$." The constant is not
specified. The statement is the library result page
[[../library/factorials_binomials/erdos_1955_consecutive_integers/theorem_1|erdos_1955_consecutive_integers / theorem_1]].
The paper does not state a binomial form. For
[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0683/_index|Problem 683]] it applies to the
numerator block $n-k+1,\ldots,n$ of $\binom nk$, whose $k$ members all exceed
$k$ when $k\le n/2$: a prime greater than $k$ dividing a member of the block
is not canceled by $k!$, so it divides $\binom nk$. Applied with the threshold
$n-k$ in place of $k$, the theorem gives a prime factor greater than $n-k$,
hence $P(\binom nk)\ge n-k+1$, whenever $k\ge f(n-k)$, which holds when $n-k$
is at most a constant multiple of $k\log k$; applied with a threshold $m$ of
order $k\log k$ satisfying $f(m)\le k$, it gives $P(\binom nk)>m$ otherwise.
Together, $P(\binom nk)\gg\min(n-k+1,k\log k)$ for $k\le n/2$, the form the
formal-conjectures file records. The site prints the bound without the
minimum, and that form fails at $n=2k$, where $P(\binom{2k}k)\le2k$.

**Covers.** The instances with $k\le n/2$ and $n-k$ at most a constant
multiple of $k\log k$, where $P(\binom nk)\ge n-k+1$ and the inequality of the
problem, read as its Formulation states, holds for every $c>0$. Elsewhere the
theorem gives only $P(\binom nk)\gg k\log k$, no bound of the form $k^{1+c}$;
those instances stay open.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki; the result rests on the cited paper,
whose statement the library result page above records.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: P. Erdős, On consecutive integers, Nieuw Arch.
Wisk. (3) 3 (1955), 124--128, a journal paper; the issue carries no month, so
the page is dated to the year. The site's commentary credits the result to
this paper, but the site labels the problem OPEN, so the remark is not
acceptance of a settling claim and no `reviewed` evidence is listed. The
formal-conjectures statement file
[`FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/683.lean`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/6fbb54f24ccc2e64dcfaffc28c58950e377110d2/FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/683.lean)
records the bound with the minimum as the variant
`erdos_683.variant.erdos_log`, tagged research solved with a `sorry` body; a
statement file is not a formalization of the result.
