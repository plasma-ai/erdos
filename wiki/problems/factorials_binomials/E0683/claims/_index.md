# problems/factorials_binomials/E0683/claims

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0683/_index|..]]

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0683/claims/1934_10_01_erdos|1934_10_01_erdos]]: Erdős's 1934 proof of the Sylvester-Schur theorem: for n at least 2k the
binomial coefficient n choose k has a prime factor greater than k; refereed
in J. London Math. Soc., and by symmetry it settles n/2 <= k <= n-1.

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0683/claims/1955_01_01_erdos|1955_01_01_erdos]]: Theorem 1 of Erdős's 1955 paper on consecutive integers: any c_1 k / log k
consecutive integers above k include one with a prime factor greater than k;
refereed in Nieuw Arch. Wisk., it gives P(n choose k) >> min(n-k+1, k log k).
