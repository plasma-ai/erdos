# Problem 685

[[problems/factorials_binomials/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Let $\epsilon>0$ and $n$ be large depending on $\epsilon$. Is it
true that for all $n^\epsilon<k\leq n^{1-\epsilon}$ the number of distinct prime
divisors of $\binom{n}{k}$ is

$$
(1+o(1))k\sum_{k<p<n}\frac{1}{p}?
$$

Or perhaps even when $k \geq (\log n)^c$?
