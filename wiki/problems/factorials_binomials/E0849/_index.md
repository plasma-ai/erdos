# Problem 849

[[problems/factorials_binomials/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Is it true that, for every integer $t\geq 1$, there is some
integer $a$ such that

$$
\binom{n}{k}=a
$$

(with $1\leq k\leq n/2$) has exactly $t$ solutions?

**Status.** Open.

**Source.** [erdosproblems.com/849](https://www.erdosproblems.com/849), accessed
2026-09-04. Cite as: T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problem #849,
https://www.erdosproblems.com/849.
