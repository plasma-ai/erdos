***

Write $L(G)$ for the set of odd cycle lengths of a graph $G$. András
Gyárfás proves, as Theorem 1 of *Graphs with k odd cycle lengths*
([[../library/graph_coloring/gyarfas_1992_graphs_k_odd_cycle_lengths/_index|card]]),
that a $2$-connected graph with minimum degree at least $2k+1$ and
$|L(G)|=k\geq1$ is $K_{2k+2}$. His Corollary is the statement of
[[problems/graph_coloring/E0058/_index|Problem 58]]: if $|L(G)|=k\geq1$ then
$\chi(G)\leq2k+1$ unless some block of $G$ is a $K_{2k+2}$, in which case
$\chi(G)=2k+2$. Thus $\chi(G)\leq2k+2$, with equality only if $G$ contains
$K_{2k+2}$; conversely, a graph containing $K_{2k+2}$ whose odd cycles have at
most $k$ distinct lengths has exactly the lengths $3,5,\dots,2k+1$ and
chromatic number exactly $2k+2$. The case $k=0$ is the bipartite case, where
the statement is immediate. The proof takes a longest odd cycle $C$ and a
longest path in $G-V(C)$ and counts the odd cycle lengths produced by the
attachments of the path's ends to $C$. Gyárfás reports that Bollobás and Erdős
conjectured the bound, that Bollobás and Shelah had checked $k=1$, and that
Gallai suspected the stronger block form proved here.

**Formalization.** The Lean 4 file linked above, in Boris Alexeev's
repository of Lean proofs, declares itself a formalization of a solution to
the problem with Gyárfás as its informal author and names Codex and GPT-5.6
Sol as its formal authors. It treats finite graphs: its theorem `erdos_58`
states that a simple graph on a finite vertex type with at most $k$ odd
cycle lengths has chromatic number at most $2k+2$, with equality exactly when
it contains $K_{2k+2}$. The formal-conjectures catalog's statement file for
the problem
([`FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/58.lean`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/6fbb54f24ccc2e64dcfaffc28c58950e377110d2/FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/58.lean),
added 2026-09-09) states the problem with its proof left as `sorry` and names
no formal proof. This corpus has not built the file, so it is a link and not
`formalized` evidence.
