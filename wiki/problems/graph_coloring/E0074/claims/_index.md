# problems/graph_coloring/E0074/claims

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0074/claims/1982_12_01_rodl|1982_12_01_rodl]]: Rödl (Combinatorica, 1982), as the site credits him, builds for every fixed
epsilon > 0 a graph of infinite chromatic number whose n-vertex subgraphs
become bipartite after deleting at most epsilon n edges; the paper is not held.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0074/claims/2026_09_03_adamczewski|2026_09_03_adamczewski]]: GPT-6 Astra's disproof, published in Tom Adamczewski's Lean repository: for
some divergent f, every graph whose n-vertex subgraphs become bipartite after
f(n) edge deletions is 3-colorable; its Lean disproof was built here.
