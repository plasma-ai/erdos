***

**Claim.** Zi-Xia Song's preprint *The Erdős-Lovász Tihany Conjecture holds
for all even-hole-free graphs* (arXiv:2607.20376) proves, as its Theorem 1.4,
the following. Let $s,t\ge2$ be integers and let $G$ be a graph with no
induced $C_4$ in which every induced subgraph has a bisimplicial vertex, a
vertex whose neighborhood is the union of two cliques. If
$\omega(G)<\chi(G)=s+t-1$, then $G$ is $(s,t)$-splittable: its vertex set
splits into two parts inducing subgraphs of chromatic numbers at least $s$ and
at least $t$. Chudnovsky and Seymour proved that every nonempty even-hole-free
graph has a bisimplicial vertex, and an even-hole-free graph has no induced
$C_4$, so Theorem 1.4 gives the paper's Theorem 1.5: every even-hole-free graph
$G$ with $\omega(G)<\chi(G)=s+t-1$ is $(s,t)$-splittable. In the terms of
[[problems/graph_coloring/E0628/_index|Problem 628]], with $k=\chi(G)$ and
$(a,b)=(s,t)$, the question has the answer yes for every even-hole-free graph
and every admissible pair. The proof reduces to a Tihany clique of order $s$
through two lemmas on common neighborhoods of cliques; no independent check of
it is recorded.

**Covers.** Graphs with no induced $C_4$ whose every induced subgraph has a
bisimplicial vertex, in particular all even-hole-free graphs, for every $k$
and every admissible pair $(a,b)$. It says nothing about graphs with an even
hole.

**Acceptance.** None: the paper is a preprint with no journal record, posted
after the site's last edit of the problem page (6 December 2025), and the
site's commentary does not credit it. A forum member linked the preprint in
the problem's discussion thread on 23 July 2026.
