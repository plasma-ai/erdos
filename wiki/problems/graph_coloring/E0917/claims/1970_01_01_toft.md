***

Bjarne Toft, *On the maximal number of edges of critical $k$-chromatic
graphs* (Studia Sci. Math. Hungar. **5** (1970), 461–470), constructs dense
critical graphs. As Luo, Ma and Yang report on p. 2 of their paper
([[../library/graph_coloring/luo_2023_maximum_number_edges_critical_graphs/_index|card]]),
the constructions give two results, both under their proper-subgraph
convention, whose maximum is at most the site's $f_k(n)$, as the problem
page's convention note explains.

First, for every $k\ge4$ there is a constant $c_k>0$ with $f_k(n)\ge c_kn^2$
for every $n\ge k$ except $n=k+1$, with $c_4\ge1/16$ and $c_5\ge4/31$. The
first question of [[problems/graph_coloring/E0917/_index|Problem 917]],
$f_k(n)\gg_kn^2$, is answered yes. Dirac's $6$-critical graphs with more than
$n^2/4$ edges (1952) and Erdős's generalization to $3\mid k$ (1969) preceded
it and cover only those $k$.

Second, for every $k\ge6$ there are infinitely many $n$ with

$$
f_k(n)\ge\left(\frac12-\frac{3}{2k-\delta_k}\right)n^2,
$$

where $\delta_k=0$, $8/7$ or $44/23$ for $k\equiv0$, $1$ or $2\pmod3$. The
same formula gives $c_4=1/16$ at $k=4$ and $c_5=4/31$ at $k=5$. For
$k\not\equiv0\pmod3$ the coefficient exceeds the proposed
$\frac12(1-1/\lfloor k/3\rfloor)$, so the asymptotic of the third question
fails along infinitely many $n$; the problem page's Progress section computes
the three coefficients.

**Covers.** The first question, for every $k\ge4$, and the third question's
universal formula, refuted for every $k\ge6$ with $k\not\equiv0\pmod3$. It
says nothing about the asymptotic $f_6(n)\sim n^2/4$, the second question, or
about the formula at the other multiples of $3$, where the displayed bound
equals the conjectured coefficient; the case $k=12$ is the subject of the
[[problems/graph_coloring/E0917/claims/2026_09_05_gu|pending claim of Gu]].

**Attribution.** The site credits the constructions for $k\ge6$ to Stiebitz,
*Subgraphs of colour-critical graphs* (Combinatorica **7** (1987), 303–312),
writing the coefficient as $\frac12(1-1/(\lfloor k/3\rfloor+\delta_k'))$ with
$\delta_k'=0$, $1/7$ or $8/23$, the same three values. Luo, Ma and Yang credit
the formula to Toft and cite Stiebitz only for the upper bound
$f_k(n)<e(T_{k-2}(n))$. This page follows Luo, Ma and Yang and records the
conflict with the site's credit.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: Studia Sci. Math. Hungar. **5** (1970), 461–470; the
volume carries no month, so the page's date is the first day of that year. The
statement follows Luo, Ma and Yang's introduction. The site's notes credit Toft
with the first question's bound and label the problem OPEN; that credit is
commentary, not acceptance evidence.
