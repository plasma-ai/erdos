***

**Claim.** Assuming the consistency of ZFC with a huge cardinal, Foreman and
Laver force a model of ZFC + GCH in which every graph of size $\aleph_2$ and
chromatic number $\aleph_2$ has an induced subgraph of size $\aleph_1$ and
chromatic number $\aleph_1$, as the zbMATH review (Zbl 0659.03033) gives the
theorem; the paper proves a second downward transfer property, for algebras,
in the same model. In that model no graph answers the first question of
[[problems/graph_coloring/E0918/_index|Problem 918]] positively, since a
graph with $\aleph_2$ vertices and chromatic number $\aleph_2$ has a
subgraph on $\aleph_1$ vertices that is not countably chromatic. So,
relative to a huge cardinal, ZFC with GCH does not prove that such a graph
exists: the first question is not provable. The large-cardinal hypothesis is
part of the claim: the consistency is relative to ZFC with a huge cardinal,
not to ZF alone.

**Covers.** The first question of Problem 918 (the part `q1`), on its not
provable side, as a consistency statement relative to a huge cardinal: in
Foreman and Laver's model no graph of the kind asked for exists, so ZFC, even
with GCH, does not prove that one does. That side alone leaves the question
open. Together with
[[problems/graph_coloring/E0918/claims/1984_03_01_baumgartner|Baumgartner's model]],
which settles the not disprovable side relative to ZF alone, it shows that the
first question is independent of ZFC + GCH if a huge cardinal is consistent.
It does not cover the second question.

**Depends on.** No other wiki page; the claim rests on the paper above.
