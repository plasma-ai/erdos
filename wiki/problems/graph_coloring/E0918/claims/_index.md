# problems/graph_coloring/E0918/claims

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0918/_index|..]]

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0918/claims/1984_03_01_baumgartner|1984_03_01_baumgartner]]: Baumgartner (J. Symbolic Logic, 1984) proves it consistent with ZFC and GCH
that a graph on aleph two vertices has chromatic number aleph two while all
its subgraphs on at most aleph one vertices are countably chromatic.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0918/claims/1988_02_01_foreman_laver|1988_02_01_foreman_laver]]: Foreman and Laver (Adv. Math., 1988) build from a huge cardinal a model of
ZFC and GCH in which every graph of size and chromatic number aleph two has
a subgraph of size and chromatic number aleph one.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0918/claims/2014_08_05_rinot|2014_08_05_rinot]]: Rinot (Combinatorica, 2015) proves from square at lambda and 2^lambda =
lambda^+ a graph on lambda^+ vertices of chromatic number aleph one whose
smaller subgraphs are countably chromatic; at lambda = aleph omega, in L.
