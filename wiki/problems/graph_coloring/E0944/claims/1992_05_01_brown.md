***

**Claim.** J. I. Brown, *A vertex critical graph without critical edges*,
Discrete Math. **102** (1992), no. 1, 99–101, gives an explicit graph with
chromatic number $5$ in which deleting any vertex lowers the chromatic number
to $4$ while deleting any one edge leaves it at $5$. This answers Dirac's 1970
question whether some vertex-critical graph has no critical edge, as the
zbMATH review (Zbl 0768.05038) states it.

**Covers.** The case $k=5$, $r=1$ of
[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/_index|Problem 944]]. Jensen's 2002 theorem
([[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims/2002_12_01_jensen|claim page]])
extends it to every $k\ge5$.

**Acceptance.** A refereed journal publication in Discrete Mathematics
(`refereed`). The site labels the problem OPEN, so its commentary crediting
the paper is not acceptance.
