***

**Claim.** T. R. Jensen, *Dense critical and vertex-critical graphs*, Discrete
Math. **258** (2002), no. 1–3, 63–84
([[../library/graph_coloring/jensen_2002_dense_critical_vertex_critical_graphs/_index|card]]),
proves in Theorem 5 that for every $k\ge5$ and every $m>0$ there is a
vertex-critical $k$-chromatic graph that stays $k$-chromatic after the
deletion of any set of fewer than $m$ edges incident with one vertex. The
graphs are spanning supergraphs of explicit circulants. In particular no
single edge is critical, so the graph is vertex-critical with no critical
edge.

**Covers.** The case $r=1$ of
[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/_index|Problem 944]] for every $k\ge5$. It
extends Brown's $k=5$ example
([[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims/1992_05_01_brown|claim page]]). An
edge set that is not incident with one vertex is not controlled, so the
theorem gives nothing for $r\ge2$, and $k=4$ is left open.

**Acceptance.** A refereed journal publication in Discrete Mathematics
(`refereed`). The site labels the problem OPEN, so its commentary crediting
the paper is not acceptance.
