***

**Claim.** J. J. Lattanzio, *A note on a conjecture of Dirac*, Discrete Math.
**258** (2002), no. 1–3, 323–330, constructs two families of circulant graphs
in the class of $(\alpha,\omega)$-graphs, and these families give, for every
$k$ with $k-1$ not prime, a vertex $k$-critical graph with no critical edge,
as the zbMATH review (Zbl 1008.05058) states the result. The paper remarks
that Jensen independently settled every $k\ge5$.

**Covers.** The case $r=1$ of
[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/_index|Problem 944]] for every $k\ge4$ with
$k-1$ not prime, which excludes $k=4$.

**Acceptance.** A refereed journal publication in Discrete Mathematics
(`refereed`). The site labels the problem OPEN, so its commentary crediting
the paper is not acceptance.
