# problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/_index|..]]

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims/1992_05_01_brown|1992_05_01_brown]]: Brown's 1992 example of a vertex 5-critical graph with no critical edge, the
case k = 5, r = 1; refereed in Discrete Math.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims/2002_12_01_jensen|2002_12_01_jensen]]: Jensen's 2002 vertex-critical k-chromatic graphs, for every k at least 5, in
which no set of fewer than m edges at one vertex is critical, giving the
case r = 1 for every k at least 5; refereed in Discrete Math.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims/2002_12_01_lattanzio|2002_12_01_lattanzio]]: Lattanzio's 2002 circulant families of vertex k-critical graphs with no
critical edge whenever k - 1 is not prime, the case r = 1 for those k;
refereed in Discrete Math.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims/2023_10_19_martinsson_steiner|2023_10_19_martinsson_steiner]]: Martinsson and Steiner prove that for every r there is k_0 such that every
k at least k_0 has a vertex-critical k-chromatic graph with no critical set
of at most r edges; refereed in Combin. Probab. Comput.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims/2025_08_12_skottova_steiner|2025_08_12_skottova_steiner]]: Skottova and Steiner's 2025 preprint proves f_k(n) = Omega(n^(1/3)) for
every fixed k at least 5, so suitable graphs exist for all k at least 5 and
all r; no journal version, unreviewed.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims/2026_09_09_chan|2026_09_09_chan]]: Alex Chan's explicit 4-chromatic graph on 60 vertices with every vertex
critical and no critical edge, the k = 4 case of Dirac's conjecture, public
from 9 September 2026; Lean certificates not built by this corpus.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims/2026_09_10_kitamura|2026_09_10_kitamura]]: Kenta Kitamura's AI-assisted Lean 4 proof of 10 September 2026 that a
4-chromatic graph on 48 vertices has every vertex critical and no critical
edge, the k = 4, r = 1 case of Dirac's conjecture; pending.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims/2026_09_11_kruer_kohlmeyer|2026_09_11_kruer_kohlmeyer]]: Lean 4 proof certified by Conjectures.io in September 2026 that for every k
at least 4 and r at least 1 some k-chromatic graph has every vertex critical
and no critical set of at most r edges; the k = 4 case for r at least 2 is new.
