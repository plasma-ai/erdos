# Problem 1104

[[problems/graph_coloring/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Let $f(n)$ be the maximum possible chromatic number of a
triangle-free graph on $n$ vertices. Estimate $f(n)$.

**Status.** Open.

**Source.** [erdosproblems.com/1104](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1104),
accessed 2026-09-04. Cite as: T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problem #1104,
https://www.erdosproblems.com/1104.
