***

Hooley, *On the difference between consecutive numbers prime to $n$. II*, Publ.
Math. Debrecen 12 (1965), 39–49, received 5 December 1963 (the page name's
date). Its Theorem 1 states that when $n\to\infty$ along integers with
$n/\phi(n)\to\infty$, the gaps $\Delta_i=a_{i+1}-a_i$ between consecutive
integers coprime to $n$, divided by their mean $n/\phi(n)$, are asymptotically
distributed as a gamma variable with parameter $1$, that is, exponentially:
with $f_n(c)$ "the number of intervals for which $\Delta_i<cn/\varphi(n)$"
(Theorem 1; the inequality is strict as printed),
$f_n(c)=\varphi(n)\{1+o(1)\}(1-e^{-c})$ uniformly for $c$ in any fixed range
bounded at both ends by positive constants. The problem's quotient counts the
gaps with $\le$ instead; since
$f_n(c)\le\#\{i:\Delta_i\le cn/\phi(n)\}\le f_n(c+\varepsilon)$ for every
$\varepsilon>0$ and $1-e^{-c}$ is continuous, the proportion of gaps at most
$c\,n/\phi(n)$ also tends to $1-e^{-c}$, uniformly on such a range. Hooley
names the primorial case $n=2\cdot3\cdots p_k$ of
[[problems/integer_sequences/E0235/_index|Problem 235]] as Erdős's conjecture;
since $N_k/\phi(N_k)\to\infty$, the theorem gives that the problem's limit
exists for every $c>0$ and equals $1-e^{-c}$ (at $c=0$ no gap is counted and
both sides vanish), a continuous function of $c$. The problem's quotient counts
the $\phi(N_k)-1$ internal gaps against the denominator $\phi(N_k)$, a
difference of one gap that does not affect the limit. The paper's digest is the
[[../library/integer_sequences/hooley_1965_difference_between_consecutive_numbers_prime/_index|library
card]].
