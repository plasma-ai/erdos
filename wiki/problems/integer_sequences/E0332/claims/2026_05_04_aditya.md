***

**Claim.** On 4 May 2026 the forum user aditya posted in the thread of
[[problems/integer_sequences/E0332/_index|Problem 332]] an observation
attributed to GPT 5.5 pro, as the post names the system: the
positive-density condition can be weakened to positive upper Banach density.
The post links a three-page note, *A Banach-density condition for bounded
gaps in an infinite difference set*, whose author line is a placeholder, so
it names no author. Its theorem: if
$d^*(A)=\limsup_{L\to\infty}\sup_M|A\cap[M,M+L)|/L>0$, then $D(A)$ has
bounded gaps; equivalently the two-sided set $\Delta(A)$ of $t$ with
$A\cap(A-t)$ infinite is syndetic in $\mathbb Z$. The proof: translates
$A-f$, $f\in F$, whose pairwise differences avoid $\Delta(A)$ meet pairwise
in finite sets, so counting on intervals where $A$ has density near $d^*(A)$
gives $|F|\le1/d^*(A)$, and a maximal such $F$ satisfies
$\mathbb Z=F+\Delta(A)$. The same conclusion follows from Theorem 4.1 of
Belgikar, Bergelson, Black and Kruzel (arXiv:2412.01185v2, 2025), which
allows any Følner sequence, applied along intervals on which $A$ has density
tending to $d^*(A)$ (an observation made here).

**Covers.** Every $A\subseteq\mathbb N$ of positive upper Banach density,
which includes every set of positive upper density
([[problems/integer_sequences/E0332/claims/1979_10_01_stewart_tijdeman|Stewart and Tijdeman's theorem]]).
Not covered: sets of upper Banach density zero.
