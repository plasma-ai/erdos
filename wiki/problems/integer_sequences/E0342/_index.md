# Problem 342

[[problems/integer_sequences/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** With $a_1=1$ and $a_2=2$ let $a_{n+1}$ for $n\geq 2$ be the least
integer $>a_n$ which can be expressed uniquely as $a_i+a_j$ for $i<j\leq n$.

What can be said about this sequence? Do infinitely many pairs $a,a+2$ occur?
Does this sequence eventually have periodic differences? Is the density $0$?

**Status.** Open.
