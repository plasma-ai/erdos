# problems/integer_sequences/E0360/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0360/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0360/claims/1995_05_15_alon_erdos|1995_05_15_alon_erdos]]: Alon and Erdős's 1996 Theorem 1.1, placing f(n) between n^{1/3}/(log n)^{4/3}
and n^{1/3}(log log n)^{1/3}/(log n)^{1/3} up to constants, so that
f(n) = n^{1/3+o(1)}; refereed, and recorded by the site.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0360/claims/2006_11_16_vu|2006_11_16_vu]]: Vu's 2007 improvement of the Alon–Erdős lower bound to f(n) at least a
constant times n^{1/3}/log n; refereed, and recorded by the site; the paper
is not held here.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0360/claims/2021_04_30_conlon_fox_pham|2021_04_30_conlon_fox_pham]]: Theorem 1.5 of the 2021 preprint of Conlon, Fox and Pham, determining f(n)
up to a constant factor as n^{1/3}(n/phi(n))/((log n)^{1/3}(log log n)^{2/3});
accepted by the site as the solution; to appear in J. Eur. Math. Soc.
