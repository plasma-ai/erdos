# problems/integer_sequences/E0436/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0436/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0436/claims/1962_01_01_lehmer_lehmer_mills_selfridge|1962_01_01_lehmer_lehmer_mills_selfridge]]: Theorem 1 of Lehmer, Lehmer, Mills and Selfridge (Math. Comp. 16 (1962))
lists the thirteen primes with no three consecutive cubic residues and shows
every other prime has such a run starting by 23532, sharp infinitely often.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0436/claims/1991_01_01_hildebrand|1991_01_01_hildebrand]]: Theorem 1 of Hildebrand (Michigan Math. J. 38 (1991)) proves that for every
k the least pair of consecutive kth power residues modulo p is bounded in
p, so Lambda(k,2) is finite and the first question of Problem 436 is yes.
