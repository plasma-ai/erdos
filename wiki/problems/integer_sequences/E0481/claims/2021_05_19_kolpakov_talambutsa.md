**Claim.** Theorem 3 of
[[../library/integer_sequences/kolpakov_2022_free_semigroups_affine_maps_real_line/_index|Kolpakov and Talambutsa]]:
if $f_i(x)=a_ix+b_i$ for $i=1,\ldots,n$ with positive integers $a_i$ and
rational $b_i$, and $\sum_i 1/a_i>1$, then the semigroup $\langle
f_1,\ldots,f_n\rangle$ is not free with that basis. The problem's maps, with
$a_i,b_i\in\mathbb N$, satisfy the hypothesis, and a relation between two
distinct words of equal length evaluated at $1$ is a repeated entry of some
$A_k$, as the page for
[[problems/integer_sequences/E0481/claims/1982_01_01_klarner|Klarner]] explains.
The authors state that their proof follows the scheme of Klarner's Theorem
1.1, reducing to the case of equal multipliers $a_1=\cdots=a_n=m$ with $n>m$,
and they supply the second part, which the 1982 paper omits. The paper's main
subject is the free direction: ping-pong criteria for freeness without
arithmetic hypotheses.

**Postings.** arXiv:2105.09387, first posted 19 May 2021 (revised 15 September
2021); Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 150 (2022), 2301--2307, published online 16
March 2022. The paper is digested on its library card. The site's
commentary of 3 December 2025 names it as a generalization of Klarner's
result.

**Acceptance.** Refereed publication in the Proceedings of the American
Mathematical Society, and the acceptance of the site's curator, Thomas Bloom,
in the commentary, which names the paper as a generalization of Klarner's
result. Nothing was reviewed here.
