# problems/integer_sequences/E0487/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0487/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0487/claims/1965_01_01_erdos|1965_01_01_erdos]]: Erdős's 1965 statement that the lcm-triple conjecture is proved, resting on
his reduction to union-free families and on the unpublished bound of Sárközy
and Szemerédi that such families have o(2^n) members; superseded by Kleitman.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0487/claims/1971_01_01_kleitman|1971_01_01_kleitman]]: Kleitman's 1971 theorem that a union-free family of subsets of an n-set has
at most (1+o(1)) times the middle binomial coefficient members; by Erdős's
reduction it settles the lcm-triple question, and the site credits it.
