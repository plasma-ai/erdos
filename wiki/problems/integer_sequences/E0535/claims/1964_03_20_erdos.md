***

**The claim.** For every $t$ and $\varepsilon>0$ there is $n_0(t,\varepsilon)$
such that for $n>n_0$

$$
2^{c_t\log n/\log\log n}<f_t(n)<n^{3/4+\varepsilon}.
$$

This is the Theorem, display (4), on p. 644 of P. Erdős, *On a problem in
elementary number theory and a combinatorial problem*, Math. Comp. 18 (1964),
no. 88, 644--646 (received 20 March 1964), paged as
[[../library/integer_sequences/erdos_1964_problem_elementary_number_theory_combinatorial_problem/theorem_p644|the theorem page]]
of
[[../library/integer_sequences/erdos_1964_problem_elementary_number_theory_combinatorial_problem/_index|Erdős (1964)]].
Erdős's $f_t(n)$, the least size that forces $t$ integers with pairwise the
same greatest common divisor, is one more than the site's $f_r(N)$ of
[[problems/integer_sequences/E0535/_index|Problem 535]]. The upper bound
comes from the Erdős–Rado sunflower bound applied to squarefree parts, the
lower bound from an explicit product construction on the first $3k$ primes
(p. 645). The page is named by the date the paper was received.

**Covers.** For every fixed $r\ge3$, $f_r(N)<N^{3/4+\varepsilon}$ for large
$N$ and $f_r(N)>N^{c_r/\log\log N}$; the order of $f_r(N)$ is not determined.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: the journal publication. The site's commentary
credits the result on a problem it labels OPEN, which is not acceptance.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki: the theorem and its proof are contained
in the cited paper, whose card is linked above.
