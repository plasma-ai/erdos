# problems/integer_sequences/E0536/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0536/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0536/claims/1966_11_17_abbott_gardner|1966_11_17_abbott_gardner]]: Abbott and Gardner's 1967 bound (Canadian Mathematical Bulletin) that some
(1 - eps) N log log N / log N integers up to N have no three with equal
pairwise least common multiples; refereed.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0536/claims/2026_05_03_saturnino|2026_05_03_saturnino]]: Brian Saturnino's note of May 2026 bounds the largest lcm-triangle-free set
up to N by (43/48 + o(1))N through disjoint forbidden triples; posted to the
site's thread, unreviewed.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0536/claims/2026_06_22_kitamura|2026_06_22_kitamura]]: Kenta Kitamura's Lean development of June 2026, prepared with Codex 5.5 and
ChatGPT 5.5 Pro, proving that an lcm-triangle-free set in {1, ..., N} has
at most N - floor(N/6) elements; unbuilt by the corpus.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0536/claims/2026_07_14_wang|2026_07_14_wang]]: Shouqiao Wang's AI-assisted manuscript of July 2026 claims f(N) = o(N) by
a cap-set argument; posted to the site's proof-claims tab, relabeled
partial by the curator since it does not estimate f(N), and unreviewed.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0536/claims/2026_08_17_logsdon|2026_08_17_logsdon]]: Sara Logsdon's Lean development of August 2026, prepared with ChatGPT and
Codex, refining the 5/6 lattice bound for lcm-triangle-free sets to
(813/1000 + o(1))N; unbuilt by the corpus.
