# problems/integer_sequences/E0540/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0540/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0540/claims/1968_07_01_olson|1968_07_01_olson]]: Olson (J. Combinatorial Theory, 1968) proves that more than (4p - 3)^{1/2}
distinct nonzero residues modulo a prime p have a nonempty zero-sum
subfamily, the prime case with the constant 2; refereed, credited by the site.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0540/claims/1969_05_15_szemeredi|1969_05_15_szemeredi]]: Szemerédi's theorem (Acta Arith., 1970) that in every abelian group of order
n, any c times the square root of n elements have a nonempty zero-sum
subset; refereed, acknowledged by Erdős and accepted by the site.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0540/claims/1996_01_18_hamidoune_zemor|1996_01_18_hamidoune_zemor]]: Hamidoune and Zémor (Acta Arith., 1996) prove that more than sqrt(2n) +
O(n^{1/3} ln n) elements of an abelian group of order n have a nonempty
zero-sum subset; refereed, credited by the site.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0540/claims/2009_07_20_balandraud|2009_07_20_balandraud]]: Balandraud (Israel J. Math., 2012) proves Selfridge's conjecture: a largest
zero-sum free subset of Z/pZ has k elements, k the greatest integer with
k(k+1)/2 < p; refereed, credited by the site.
