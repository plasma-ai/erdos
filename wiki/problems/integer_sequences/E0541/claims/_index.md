# problems/integer_sequences/E0541/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0541/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0541/claims/1974_02_14_erdos_szemeredi|1974_02_14_erdos_szemeredi]]: Erdős and Szemerédi (Publ. Math. Debrecen, 1976) prove Graham's conjecture
for p nonzero residues modulo every sufficiently large prime p; refereed,
credited by the site, and partial: small primes and the residue 0 are left.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0541/claims/2009_02_27_gao_hamidoune_wang|2009_02_27_gao_hamidoune_wang]]: Theorem 1.1 of Gao, Hamidoune and Wang (J. Number Theory, 2010): n integers
in [0, n-1] taking at least three values have two nonempty zero-sum
subsequences modulo n of distinct lengths; refereed, credited by the site.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0541/claims/2009_03_18_grynkiewicz|2009_03_18_grynkiewicz]]: Theorem 3.4 of Grynkiewicz (European J. Combin., 2011): a sequence of n
elements of a finite abelian group of order n whose nonempty zero-sum
subsequences all have one length has at most two distinct terms; refereed.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0541/claims/2025_12_31_alexeev|2025_12_31_alexeev]]: A Lean 4 proof of the prime-modulus statement, produced by Boris Alexeev
with Aristotle and ChatGPT and posted in the site's thread on 31 December
2025; a later revision was built and audited by this corpus, so accepted.
