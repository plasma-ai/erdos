# problems/integer_sequences/E0587/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0587/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0587/claims/2008_11_09_nguyen_vu|2008_11_09_nguyen_vu]]: Theorem 1.4 of Nguyen and Vu (Squares in sumsets, 2010): a square-sum-free
subset of the first N integers has at most N^{1/3} (log N)^C elements, so the
largest has size N^{1/3+o(1)}; the site's curator credits it as the solution.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0587/claims/2026_08_27_alexeev|2026_08_27_alexeev]]: A Lean development in Alexeev's repository, with Codex and GPT-5.6 Sol as
formal authors, proves SF(N) at most a constant times N^{1/3}(log log N)^16
without Nguyen and Vu's Lemma 4.2, giving the order N^{1/3+o(1)}.
