**The claim.** For every positive integer $m$,
$f(m)=\min(m,\lceil2\sqrt m\rceil)$, and so $f(m)=\lceil2\sqrt m\rceil$ for
$m\ge4$ (Theorem 2.1 and Remark 2.2), where $f(m)$ is the largest $r$ such
that every set $A$ of $m$ positive integers and every interval of length
$2\max A$ contain $r$ distinct integers matched to $r$ distinct members of
$A$ dividing them. This is the estimate asked for by
[[problems/integer_sequences/E0650/_index|Problem 650]], whose $f$ (sets
$A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ and intervals of length $2N$) agrees with the
paper's by the remark under Formulation on the problem page; and it answers
the displayed question $f(m)\le\sqrt m$ in the negative for every $m\ge2$,
with equality only at $m=1$. The upper bound is Theorem 3.1, $f(st)\le s+t$
for all positive $s,t$, by a Chinese-remainder set of $st$ integers with at
most $s+t$ matched multiples in an interval of length $2\max A$; the lower
bound is Theorem 4.1, $f(m)\ge\min(m,\lceil2\sqrt m\rceil)$, by the defect
form of Hall's theorem and the neighborhood bound
$\lvert\Gamma(S)\rvert\ge2\sqrt{\lvert S\rvert}$ in the divisibility graph.
The source is W. van Doorn, Y. Li and Q. Tang, *Optimal bounds for an Erdős
problem on matching integers to distinct multiples*, arXiv:2603.28636v1 (30
March 2026; the only version and no journal reference on 2026-09-18), paged
as the
[[../library/primes/doorn_2026_optimal_bounds_erdos_problem_matching_integers/source_digest|source digest]]
of
[[../library/primes/doorn_2026_optimal_bounds_erdos_problem_matching_integers/_index|van Doorn, Li and Tang (2026)]].
Read depth: the definition, Theorem 2.1, Remark 2.2, Lemma 2.3, Theorems 3.1
and 4.1 and the interval remark are checked against the paper; the proofs
(Sections 3 and 4) are not.

**Dating.** The result was first posted in the site's thread: the equality
$f(m)=\lceil2\sqrt m\rceil$ for $m\ge4$ with a proof draft on 7 March 2026
(the date this page is named by; the post and the draft, Tang's note on
Problem 650 in Tang's repository, are linked above), after the upper bound
$f(m)\le2\lceil\sqrt m\rceil$ on 6 March (a reproof of the Erdős–Selfridge
bound, later improved to $f(m)\le\lceil2\sqrt m\rceil$ through
$f(st)\le s+t$), whose write-up Tang posted with Yixin He and Yanyang Li (also
linked above), matching the site's credit to GPT 5.4 Pro prompted by He, Li
and Tang; the formalization of the equality followed on 8 March in van Doorn's
post (linked above) and was extended to $\min(m,\lceil2\sqrt m\rceil)$; the
paper was posted on 30 March. The paper's Section 5 and the thread record that
the draft's lower bound argued, in one case, as if the next multiple after the
largest multiple in one block had to lie in the other, which is false, and
that the formalization found a working variant of the injection, which the
paper adopts. Tang's note on the gap, added to the same repository on 21 March
2026, is linked above; both repository links are pinned to its commit of that
day.
