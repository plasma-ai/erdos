**Claim.** For $n\ge2$ let $K(n)$ be the least base $k\ge2$ with
$\gcd(k^n-1,2^n-1)=1$ and $H(n)$ the least $b\ge3$ admitting a partner
$2\le a<b$ with $\gcd(a^n-1,b^n-1)=1$. The manuscript *Coprime Power
Differences* proves, with one absolute constant $C$,

$$
\log K(n)\le C\,\tau(n)\,(\log(n+2))^2\qquad(n\ge2),
$$

where $\tau(n)$ counts the positive divisors of $n$, and deduces from the
elementary maximal order of $\tau$ that for every $\epsilon>0$ and all
sufficiently large $n$

$$
H(n)\le K(n)<\exp\!\left(n^{(\log2+\epsilon)/\log\log n}\right).
$$

The proof is a finite sieve: the primes that must divide an admissible base
are separated from the sparse forbidden residue classes, the total density of
those classes is bounded through the divisor function, and a truncated
inclusion-exclusion finds a base avoiding all of them. The
[[../library/integer_sequences/price_2026_coprime_power_differences/_index|library card]]
covers the manuscript; its pages state the results and sketch the arguments
([[../library/integer_sequences/price_2026_coprime_power_differences/theorem_1_1|Theorem 1.1]],
[[../library/integer_sequences/price_2026_coprime_power_differences/lemma_3_1|Lemma 3.1]],
[[../library/integer_sequences/price_2026_coprime_power_differences/corollary_1_2|Corollary 1.2]]).

**Covers.** Question 3 of
[[problems/integer_sequences/E0820/_index|Problem 820]], yes:
$K(n)<\exp(n^{(\log2+\epsilon)/\log\log n})$ for every $\epsilon>0$ and
all large $n$, and with it the eventual upper half of question 2, since
$H(n)\le K(n)$. The corpus's
[[../library/integer_sequences/fan_2025_maximal_order_shifted_prime_divisor_function/h_n_corollary|corollary]]
of Fan and Pollack's Theorem 1.1, through Erdős's Fermat argument, gives the
infinitely-often lower bound $H(n)>\exp(n^{0.6736\log2/\log\log n})$. With
that bound, the claim answers question 2 yes: the limit superior $c_H$ lies
in $[0.6736\log2,\log2]$ and serves as the constant. That combination is
the library's
[[../library/integer_sequences/price_2026_coprime_power_differences/growth_constant|growth-constant page]],
not a statement of the manuscript. Not covered: whether $H(n)=3$ infinitely
often; the value of $c_H$; and whether the constant for $K(n)$ equals
$c_H$.

**Formalization.** Price's comment of 16 July 2026 in the thread (the third
link) carries a Lean playground link selecting the project `mathlib-v4.28.0`.
That URL encodes the whole Lean file, too long for the claim form, so it is
not listed in `links` and is reached through the third link. Its decoded file declares itself a single-file
formalization of the manuscript, built against Lean 4.28.0 and Mathlib v4.28.0
and free of `sorry`, and names the eventual theorems `K_lt_exp`,
`H_le_K_and_K_lt_exp` and `H_lt_exp` with the displayed exponent
$\log2+\epsilon$, under the positive-base convention for $n\ge2$; its uniform
bound states a numerical constant $500$. The file was neither built nor
audited here, so `formalized` is not listed, and the file's own build assertion
and the constant $500$ are the source's claims.
