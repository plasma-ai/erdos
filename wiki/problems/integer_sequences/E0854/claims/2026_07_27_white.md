***

**Claim.** Write $F(k)$ for the least even integer that is not a difference
$a_{i+1}-a_i$ of consecutive integers coprime to the $k$th primorial, as in
[[problems/integer_sequences/E0854/_index|Problem 854]]. The Erdős
Problem-a-Day working report on Problem 854, of 27 July 2026, proves

$$
\liminf_{k\to\infty}\frac{F(k)}{k\log\log k}\ge e^{\gamma},
$$

with $\gamma$ Euler's constant, so $F(k)\ge(e^{\gamma}-o(1))\,k\log\log k$,
and as a corollary that at least $(e^{\gamma}/2-o(1))\,k\log\log k$
distinct even gaps occur. The report also computes $F(k)$ exactly for
$2\le k\le13$ (from $F(2)=6$ to $F(13)=76$) and certifies $F(100)\ge482$ by
an explicit covering.

**Covers.** The lower bounds above for the least even non-gap and for the
number of distinct even gaps. Not covered: an upper bound for $F(k)$, and the
displayed question whether $\gg\max_i(a_{i+1}-a_i)$ even integers occur as
gaps.

**Standing.** The claimant is Patrick White, who published the result on
2026-07-27 as a working report on erdosproblemaday.com, a public ledger of
AI-assisted reports that names the report's authors as Patrick White and
