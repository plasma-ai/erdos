***

**Claim.** The note *Weighted sunflower pressure and the exact polylogarithmic
exponent in harmonic LCM patterns* (Chojecki, dated 15 April 2026, posted the
same day in the discussion thread of
[[problems/integer_sequences/E0856/_index|Problem 856]] with the statement that
it was developed with GPT-5.4 Pro) claims an exact exponent for $f_k(N)$, the
largest harmonic sum of a set $A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ with no $k$ distinct
elements whose pairwise least common multiples all agree. For $z>0$ it defines
the weighted partition function

$$
W_k(n;z)=\max\Bigl\{\sum_{S\in\mathcal F}z^{|S|}:\ \mathcal F\subseteq2^{[n]}
\text{ has no }k\text{-sunflower}\Bigr\},
\qquad \Lambda_k(z)=\lim_{n\to\infty}W_k(n;z)^{1/n},
$$

proves the two weighted bounds $f_k(N)\le(\log N)^{\Lambda_k(z)-z+o(1)}$ and
$f_k(N)\ge(\log N)^{\log(z\Lambda_k(1/z))/z-o(1)}$ for every fixed $z>0$, and
concludes by a squeezing argument that

$$
f_k(N)=(\log N)^{\gamma_k+o(1)},\qquad
\gamma_k=\lim_{z\to\infty}\bigl(\Lambda_k(z)-z\bigr)
=\inf_{z>0}\bigl(\Lambda_k(z)-z\bigr).
$$

At $z=1$ the bounds reduce to those of Tang and Zhang
([[problems/integer_sequences/E0856/claims/2025_12_23_tang_zhang|claim page]];
$\Lambda_k(1)$ is their sunflower-free capacity $\mu_k^S$), and the note says
that $\gamma_k=1$ when $\mu_k^S=2$. The upper bound transports the harmonic
weight along squarefree multipliers chosen with probability proportional to
$z^{\omega(q)}/q$, so that the multiplier histories landing at one endpoint form
a sunflower-free family; the lower bound is a weighted bucketing construction.
The note states that computing $\gamma_k$, or expressing it through $\mu_k^S$,
remains open.

**Submission note.** Posted to the site's forum by Przemek Chojecki on 15 April
2026:

> With GPT-5.4 Pro I've developped a weighted version of the Tang-Zhang
> sunflower-capacity argument that gives the exact estimate. The written note is
> here.
>
> The starting point is the same mass-transport idea that Liam Price emphasized
> in his recent Markov-chain discussion of [1196]: after truncating to
> squarefree multipliers $q\le N$, one chooses $q$ with probability proportional
> to $z^{\omega(q)}/q$, so that the harmonic weight $1/a$ is transported to the
> endpoint $m=aq$ with density proportional to $1/m$. The admissible multiplier
> histories landing at a fixed endpoint form a $k$-sunflower-free family, and
> this yields a weighted upper bound involving the partition function
> $W_k(n;z)$.

**Covers.** If it stands, $f_k(N)=(\log N)^{\gamma_k+o(1)}$ with
$\gamma_k=\inf_{z>0}(\Lambda_k(z)-z)$: the existence of an exact exponent and
its characterization through the weighted sunflower-free partition function. Not
covered: the value of $\gamma_k$. The problem asks for an estimate of $f_k(N)$,
which for a function of polylogarithmic growth is its exponent, and the note
leaves that value open on its own account, so the claim is recorded as partial
although the note presents the exponent as the answer. The later claim of
[[problems/integer_sequences/E0856/claims/2026_07_18_rayyoung_zhu_luo|RayYoung,
Zhu and Luo]] asserts an exponent of the same shape through a different extremal
quantity; the two are recorded separately, and no comparison of the two
exponents is recorded.

(pp. 1--2); its proofs are not assessed. Nothing here is this project's own
review.
