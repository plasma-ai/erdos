***

**Claim.** In *Some of my forgotten problems in number theory*
(Hardy-Ramanujan J. **15** (1992), 34–50; library card
[[../library/integer_sequences/erdos_1992_my_forgotten_problems_number_theory/_index|erdos_1992_my_forgotten_problems_number_theory]]),
Erdős introduces the problem of
[[problems/integer_sequences/E0873/_index|Problem 873]] as joint with
Szemerédi (pp. 47–48) and reports on p. 48 that they proved, for every
sequence $A$,

$$
F(A,X,3)<c_1X^{1/3}\log X,
$$

and that there is a sequence $A$ with $F(A,X,3)>c_2X^{1/3}\log X$ for
infinitely many $X$. No proof is given.

**Covers.** The question for every $\varepsilon>1/3$, with $k=3$ and $X$
large. The second bound shows that $k=3$ gives nothing below the exponent
$1/3$.

**Standing.** Claimed: the paper states the result without proof, so it is
not listed as refereed. The site credits the result to Erdős and Szemerédi on
a problem it labels OPEN; that credit is commentary, not acceptance.
