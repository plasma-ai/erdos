# problems/integer_sequences/E0873/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0873/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0873/claims/1992_01_01_erdos_szemeredi|1992_01_01_erdos_szemeredi]]: Erdős reports in 1992, without proof, that he and Szemerédi proved
F(A,X,3) < c X^{1/3} log X for every sequence and found a sequence meeting
this bound for infinitely many X.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0873/claims/2026_04_29_nayak|2026_04_29_nayak]]: Ritvik Nayak's 2026 note, checked with GPT 5.4 Thinking, proves
F(A,X,11) << X^{223/676} log X and F(A,X,6) << X^{437/1320} log X, so the
question holds for every epsilon above 223/676.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0873/claims/2026_04_30_old_bielefelder|2026_04_30_old_bielefelder]]: Two 2026 notes posted by old-bielefelder, written by ChatGPT 5.5 Thinking and
ChatGPT 5.6 Sol, prove that for every epsilon above 1/4 some k gives
F(A,X,k) << X^epsilon uniformly in A; the range up to 1/4 is not covered.
