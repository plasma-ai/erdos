**Claim.** Let $F(n)$ be the largest size of a set $A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}$
such that $ad=bc$ whenever $a\le b\le c\le d$ lie in $A$ and $abcd$ is a square.
Then, as $n\to\infty$,

$$
F(n)\sim\frac{n\log\log n}{\log n},
$$

so the leading constant of the accepted order of magnitude is one. Theorem 1.1
of the note *The leading constant in Erdős Problem 888* (draft dated
16 September 2026, no author line) proves the equivalent statement
$0\le F(n)-Q_2(n)=o(n\log\log n/\log n)$, where $Q_2(n)$ counts the squarefree
semiprimes up to $n$, whose Landau asymptotic gives the lower bound. The upper
half keeps the square-part reduction and the two-largest-prime encoding
$a=k^2cpq$ of the earlier proof and truncates its parameters: elements with
$k>K$ or $c>2^L$ contribute at most a fraction of the main term that vanishes as
$K$ and then $L$ grow, by the earlier colored-graph estimates; for each of the
finitely many remaining multipliers $b=k^2c$ the retained pairs $\{p,q\}$ form a
graph $H_b$ on the primes, the union of these graphs has at most $Q_2(n)$ edges,
and any two of them intersect in a graph without four-cycles, which has
$O(n/\log n)$ edges. This page rests on the note's abstract, introduction and
closing sections.

**Submission note.** Posted to erdosproblems.com as a proof claim by Rogerhu
(account Rogerhu) on 17 September 2026, giving "GPT-6 Astra" as the AI used:

> We prove \(F(n)\sim h(n):=n\log\log n/\log n\). Using estimates from the
> earlier proof, restricting the representation \(a=k^2cpq\) to \(k\le K\) and
> \(c\le2^L\) discards at most\[
> \left(\frac{C_1}{K}+C_2K\eta(L)\right)h(n)+o_{K,L}(h(n)), \qquad
> \eta(L)\to0\quad(L\to\infty). \]There are only finitely many possible
> remaining \(b=k^2c\), independently of \(n\). For each \(b\), let \(H_b\)
> contain the edge \(\{p,q\}\) for each retained representation \(a=bpq\). The
> union of their edge sets has size at most \(Q_2(n)\), the number of squarefree
> semiprimes up to \(n\). For \(b_1\ne b_2\), the intersections \(H_{b_1}\cap
> H_{b_2}\) are \(C_4\)-free, so the total overcount is \(O_{K,L}(n/\log n)\).
> Letting \(n\to\infty\), then \(L\to\infty\), and finally \(K\to\infty\), and
> using the existing matching lower bound, gives the asymptotic. Notes: The
> proof of the leading-constant refinement was found by GPT-6 Astra. The writeup
> and Lean formalization were developed using OpenAI Codex. The summary was
> prepared from my Chinese draft with AI-assisted translation and editing.
