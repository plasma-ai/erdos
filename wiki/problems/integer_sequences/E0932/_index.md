# Problem 932

[[problems/integer_sequences/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Let $p_k$ denote the $k$th prime. For infinitely many $r$ there
are at least two integers $p_r<n<p_{r+1}$ all of whose prime factors are
$<p_{r+1}-p_r$.
