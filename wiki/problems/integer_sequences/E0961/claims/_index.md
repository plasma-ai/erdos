# problems/integer_sequences/E0961/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0961/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0961/claims/1934_10_01_erdos|1934_10_01_erdos]]: Erdős gives a short proof of the theorem of Sylvester and Schur that among
any k consecutive integers above k one has a prime factor greater than k,
which is the upper bound f(k) at most k.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0961/claims/1955_01_01_erdos|1955_01_01_erdos]]: Erdős proves that every block of c k over log k consecutive integers above
k, for some constant c greater than 1, contains a multiple of a prime
greater than k.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0961/claims/1973_01_01_jutila_ramachandra_shorey|1973_01_01_jutila_ramachandra_shorey]]: Jutila, Ramachandra and Shorey bound f(k) by a constant times k log log log
k over log k log log k, the record upper bound, as Erdős's 1976 survey
attests it.
