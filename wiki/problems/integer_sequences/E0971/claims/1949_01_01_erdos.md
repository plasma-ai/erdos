***

**Claim.** Theorem 1 of P. Erdős, *On some applications of Brun's method*,
Acta Univ. Szeged. Sect. Sci. Math. **13** (1949), 57–63
([[../library/primes/erdos_1949_applications_brun_s_method/_index|card]],
[[../library/primes/erdos_1949_applications_brun_s_method/theorem_1|Theorem 1]]):
there are $c_1>0$ and infinitely many $d$ for which
$p(a,d)>(1+c_1)\phi(d)\log d$ holds for more than $c_2\phi(d)$ reduced
residues $a$ modulo $d$. The proof takes $c_1=c_2=\delta^{20}$ for a small
fixed $\delta>0$. It finds such a modulus in every interval $[n,2n]$ for large
$n$, but it does not identify one.

**Covers.** The assertion of
[[problems/integer_sequences/E0971/_index|Problem 971]] along an infinite
sequence of moduli, with $c=C=c_1$; not the assertion for all large $d$. The
[formal-conjectures file](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/9d259649abe0b02d7a25f7589b872db679b35e21/FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/971.lean)
states it as the solved variant `erdos_971.variants.infinite_sequence`.
Theorem 2 of the paper is the complementary bound
$p(a,d)<\varepsilon\phi(d)\log d$ for $\gg_\varepsilon\phi(d)$ residues, and
it settles no part of the question.

**Acceptance.** A refereed journal publication (`refereed`). The site labels
the problem OPEN, so its commentary is not acceptance.
