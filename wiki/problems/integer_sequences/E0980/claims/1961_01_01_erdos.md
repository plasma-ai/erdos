***

**Claim.** Equation (3) of P. Erdős, *Számelméleti megjegyzések I*, Mat.
Lapok **12** (1961), 10–17
([[../library/integer_sequences/erdos_1961_szamelmeleti_megjegyzesek/_index|card]]):

$$
\sum_{p<x}n_2(p)=(1+o(1))\frac{x}{\log x}\sum_{k\ge1}\frac{p_k}{2^k},
$$

where $n_2(p)$ is the least quadratic nonresidue modulo $p$ and
$p_1<p_2<\cdots$ are the primes. It answers a question of Mirsky and is proved
with Linnik's large sieve together with Brun's sieve and the prime number
theorem for progressions.

**Covers.** The case $k=2$ of the Statement of
[[problems/integer_sequences/E0980/_index|Problem 980]].

**Acceptance.** A refereed journal publication (`refereed`). The curator's
PROVED label credits Elliott for the general case, and $k=2$ is not a part
under the parts rule, so the label is not curator credit for this page.
