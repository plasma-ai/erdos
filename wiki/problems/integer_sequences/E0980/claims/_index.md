# problems/integer_sequences/E0980/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0980/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0980/claims/1961_01_01_erdos|1961_01_01_erdos]]: Erdős's 1961 theorem that the sum over primes below x of the least quadratic
nonresidue is asymptotic to (sum of p_k/2^k) x/log x, the case k = 2;
refereed in Mat. Lapok.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0980/claims/1967_01_09_elliott|1967_01_09_elliott]]: Elliott proves the asymptotic for every k >= 2 under the convention
n_k(p)=0 for p not 1 mod k, which the page adopts; refereed in Acta
Arithmetica and credited as the proof by the catalog.
