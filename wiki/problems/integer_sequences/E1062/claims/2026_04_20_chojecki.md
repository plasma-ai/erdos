**Claim.** *A note on Erdős problem #1062* has the byline OpenAI and is dated
15 April 2026. Przemek Chojecki posted it in the site's thread on 20 April 2026
as notes generated with GPT-5.4 Pro. Its Theorem 3.2, from McNew's
local-to-global theorem for divisor-graph statistics, proves
$f(n)=\lambda n+O_\varepsilon(n\exp(-(1-\varepsilon)\sqrt{\log n\log\log n}))$
for an effectively computable $\lambda\in[0,1]$, so that $\lim f(n)/n=\lambda$
exists. Its Section 5 evaluates the first four layers of the series for
$\lambda$ as $\tfrac12,\tfrac16,0,\tfrac1{270}$, the fourth by a finite
computer check, so that $\lambda=\tfrac23+\tfrac1{270}+\sum_{i\ge5}w_i$
(Corollary 5.4).
