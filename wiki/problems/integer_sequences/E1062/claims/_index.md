[[problems/integer_sequences/E1062/claims/1977_06_01_lebensold|1977_06_01_lebensold]]: Lebensold's 1977 bound 0.6725 n <= f(n) <= 0.6736 n for all large n, from a
decomposition of {1, ..., n} into divisibility chains; refereed in Studies in
Applied Mathematics.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1062/claims/2026_04_19_davis|2026_04_19_davis]]: Davis's arXiv paper of 19 April 2026 proves f(n) = c n + o(n) for an

asymptotic form and leaving irrationality open; an unrefereed preprint.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1062/claims/2026_04_20_chojecki|2026_04_20_chojecki]]: A note dated 15 April 2026, generated with GPT-5.4 Pro and posted by Przemek

effectively computable; unreviewed.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1062/claims/2026_09_21_jenw1n|2026_09_21_jenw1n]]: A Lean proof submitted to the bounty site Conjectures.io under the username
JenW1N, kernel-verified there on 21 September 2026 and certified on 23
September 2026, proves that f(n)/n converges to an irrational limit.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1062/claims/2026_09_23_turturean|2026_09_23_turturean]]: A thread post of 23 September 2026 announces an independent manuscript giving

the limit of f(n)/n, with the argument credited to GPT-6-Astra Pro; unreviewed.
