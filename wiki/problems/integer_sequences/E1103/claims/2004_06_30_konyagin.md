***

**Claim.** Let $\mathrm{ES}_N$ be the largest size of a set
$A\subseteq\{1,\dots,N\}$ with $a+a'$ squarefree for all $a,a'\in A$, the case
$a=a'$ included. Theorem 1 (1.4) of S. V. Konyagin, *Problems on the set of
squarefree numbers*, Izv. Math. 68 (2004), no. 3, 493--520 (English translation
of Izv. Ross. Akad. Nauk Ser. Mat. 68 (2004), no. 3, 63--90), states that there
is an effective constant $C_2$ with

$$
\mathrm{ES}_N\le N^{11/15}\exp\left(C_2\frac{\log N}{\sqrt{\log\log N}}\right)
$$

for all $N\ge3$. If $A=\{a_1<a_2<\cdots\}$ is an infinite set with squarefree
sums, then $A\cap\{1,\dots,N\}$ is one of the sets counted by $\mathrm{ES}_N$,
so inverting the bound gives

$$
a_j\gg j^{15/11}\exp\left(-O\left(\frac{\log j}{\sqrt{\log\log j}}\right)\right),
$$

that is $a_j\ge j^{15/11-o(1)}$, for all large $j$. The paper treats the finite
problem; the inversion is stated on p. 4 of the second arXiv version of van
Doorn and Tao, arXiv:2512.01087v2. The source card is
[[../library/integer_sequences/konyagin_2004_problems_set_square_free_numbers/_index|Konyagin 2004]];
the page name's date is the issue date in the DOI record.

**Covers.** A necessary growth for
[[problems/integer_sequences/E1103/_index|Problem 1103]]: every infinite set
with squarefree sums satisfies $a_j\ge j^{15/11-o(1)}$, the best lower bound
known, which the site's commentary credits to Konyagin. The true growth rate is
not determined.
