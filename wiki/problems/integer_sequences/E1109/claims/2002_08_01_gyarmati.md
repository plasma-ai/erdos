***

**Claim.** Theorem 3 a) of K. Gyarmati, *On divisibility properties of integers
of the form $ab+1$*, Period. Math. Hungar. 43 (2001), no. 1--2, 71--79, states
that there is a set $\mathcal A\subseteq\{1,2,\dots,N\}$ with
$|\mathcal A|\gg\log N$ and $a+a'$ squarefree for all $a,a'\in\mathcal A$. So
$f(N)\gg\log N$ for the $f$ of
[[problems/integer_sequences/E1109/_index|Problem 1109]]. The paper says that
Theorem 3 a) is due to Erdős and Sárközy and gives another proof, based, like
its other lower bounds, on graph theory. The paper's main subject is the
multiplicative analogue, sets with $ab+1$ squarefree; its Theorem 2 a) also
gives sets $\mathcal A,\mathcal B\subseteq\{1,\dots,N\}$ with
$|\mathcal A|=|\mathcal B|\gg(\log N)^2$ and $a+b$ squarefree for all
$a\in\mathcal A$, $b\in\mathcal B$, a bound for the two-set variant, not for
$f(N)$. The `preprint` link is the author's copy. The DOI record dates the issue
August 2002, while the volume and the author's publication list give 2001; the
page is named by the first day of the record's month.

**Covers.** The lower bound $f(N)\gg\log N$, by a second proof; the bound is
[[problems/integer_sequences/E1109/claims/1987_03_01_erdos_sarkozy|Erdős and Sárközy's]]
and is improved by
[[problems/integer_sequences/E1109/claims/2004_06_30_konyagin|Konyagin 2004]].
Neither question of the problem is answered.

`reviewed` evidence. The proof is not checked in this corpus.
