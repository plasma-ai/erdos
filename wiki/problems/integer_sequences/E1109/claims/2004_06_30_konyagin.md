***

**Claim.** Let $f(N)$ be the largest size of a set $A\subseteq\{1,\dots,N\}$
with $a+a'$ squarefree for all $a,a'\in A$, the case $a=a'$ included; S. V.
Konyagin, *Problems on the set of squarefree numbers*, Izv. Math. 68 (2004), no.
3, 493--520 (English translation of Izv. Ross. Akad. Nauk Ser. Mat. 68 (2004),
no. 3, 63--90), calls this quantity $\mathrm{ES}_N$. Theorem 1 (1.4) and Theorem
3 (1.6) of the paper state that there are effective positive constants $c_1$ and
$C_2$ with

$$
c_1\log^2N\log\log N\le f(N)\le N^{11/15}\exp\left(C_2\frac{\log N}{\sqrt{\log\log N}}\right)
$$

for all $N\ge3$. The upper bound comes from a large sieve inequality for square
moduli (Theorem 2), the lower bound from Brun's sieve with quadratic moduli
(Section 4) and a balanced sifting argument. The paper also records the
expectation $f(N)=o(N^\varepsilon)$ for every $\varepsilon>0$, the first
question of [[problems/integer_sequences/E1109/_index|Problem 1109]], as
unproved. The source card is
[[../library/integer_sequences/konyagin_2004_problems_set_square_free_numbers/_index|Konyagin 2004]];
the page name's date is the issue date in the DOI record.

**Covers.** The best known estimates of $f(N)$:
$\log^2N\log\log N\ll f(N)\ll N^{11/15+o(1)}$, improving both bounds of
[[problems/integer_sequences/E1109/claims/1987_03_01_erdos_sarkozy|Erdős and Sárközy 1987]].
Neither question of the problem is answered.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: Izv. Math. 68 (2004), no. 3, 493--520. The site labels
the problem OPEN, so its commentary crediting the bounds is not `reviewed`
evidence. The proof is not checked in this corpus.
