[[problems/integer_sequences/E1134/claims/2016_09_28_lagarias|2016_09_28_lagarias]]: Lagarias's 2016 Monthly paper publishes a reconstructed proof of Crampin
and Hilton's 1972 negative answer: the set has O(T^(τ1+ε)) elements up to
T with τ1 ≈ 0.900526, so density zero; refereed, accepted by the curator.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1134/claims/2026_06_19_axiommath|2026_06_19_axiommath]]: AxiomProver's Lean 4 file, posted to the site's thread in June 2026, proves
that the set generated from 1 by 2x+1, 3x+1 and 6x+1 has lower density
zero; not built or audited by this corpus.
