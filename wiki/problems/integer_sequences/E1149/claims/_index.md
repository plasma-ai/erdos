# problems/integer_sequences/E1149/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1149/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1149/claims/1955_01_01_lambek_moser|1955_01_01_lambek_moser]]: Lambek and Moser (Canad. J. Math. 1955): a density criterion for n coprime
to f(n), giving density 6/pi^2 for f(n) the integer part of n^{1/k} with
k >= 2 an integer; refereed.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1149/claims/2002_09_01_delmer_deshouillers|2002_09_01_delmer_deshouillers]]: Delmer and Deshouillers (Period. Math. Hungar. 2002): for every positive
non-integer c, the integers n coprime to the integer part of n^c have
density 6/pi^2; refereed.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1149/claims/2016_11_24_bergelson_richter|2016_11_24_bergelson_richter]]: Theorem 1 of Bergelson and Richter (arXiv 2016, Springer 2017): for a
Hardy-field f above log t log_4 t and strictly between consecutive powers
of t, n and the integer part of f(n) are coprime with density 6/pi^2.
