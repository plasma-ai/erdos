***

**Claim.** C. Smet and W. Van Assche, *Irrationality proof of a q-extension
of ζ(2) using little q-Jacobi polynomials*, Acta Arith. 138 (2009), no. 2,
165--178 (arXiv:0809.2501, first posted 15 September 2008, the date this
page carries).
[[../library/irrationality/smet_2009_irrationality_proof_q_extension_zeta_2/theorem_1_1|Theorem 1.1]]
(p. 2 of the arXiv version): for $q=1/p$ with $p\in\{2,3,\dots\}$, the number
$\zeta_q(2)=\sum_{k\ge1}kq^k/(1-q^k)$ is irrational, with irrationality
measure $\mu(\zeta_q(2))\le10\pi^2/(5\pi^2-24)=3.8936\ldots$. At $p=2$ the
number is $\sum_{n\ge1}\sigma(n)/2^n$, the series of
[[problems/irrationality/E0250/_index|Problem 250]], so the theorem answers
the question yes by a proof independent of Duverney's and Nesterenko's. The
paper credits Duverney with the first proof of the irrationality.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki; the claim is the cited paper's theorem.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: Acta Arithmetica, volume 138. The proof is
recorded by statement and pointer only.
