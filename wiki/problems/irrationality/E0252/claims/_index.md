# problems/irrationality/E0252/claims

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/_index|..]]

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/claims/1952_06_01_erdos|1952_06_01_erdos]]: Erdős's Monthly Problem 4493 of 1952, solved in print by J. B. Kelly in
1953, states that the sum of sigma(n)/n! is irrational, the case k = 1.

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/claims/1953_01_01_erdos_kac|1953_01_01_erdos_kac]]: Erdős and Kac's Monthly Problem 4518 of 1953, solved in print by R. Breusch
in 1954, states that the sum of sigma_2(n)/n! is irrational, the case
k = 2; every later paper on the series cites it as settled there.

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/claims/1971_03_01_erdos_straus|1971_03_01_erdos_straus]]: Erdős and Straus's general theorems on series over products a_1 through
a_n (1971, Theorem 2.26; 1974, Theorem 3.7) give, with a_n = n, that the
sum of sigma(n)/n! is irrational, the case k = 1.

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/claims/2006_12_01_schlage_puchta|2006_12_01_schlage_puchta]]: Schlage-Puchta's 2006 paper proves by a sieve count and exponential-sum
estimates that the sum of sigma_3(n)/n! is irrational, the case k = 3,
independently of Friedlander, Luca and Stoiciu; refereed in Ramanujan J.

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/claims/2006_12_01_schlage_puchta_conditional|2006_12_01_schlage_puchta_conditional]]: Schlage-Puchta's 2006 paper proves that Schinzel's Hypothesis H implies
that the sum of sigma_k(n)/n! is irrational for every k; the hypothesis is
unproven, so the page derives nothing for the problem's standing.

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/claims/2007_07_03_friedlander_luca_stoiciu|2007_07_03_friedlander_luca_stoiciu]]: Friedlander, Luca and Stoiciu's 2007 paper proves by a Chen-type sieve
theorem that the sum of sigma_3(n)/n! is irrational, the case k = 3,
independently of Schlage-Puchta; refereed in Integers.

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/claims/2007_07_03_friedlander_luca_stoiciu_conditional|2007_07_03_friedlander_luca_stoiciu_conditional]]: Friedlander, Luca and Stoiciu's 2007 paper proves that the prime k-tuples
conjecture in Dickson's form implies that the sum of sigma_k(n)/n! is
irrational for every k; the hypothesis is unproven.

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/claims/2022_09_22_pratt|2022_09_22_pratt]]: Pratt's paper, posted in 2022 and published in Acta Arithmetica in 2023,
proves by sieve methods and exponential sums that the sum of sigma_4(n)/n!
is irrational, the case k = 4.

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/claims/2026_09_08_tokengrinder|2026_09_08_tokengrinder]]: A kernel-checked Lean 4 development, published anonymously in September
2026 under the name Tokengrinder, proves that the sum of sigma_k(n)/n! is
irrational for every natural k; rebuilt and statement-audited here.
