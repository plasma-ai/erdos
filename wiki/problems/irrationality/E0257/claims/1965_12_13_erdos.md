***

**Claim.** The Theorem on p. 222 of P. Erdős, *On the irrationality of certain
series*, Math. Student 36 (1968), 222--226, received 13 December 1965, states
that if the integers $n_1<n_2<\cdots$ are pairwise coprime and
$\sum_i1/n_i<\infty$, then

$$
\sum_{i\ge1}\frac{1}{t^{n_i}-1}
$$

is irrational for every integer $t\ge2$. At $t=2$ every such set
$A=\{n_i\}$ is an instance of
[[problems/irrationality/E0257/_index|Problem 257]], answered yes. The proof
writes the sum as $\sum_mV^*(m)/t^m$, equation (3), where $V^*(m)$ counts the
$n_i$ dividing $m$, and shows that the base-$t$ expansion of this value is
infinite but contains arbitrarily long blocks of zeros: $k$ simultaneous
congruences, equation (4), choose $y$ with $V^*(y+i)=t^i$ for $i=1,\ldots,k$,
which pairwise coprimality makes possible, and the estimates (5) to (14)
bound the digits that follow. Erdős writes that more complicated arguments
show the coprimality condition to be superfluous, giving no details, that the
convergence condition could be replaced by a weaker one, and that he expects
the series to be irrational whenever $n_{k+1}-n_k\to\infty$ and perhaps
whenever $n_k/k\to\infty$; on p. 226 he adds that without coprimality the
proof needs the fact that a number whose multiples $t^n\alpha$ have
infinitely many distinct fractional parts is irrational, and that he cannot
handle the case in which the $n_i$ are all the primes. That case is Problem
69, settled by Tao and Teräväinen; see
[[problems/irrationality/E0257/claims/2025_12_01_tao_teravainen|their claim page]].
The source card is
[[../library/irrationality/erdos_1969_irrationality_certain_series/_index|erdos_1969_irrationality_certain_series]].

**Covers.** Every infinite pairwise coprime $A$ with $\sum_{a\in A}1/a<\infty$,
for the base $2$ of the question and for every integer base $t\ge2$. Not
covered: supports in which some pair shares a factor, and supports with
divergent reciprocal sum. Erdős's statement that coprimality is superfluous
is made without details, so the sets it would add are not covered here;
[[problems/irrationality/E0257/claims/2026_09_11_cook|Cook's pending claim]]
writes out an argument for that extension.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: the Mathematics Student, volume 36 (1968), pp.
222--226, received 13 December 1965 as the paper's header prints. The site
labels the problem OPEN, and its remark crediting the paper with this class
is commentary on an open problem, not acceptance, so no `reviewed` evidence is
listed. The proof is not checked here.
