# problems/irrationality/E0257/claims

[[problems/irrationality/E0257/_index|..]]

[[problems/irrationality/E0257/claims/1948_07_08_erdos|1948_07_08_erdos]]: Erdős's 1948 theorem that the sum of 1/(t^n - 1) over all n is irrational
for every integer t with |t| > 1 settles the problem for the support N and,
through the base t = 2^d, for every set of multiples dN.

[[problems/irrationality/E0257/claims/1965_12_13_erdos|1965_12_13_erdos]]: Erdős's 1968 paper in the Mathematics Student proves that the sum of
1/(t^n - 1) over a pairwise coprime set with convergent reciprocal sum is
irrational for every integer base t at least 2, a class of supports.

[[problems/irrationality/E0257/claims/2019_08_14_duverney_tachiya|2019_08_14_duverney_tachiya]]: Duverney and Tachiya's 2019 paper proves the sum of 1/(q^n - 1) over the
products of powers below s of a pairwise coprime, polynomially bounded
sequence, the squarefree integers among them, irrational.

[[problems/irrationality/E0257/claims/2025_12_01_tao_teravainen|2025_12_01_tao_teravainen]]: Tao and Teräväinen's theorem that the sum over primes p of 1/(2^p - 1) is
irrational settles the problem for the support consisting of the primes,
the case that is Problem 69.

[[problems/irrationality/E0257/claims/2026_09_11_cook|2026_09_11_cook]]: Cook's 2026 note, drafted with AI agents, claims that the sum of
1/(b^a - 1) over any infinite set with convergent reciprocal sum is
irrational at every integer base b at least 2.
