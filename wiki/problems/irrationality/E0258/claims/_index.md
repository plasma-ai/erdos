[[problems/irrationality/E0258/claims/1971_03_01_erdos_straus|1971_03_01_erdos_straus]]: Erdős and Straus's 1971 paper proves the series irrational for every
nondecreasing integer sequence with a_1 at least 2 (Theorem 2.23) and for
every sequence with |a_n| above a constant times (log n)^(3/4) (Lemma 2.14).

[[problems/irrationality/E0258/claims/2026_04_14_chojecki|2026_04_14_chojecki]]: Chojecki, working with GPT-5.4 Pro, deduces the irrationality of the
series for every positive-integer sequence tending to infinity from Tao and
Teräväinen's theorem on prime factors of consecutive integers.
