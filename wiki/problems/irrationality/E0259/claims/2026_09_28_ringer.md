***

**Claim.** Stefan Ringer, *Normality of squarefree-weighted series*, dated
27 September 2026, four pages, in the author's GitHub repository
`StefanRinger/erdos-259-research` (committed 2026-09-28). Its Theorem 1
states that for every fixed integer $B\ge2$ the number

$$
\alpha_B=\sum_{n\ge1}n\,\mu(n)^2B^{-n}
$$

is normal to base $B$, with the discrepancy of the orbit
$(\{B^n\alpha_B\})_{n\le M}$ bounded by a constant times $(\log\log M)^{-1}$;
the base and the number vary together, and no absolute normality is
asserted. At $B=2$, $\alpha_2$ is the sum of
[[problems/irrationality/E0259/_index|Problem 259]], and a number normal to
some base is irrational, so the theorem claims more than the question asks.
The manuscript cites Chen and Ruzsa's proof of the irrationality, on
[[problems/irrationality/E0259/claims/1999_02_01_chen_ruzsa|their claim page]],
as already settling the question. Its footnote says that the AI systems GPT 6
Astra and Fable 5.1 assisted with the development and checking of the proofs;
the author's thread comment of 2026-09-28, which posts the manuscript, repeats
that attribution and says a Lean formalization will follow. The repository's
`lean` folder is empty at the pinned commit.

**Standing.** Claimed. The manuscript is not refereed; no
outside review of it and no acknowledgment by the site's curator is recorded,
and the corpus records no check of its proof; it adds nothing to the
problem's standing, which rests on Chen and Ruzsa's refereed proof.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki.
