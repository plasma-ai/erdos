# problems/irrationality/E0260/claims

[[problems/irrationality/E0260/_index|..]]

[[problems/irrationality/E0260/claims/1981_05_11_erdos|1981_05_11_erdos]]: Erdős's 1981 note in the Comptes Rendus proves that the sum of a-n over
two to the a-n is irrational for every increasing integer sequence whose
consecutive gaps tend to infinity, a class of instances of the question.

[[problems/irrationality/E0260/claims/2026_06_23_wang_grau_ribas|2026_06_23_wang_grau_ribas]]: Wang and Grau Ribas's 2026 preprint (later versions by Wang alone) claims
that a rational sum of n/2^n over an infinite set has positive density on
every large dyadic block, so Problem 260's series is irrational.
