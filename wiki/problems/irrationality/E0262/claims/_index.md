# problems/irrationality/E0262/claims

[[problems/irrationality/E0262/_index|..]]

[[problems/irrationality/E0262/claims/1975_01_01_erdos|1975_01_01_erdos]]: Erdős's 1975 paper proves that every sum of reciprocals of positive
multiples of 2^(2^k) is irrational, so 2^(2^n) is an irrationality sequence
of the problem's kind.

[[problems/irrationality/E0262/claims/1991_01_01_hancl|1991_01_01_hancl]]: Hančl proves that an irrationality sequence must satisfy limsup of
log_2 log_2 a_n over n at least 1, which with Erdős's example 2^(2^n)
pins the slowest growth at doubly exponential.
