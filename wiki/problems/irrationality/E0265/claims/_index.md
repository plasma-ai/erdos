# problems/irrationality/E0265/claims

[[problems/irrationality/E0265/_index|..]]

[[problems/irrationality/E0265/claims/2024_11_27_kovac_tao|2024_11_27_kovac_tao]]: Kovač and Tao construct a strictly increasing integer sequence with both
reciprocal sums rational and a_n^(1/beta^n) tending to infinity for any base
beta below the square root of 6/5; the exact exponent is not determined.

[[problems/irrationality/E0265/claims/2026_08_28_cam|2026_08_28_cam]]: A 2026 write-up posted to the problem's proof-claims thread asserts a
construction, for every beta below (sqrt(13)-1)/2, of an increasing integer
sequence with both reciprocal sums rational and a_n^(1/beta^n) unbounded.

[[problems/irrationality/E0265/claims/2026_09_07_kitamura|2026_09_07_kitamura]]: Kenta Kitamura's Lean 4 development, developed with ChatGPT and OpenAI Codex
using GPT-6 (Astra), claims that no increasing sequence with both reciprocal
sums rational has limsup of a_n^(1/2^n) above 1; the exact exponent is open.
