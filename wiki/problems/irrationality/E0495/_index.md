# Problem 495

[[problems/irrationality/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Let $\alpha,\beta \in \mathbb{R}$. Is it true that

$$
\liminf_{n\to \infty} n \| n\alpha \| \| n\beta\| =0
$$

where $\|x\|$ is the distance from $x$ to the nearest integer?

**Status.** Open.
