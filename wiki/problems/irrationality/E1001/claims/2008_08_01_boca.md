***

**Claim.** The limit $f(A,c)=\lim_{N\to\infty}S(N,A,c)$ exists for all $A>0$ and
$c>1$, and the paper identifies it, answering both questions of
[[problems/irrationality/E1001/_index|Problem 1001]]. The paper is F. P. Boca, A
problem of Erdős, Szüsz and Turán concerning Diophantine approximations, Int. J.
Number Theory 4 (2008), no. 4, 691--708, published in the August 2008 issue (the
page's date is the issue month; the issue gives no day). The card
[[../library/irrationality/boca_2008_problem_erdos_szusz_turan_diophantine_approximations/_index|boca_2008_problem_erdos_szusz_turan_diophantine_approximations]]
records the paper and its abstract, which does not say whether the
identification is a closed form or an integral expression.

**Acceptance.** The `refereed` evidence is the journal publication cited
above. The `reviewed` evidence is the documented acceptance by the catalog
erdosproblems.com, whose page for the problem (the `discussion` link)
carries the label SOLVED and whose curator, Thomas Bloom, credits Boca,
independently of Xiong and Zaharescu, with an alternative, more explicit
proof of the existence of the limit; the SOLVED label
is read as resting on these two proofs for the explicit form. Existence was
first proved by
[[problems/irrationality/E1001/claims/1966_01_01_kesten_sos|Kesten and Sós]];
the independent explicit treatment of
[[problems/irrationality/E1001/claims/2006_01_01_xiong_zaharescu|Xiong and Zaharescu]]
appeared two years earlier. No independent check of the proof is recorded.
