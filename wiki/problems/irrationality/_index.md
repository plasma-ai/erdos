# Irrationality and Diophantine Approximation

irrational.

[[problems/irrationality/E0243/_index|E0243/]]: Asks whether a sequence whose

reciprocal sum, must eventually satisfy a fixed recurrence.

[[problems/irrationality/E0247/_index|E0247/]]: Asks whether the sum of two to
the power minus a-n is transcendental whenever the increasing integer sequence
a-n has unbounded ratio to n.

[[problems/irrationality/E0249/_index|E0249/]]: Asks whether the sum over n of
Euler's totient function of n divided by two to the n is irrational.

[[problems/irrationality/E0250/_index|E0250/]]: Asks whether the sum over n of
the sum-of-divisors function of n divided by two to the n is irrational.

the nth prime divided by two to the n is irrational.

k, the sum over n of the sum of kth powers of the divisors of n divided by n
factorial is irrational; answered yes by a 2026 kernel-checked Lean proof
rebuilt, replayed and statement-audited here.

[[problems/irrationality/E0257/_index|E0257/]]: Asks whether the sum of one over
two to the n minus one, taken over any infinite set of naturals, is irrational.

[[problems/irrationality/E0258/_index|E0258/]]: States that the divisor-function
product-denominator series is irrational for every positive-integer sequence
tending to infinity.

[[problems/irrationality/E0259/_index|E0259/]]: Asks whether the sum over
squarefree n of n divided by two to the n is irrational.

[[problems/irrationality/E0260/_index|E0260/]]: Asks whether the sum of a-n
divided by two to the a-n is irrational for every increasing sequence whose
ratio to n tends to infinity.

[[problems/irrationality/E0262/_index|E0262/]]: Asks how slowly an increasing
integer sequence can grow while every sum of reciprocals of positive integer
multiples of its terms stays irrational.

[[problems/irrationality/E0263/_index|E0263/]]: Asks whether two to the two to
the n keeps reciprocal sums irrational under all asymptotically equal
replacements, and whether such sequences must grow fast.

[[problems/irrationality/E0264/_index|E0264/]]: The powers-of-two case is false;
asks whether factorial denominators keep their reciprocal sum irrational under
every bounded nonzero integer perturbation.

[[problems/irrationality/E0265/_index|E0265/]]: Asks how fast an increasing
integer sequence can grow when the sum of reciprocals of its terms and of its
terms minus one are both rational.

[[problems/irrationality/E0266/_index|E0266/]]: Asks whether every sequence of
positive integers with convergent reciprocal sum admits a positive integer shift
making the shifted reciprocal sum irrational.

[[problems/irrationality/E0267/_index|E0267/]]: Asks whether the sum of
reciprocals of Fibonacci numbers along any geometrically growing index sequence
must be irrational.

[[problems/irrationality/E0269/_index|E0269/]]: Asks whether the sum of
reciprocals of the least common multiples of the first n integers built from a
fixed finite set of primes is irrational.

[[problems/irrationality/E0270/_index|E0270/]]: Asks whether the sum over n of
one over the product of the f of n consecutive integers starting at n plus one
is irrational whenever f tends to infinity.

[[problems/irrationality/E0495/_index|E0495/]]: Asks whether, for all pairs of
real numbers, the product of n with the distances from n times each number to
the nearest integer has limit inferior zero.

[[problems/irrationality/E0496/_index|E0496/]]: Asks whether, for every positive
irrational alpha, sums of two positive squares come arbitrarily close to alpha
times a positive square; the site's wording over every irrational alpha fails at
negative ones.

[[problems/irrationality/E0998/_index|E0998/]]: Asks whether an interval with

both endpoints, is false.

[[problems/irrationality/E0999/_index|E0999/]]: Asks whether almost-all
approximability of alpha by fractions within f of q over q equals divergence of
the sum of the totient of q times f of q over q.

[[problems/irrationality/E1000/_index|E1000/]]: Concerns how many fractions with

equal to an earlier term of the sequence.

[[problems/irrationality/E1001/_index|E1001/]]: Asks whether the measure of
reals well approximated by fractions with denominator between N and cN tends to
a limit, and what that limit is explicitly.

[[problems/irrationality/E1002/_index|E1002/]]: Asks whether the logarithmically

irrational has an asymptotic distribution function.

[[problems/irrationality/E1049/_index|E1049/]]: Asks whether the sum over all n

[[problems/irrationality/E1050/_index|E1050/]]: Asks whether the sum over all n
