# problems/number_theory/E0034/claims

[[problems/number_theory/E0034/claims/1986_03_01_hegyvari|1986_03_01_hegyvari]]: Hegyvári's Theorem 1: (1/3 + o(1))n distinct integers in [1, n] with all
consecutive sums distinct; heading a permutation, they give at least
(1/18 + o(1))n^2 distinct consecutive sums, the first disproof of Problem 34.

[[problems/number_theory/E0034/claims/2015_04_27_konieczny|2015_04_27_konieczny]]: Konieczny's Proposition 1.1: the permutation 1, n, 2, n-1, 3, n-2, ... has
pairwise distinct consecutive sums of odd length, hence at least n^2/4
distinct consecutive sums, so S(pi) = o(n^2) fails, refuting Problem 34.
