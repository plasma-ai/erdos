***

Kovač and Tao prove, as Theorem 2.8, that for every positive integer $d$
there is $\beta>1$ such that the $d$-tuples
$(\sum_k1/a_k,\sum_k1/(a_k+1),\ldots,\sum_k1/(a_k+d-1))$ over strictly
increasing sequences with $a_k^{1/\beta^k}\to\infty$ fill a set with
nonempty interior, and draw from it Corollary 2.10: for every $d$ the set of
such $d$-tuples over all infinite $A\subseteq\mathbb N$ with
$\sum_{n\in A}1/n<\infty$ has nonempty interior. At $d=3$ this is the set
$X$ of [[problems/number_theory/E0268/_index|Problem 268]], so the corollary
answers the question yes a second time, by a different route, through the
rapidly growing sequences of Theorem 2.8, from the explicit ball of
[[problems/number_theory/E0268/claims/2024_05_13_kovac|Kovač's page]].
Corollary 2.9 adds, for every $d$, one such sequence for which all $d$
shifted sums are rational.
