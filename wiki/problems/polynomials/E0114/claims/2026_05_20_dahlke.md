**Claim.** Theorem 1.1 of B. E. Dahlke, *The cubic Erdős–Herzog–Piranian
lemniscate inequality* (Zenodo, 2026): for every monic cubic $p$ the length
$L(p)$ of the lemniscate $\{z:\lvert p(z)\rvert=1\}$ satisfies
$L(p)\le L(z^3-1)=2^{1/3}B(\tfrac16,\tfrac12)$. This is the degree-3 inequality
of [[problems/polynomials/E0114/_index|Problem 114]]; the manuscript proves the
inequality only and has no uniqueness clause. The argument takes the
Eremenko–Hayman reduction as an external theorem, in the form of Proposition 1.2
of
[[../library/polynomials/tao_2025_maximal_length_erdos_herzog_piranian_lemniscate/_index|Tao 2025]]:
some monic cubic maximizing the length has, after the length-preserving
normalizations, no $z^2$ term, a real non-positive constant term, a connected
lemniscate and all its critical points on the lemniscate. Up to those symmetries
such cubics form the one-parameter family $p_\phi(z)=z^3-3c_\phi^2z-\cos\phi$
with $2c_\phi^3=i\sin\phi$ and $0\le\phi\le\pi/2$, where $\phi=0$ gives $z^3-1$.
The scaling $z=2c_\phi x$ turns $\lvert p_\phi(z)\rvert=1$ into a condition on
the Chebyshev polynomial $T_3(x)$, the substitution $x=(u+u^{-1})/2$ (the
Joukowski map) turns that condition into two problems on rays, and a
three-branch estimate followed by a one-variable comparison shows that every
$\phi>0$ gives a strictly shorter lemniscate than $\phi=0$. The author announced
the manuscript on the site's discussion thread on 2026-05-21; the post drew no
reply.

**Covers.** Degree $3$ only, the inequality $L(p)\le L(z^3-1)$ for monic cubics,
resting on the Eremenko–Hayman reduction as Tao states it. Degree $2$ is settled
by [[problems/polynomials/E0114/claims/1998_01_01_wang|Wang 1998]] and
[[problems/polynomials/E0114/claims/1999_09_01_eremenko_hayman|Eremenko–Hayman 1999]],
and all sufficiently large degrees are claimed, pending, by
[[problems/polynomials/E0114/claims/2025_12_13_tao|Tao 2025]].
[[problems/polynomials/E0114/claims/2026_08_22_chatelet|Chatelet 2026]] later
claimed the same degree, with a uniqueness clause.
