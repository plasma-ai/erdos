# problems/polynomials/E0116/claims

[[problems/polynomials/E0116/_index|..]]

[[problems/polynomials/E0116/claims/1961_01_01_pommerenke|1961_01_01_pommerenke]]: Pommerenke proves that a monic polynomial with zeros in the closed unit disk
has modulus at most one on a disk of radius 1/(2e n^2), so on a set of area
at least a constant over n^4; refereed and credited by the site.

[[problems/polynomials/E0116/claims/2025_03_24_krishnapur_lundberg_ramachandran|2025_03_24_krishnapur_lundberg_ramachandran]]: Krishnapur, Lundberg and Ramachandran prove that the least area where a monic
degree n polynomial with zeros in the closed unit disk has modulus below one
lies between c/log n and C/log log n; a 2025 preprint credited by the site.
