# problems/polynomials/E0230/claims

[[problems/polynomials/E0230/_index|..]]

[[problems/polynomials/E0230/claims/1980_09_01_kahane|1980_09_01_kahane]]: Kahane's 1980 theorem: for every n there is a polynomial of degree n with
complex coefficients of modulus one whose modulus on the unit circle is
(1+o(1)) times the square root of n uniformly, so the answer is no.

[[problems/polynomials/E0230/claims/2009_06_30_bombieri_bourgain|2009_06_30_bombieri_bourgain]]: Theorems 4 and 7 of Bombieri and Bourgain: unimodular polynomials whose
modulus on the unit circle is the square root of n up to an error of order
n to the power 7/18 plus epsilon, also by an effective construction.

[[problems/polynomials/E0230/claims/2026_09_23_openai|2026_09_23_openai]]: Theorem 1.1 of the OpenAI release manuscript of 23 September 2026: for
every positive eta and every large N some signs plus or minus one give a
polynomial of maximum modulus at most (1+eta) times the square root of N.
