# problems/polynomials/E0485/claims

[[problems/polynomials/E0485/claims/1987_01_01_schinzel|1987_01_01_schinzel]]: Proves that the square of a polynomial with k nonzero terms has more than
log log k over log 2 terms, so the minimum tends to infinity, answering the
question yes; refereed in Acta Arithmetica, 1987.

[[problems/polynomials/E0485/claims/2009_03_31_schinzel_zannier|2009_03_31_schinzel_zannier]]: Proves that the square of a polynomial with k nonzero terms has at least
2 plus log(k minus 1) over log 8 terms, so the minimum grows at least
logarithmically; refereed in Rendiconti Lincei, 2009.
