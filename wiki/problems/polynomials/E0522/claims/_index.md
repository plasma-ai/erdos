# problems/polynomials/E0522/claims

[[problems/polynomials/E0522/_index|..]]

[[problems/polynomials/E0522/claims/2026_04_20_chojecki|2026_04_20_chojecki]]: Chojecki, working with GPT-5.4 Pro and GPT-5.5 Pro, claims that the number
of roots in the closed unit disk is n over two plus a power saving almost
surely; two notes on the site's thread, revised after a reader found an error.

[[problems/polynomials/E0522/claims/2026_04_28_kwon_zou|2026_04_28_kwon_zou]]: Kwon and Zou, working with ChatGPT 5.5 Pro, claim that the number of roots
in the closed unit disk is n over two up to n to the seven eighths plus delta
almost surely; a note in a public repository, no review.

[[problems/polynomials/E0522/claims/2026_07_23_snyder|2026_07_23_snyder]]: A Lean 4 theorem credited to Colin Snyder of Star Fleet Math states that the
number of roots in the closed unit disk divided by n over two tends to one
almost surely for independent fair signs; lean-proofs repository, no write-up.

[[problems/polynomials/E0522/claims/2026_09_25_kawada|2026_09_25_kawada]]: Kawada claims an almost-sure law for the number of roots in disks of radius
one plus x over n, whose case x equal to zero is the asked convergence, with
a Lean 4 formalization; a Zenodo preprint and a claim on the site's tab.

[[problems/polynomials/E0522/claims/2026_09_25_kitamura|2026_09_25_kitamura]]: Kitamura, with AI assistance, gives a Lean 4 proof that the number of roots
in the closed unit disk divided by n over two tends to one almost surely, for
sign and for zero-one coefficients; linked by formal-conjectures, not audited.
