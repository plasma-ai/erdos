[[problems/polynomials/E0524/claims/2026_01_30_chojecki|2026_01_30_chojecki]]: Chojecki's note, written with GPT 5.2, reproves the Salem–Zygmund upper
envelope of M_n(t) and finds the scale of its smallest values along a
sparse subsequence; it does not determine the lower envelope.

[[problems/polynomials/E0524/claims/2026_04_21_letwin_sawhney|2026_04_21_letwin_sawhney]]: Letwin and Sawhney prove that almost surely the lower envelope of the
maximum of a random Littlewood polynomial on [-1,1] is sqrt(n) times
exp(-(3 pi^2/4)^{1/3} (log log n)^{1/3}) to first order in the exponent.
