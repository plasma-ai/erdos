***

**Claim.** For every monic $f$ with all roots in the open unit disk, two
roots are joined inside $\{z:\lvert f(z)\rvert<1\}$ by a path of length
less than $2$, the affirmative answer to
[[problems/polynomials/E1041/_index|Problem 1041]]. The user kasko37 posted
the write-up on the site's discussion thread on 2026-04-22 as a solution
announced by GPT-5.4 Thinking, without a summary of the argument. Nat
Sothanaphan noted at first that the write-up avoided the gap of the earlier
attempt on [[problems/polynomials/E1041/claims/2026_03_17_ani|ani 2026]] and
at least had a chance against Mac Lane's winding lemniscates.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki.

**Standing.** Rejected. Sothanaphan's AI check with GPT-5.5 (2026-04-27)
found a serious gap in the proof of Lemma 6, where subharmonicity is used to
bound $\lvert\phi'(r)\rvert$ by the circle average of $\lvert\phi'\rvert$,
while the inequality holds for the value at the circle's center; the check
judged the gap not easily repairable, and Ho Boon Suan had earlier pointed
out the same gap to Sothanaphan. The thread records no repair, no proof
claim was registered on the site's proof-claims tab, and the later
degree-seven counterexample on
[[problems/polynomials/E1041/claims/2026_09_07_ani|ani 2026 (counterexample)]],
if correct, shows that the claimed statement is false.
