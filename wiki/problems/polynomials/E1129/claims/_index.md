# problems/polynomials/E1129/claims

[[problems/polynomials/E1129/_index|..]]

[[problems/polynomials/E1129/claims/1978_12_01_deboor_pinkus|1978_12_01_deboor_pinkus]]: De Boor and Pinkus prove that among node systems containing both endpoints
exactly one equioscillates and it alone minimizes the Lebesgue constant;
refereed in J. Approx. Theory and credited by the site's curator.

[[problems/polynomials/E1129/claims/1978_12_01_kilgore|1978_12_01_kilgore]]: Kilgore proves Bernstein's conjecture that, among node systems containing
both endpoints, the Lebesgue constant is minimized exactly by the
equioscillating system, by an argument independent of de Boor and Pinkus.

[[problems/polynomials/E1129/claims/2015_06_01_rack_vajda|2015_06_01_rack_vajda]]: Rack and Vajda describe explicitly every four-node system minimizing the
Lebesgue constant on [-1,1] and prove free-node minimizers non-unique for
every n >= 3; refereed in Studia UBB Mathematica, credited by the curator.
