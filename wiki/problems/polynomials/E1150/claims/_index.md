# problems/polynomials/E1150/claims

[[problems/polynomials/E1150/_index|..]]

[[problems/polynomials/E1150/claims/2016_09_12_el_abdalaoui|2016_09_12_el_abdalaoui]]: A 2016 preprint claims that no sequence of plus or minus one polynomials is
square L^2-flat, that is L^4-flat, so Erdős's ultraflat conjecture holds;
rejected after the accepted disproof.

[[problems/polynomials/E1150/claims/2025_04_30_el_abdalaoui|2025_04_30_el_abdalaoui]]: A 2025 preprint claims that plus or minus one polynomials are never L^alpha
flat for even alpha above two, which would give the universal gap asked
for; rejected after objections in the site's thread and the accepted disproof.

[[problems/polynomials/E1150/claims/2025_09_04_el_abdalaoui|2025_09_04_el_abdalaoui]]: A September 2025 preprint claims that plus or minus one polynomials are not
L^alpha-flat for any alpha > 0, so their maximum modulus exceeds (1+d) times
the square root of n; rejected after the accepted disproof.

[[problems/polynomials/E1150/claims/2026_09_23_openai|2026_09_23_openai]]: Theorem 1.1 of the OpenAI release manuscript of 23 September 2026: for
every positive eta and every large N some signs give a polynomial of maximum
modulus at most (1+eta) times the square root of N, so the answer is no.
