[[problems/primes/E0004/claims/1938_10_01_rankin|1938_10_01_rankin]]: Rankin's 1938 lower bound for the largest prime gap below X, with the
constant 1/3, answers the question yes for every C below 1/3; refereed in
the Journal of the London Mathematical Society.

[[problems/primes/E0004/claims/2014_08_20_ford_green_konyagin_tao|2014_08_20_ford_green_konyagin_tao]]: Proves that the largest prime gap below X exceeds Rankin's 1938 scale by a
factor tending to infinity, so the question's bound holds for every
constant; refereed in the Annals and credited by the site's curator.

[[problems/primes/E0004/claims/2014_08_21_maynard|2014_08_21_maynard]]: Proves that the ratio of the prime gap to Rankin's scale has infinite limit

[[problems/primes/E0004/claims/2026_08_26_alexeev|2026_08_26_alexeev]]: Two Lean modules in Boris Alexeev's lean-proofs repository prove the question

[[problems/primes/E0004/claims/2026_08_26_dottedcalculator|2026_08_26_dottedcalculator]]: A manuscript written by an AI system and posted by the forum user
