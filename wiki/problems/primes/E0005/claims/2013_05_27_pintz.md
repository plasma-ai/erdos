***

**Claim.** Write $J$ for the set of limit points of $(p_{n+1}-p_n)/\log n$.
Theorem 3 of J. Pintz, *Polignac numbers, conjectures of Erdős on gaps between
primes, arithmetic progressions in primes, and the bounded gap conjecture*, From
Arithmetic to Zeta-Functions, Springer (2016), 367--384, states: "There is an
ineffective constant $c>0$ such that $[0,c]\subset J$." Each $C$ in $[0,c]$ is
then the limit of $(p_{n_i+1}-p_{n_i})/\log n_i$ along some sequence $n_i$,
which answers the question of [[problems/primes/E0005/_index|Problem 5]] yes for
those $C$. The chapter is described on
[[../library/primes/pintz_2016_polignac_numbers_conjectures_erdos_gaps_primes/_index|its library card]].

**Covers.** $C=0$ and every $C$ in $[0,c]$ for an ineffective $c>0$ that the
paper does not determine; no positive $C$ is named.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki.

**Standing.** Claimed. The result appeared as a chapter of the edited volume
From Arithmetic to Zeta-Functions (Springer 2016), with no evidence that the
chapter was refereed, so no `refereed` evidence is listed. Not reviewed: the
site's commentary credits $[0,c]\subset S$ to [Pi16], but the site labels the
problem OPEN, so that commentary is not acceptance.
