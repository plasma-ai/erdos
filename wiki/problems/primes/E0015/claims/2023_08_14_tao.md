***

**Claim.** Assume Conjecture 1.3 of the paper, a quantitative form of the
Hardy-Littlewood prime tuples conjecture. Then the series

$$
\sum_{n\ge2}\frac{(-1)^{\pi(n)}}{n\log n}
$$

converges, and therefore so does the series $\sum_{n\ge1}(-1)^n n/p_n$ of
[[problems/primes/E0015/_index|Problem 15]]. This is Theorem 1.4 of Tao's
paper. The two series stand or fall together by an observation of Said that
Section 2 of the paper proves as relation (2.1): the sign $(-1)^{\pi(n)}$
changes exactly at the primes, and the partial sums of $\sum(-1)^nn/p_n$
differ from half of the partial sums of the second series, taken up to
$x\log x$, by a quantity that converges (a constant plus $o(1)$); the
reconstruction of that relation is filed at
[[research/erdos_15/relation_2_1_reconstruction|relation (2.1)]]. The proof
of the theorem applies the van der Corput $A$-process,
which reduces convergence to the equidistribution of the parity of the number
of primes in short intervals $(x,x+\lambda\log x]$ with $\lambda$ of order
$(\log\log x)^{4.4}$, and then replaces the primes by the random sifted model
of Banks, Ford and Tao, in which the sifting steps damp the irregularities of
that parity; the cancellation between tuples of different sizes is what the
uniform range of the hypothesis supplies.

**Hypothesis.** Conjecture 1.3 asks that, for $x\ge10$, every tuple size
$k\le(\log\log x)^5$ and every choice of $k$ distinct shifts in
$[0,\log^2x]$, the number of $n\le x$ for which all $k$ shifted values are
prime equals the singular-series prediction of Hardy and Littlewood up to a
power-saving error, uniformly in $k$ and in the shifts. It is Tao's
restatement of a conjecture of Kuperberg, filed as
[[../library/primes/kuperberg_2023_sums_singular_series_large_sets_tail/conjecture_1_3|Kuperberg's Conjecture 1.3]],
with the tuple-size range widened from $(\log\log x)^3$ to $(\log\log x)^5$
and the admissibility requirement dropped. The hypothesis is unproved, and
the claim gives no unconditional answer.

**Scope.** The claim is conditional and settles no standing of the problem by
itself: unconditionally, whether the partial sums of $\sum(-1)^nn/p_n$
converge remains open. The absolute series $\sum n/p_n$ diverges, since
$n/p_n\sim1/\log n$, so any convergence is conditional in the analytic sense
as well. The paper's numerical computation (p. 1) suggests slow convergence
to roughly $-0.052161$.

The library card
[[../library/primes/tao_2023_convergence_alternating_series_erdos_assuming_hardy/_index|Tao 2023]]
records the paper in its arXiv v3 text; the published text has not been compared
with it. A reconstruction of the theorem and its same-paper lemmas is filed
under [[research/erdos_15/_index|the Problem 15 research folder]]; its four
pages were each independently reviewed in a focused review on 2026-09-28, with
the verdicts fidelity faithful and the Theorem 1.4 argument sound conditional on
Conjecture 1.3, and no tier assigned. Those reviews cover the reconstruction and
are not acceptance evidence for this claim, whose only listed evidence is the
refereed publication; the paper's own text has not been independently reviewed
here.

**Acceptance.** The result is refereed: T. Tao, *The convergence of an
alternating series of Erdős, assuming the Hardy--Littlewood prime tuples
conjecture*, Comm. Amer. Math. Soc. 4 (2024), no. 3, 80--96, DOI
10.1090/cams/29. The site's commentary records that the paper proves
convergence under a strong form of the prime tuples conjecture and keeps the
problem open, so no curator acceptance of a solution is listed. The page is
dated by the arXiv posting of 14 August 2023.

**Depends on.** Nothing on this wiki beyond the cited paper; its hypothesis,
Conjecture 1.3, is stated above.
