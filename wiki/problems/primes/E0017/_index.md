# Problem 17

[[problems/primes/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Are there infinitely many primes $p$ such that every even number
$n\leq p-3$ can be written as a difference of primes $n=q_1-q_2$ where
$q_1,q_2\leq p$?

**Status.** Open.

**Source.** [erdosproblems.com/17](https://www.erdosproblems.com/17), accessed
2026-09-04. Cite as: T. F. Bloom, Erdős Problem #17,
https://www.erdosproblems.com/17.
