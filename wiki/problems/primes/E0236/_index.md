# Problem 236

[[problems/primes/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Let $f(n)$ count the number of solutions to $n=p+2^k$ for prime
$p$ and $k\geq 0$. Is it true that $f(n)=o(\log n)$?

**Status.** Open.
