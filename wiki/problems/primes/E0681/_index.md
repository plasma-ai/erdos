# Problem 681

[[problems/primes/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Is it true that for all large $n$ there exists $k$ such that
$n+k$ is composite and

$$
p(n+k)>k^2,
$$

where $p(m)$ is the least prime factor of $m$?
