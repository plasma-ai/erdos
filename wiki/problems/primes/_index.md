# Primes

[[problems/_index|..]]

[[problems/primes/E0004/_index|E0004/]]: Asks whether prime gaps exceed any
given constant times log n times a slowly growing factor built from repeated
logarithms infinitely often.

[[problems/primes/E0005/_index|E0005/]]: Asks whether, for every constant C at
least 0, the gap after the nth prime divided by log n tends to exactly C along
some sequence of indices n.

[[problems/primes/E0006/_index|E0006/]]: Asks whether there are infinitely many
n for which three consecutive prime gaps are strictly increasing.

[[problems/primes/E0015/_index|E0015/]]: Asks whether the alternating sum of n
divided by the nth prime converges; open, with Tao's proof of convergence
conditional on a strong Hardy-Littlewood prime tuples conjecture.

[[problems/primes/E0017/_index|E0017/]]: Asks whether infinitely many primes p
have every even number up to p minus 3 expressible as a difference of two primes
not exceeding p.

[[problems/primes/E0049/_index|E0049/]]: Asks whether a set of integers up to N
on which Euler's totient function is strictly increasing has at most
(1+o(1))π(N) elements, or even o(N); Erdős's exact conjecture, that the primes
are a largest such set, is open.

[[problems/primes/E0218/_index|E0218/]]: Asks whether consecutive prime gaps
increase half the time and decrease half the time, and whether two consecutive
gaps are equal infinitely often.

[[problems/primes/E0233/_index|E0233/]]: Asks whether the sum of the squares of
the first N prime gaps is at most a constant times N times the square of the
logarithm of N.

[[problems/primes/E0234/_index|E0234/]]: Asks whether the density of primes
whose gap to the next prime is below c times the logarithm exists for every c
and varies continuously in c.

[[problems/primes/E0236/_index|E0236/]]: Asks whether the number of ways to
write n as a prime plus a power of two is always small compared with the
logarithm of n.

[[problems/primes/E0237/_index|E0237/]]: Asks whether any set of integers with
at least logarithmically many elements up to N gives some integers unboundedly
many representations as prime plus member.

[[problems/primes/E0238/_index|E0238/]]: Asks whether for any positive constants
there are, below every large x, more than a multiple of log x consecutive primes
that are pairwise far apart.

[[problems/primes/E0240/_index|E0240/]]: Asks whether some infinite set of
primes has the property that the gaps between consecutive integers built only
from those primes tend to infinity.

[[problems/primes/E0244/_index|E0244/]]: Asks whether, for a constant greater
than 1, the integers formed as a prime plus the integer part of a power of that
constant have positive density.

[[problems/primes/E0427/_index|E0427/]]: Asks whether, for every n and d, some
run of consecutive primes starting after the n-th prime has sum divisible by d.

[[problems/primes/E0428/_index|E0428/]]: Asks for a set with positive relative
density among the primes such that, for infinitely many n, subtracting each
member from n always gives a prime.

[[problems/primes/E0431/_index|E0431/]]: Asks whether two infinite sets have a
sumset that agrees with the set of primes apart from finitely many exceptions.

[[problems/primes/E0454/_index|E0454/]]: Asks whether the least sum of a
symmetric pair of primes around the nth prime exceeds twice the nth prime by an
unbounded amount infinitely often.

[[problems/primes/E0458/_index|E0458/]]: Asks whether the least common multiple
up to one below the next prime is always less than the previous prime times the
least common multiple up to it.

[[problems/primes/E0459/_index|E0459/]]: Estimates the largest v such that no
integer strictly between u and v is built only from primes dividing the product
of u and v.

[[problems/primes/E0461/_index|E0461/]]: Asks whether the number of distinct
smooth parts, using primes below t, of the t consecutive integers after n is
always at least a constant times t.

[[problems/primes/E0462/_index|E0462/]]: Asks whether the sum of the least prime
factor over n, over a short interval near x, is always bounded below by a
constant for large x.

[[problems/primes/E0463/_index|E0463/]]: Asks whether some function tending to
infinity admits, for every large n, a composite number above n plus that
function but below n plus its least prime factor.

[[problems/primes/E0680/_index|E0680/]]: Asks whether every sufficiently large n
admits some k for which the least prime factor of n plus k exceeds k squared
plus one.

[[problems/primes/E0681/_index|E0681/]]: Asks whether every large n has some k
for which n plus k is composite and the least prime factor of n plus k exceeds k
squared.

[[problems/primes/E0682/_index|E0682/]]: Asks whether almost every n admits an
integer strictly between the nth and next prime whose least prime factor is at
least the gap between those primes.

[[problems/primes/E0779/_index|E0779/]]: Asks whether, for the product P of the
first n primes, there is always a prime p between the n-th prime and P such that
P plus p is prime.

[[problems/primes/E0850/_index|E0850/]]: Asks whether two distinct integers can
agree in prime factors, with their successors also agreeing and the next
integers after those agreeing too.

[[problems/primes/E0852/_index|E0852/]]: Estimates the longest run of pairwise
distinct consecutive prime gaps starting at an index below x, asking whether it
exceeds a power of log x and whether it is o(log x).

[[problems/primes/E0853/_index|E0853/]]: Asks whether the smallest even number
missing from the first x prime gaps tends to infinity, and whether it grows
faster than log x.

[[problems/primes/E0855/_index|E0855/]]: Asks whether the number of primes up to
x plus y is at most the number up to x plus the number up to y, for all large x
and y.

[[problems/primes/E0860/_index|E0860/]]: Estimates the shortest interval length
that always contains distinct integers, one divisible by each prime up to n.

[[problems/primes/E0890/_index|E0890/]]: Asks whether the summed count of large
distinct prime factors over k consecutive integers is infinitely often at most
k, and about its extreme growth rate.

[[problems/primes/E0950/_index|E0950/]]: Asks for the limit inferior, limit
superior and growth of the sum of the reciprocals of n minus p taken over all
primes p below n.

[[problems/primes/E1055/_index|E1055/]]: Concerns the classification of primes
into classes by repeatedly factoring p plus one, starting from primes whose only
such factors are two and three.

[[problems/primes/E1059/_index|E1059/]]: Asks whether infinitely many primes p
have p minus k factorial composite for every k with k factorial less than p.

[[problems/primes/E1137/_index|E1137/]]: Asks whether the largest product of two
consecutive prime gaps below x is negligible compared with the square of the
largest prime gap below x.

[[problems/primes/E1138/_index|E1138/]]: Asks whether an interval of length a
constant times the maximal prime gap below x contains the expected number of
primes, for y between x halved and x.

[[problems/primes/E1139/_index|E1139/]]: Asks whether the gaps in the sequence
of integers with at most two prime factors are infinitely often much larger than
the logarithm of the index.

[[problems/primes/E1141/_index|E1141/]]: Asks whether infinitely many n have n
minus k squared prime for every k coprime to n with k squared below n.

[[problems/primes/E1142/_index|E1142/]]: Asks whether infinitely many n, or any
n above one hundred and five, make n minus every power of two greater than one
and less than n prime.

[[problems/primes/E1143/_index|E1143/]]: Estimates how many multiples of at
least one of finitely many given primes every interval of k consecutive positive
integers must contain.

[[problems/primes/E1184/_index|E1184/]]: Asks whether the count of integers just
above n whose largest prime factor exceeds k follows the prediction given by the
Dickman function.

[[problems/primes/E1200/_index|E1200/]]: Asks for primes below x with bounded
reciprocal sum together with residues covering every integer less than x.

[[problems/primes/E1201/_index|E1201/]]: Asks whether, for every epsilon and
eta, some k makes the largest prime factor of n(n+1)...(n+k) exceed n to the one
minus epsilon for a set of n of density at least 1 - eta, the density read as
lower density following the site's curator.

[[problems/primes/E1202/_index|E1202/]]: Asks whether some k exists so that
sieving half the residue classes modulo each of k primes below n to the one
minus epsilon leaves few integers up to n.

[[problems/primes/E1212/_index|E1212/]]: Asks for a path to infinity through
coprime pairs above 1 with a composite coordinate, steps changing one coordinate
by one; Erdős first asked it without the composite condition, which Stewart
quickly answered yes.
