# problems/ramsey_theory/E0080/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0080/claims/1979_01_01_khadzhiivanov_nikiforov|1979_01_01_khadzhiivanov_nikiforov]]: Khadzhiivanov and Nikiforov's 1979 theorem, proved in full in Khadzhiivanov's
1988 account, that more than n²/4 edges force an edge on at least n/6
triangles, so in Problem 80 the forced book is linear above density 1/4.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0080/claims/2011_06_01_fox_loh|2011_06_01_fox_loh]]: Theorem 1.1 of Fox and Loh (Combinatorica 2012): for every fixed density
below one quarter the largest forced book is n to the o(1), so the first
closing question of Problem 80 is answered no; refereed.
