[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0172/claims/1979_01_01_hindman|1979_01_01_hindman]]: Theorems 4.3 (Graham) and 4.4 of Hindman (Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 1979):
every two-coloring of the positive integers has distinct x, y with x, y, x+y
and xy in one color, the case of two colors and |A| = 2.

integers with all nonempty subset sums and products in one color.
