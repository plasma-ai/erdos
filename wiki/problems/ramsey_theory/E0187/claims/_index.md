# problems/ramsey_theory/E0187/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0187/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0187/claims/1973_01_01_erdos|1973_01_01_erdos]]: Erdős's 1973 observation that coloring n by the fractional part of n times
a quadratic irrational bounds every monochromatic progression of difference
d by a constant times d, so the best f is at most linear in d.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0187/claims/1980_11_01_beck|1980_11_01_beck]]: Beck's 1980 theorem gives, for every epsilon > 0, a two-coloring of the
integers in which no monochromatic progression of difference d is longer
than (1 + epsilon) log_2 d for large d; the best f is at most (1 + o(1)) log_2 d.
