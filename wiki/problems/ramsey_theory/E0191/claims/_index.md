[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0191/claims/2003_04_23_rodl|2003_04_23_rodl]]: Rödl's 2003 paper proves that the least weight of a monochromatic clique
forced by every two-coloring of the pairs of {2, ..., n} tends to infinity
with n, so the answer is yes; the paper is not held here.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0191/claims/2011_12_07_conlon_fox_sudakov|2011_12_07_conlon_fox_sudakov]]: Theorem 1.1 of Conlon, Fox and Sudakov (Duke Math. J. 2013): for large n,
every two-coloring of the pairs of {2, ..., n} has a monochromatic clique
of weight at least 2^(-8) log log log n, so the answer is yes.
