# problems/ramsey_theory/E0547/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0547/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0547/claims/1967_01_01_gerencser_gyarfas|1967_01_01_gerencser_gyarfas]]: Theorem 1 of Gerencsér and Gyárfás (Ann. Univ. Sci. Budapest. 1967) gives
the path on n vertices the Ramsey number n - 1 + floor(n/2), at most
2n - 2: the corrected statement of Problem 547 for every path.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0547/claims/1979_01_01_grossman_harary_klawe|1979_01_01_grossman_harary_klawe]]: Theorem 3.1 of Grossman, Harary and Klawe (Discrete Math. 1979) bounds the
Ramsey number of every double star S(n,m) by 2n + m + 2, at most 2N - 2 on
its N vertices: the corrected statement of Problem 547 for double stars.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0547/claims/2011_02_04_zhao|2011_02_04_zhao]]: Corollary 2.2 of Zhao's proof of Loebl's conjecture for large n (Electron.
J. Combin. 2011): every tree on n vertices has Ramsey number at most 2n
minus 2 once n is large; the threshold is not explicit.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0547/claims/2025_09_09_montgomery_pavez_signe_yan|2025_09_09_montgomery_pavez_signe_yan]]: A 2025 preprint proving R(T) = max(2t_1, t_1 + 2t_2) - 1 for every tree T
with maximum degree at most cn, at most 2n - 2: the corrected statement
of Problem 547 for those trees; a preprint credited in the site's remarks.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0547/claims/2026_08_26_alexeev|2026_08_26_alexeev]]: A Lean development in Alexeev's repository proves, by its own route, that
every tree on n vertices has Ramsey number at most 2n minus 2 once n is
large; the threshold is not explicit, and it is not built here.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0547/claims/2026_08_26_alexeev_literal|2026_08_26_alexeev_literal]]: Answers the site's wording (every n, the one-vertex tree included), not the
corrected Statement (trees on n at least 2 vertices), so it does not count
toward the problem's standing. Two Lean theorems in Alexeev's repository
prove the site's wording false for the one-vertex tree.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0547/claims/2026_09_03_adamczewski|2026_09_03_adamczewski]]: The sharp tree-free edge bound proved for Problem 548 (a GPT-6 Astra proof
in Adamczewski's repository) gives R(T) at most 2n minus 2 for every tree
on n at least 2 vertices; accepted on a third party's Lean proof built here.
