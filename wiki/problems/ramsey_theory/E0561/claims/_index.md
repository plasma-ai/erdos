# problems/ramsey_theory/E0561/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0561/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0561/claims/1978_01_01_burr_erdos_faudree_rousseau_schelp|1978_01_01_burr_erdos_faudree_rousseau_schelp]]: Theorem 1 of Burr, Erdős, Faudree, Rousseau and Schelp (Indag. Math. 1978)
gives the size Ramsey number of m copies of one star against n copies of
another, the conjectured formula when all stars of each forest are equal.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0561/claims/2002_04_01_gyori_schelp|2002_04_01_gyori_schelp]]: Theorem 2 of Győri and Schelp (Discrete Math. 2002) proves the star-forest
formula whenever the binomial coefficient of each diagonal maximum exceeds
the sum of that maximum and all later ones.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0561/claims/2021_11_03_davoodi_javadi_kamranian_raeisi|2021_11_03_davoodi_javadi_kamranian_raeisi]]: Theorems 2.3 to 2.6 of Davoodi, Javadi, Kamranian and Raeisi (Ars Math.
Contemp. 2025) prove the star-forest formula for one star, two equal stars,
all sizes odd, and equal odd stars against a forest with odd largest star.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0561/claims/2026_06_03_fu_luo_ni|2026_06_03_fu_luo_ni]]: The first arXiv version of a June 2026 preprint claimed the size Ramsey
formula for all pairs of star forests; the second version, a day later,
was retitled and dropped the claim, and the third treats uniform forests.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0561/claims/2026_08_06_cipollini|2026_08_06_cipollini]]: A three-page AI-assisted argument of August 2026: the size Ramsey number of
two star forests is at least the conjectured sum of diagonal maxima minus
at most one edge per diagonal; unreviewed, with no journal or review record.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0561/claims/2026_09_04_tienxion|2026_09_04_tienxion]]: A partial proof claim of September 2026: the star-forest formula holds when
every diagonal maximum not attained by an odd pair or a single-edge star is
followed by a drop of three, or of two and then two; unreviewed.
