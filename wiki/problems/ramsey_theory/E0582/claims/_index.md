# problems/ramsey_theory/E0582/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0582/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0582/claims/1970_01_01_folkman|1970_01_01_folkman]]: Folkman's Theorem 1 (SIAM J. Appl. Math. 18, 1970) gives a graph of clique
number max(k_1, k_2) whose every two-coloring of the edges has a red K_{k_1}
or a blue K_{k_2}; with k_1 = k_2 = 3 it is the K_4-free graph asked for.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0582/claims/1986_12_01_frankl_rodl|1986_12_01_frankl_rodl]]: Frankl and Rödl (Graphs Combin. 2, 1986) give the first concrete bound: some
K_4-free graph on fewer than 7.02·10^11 vertices forces a monochromatic
triangle in every two-coloring of its edges; refereed.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0582/claims/1988_11_01_spencer|1988_11_01_spencer]]: Spencer (J. Combin. Theory Ser. A 49, 1988, corrected by an erratum in vol.
50) proves by a probabilistic argument that some K_4-free graph on at most
3·10^9 vertices forces a monochromatic triangle in every two-coloring.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0582/claims/2008_01_01_dudek_rodl|2008_01_01_dudek_rodl]]: Dudek and Rödl (Experiment. Math. 17, 2008) show that the circulant graph
G(941,5) is K_4-free and forces a monochromatic triangle in every
two-coloring, through a MAX-CUT criterion, so f(2,3,4) <= 941; refereed.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0582/claims/2008_01_22_lu|2008_01_22_lu]]: Lu (SIAM J. Discrete Math. 21, 2008), Theorem 1: the explicit circulant
graph L(9697,4) is K_4-free and forces a monochromatic triangle in every
two-coloring, so f(2,3,4) <= 9697; refereed.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0582/claims/2012_07_16_lange_radziszowski_xu|2012_07_16_lange_radziszowski_xu]]: Lange, Radziszowski and Xu (arXiv 2012; J. Combin. Math. Combin. Comput.
88, 2014), Theorem 3: F_e(3,3;4) <= 786, by a K_4-free graph whose arrowing
is certified by a semidefinite MAX-CUT bound; refereed.
