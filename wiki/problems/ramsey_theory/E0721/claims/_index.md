# problems/ramsey_theory/E0721/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0721/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0721/claims/2020_06_04_schoen|2020_06_04_schoen]]: Schoen's Theorem 1 (Electron. J. Combin. 2021): W(3,k) is at most
exp(C k^(1-c)) for absolute constants C, c > 0, the first bound of the shape
Erdős asked for; refereed, credited by the site's curator.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0721/claims/2021_02_02_green|2021_02_02_green]]: Green's Theorem 1.1 (Forum of Mathematics, Pi 2022): W(3,k) is at least k to
the power c (log k / log log k)^(1/3), the first superpolynomial lower bound,
refuting the quadratic conjecture of Ahmed, Kullmann and Snevily; refereed.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0721/claims/2021_11_01_hunter|2021_11_01_hunter]]: Hunter's Theorem 1 (Combinatorica 2022): W(3,k) is at least k to the power
c log k / log log k, improving Green's bound; the sources expect W(3,k) at
most k^(O(log k)); refereed, credited by the site's curator.
