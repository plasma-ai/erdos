# problems/ramsey_theory/E0811/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0811/claims/1993_01_01_erdos_tuza|1993_01_01_erdos_tuza]]: Erdős and Tuza (Ann. Discrete Math. 55 (1993)) bound d(n,F) for the
triangle, the four-cycle and every forest, which puts these graphs in the
answer set; a book chapter with no documented refereeing.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0811/claims/2022_09_28_axenovich_clemen|2022_09_28_axenovich_clemen]]: Axenovich and Clemen (J. Graph Theory 106 (2024)) exclude from the answer
set the cliques K_q with q at least 10 and q = 2, 3 mod 4, further graphs
with an odd number of edges, and almost all clique sizes; refereed.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0811/claims/2023_03_26_clemen_wagner|2023_03_26_clemen_wagner]]: Theorem 1.2 of Clemen and Wagner (Electron. J. Combin. 30 (2023)) gives
balanced six-colorings of K_{13^k} with no rainbow K_4, so K_4 is outside
the answer set; refereed.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0811/claims/2026_09_15_kitamura|2026_09_15_kitamura]]: A forum claim of 15 September 2026 of a Lean-verified proof of the
Axenovich--Clemen conjecture, which puts every clique on at least four
vertices outside the answer set; partial, unreviewed and unread here.
