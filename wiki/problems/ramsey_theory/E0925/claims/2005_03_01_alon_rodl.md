**Claim.** Let $r_k(K_3;K_m)$ be the least $N$ such that every
$(k+1)$-coloring of the edges of $K_N$ has a monochromatic triangle in one
of the first $k$ colors or a monochromatic $K_m$ in the last. Theorem 3.2
of Alon and Rödl states that for every fixed $k\ge1$,
$r_k(K_3;K_m)=\tilde\Theta(m^{k+1})$, and the lower bound inside its proof
is $r_k(K_3;K_m)\ge\Omega(m^{k+1}/(\log m)^{2k+\delta})$ for every $\delta>0$
and all large $m$. The construction (p. 7 of the authors' final
manuscript): for $n=2^{3f}$ with $3\nmid f$, Alon's explicit triangle-free
$(n,d,\lambda)$-graph is blown up by a factor $r=n^{k/3-2/3}(\log n)^{2-\delta}$
to a triangle-free graph $G$ on $N=nr$ vertices with so few independent sets
of size $m=c(k)\,n^{1/3}(\log n)^2$ that $k$ random shifts of $G$ give a
$(k+1)$-coloring of $K_N$ with no monochromatic triangle in the first $k$
colors and no $K_m$ in color $k+1$. The theorem is paged at
[[../library/ramsey_theory/alon_2005_sharp_bounds_some_multicolor_ramsey_numbers/theorem_3_2|Theorem 3.2]]
of the library's
[[../library/ramsey_theory/alon_2005_sharp_bounds_some_multicolor_ramsey_numbers/_index|source card]].

**What it settles.** The paper never states
[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0925/_index|Problem 925]]; its Conjecture 1.1
concerns the ratio $R(3,3,m)/R(3,m)$ of Problem 553. The conversion is
elementary and is written on the problem page: at $k=2$, the graph $H$
formed by the edges of the first two colors has $N=n(\log n)^{2-\delta}$
vertices, its edges are $2$-colored with no monochromatic triangle, so $H$
is not Ramsey for $K_3$, and its independent sets are cliques of the third
color, so $\alpha(H)<c\,n^{1/3}(\log n)^2\le c\,N^{1/3}(\log N)^2$. For every
$\delta'>0$ this is below $N^{1/3+\delta'}$ once $N$ is large, so along the
infinite sequence of these $N$ no independent set of size $N^{1/3+\delta'}$
exists, and the problem's question, which asks for one in every such graph
for all large $n$, has answer no for every $\delta>0$. In the site's
reformulation, the lower bound $R(3,3,m)\ge\Omega(m^3/(\log m)^{4+\delta})$
refutes $R(3,3,m)\ll m^{3-c}$ for every $c>0$; the site records the same
resolution through that reformulation.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki; the result rests on the cited paper
alone, and the elementary conversion above is restated on the problem page.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: N. Alon and V. Rödl, Sharp bounds for some
multicolor Ramsey numbers, Combinatorica 25 (2005), no. 2, 125--141 (the
Crossref record, places the article in the March 2005
issue, which this page is named by; the day is a placeholder). Reviewed:
the site's curator, T. F. Bloom, records the problem as disproved by Alon
and Rödl in the problem's commentary, with the two-sided bound on $R(3,3,m)$
and Sudakov's removal of the $\log\log m$ factor from the upper bound (page
labeled DISPROVED). Semantic Scholar's 80 citing records, scanned by title,
include no dispute or retraction.

**Read depth.** The pages cited are those of the authors' final manuscript
on the first author's publication list, not compared with the journal
typesetting. Theorem 3.2, its two bounds, the construction's parameters
inside its proof and the Remark (pp. 6--7) are checked as claims and the
proof is read for structure only; the construction's inputs, Alon's explicit
graphs and the paper's Theorem 2.1, rest on the paper's citations, and the
elementary conversion above is the only argument checked in this corpus.
