***

**Claim.** Theorem 2.1 of J. Spencer, Asymptotic lower bounds for Ramsey
functions, Discrete Math. 20 (1977), 69--76 (p. 72): "$R(3,t)\ge
(c-o(1))(t/\ln t)^2$, $c=1/27$." The theorem is paged at
[[../library/ramsey_theory/spencer_1977_asymptotic_lower_bounds_ramsey_functions/theorem_2_1|Theorem 2.1]].
In the letters of [[problems/ramsey_theory/E0986/_index|Problem 986]] it
gives $R(3,k)\gg k^2/(\log k)^2$, the statement at $s=3$ with $c(3)=2$.
The paper adds that the result "is originally due to Erdős [2] (without
explicit calculation of the constant) using a very different method"; the
constant $1/27$ and the proof by the local lemma are Spencer's.

**Covers.** The case $s=3$ of the statement only.

**Depends on.**
[[../library/ramsey_theory/spencer_1977_asymptotic_lower_bounds_ramsey_functions/theorem_2_1|Theorem 2.1 of Spencer 1977]],
the result page of the cited paper.

**Dating.** The paper appeared in volume 20, number 1, of 1977; the
Crossref record gives only the year, so the month and day in the page name
are placeholders.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: Discrete Mathematics 20 (1977), no. 1, 69--76.
The site's commentary credits Spencer with the case $s=3$, but its PROVED
label settles the problem through
[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0986/claims/2026_06_16_bradac|Bradač's claim]],
so the curator's credit is not listed as review of this one.
