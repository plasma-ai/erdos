# problems/ramsey_theory/E0986/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0986/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0986/claims/1977_01_01_spencer|1977_01_01_spencer]]: Spencer's Theorem 2.1, R(3,t) at least (1/27 - o(1))(t/ln t)^2, from the
local lemma, which proves the case s = 3 with c(3) = 2; refereed in
Discrete Mathematics (1977).

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0986/claims/2023_06_06_mattheus_verstraete|2023_06_06_mattheus_verstraete]]: Mattheus and Verstraete's Theorem 1, r(4,t) at least a constant times t^3
over the fourth power of log t, which proves the case s = 4 with c(4) = 4;
refereed in the Annals (2024).

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0986/claims/2026_06_16_bradac|2026_06_16_bradac]]: Bradač's Theorem 1.1 gives, for every fixed s at least 3, the lower bound
r(s,k) at least c_s k^{s-1}/(log k)^{2s-4}, which answers the problem with
c(s) = 2s-4; an arXiv preprint accepted by the site's curator, not refereed.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0986/claims/2026_09_24_openai|2026_09_24_openai]]: Two manuscripts of the OpenAI mathematics release prove r(s,t) =
t^{s-1}/(log t)^{s-2+o(1)} for s = 5 and for every fixed s at least 6,
accepted as a partial answer on the two Lean declarations the corpus's
verification built and audited; the manuscripts are unreviewed.
