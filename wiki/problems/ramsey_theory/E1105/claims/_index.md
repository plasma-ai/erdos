# problems/ramsey_theory/E1105/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E1105/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E1105/claims/1984_03_01_simonovits_sos|1984_03_01_simonovits_sos]]: Theorem B of the 1984 Combinatorica paper: for t at least 5 and n > ct²,
the largest number of colors on K_n without a rainbow path on 2t+3+ε₀
vertices is tn − C(t+1,2) + 1 + ε₀, the large-n regime of the path half.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E1105/claims/2005_09_01_montellano_ballesteros_neumann_lara|2005_09_01_montellano_ballesteros_neumann_lara]]: Theorem 5 of the 2005 Graphs and Combinatorics paper: for all n ≥ p ≥ 3
the least number of colors forcing a rainbow p-cycle in K_n is the value
E(n,p) conjectured in 1975, so AR(n,C_k) = E(n,k) − 1: the cycle half.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E1105/claims/2021_02_01_yuan|2021_02_01_yuan]]: Theorem 1 of Yuan's 2021 arXiv preprint gives AR(n,P_k) for all n ≥ k ≥ 5
as the maximum of two terms, the 1975 formula of Erdős, Simonovits and
Sós; a preprint the site's curator credits as the proof of the path half.
