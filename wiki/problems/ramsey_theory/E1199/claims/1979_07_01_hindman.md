**Claim.** Let $\{A_0,A_1\}$ be an admissible partition of the positive
integers $N$. Then there are $i<2$ and a sequence
$\langle x_n\rangle_{n<\omega}$ of distinct members of $N$ with
$x_m+x_n\in A_i$ whenever $\{m,n\}\subseteq\omega$. This is
[[../library/ramsey_theory/hindman_1979_partitions_sums_integers_repetition/corollary_2_10|Corollary 2.10]]
of N. Hindman, Partitions and sums of integers with repetition (p. 27),
the case $A_0=\emptyset$ of his Theorem 2.9 (p. 26). The pairs
$\{m,n\}$ include $m=n$, so the infinite set $A=\{x_n:n<\omega\}$ has
$A+A$, doubles included, inside one cell: the statement of
[[problems/ramsey_theory/E1199/_index|Problem 1199]] for admissible
two-colorings.

**Covers.** The admissible two-colorings: those in which one class
contains, for some $d\in N$ and every $n$, a progression
$\{x+kd:k\le n\}$ with $x$ even (Definition 2.2, pp. 20--21). The
non-admissible two-colorings are the open remainder, which the pending
[[problems/ramsey_theory/E1199/claims/2026_07_19_huang_lian_shao_xiao_xu_zhang|2026 claim]]
addresses. The same paper's three-cell counterexample (Theorem 2.4, p. 21)
answers a variant with three colors and settles no instance of the
two-color question, so it has no claim page; the problem page records it.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: N. Hindman, J. Combin. Theory Ser. A 27 (1979),
no. 1, 19--32, received 28 April 1977, published in the July 1979 issue
per the Crossref record. The record gives no day, so the day in the page
name is a placeholder. The evidence is not `reviewed`: the site labels
the problem OPEN, and its commentary credits the paper only with the
three-color result.

**Read depth.** The statements of Corollary 2.10, Theorem 2.9 and
Definition 2.2 were checked; the proof of Theorem 2.9 (pp. 26--27), which
runs through Lemmas 2.5--2.8 to Ramsey's theorem and the finite-unions
form of Hindman's theorem, was followed for structure only. Nothing here
is independent review.
