***

**Claim.** The site's commentary credits B. Gyires [Gy80], *The common source
of several inequalities concerning doubly stochastic matrices*, with a proof
of van der Waerden's conjecture: every doubly stochastic $n\times n$ matrix
$X$ satisfies $\operatorname{per}(X)\ge n!/n^n$. By averaging the $n!$
diagonal products, that conjecture would give the statement of
[[problems/set_systems/E0499/_index|Problem 499]]. The paper does not prove
the conjecture. Its Theorems 1.1–1.4 are inequalities for weighted sums of
permanental quantities of $(AA^*)^{1/2}$, $(A^*A)^{1/2}$ and $A$, not bounds
on $\operatorname{per}(A)$. On p. 293, van der Waerden's conjecture appears as
a special case of the paper's own Conjectures 1.3 and 1.5. Corollaries 3.3 and
3.4 (pp. 299–300) give those conjectures for symmetric positive semidefinite
matrices. Section 4 (p. 301) says the author could not prove them for
arbitrary doubly stochastic matrices, beyond the cases $k=1,2$ of Conjecture
1.1 (Theorems 4.5 and 4.6). Theorem 4.9 and Corollary 4.1 (p. 304) give the
bound only for the matrices $(1-x)A_0+xA$ with $0<x\le\delta$, where $A_0$
has every entry $1/n$. The paper ends by showing the conjecture equivalent to
its Conjecture 4.1, which it leaves open. It proves no lower bound on the
permanent of an arbitrary doubly stochastic matrix.

**Why the attribution is rejected.** The publication the site cites, Publ.
Math. Debrecen 27 (1980), no. 3-4, 291–304, does not contain the theorem
credited to it. Van der Waerden's conjecture was proved by
[[problems/set_systems/E0499/claims/1980_01_01_egorychev|Egorychev]] and
[[problems/set_systems/E0499/claims/1981_06_01_falikman|Falikman]], and the
problem's statement by
[[problems/set_systems/E0499/claims/1962_08_01_marcus_minc|Marcus and Minc]].
The rejection concerns the attribution; the paper's own theorems are not in
question.
