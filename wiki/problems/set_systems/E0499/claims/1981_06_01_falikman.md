***

**Claim.** Theorem 1 of
[[../library/set_systems/falikman_1981_proof_van_der_waerden_conjecture_permanent/_index|Falikman's paper]]
is van der Waerden's conjecture: every doubly stochastic $n\times n$ matrix
$X$ satisfies $\operatorname{per}(X)\ge n!/n^n$. Theorem 2 adds that among
the doubly stochastic matrices with all entries nonzero, the only one with
$\operatorname{per}(A)=n!/n^n$ is the matrix whose entries are all $1/n$; the
unrestricted equality case is not proved in this paper. The permanent is the
sum over the $n!$ permutations $\sigma$ of the diagonal products
$\prod_i x_{i\sigma(i)}$, so their average is at least $n^{-n}$ and some
$\sigma$ attains $\prod_i x_{i\sigma(i)}\ge n^{-n}$, which is the statement
of [[problems/set_systems/E0499/_index|Problem 499]]. The argument minimizes
the perturbed functional $\operatorname{per}(X)+\varepsilon/\prod_{i,j}x_{ij}$
over the doubly stochastic matrices with positive entries and lets
$\varepsilon$ tend to zero; the library card summarizes it. Egorychev proved
the same conjecture independently
([[problems/set_systems/E0499/claims/1980_01_01_egorychev|claim page]]); the
site also credits Gyires
([[problems/set_systems/E0499/claims/1980_01_01_gyires|claim page]]), whose
paper does not prove the conjecture, so that attribution is rejected.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: Mat. Zametki **29** (1981), no. 6, 931–938, 957,
with the English translation in Math. Notes **29** (1981), no. 6, 475–479;
the issue is dated June 1981, and the page is dated to the first day of that
month. The site's curator records the proofs of van der Waerden's
conjecture but credits the problem's own statement to Marcus and Minc, whose
earlier direct proof is the
[[problems/set_systems/E0499/claims/1962_08_01_marcus_minc|credited claim]];
this page therefore lists no `reviewed` evidence. The deduction from the
permanent bound to the diagonal bound is the one-line averaging above.
