***

**Claim.** Theorem 1.4 of Leilei Zhang, *Extremal 3-uniform hypergraphs
without generalized 4-cycles* (arXiv:2610.34038v1, 25 October 2026), states:
for every integer $n\ge1$, $f_3(n)\le\binom n2$, with equality whenever
$n\ge5$ and $n\equiv1,5\pmod{20}$
([[../library/set_systems/zhang_2026_extremal_3_uniform_hypergraphs_without_generalized/theorem_1_4|Theorem 1.4]]).
Here $f_3(n)$ is the largest number of edges of an $n$-vertex 3-uniform
hypergraph with no generalized 4-cycle, four distinct edges $A,B,C,D$ with
$A\cup B=C\cup D$ and $A\cap B=C\cap D=\emptyset$. The paper calls the bound
the 3-uniform case of the Erdős–Füredi conjecture, which asks for it only for
large $n$, and proves it from the stronger inequality
$2\lvert E(H)\rvert\le\lvert\partial H\rvert+\lvert D(H)\rvert$
([[../library/set_systems/zhang_2026_extremal_3_uniform_hypergraphs_without_generalized/theorem_1_3|Theorem 1.3]])
by a charging argument with a transfer step. The $f(n;t)$ of
[[problems/set_systems/E0643/_index|Problem 643]] is $f_t(n)+1$, so
$f(n;3)\le\binom n2+1$ for every $n$, and with Füredi's lower bound
$f(n;3)\ge\binom{n-1}2+\lfloor(n-1)/3\rfloor+1$ for $n\ge6$
([[../library/set_systems/furedi_1984_hypergraphs_which_all_disjoint_pairs_have/theorem_1_2|Füredi's Theorem 1.2]])
this gives $f(n;3)=(1+o(1))\binom n2$, the problem's question answered yes for
$t=3$. For $n\ge5$ with $n\equiv1,5\pmod{20}$ the value is exact,
$f(n;3)=\binom n2+1$, attained by Füredi's design construction. The
[[../library/set_systems/zhang_2026_extremal_3_uniform_hypergraphs_without_generalized/_index|source card]]
holds the digest.

**Covers.** The question for $t=3$, the part `three_uniform`, with the upper
bound $f(n;3)\le\binom n2+1$ for every $n$ and its exactness for
$n\equiv1,5\pmod{20}$. It leaves the exact value of $f(n;3)$ for other $n$
open, and it says nothing about $t\ge4$, the part `higher_uniformity`, which
[[problems/set_systems/E0643/claims/2026_09_29_huang_ma_yang|Huang, Ma and Yang's preprint]]
claims.

**Depends on.**

of the cited preprint, with
[[../library/set_systems/zhang_2026_extremal_3_uniform_hypergraphs_without_generalized/theorem_1_3|Theorem 1.3]],
for the upper bound, and Füredi's lower bound
[[../library/set_systems/furedi_1984_hypergraphs_which_all_disjoint_pairs_have/theorem_1_2|Theorem 1.2]]
for the asymptotic. Nothing in this wiki.
