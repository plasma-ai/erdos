# problems/set_systems/E0643/claims

[[problems/set_systems/E0643/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_systems/E0643/claims/2026_09_29_huang_ma_yang|2026_09_29_huang_ma_yang]]: Huang, Ma and Yang's 2026 preprint claims a proof of Füredi's conjecture: for
fixed $t\ge4$ and large $n$ the extremal number is
$\binom{n-1}{t-1}+\lfloor(n-1)/t\rfloor$. This gives the asymptotic for every
$t\ge4$.

[[problems/set_systems/E0643/claims/2026_10_25_zhang|2026_10_25_zhang]]: Zhang's 2026 preprint proves that an $n$-vertex 3-uniform hypergraph with no
generalized 4-cycle has at most $\binom n2$ edges for every $n$, so
$f(n;3)\le\binom n2+1$. This gives the asymptotic for $t=3$.
