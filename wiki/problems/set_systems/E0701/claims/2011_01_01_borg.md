***

**Claim.** P. Borg, *On Chvátal's conjecture and a conjecture on families of
signed sets*, European J. Combin. 32 (2011), no. 1, 140--145, proposes a
weighted version of Chvátal's conjecture, the corrected Statement of
[[problems/set_systems/E0701/_index|Problem 701]], and proves the weighted
version for weighted families having a dominant element, as the zbMATH
review of the paper (Zbl 1207.05002) states the result. Unit weights are a
special case, so the corrected Statement holds for every family with a
dominant element in the paper's sense.

**Covers.** Families closed under taking subsets that have a dominant
element, in the sense of the paper. The paper's other conjecture, on families
of signed sets, is not part of the problem.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: European J. Combin. 32 (2011), no. 1, 140--145.
The site's curator credits the result, but the site labels the problem OPEN,
so no review is listed.
