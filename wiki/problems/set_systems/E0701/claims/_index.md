# problems/set_systems/E0701/claims

[[problems/set_systems/E0701/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_systems/E0701/claims/2011_01_01_borg|2011_01_01_borg]]: Borg (European J. Combin. 2011) proposes a weighted form of Chvátal's
conjecture and proves it for weighted families with a dominant element,
which with unit weights covers such families; refereed; partial.

[[problems/set_systems/E0701/claims/2018_09_05_eifler_gleixner_pulaj|2018_09_05_eifler_gleixner_pulaj]]: Eifler, Gleixner and Pulaj (ACM Trans. Math. Softw. 2022) verify Chvátal's
conjecture for every downset on at most seven elements by exact integer
programming with checked certificates; refereed; partial.

[[problems/set_systems/E0701/claims/2022_01_11_frankl_kupavskii|2022_01_11_frankl_kupavskii]]: Frankl and Kupavskii (Discrete Math. 2023) prove Chvátal's conjecture for
intersecting subfamilies of covering number at most 2, through a
disjointness matching between two down-sets; refereed; partial.

[[problems/set_systems/E0701/claims/2026_09_16_chang_liu_liu|2026_09_16_chang_liu_liu]]: Chang, Liu and Liu's September 2026 preprint proves Chvátal's conjecture
for subsets of a finite set through a sharp correlation inequality for
increasing Boolean functions; not refereed, third-party Lean not built.

[[problems/set_systems/E0701/claims/2026_09_20_keevash|2026_09_20_keevash]]: Keevash's September 2026 preprint proves Kahn's flow conjecture in full
generality, which implies Kleitman's and Chvátal's conjectures, sharpening
the analysis of Chang, Liu and Liu; found with GPT-6 Astra, not refereed.

[[problems/set_systems/E0701/claims/2026_09_23_ellis_filmus_friedgut|2026_09_23_ellis_filmus_friedgut]]: Ellis, Filmus and Friedgut's September 2026 note gives a two-page spectral
proof of Chvátal's conjecture, a spectral proof of Kleitman's conjecture and
a projection-packing strengthening; found with ChatGPT-6 Astra, unrefereed.
