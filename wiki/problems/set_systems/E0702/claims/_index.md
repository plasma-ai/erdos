[[problems/set_systems/E0702/claims/1977_08_01_frankl|1977_08_01_frankl]]: Frankl (1977) proves that for k at least 4 and n large in terms of k, more
than binom(n-2,k-2) k-subsets of an n-set include two meeting in one point;
refereed, credited by the site's curator and formalized.

[[problems/set_systems/E0702/claims/2026_08_18_alexeev|2026_08_18_alexeev]]: Answers the site's wording (every n), not the corrected Statement (n >
n_0(k), Erdős's own range), so it does not count toward the problem's
standing. A Lean theorem in Alexeev's repository refutes that wording at the
five 4-subsets of a 5-set.
