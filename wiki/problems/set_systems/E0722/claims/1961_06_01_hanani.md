**Claim.** Hanani's *The existence and construction of balanced incomplete
block designs* proves that the necessary conditions $(k-1)\mid\lambda(v-1)$
and $k(k-1)\mid\lambda v(v-1)$ are sufficient for a balanced incomplete block
design on $v$ points, a family of $k$-subsets containing every pair of points
in exactly $\lambda$ blocks, for block size $k=3$ and $k=4$ with every index
$\lambda$, and for $k=5$ with $\lambda=1$, $4$ and $20$, the case $k=5$,
$\lambda=1$ with the possible exception of $v=141$. The exception was removed
by Hanani's note *A balanced incomplete block design*, Ann. Math. Statist.
**36** (1965), no. 2, 711, which constructs the design with $k=5$,
$\lambda=1$, $v=141$. Keevash's survey of earlier work reads the paper as
answering Steiner's problem for $(q,r)\in\{(4,2),(5,2)\}$ and all $n$. For
$\lambda=1$ the conditions are the divisibility conditions of
[[problems/set_systems/E0722/_index|Problem 722]] for $r=2$,
$(k-1)\mid(n-1)$ and $k(k-1)\mid n(n-1)$, that is $n\equiv1$ or
$4\pmod{12}$ for $k=4$ and $n\equiv1$ or $5\pmod{20}$ for $k=5$, so the
answer is yes for $(r,k)=(2,4)$ and every $n$, and yes for $(2,5)$ and every
admissible $n$ except possibly $141$ by this paper, and for every $n$ with the
1965 note. For $k=3$ the paper proves again the case
[[problems/set_systems/E0722/claims/1847_01_01_kirkman|Kirkman's triple systems]]
settled. The constructions are recursive, composing designs on smaller
point sets through pairwise balanced designs and group divisible designs.

**Covers.** The cases $(r,k)=(2,4)$ and $(2,5)$ of the problem, for all
large $n$: the divisibility conditions suffice for Steiner systems $S(2,4,n)$
and, for every $n\ne141$, $S(2,5,n)$. The paper treats $2$-designs only, so
it says nothing about $(3,4)$, which is
[[problems/set_systems/E0722/claims/1960_01_01_hanani|Hanani's quadruple systems]],
or about $(2,k)$ for $k\ge6$, which is
[[problems/set_systems/E0722/claims/1975_01_01_wilson|Wilson's existence theorem]]
for large $n$.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: H. Hanani, The existence and construction of
balanced incomplete block designs, Ann. Math. Statist. **32** (1961), no. 2,
361–386; the issue is dated June 1961, and the page is dated to the first
day of that month. The site's commentary credits the cases $(2,4)$ and
$(2,5)$, and also $(3,4)$, to Hanani under its key [Ha61], which resolves to
this paper; the $(3,4)$ case is proved in his 1960 paper on quadruple
systems. The curator names these cases in the progression that ends with
Keevash while crediting the problem's PROVED label to Keevash, which does
not settle the problem on this result, so the page lists no `reviewed`
evidence. The proof has not been reconstructed or independently reviewed in
this corpus.
