# problems/set_systems/E0723/claims

[[problems/set_systems/E0723/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_systems/E0723/claims/1949_02_01_bruck_ryser|1949_02_01_bruck_ryser]]: Bruck and Ryser (1949): a finite projective plane of order n congruent to 1
or 2 mod 4 exists only if n is a sum of two squares, excluding orders 6,
14, 21, 22 and infinitely many more; a partial answer to the problem.

[[problems/set_systems/E0723/claims/1989_12_01_lam_thiel_swiercz|1989_12_01_lam_thiel_swiercz]]: Lam, Thiel and Swiercz (1989) report the computer search showing that no
finite projective plane of order 10 exists, the smallest order that is not
a prime power and passes the Bruck–Ryser test; a partial answer.
