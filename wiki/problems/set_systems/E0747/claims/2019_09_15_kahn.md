***

**Claim.** The answer to [[problems/set_systems/E0747/_index|Problem 747]] is
$\ell(n)\sim n\log n$. Theorem 1 of *Asymptotics for Shamir's problem*
([[../library/set_systems/kahn_2023_asymptotics_shamir_s_problem/_index|card]])
states that for fixed $r\ge3$ and $\varepsilon>0$, the random $r$-uniform
hypergraph on $N$ vertices with $M>(1+\varepsilon)(N/r)\log N$ edges contains
a perfect matching with probability tending to $1$. With $r=3$ and $N=3n$
this gives $n$ vertex-disjoint edges once $M>(1+\varepsilon)n\log(3n)$, and
with fewer than $(1-\varepsilon)n\log(3n)$ edges some vertex is almost surely
isolated, so the threshold is $(1+o(1))n\log n$ and isolated vertices are
asymptotically the only obstruction. This sharpens the order-of-magnitude
result of
[[problems/set_systems/E0747/claims/2008_03_24_johansson_kahn_vu|Johansson, Kahn and Vu's threshold of order n log n]]
by the same entropy and counting approach with the constant pushed to its
correct value. The paper's Theorem 2, the hitting-time statement that the
random hypergraph process has a perfect matching as soon as its last isolated
vertex disappears, is shown there to follow from a conditional strengthening
of Theorem 1 that the paper defers to J. Kahn, Hitting times for Shamir's
problem, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 375 (2022), no. 1, 627–668 (arXiv:2008.01605);
it is not needed for the threshold. The library card records the statements;
the corpus holds no review of the proof.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: Kahn, J., Asymptotics for Shamir's problem, Adv.
Math. 422 (2023), Paper No. 109019, 39 pp.; the preprint is arXiv:1909.06834,
posted 2019-09-15, the date of this page. Reviewed: the site's curator, Thomas
Bloom, labels the problem solved and credits [Ka23] with the asymptotic
$\ell(n)\sim n\log n$, independently of its author. No formalization is
recorded.
