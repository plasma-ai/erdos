***

**Claim.** There is a function $g(n)\to0$ such that every $n$-uniform
hypergraph with at most $n^{1/3-g(n)}2^n$ edges is 2-colorable; hence
$m(n)>n^{1/3-o(1)}2^n$ for the function $m(n)$ of
[[problems/set_systems/E0901/_index|Problem 901]]. This is the form in
which Radhakrishnan and Srinivasan state the theorem (Random Structures
Algorithms 16 (2000), 4--32, Section 1), and the publisher's abstract gives
the hypothesis as a bound of order $n^{1/3}2^n$ on the number of edges;
Erdős's surveys of 1979 and 1982 report the result as $m(n)>cn^{1/3}2^n$.
The proof recolors a random 2-coloring, and Spencer later gave a shorter
probabilistic proof of the same bound. The paper also treats hypergraphs
that are not uniform: it shows that a hypergraph whose edges all have at
least $k$ points is 2-colorable when $\sum_e2^{-|e|}$ is at most a function
of $k$ of order $\log^*k$, answering a question of Erdős.

**Covers.** The lower bound $m(n)>n^{1/3-o(1)}2^n$, which improved Beck's
own
[[problems/set_systems/E0901/claims/1977_01_01_beck|$c(\log n)2^n$]]. The
order of $m(n)$ stays open: Radhakrishnan and Srinivasan's
[[problems/set_systems/E0901/claims/2000_01_01_radhakrishnan_srinivasan|$0.7\sqrt{n/\ln n}\,2^n$]]
supersedes the bound, and the upper bound is Erdős's
[[problems/set_systems/E0901/claims/1964_09_01_erdos|$n^22^{n+1}$]].

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki.

**Acceptance.** J. Beck, *On 3-chromatic hypergraphs*, Discrete Math. 24
(1978), no. 2, 127--137, a refereed journal (`refereed`); the record gives
only the year, so the page is dated to its first day. The curator of
erdosproblems.com, Thomas Bloom, credits the bound to this paper in the
problem's commentary, but the site labels the problem OPEN, so that credit
is not acceptance of this partial claim.
