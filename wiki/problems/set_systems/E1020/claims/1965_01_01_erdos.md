***

**Claim.** There is a constant $c_r$, depending only on $r$, such that for
$n>c_rk$

$$
f(n;r,k)=\binom nr-\binom{n-k+1}{r},
$$

the number of $r$-subsets of an $n$-set meeting a fixed $(k-1)$-set, which is
the covering term of the maximum in
[[problems/set_systems/E1020/_index|Problem 1020]]. The paper writes
$f(n;r,k)$ for the least number of edges forcing $k$ pairwise disjoint edges
and $g(n;r,k-1)$ for the covering count, and its single
[[../library/set_systems/erdos_1965_problem_independent_tuples/theorem|Theorem]]
states $f(n;r,k)=1+g(n;r,k-1)$ for $n>c_rk$; the proof is an
induction on $k$ that splits on the maximum degree, a small maximum degree
forcing a large maximal set of disjoint edges and a vertex of large degree
being deleted for the induction. The paper poses the conjecture for every
$n$, recalls the Erdős–Ko–Rado case $k=2$ and the Erdős–Gallai formula for
$r=2$, and gives no value of $c_r$. The paper is P. Erdős, A problem on
independent $r$-tuples, Ann. Univ. Sci. Budapest. Eötvös Sect. Math. 8
(1965), 93–95, carded at
[[../library/set_systems/erdos_1965_problem_independent_tuples/_index|A problem on independent r-tuples]].

**Covers.** The range $n>c_rk$ with $c_r$ unspecified. Explicit ranges
of the same kind are the claims on
[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/1976_01_01_bollobas_daykin_erdos|Bollobás, Daykin and Erdős 1976]],
[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2011_07_27_huang_loh_sudakov|Huang, Loh and Sudakov 2012]],
[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2012_06_13_frankl_luczak_mieczkowska|Frankl, Łuczak and Mieczkowska 2012]],
[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2013_07_01_frankl|Frankl 2013]] and
[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2018_06_22_frankl_kupavskii|Frankl and Kupavskii 2022]].
