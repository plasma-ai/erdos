***

**Claim.** The maximum number of edges of a $3$-uniform hypergraph on $n$
vertices with matching number $s$ is
$\max\{\binom{3s+2}{3},\binom n3-\binom{n-s}{3}\}$ for all $n$ and $s$ with
$n\ge3s+2$, as the paper's abstract states its main theorem. In the notation
of [[problems/set_systems/E1020/_index|Problem 1020]], with $k=s+1$,

$$
f(n;3,k)=\max\left(\binom{3k-1}{3},\binom n3-\binom{n-k+1}{3}\right)
\qquad(n\ge3k-1),
$$

which is the whole range in which the conjecture is meaningful at $r=3$, so
the case $r=3$ of the conjecture is settled in full. The paper is P. Frankl,
On the maximum number of edges in a hypergraph with given matching number,
Discrete Appl. Math. 216 (2017), 562–581.

**Covers.** The case $r=3$ for every $n$ and $k$. Its predecessors are
[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2012_02_02_frankl_rodl_rucinski|Frankl, Rödl and Ruciński 2012]]
for $n\ge4k$ and
[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2012_02_19_luczak_mieczkowska|Łuczak and Mieczkowska 2014]]
for $n$ large. It says nothing about $r\ge4$.
