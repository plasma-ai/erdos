***

**Claim.** For a family of $k$-subsets of an $n$-set with no $s+1$ pairwise
disjoint members, $n\ge(2s+1)k-s$ implies that the family has at most
$\binom nk-\binom{n-s}{k}$ members, the paper's main theorem in its own
notation. In the notation of
[[problems/set_systems/E1020/_index|Problem 1020]], with $r$ for the
uniformity and $k-1$ for the matching number,

$$
f(n;r,k)=\binom nr-\binom{n-k+1}{r}\qquad(n\ge(2k-1)r-(k-1)),
$$

the conjectured value in that range, where the covering term is the larger.
The paper is P. Frankl, Improved bounds for Erdős' Matching Conjecture,
J. Combin. Theory Ser. A 120 (2013), 1068–1072.

**Covers.** The range $n\ge(2k-1)r-(k-1)$, about $2rk$. It supersedes the
ranges of order $r^2k$ on
[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2011_07_27_huang_loh_sudakov|Huang, Loh and Sudakov 2012]]
and
[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2012_06_13_frankl_luczak_mieczkowska|Frankl, Łuczak and Mieczkowska 2012]],
and was lowered to about $\tfrac53rk$ for large $k$ on
[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2018_06_22_frankl_kupavskii|Frankl and Kupavskii 2022]].
