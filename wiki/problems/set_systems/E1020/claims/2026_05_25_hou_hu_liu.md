***

**Claim.** The preprint *The Erdős Matching Conjecture for 4-uniform
hypergraphs* by Jianfeng Hou, Caiyun Hu and Xizhi Liu, arXiv:2605.26060
(posted 2026-05-25, revised 2026-09-11), states that for integers $s\ge6004$
and $n\ge4s+4$, an $n$-vertex $4$-uniform hypergraph with no matching of size
$s+1$ has at most $\max\{\binom{4s+3}{4},\binom n4-\binom{n-s}{4}\}$ edges.
In the notation of [[problems/set_systems/E1020/_index|Problem 1020]], with
$k=s+1$,

$$
f(n;4,k)=\max\left(\binom{4k-1}{4},\binom n4-\binom{n-k+1}{4}\right)
\qquad(k\ge6005,\ n\ge4k).
$$

The abstract describes a finite-board reduction for general uniformity,
which reduces the conjecture to a lower-uniformity bound and a fixed finite
optimization at the two adjacent vertex numbers where the two candidate
constructions exchange dominance; in the $4$-uniform case the board has $19$
vertices, its weighted inequality splits into layers of $19$, $15$ and $11$
vertices, the hardest layer is settled by exact rational dual certificates
and deterministic integer searches, and every computer-assisted step is
checked in exact arithmetic by verifiers that rebuild the finite systems from
their definitions.

**Covers.** The case $r=4$ for $k\ge6005$ and $n\ge4k$. The matching numbers
below $6004$ are the claimed novelty of
[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2026_09_13_babanskyy|Babanskyy 2026]],
which develops this preprint's method; the range $n\ge5(k-1)$ with $n$ large
is the earlier claim on
[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2026_02_22_frankl_lu_ma_wu|Frankl, Lu, Ma and Wu 2026]].
