# problems/set_systems/E1020/claims

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/1961_01_01_erdos_ko_rado|1961_01_01_erdos_ko_rado]]: Erdős, Ko and Rado (1961) prove that an intersecting family of r-subsets of
an n-set with n at least 2r has at most binomial(n-1, r-1) members, which
is the matching conjecture for k = 2 and every uniformity; refereed.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/1965_01_01_erdos|1965_01_01_erdos]]: Erdős (1965) proves the conjectured value of f(n; r, k) whenever n is at
least c_r k, for a constant c_r depending only on r, the range in which the
covering term dominates; refereed, with the constant unspecified.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/1968_09_01_kleitman|1968_09_01_kleitman]]: Kleitman (1968) bounds families of subsets of an n-set with no k pairwise
disjoint members, sharply when k divides n or n + 1; the r-uniform case at
n = rk is the matching conjecture there; refereed.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/1976_01_01_bollobas_daykin_erdos|1976_01_01_bollobas_daykin_erdos]]: Bollobás, Daykin and Erdős (1976) prove that for n > 2 r^3 (k - 1) an
r-uniform hypergraph with no k pairwise disjoint edges and more edges than
the covering family minus a correction is contained in it; refereed.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2011_07_27_huang_loh_sudakov|2011_07_27_huang_loh_sudakov]]: Huang, Loh and Sudakov (2012) prove the matching conjecture whenever
n > 3 r^2 k, by shifting and through an asymptotic rainbow-matching
strengthening; refereed in Combin. Probab. Comput.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2012_02_02_frankl_rodl_rucinski|2012_02_02_frankl_rodl_rucinski]]: Frankl, Rödl and Ruciński (2012) prove the matching conjecture for
3-uniform hypergraphs whenever n is at least four times the forbidden
matching size, that is n at least 4k; refereed in Combin. Probab. Comput.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2012_02_19_luczak_mieczkowska|2012_02_19_luczak_mieczkowska]]: Łuczak and Mieczkowska (2014) prove the matching conjecture for 3-uniform
hypergraphs on n vertices for every matching number once n is large, with
the cover and the clique the only extremal hypergraphs; refereed.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2012_05_30_frankl|2012_05_30_frankl]]: Frankl (2017) proves the matching conjecture for 3-uniform hypergraphs for
every n and every matching number, completing the case r = 3; refereed in
Discrete Applied Mathematics.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2012_06_13_frankl_luczak_mieczkowska|2012_06_13_frankl_luczak_mieczkowska]]: Frankl, Łuczak and Mieczkowska (2012) prove that an r-uniform hypergraph
whose largest matching has k - 1 edges has at most the covering family's
size once n > 2 r^2 (k - 1) / log r, the cover alone extremal; refereed.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2013_07_01_frankl|2013_07_01_frankl]]: Frankl (2013) proves the matching conjecture whenever n is at least
(2s + 1) r - s for the matching number s = k - 1, the first range linear in
both parameters; refereed in J. Combin. Theory Ser. A.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2017_10_01_frankl|2017_10_01_frankl]]: Frankl (2017) proves the matching conjecture when k - 1 > r and n lies in
rk <= n < k(r + 1/(2 r^(2r+1))), the almost-perfect range where the clique
term is the maximum; refereed in Israel J. Math.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2018_06_22_frankl_kupavskii|2018_06_22_frankl_kupavskii]]: Frankl and Kupavskii (2022) prove the matching conjecture for large
matching number s = k - 1 whenever n is at least (5/3) s r - (2/3) s, by
concentration inequalities; refereed in J. Combin. Theory Ser. B.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2022_06_03_kolupaev_kupavskii|2022_06_03_kolupaev_kupavskii]]: Kolupaev and Kupavskii (2023) prove the matching conjecture for r >= 5 and
k - 1 > 101 r^3 whenever rk <= n < k(r + 1/(100 r)), widening Frankl's
window above n = rk; refereed in Discrete Mathematics.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2026_02_01_mishra|2026_02_01_mishra]]: Mishra's 2026 preprint claimed a complete proof of the Erdős matching
conjecture; a reader located an error in its Lemma 4 on the site's thread,
and the author withdrew the preprint on 1 June 2026.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2026_02_22_frankl_lu_ma_wu|2026_02_22_frankl_lu_ma_wu]]: Frankl, Lu, Ma and Wu (2026) claim the matching conjecture for 4-uniform
hypergraphs whenever n is at least five times the matching number k - 1 and
n is large, through a stability theorem; an arXiv preprint, not refereed.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2026_05_25_hou_hu_liu|2026_05_25_hou_hu_liu]]: Hou, Hu and Liu (2026) claim the matching conjecture for 4-uniform
hypergraphs for every matching number s at least 6004 and n at least 4s + 4,
by a finite-board reduction checked in exact arithmetic; not refereed.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2026_08_19_cao_liu_zhang|2026_08_19_cao_liu_zhang]]: Cao, Liu and Zhang (2026) claim the matching conjecture for every fixed
uniformity r once the matching number k - 1 is large and n is at least
(r + 1)(k - 1), the conjecture's crossover; an arXiv preprint, not refereed.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2026_09_13_babanskyy|2026_09_13_babanskyy]]: Babanskyy's 2026 manuscript claiming the Erdős matching conjecture for
4-uniform hypergraphs and every matching size, by the finite-board method
with rational certificates; AI-assisted, unreviewed, not refereed.
