# Set Systems, Designs and Hypergraphs

[[problems/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_systems/E0020/_index|E0020/]]: Asks whether the number of
n-element sets needed to force a k-sunflower grows only exponentially in n, with
a base depending on k.

[[problems/set_systems/E0021/_index|E0021/]]: Asks whether the smallest
intersecting family of n-element sets in which every set of size at most n minus
1 misses a member has size linear in n.

[[problems/set_systems/E0083/_index|E0083/]]: Bounds how large a family of
half-size subsets of a set of 4n elements can be when every two members share at
least two elements.

[[problems/set_systems/E0207/_index|E0207/]]: Asks whether, for every g at least
2, large Steiner triple systems exist in which any j edges span at least j plus
3 vertices for j up to g.

[[problems/set_systems/E0231/_index|E0231/]]: Asks whether every string of
length 2^k over k letters contains two adjacent blocks that are permutations of
each other; the site prints 2^k - 1, Erdős's misprint, and Keränen's word on
four letters disproves it.

[[problems/set_systems/E0447/_index|E0447/]]: The largest family of subsets of
one through n in which no set is the union of two other distinct members of the
family.

[[problems/set_systems/E0497/_index|E0497/]]: Determines the number of
antichains of subsets of an n-element set, that is, families in which no member
contains another.

[[problems/set_systems/E0499/_index|E0499/]]: Asks whether every n by n doubly
stochastic matrix has a permutation along which the product of entries is at
least n to the power minus n.

[[problems/set_systems/E0624/_index|E0624/]]: Asks to prove that H(n) minus the
base-two logarithm of n tends to infinity, where H(n) is the least size such
that some map from the subsets of an n-element set X to X sends the subsets of
each set that large onto X.

[[problems/set_systems/E0643/_index|E0643/]]: Estimates how many edges force a
t-uniform hypergraph on n vertices to have four edges with A union B equal to C
union D and A, B and C, D disjoint.

[[problems/set_systems/E0644/_index|E0644/]]: Bounds the size of a set meeting
all k-element sets of a family in which every r of them share a piercing pair,
asking if it is a constant times k.

[[problems/set_systems/E0664/_index|E0664/]]: Concerns families of subsets of
the first n integers, each of size above a constant times the square root of n,
with any two sharing at most one element.

[[problems/set_systems/E0665/_index|E0665/]]: Concerns pairwise balanced designs
on the first n integers, families of sets in which every pair of distinct
elements lies in exactly one set.

[[problems/set_systems/E0701/_index|E0701/]]: Asks whether a family of sets
closed under subsets always has an element contained in as many members as the
largest intersecting subfamily has sets.

[[problems/set_systems/E0702/_index|E0702/]]: Asks whether, for k at least 4 and
n large in terms of k, more k-subsets of the first n integers than those through
a fixed pair force two meeting in one point; Frankl proved it, while the site's
wording, with no range, fails.

[[problems/set_systems/E0703/_index|E0703/]]: The largest family of subsets of
the first n integers in which no two members intersect in exactly r elements.

[[problems/set_systems/E0716/_index|E0716/]]: Asks whether the largest 3-uniform
hypergraph on n vertices containing no three edges spanning six vertices has o(n
squared) edges, fewer than any fixed fraction of n squared for large n.

[[problems/set_systems/E0719/_index|E0719/]]: Asks whether every r-uniform
hypergraph on n vertices is a union of at most ex_r(n; K_{r+1}^r) edges and
(r+1)-cliques, no two sharing an edge; the Erdős–Sauer conjecture, known for r = 2.

[[problems/set_systems/E0722/_index|E0722/]]: Asks whether a Steiner system on n
points with blocks of size k covering every r-set once exists for large n
whenever the divisibility conditions hold.

[[problems/set_systems/E0723/_index|E0723/]]: Asks whether the order of a finite
projective plane must always be a power of a prime.

[[problems/set_systems/E0724/_index|E0724/]]: Asks whether the largest number of
mutually orthogonal Latin squares of order n grows at least as fast as a
constant times the square root of n.

[[problems/set_systems/E0725/_index|E0725/]]: Asks for an asymptotic formula for
the number of Latin rectangles with k rows and n columns.

[[problems/set_systems/E0732/_index|E0732/]]: Asks whether at least exp(c
n^(1/2) log n) increasing sequences of block sizes arise from a design on n
points in which every pair of points lies in exactly one block.

[[problems/set_systems/E0734/_index|E0734/]]: Asks for a non-trivial pairwise
balanced design on n points in which each block size is used at most about the
square root of n times.

[[problems/set_systems/E0747/_index|E0747/]]: Determines how many edges a random
three-uniform hypergraph on three n vertices needs so that it almost surely has
n disjoint edges.

[[problems/set_systems/E0775/_index|E0775/]]: Asks whether a three-uniform
hypergraph on n vertices can have at least n minus O(1) different sizes of
maximal complete subgraphs.

[[problems/set_systems/E0776/_index|E0776/]]: Asks how large n must be, in terms
of r, for an antichain of subsets of one to n in which every occurring size is
used at least r times to reach n minus 3 distinct sizes; three claims are
pending, none accepted.

[[problems/set_systems/E0834/_index|E0834/]]: Asks whether there is a
three-critical three-uniform hypergraph in which every vertex has degree at
least seven.

[[problems/set_systems/E0835/_index|E0835/]]: Asks whether some k above two lets
the k-element subsets of the integers up to two k get k plus one colors so every
k plus one of them sees all colors.

[[problems/set_systems/E0837/_index|E0837/]]: Asks for the set A_3 of densities
alpha such that 3-uniform hypergraphs of limiting density above alpha must have
growing subgraphs of density above some fixed beta > alpha, while limiting
density at least alpha need not.

[[problems/set_systems/E0857/_index|E0857/]]: Estimates the least number of
subsets of the integers up to n that forces a sunflower of size k, meaning k of
them with equal pairwise intersections.

[[problems/set_systems/E0901/_index|E0901/]]: Estimates the least number of
edges in an n-uniform hypergraph that cannot be colored with two colors but can
with three.

[[problems/set_systems/E0903/_index|E0903/]]: Concerns block designs on p
squared plus p plus one points, for a prime power p, in which every pair of
points lies in exactly one block.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/_index|E1020/]]: Asks whether, for r at least 3 and
n at least rk, the most edges in an r-uniform hypergraph on n vertices with no k
pairwise disjoint edges is the larger of the clique count and the star count.

[[problems/set_systems/E1022/_index|E1022/]]: Asks whether sets of size at least
t, few of which lie inside any given set relative to its size, can always be
two-colored with no monochromatic member.

[[problems/set_systems/E1023/_index|E1023/]]: Asks whether the largest family of
subsets of the first n integers with no member a union of others is a constant
times two to the n over the square root of n.

[[problems/set_systems/E1024/_index|E1024/]]: Estimates the largest independent
set guaranteed in a three-uniform hypergraph on n vertices in which any two
edges share at most one vertex.

[[problems/set_systems/E1025/_index|E1025/]]: Independent sets for a function
assigning to each pair from the first n integers a third value, meaning sets
closed away from the values of their own pairs.

[[problems/set_systems/E1026/_index|E1026/]]: Determines the largest possible
sum of a monotonic subsequence of a sequence of n distinct real numbers.

[[problems/set_systems/E1027/_index|E1027/]]: Concerns the union of a family of
at most a constant times two to the n many sets each of size n, for large n.

[[problems/set_systems/E1075/_index|E1075/]]: Concerns a constant larger than r
to the power minus r, for each r at least three, in a property of r-uniform
hypergraphs on many vertices.

[[problems/set_systems/E1076/_index|E1076/]]: Asks whether, for k at least five,
the most edges of a 3-uniform hypergraph on n vertices with no j vertices
spanning j minus two edges for any j from four to k is asymptotic to n squared
over six; false for the single family as printed.

[[problems/set_systems/E1159/_index|E1159/]]: Asks whether there is a constant
greater than one for which a stated combinatorial property holds.

[[problems/set_systems/E1178/_index|E1178/]]: Determines the least number of
vertices d such that forbidding all r-uniform hypergraphs with d vertices and e
edges forces subquadratically many edges.
