# problems/set_theory/E0118/claims

[[problems/set_theory/E0118/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_theory/E0118/claims/1999_01_01_schipperus|1999_01_01_schipperus]]: Schipperus (thesis 1999, Ann. Pure Appl. Logic 2010) proved that the
ordinal omega^(omega^2) satisfies the arrow relation for a triangle but not
for a complete graph on six vertices, answering the question no.

[[problems/set_theory/E0118/claims/1999_07_01_darby|1999_07_01_darby]]: Darby (J. Combin. Theory Ser. B, 1999) is credited, independently of
Schipperus, with the counterexample omega^(omega^2), a partition ordinal
that fails the arrow relation at six; his paper is not held here.

[[problems/set_theory/E0118/claims/2000_09_01_larson|2000_09_01_larson]]: Larson (J. Symbolic Logic, 2000) proved that omega^(omega^2) fails the arrow
relation for a complete graph on five vertices, sharpening the counterexample
of Schipperus and Darby; the site's curator credits it.
