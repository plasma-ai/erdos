# problems/set_theory/E0474/claims

[[problems/set_theory/E0474/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_theory/E0474/claims/1965_03_01_erdos_hajnal_rado|1965_03_01_erdos_hajnal_rado]]: Theorem 17 of Erdős, Hajnal and Rado (Acta Math. Acad. Sci. Hungar., 1965)
gives Problem 474's three-coloring of pairs of reals under CH, which Gödel
showed consistent with ZFC, so ZFC does not refute that the coloring exists.

[[problems/set_theory/E0474/claims/1988_10_01_shelah|1988_10_01_shelah]]: Shelah (Israel J. Math., 1988) proved, from a large cardinal, that it is
consistent that every three-coloring of pairs of reals has an uncountable
set missing a color, so the coloring Erdős asked for is not provable in ZFC.

[[problems/set_theory/E0474/claims/2026_01_06_shelah|2026_01_06_shelah]]: Shelah (arXiv, 2026) claims consistency of positive square-bracket relations
below a small continuum with no large cardinal, read in the problem's thread
as settling Problem 474 without large cardinal strength.
