***

**Claim.** $\omega^\omega\to(\omega^\omega,3)^2$: in every red/blue coloring
of the edges of $K_\alpha$ with $\alpha=\omega^\omega$ there is a red
$K_\alpha$ or a blue $K_3$ (the Theorem, p. 396; which color carries the
triangle is a convention, and the paper puts it the other way round). This
is the question of [[problems/set_theory/E0590/_index|Problem 590]], which the
paper records as Problem 7 of the Erdős–Hajnal list. The proof is by
induction from the Normal Form, Super Form, Transitivity and
Well-Foundedness Lemmas (pp. 403–405), using Erdős's relation
$\omega^{2n+1}\to(\omega^{n+1},4)^2$; the author calls it long and
complicated.

**Source.** C. C. Chang, *A partition theorem for the complete graph on
$\omega^\omega$*, J. Combinatorial Theory Ser. A 12 (1972), no. 3, 396–452,
received 1970-02-24, issue dated May 1972; this page is dated by the issue
month, since the issue prints no day. The Theorem, the lemma statements and
the proof of the Theorem from the lemmas are the basis of this page,
recorded on
[[../library/set_theory/chang_1972_partition_theorem_complete_graph_omega_omega/_index|the source card]]
and its
[[../library/set_theory/chang_1972_partition_theorem_complete_graph_omega_omega/theorem_p396|theorem page]];
the proofs of the lemmas are followed for structure only and nothing is
independently reviewed. Footnote 1 (p. 397) reports Milner's extension
$\omega^\omega\to(\omega^\omega,m)^2$ for every finite $m$, communicated by
letter without a printed proof, which is disclosed here rather than given a
page, and Larson's shorter proof of both results in her 1972 Dartmouth
thesis, published in 1973 and recorded on
[[problems/set_theory/E0590/claims/1973_12_01_larson|Larson's claim page]].

**Acceptance.** Refereed: the Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A.
Reviewed: the curator of erdosproblems.com (T. F. Bloom) labels the problem
PROVED (LEAN) and credits Chang [Ch72] with the proof in the problem's
commentary, with Milner's extension and Larson's shorter proof noted. The
curator is independent of the author.
