# problems/set_theory/E0590/claims

[[problems/set_theory/E0590/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_theory/E0590/claims/1972_05_01_chang|1972_05_01_chang]]: Chang proves ω^ω → (ω^ω, 3)^2, that every red-blue coloring of the pairs
from the ordinal ω^ω has a red set of order type ω^ω or a blue triangle,
the statement of Problem 590, in a 57-page paper of 1972.

[[problems/set_theory/E0590/claims/1973_12_01_larson|1973_12_01_larson]]: Larson proves ω^ω → (ω^ω, m)^2 for every finite m by a short argument; the
case m = 3 is the statement of Problem 590, and the Lean formalization in
Boris Alexeev's repository follows her proof.
