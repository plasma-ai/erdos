***

**Claim.** In the reading $\alpha=\omega^\beta$ of
[[problems/set_theory/E0592/_index|Problem 592]] (its Formulation), Specker
answers the question at every finite exponent $\beta\ge2$. He proves
$\omega^2\to(\omega^2,n)^2$ for every finite $n$, so $\beta=2$ has the
property, and $\omega^3\not\to(\omega^3,3)^2$. He introduces pinning maps (a
map from $\alpha$ into $\beta$ sending every subset of order type $\alpha$ to
a set of order type $\beta$), observes that a partition relation
$\alpha\to(\alpha,\chi)^2$ passes to every $\beta$ to which $\alpha$ can be
pinned, and shows that $\omega^m$ can be pinned to $\omega^3$ for
$3\le m<\omega$. Together these give $\omega^m\not\to(\omega^m,3)^2$ for every
finite $m\ge3$, so no finite $\beta\ge3$ has the property. The paper is
E. Specker, *Teilmengen von Mengen mit Relationen*, Comment. Math. Helv. 31
(1956/57), 302--314, DOI 10.1007/BF02564361, the site's [Sp57]; the
publisher's record gives receipt on 8 October 1956 and the issue date
December 1956, and the page name carries the first day of that month, since
the record gives no day. The paper is not filed in the library: the
statements above follow the account of it in the introduction of Galvin and
Larson's *Pinning countable ordinals*
([[../library/set_theory/galvin_nd_pinning_countable_ordinals/_index|card]])
and in Schipperus's history on p. 1196 of *Countable partition ordinals*
([[../library/set_theory/schipperus_2010_countable_partition_ordinals/_index|card]]),
not the paper's pages.

**Covers.** The exponents $\beta=2$ (the property holds) and every finite
$\beta\ge3$ (it fails). Not covered: every infinite $\beta$, among them
[[problems/set_theory/E0592/claims/1972_05_01_chang|Chang's]] $\beta=\omega$.
