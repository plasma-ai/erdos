# problems/set_theory/E0592/claims

[[problems/set_theory/E0592/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_theory/E0592/claims/1956_12_01_specker|1956_12_01_specker]]: Specker (Comment. Math. Helv. 31, 1956/57) proves omega^2 -> (omega^2, n)^2
for every finite n and omega^m -/-> (omega^m, 3)^2 for finite m >= 3: yes
at beta = 2 and no at every finite beta >= 3; refereed.

[[problems/set_theory/E0592/claims/1972_05_01_chang|1972_05_01_chang]]: Chang (J. Combin. Theory Ser. A 12, 1972) proves omega^omega ->
(omega^omega, 3)^2, the case beta = omega of the question, yes; refereed.

[[problems/set_theory/E0592/claims/1975_01_01_galvin_larson|1975_01_01_galvin_larson]]: Galvin and Larson (Fund. Math. 82, 1974/75) show that a countable partition
ordinal is 0, 1, omega^2 or omega^(omega^gamma), so omega^beta fails the
relation for every decomposable beta >= 3; refereed.

[[problems/set_theory/E0592/claims/2010_05_13_schipperus|2010_05_13_schipperus]]: Schipperus (Ann. Pure Appl. Logic 161, 2010) proves the relation for
beta = omega^gamma with gamma the sum of one or two indecomposables and
refutes it for four or more; three summands stay open; refereed.
