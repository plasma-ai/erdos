# problems/set_theory/E0594/claims

[[problems/set_theory/E0594/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_theory/E0594/claims/1966_03_01_erdos_hajnal|1966_03_01_erdos_hajnal]]: Erdős and Hajnal (Acta Math. Acad. Sci. Hungar. 17, 1966) state, without
proof, that a graph of chromatic number above omega_2 contains all
sufficiently long odd circuits, the question of Problem 594 in a special case.

[[problems/set_theory/E0594/claims/1974_01_01_erdos_hajnal_shelah|1974_01_01_erdos_hajnal_shelah]]: Erdős, Hajnal and Shelah prove that every graph of chromatic number greater
than aleph_0 contains odd cycles of every sufficiently large length, the
statement of Problem 594, as Theorem 3 of their 1974 paper.
