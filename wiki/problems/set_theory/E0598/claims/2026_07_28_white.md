***

**Claim.** Write $\kappa=(2^{\aleph_0})^+$ and
$\mathrm{Col}_\omega(m,\kappa)$ for the coloring property asked by
[[problems/set_theory/E0598/_index|Problem 598]]. Theorem 5.3 of the
report: if the coloring fails for some $\lambda$, then $0^\sharp$ exists;
so in every model without $0^\sharp$, in particular under $V=L$, the
answer is positive for every $m$. Theorem 6.1: assuming the consistency of
the rank-into-rank hypothesis I1, it is consistent that the problem has a
negative instance. Together, as the report presents them, the problem read
as one question about every infinite $m$ is independent of ZFC relative to
the consistency of I1. Theorem 4.1 adds a refinement that settles no
instance: if a failure occurs, the least bad cardinal
$\lambda^*=\min\{\lambda\ge\kappa:\neg\mathrm{Col}_\omega(\lambda,\kappa)\}$
is regular and $\aleph_0$-closed ($\mu^\omega<\lambda^*$ for every
cardinal $\mu<\lambda^*$), so in particular $\kappa^\omega<\lambda^*$, and
$\mathrm{Col}_\omega(m,\kappa)$ holds exactly for $m<\lambda^*$.

**Hypotheses.** The report labels Theorem 5.3 as proved modulo the
condensation lemma and Jech's embedding criterion for $0^\sharp$, and
Theorem 6.1 as proved modulo Claim 1.10(a) of Garti and Hayut (Math. Log.
Q. 65 (2019), 95–104;
[[../library/set_theory/garti_2019_first_omitting_cardinal_magidority/_index|library card]]).
The negative half is relative to I1 and settles nothing in ZFC.
