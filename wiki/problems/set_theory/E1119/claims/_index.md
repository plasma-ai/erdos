# problems/set_theory/E1119/claims

[[problems/set_theory/E1119/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_theory/E1119/claims/1964_03_01_erdos|1964_03_01_erdos]]: Erdős (Michigan Math. J., 1964) proves that when some cardinal lies strictly
between m and the continuum, analytic functions taking at most m values at
each point number at most m; formalized by others.

[[problems/set_theory/E1119/claims/2017_01_05_kumar_shelah|2017_01_05_kumar_shelah]]: Shows that after adding aleph one Cohen reals to a model with continuum
aleph two, every continuum-sized family of entire functions takes continuum
many values at some point, so a yes answer is consistent; refereed in 2017.

[[problems/set_theory/E1119/claims/2023_10_30_schilhan_weinert|2023_10_30_schilhan_weinert]]: Forces, over a model of GCH, aleph two entire functions taking at most
aleph one values at each point while the continuum is aleph two, so a no
answer is consistent; with Kumar and Shelah's model, independence of ZFC.
