# problems/set_theory/E1123/claims

[[problems/set_theory/E1123/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_theory/E1123/claims/1984_09_01_just_krawczyk|1984_09_01_just_krawczyk]]: Just and Krawczyk (Trans. Amer. Math. Soc., 1984) prove under the continuum
hypothesis that the algebras of sets of integers modulo density zero and
modulo logarithmic density zero are isomorphic, so ZFC cannot prove otherwise.

[[problems/set_theory/E1123/claims/2000_01_01_farah|2000_01_01_farah]]: Farah (Mem. Amer. Math. Soc., 2000) proves that the Open Coloring Axiom with
Martin's Axiom makes the two density quotient algebras non-isomorphic; with
Just and Krawczyk's isomorphism under CH, the problem is independent of ZFC.
