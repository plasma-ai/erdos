***

**Claim.** Assuming the continuum hypothesis, there is a graph $G$ whose
vertex set is ordered in type $\omega_1^2$ such that $G$ contains no
triangle and no complete bipartite graph $K_{\aleph_0,\aleph_0}$, and no set
of vertices of order type $\omega_1^2$ is independent in $G$ (the abstract,
in the corpus's words). Coloring a pair of vertices red when it is an edge
of $G$ and blue otherwise gives a $2$-coloring of $[\omega_1^2]^2$ with no
red triangle and no blue set of type $\omega_1^2$, that is,

$$
\omega_1^2\not\to(\omega_1^2,3)^2 .
$$

So the relation of [[problems/set_theory/E1169/_index|Problem 1169]] holds
in every model of CH, and ZFC does not refute it, which is what the site's
label, not disprovable, records. Whether ZFC proves it, equivalently whether
$\omega_1^2\to(\omega_1^2,3)^2$ is consistent, is open: Komjáth's survey
(Problem 13 commentary, on the corpus's
[[../library/set_theory/komjath_2025_erdos_hajnal_problem_list/_index|source card]])
says the consistency is open as far as its author knows, and records, under
the GCH heading of the survey's Problem 13, that the theorem is the case
$\kappa=\omega$ of Hajnal's $(\kappa^+)^2\not\to((\kappa^+)^2,3)^2$ for
$\kappa^{<\kappa}=\kappa$, that is, for regular $\kappa$ under GCH, so with
the cardinal-arithmetic hypothesis $\kappa^{<\kappa}=\kappa$ together with
$2^\kappa=\kappa^+$; at $\kappa=\omega$ the first condition holds in ZFC and
the second is CH, which Hajnal's abstract assumes. Komjáth adds
Baumgartner's extensions to singular $\lambda$ with $2^\lambda=\lambda^+$
and to regular $\kappa$ carrying a $\kappa$-Suslin tree, the latter recorded
on
[[problems/set_theory/E1169/claims/1975_12_01_baumgartner|Baumgartner's page]].
The graph's omission of $K_{\aleph_0,\aleph_0}$ is more than the relation
needs.

**Covers.** One side of an independence result: the relation holds in every
model of CH, so ZFC does not refute it, but nothing here shows that ZFC does
not prove it. One side alone leaves the question open, so the result leaves
[[problems/set_theory/E1169/_index|Problem 1169]] open. It would be settled
as independent by a model of $\omega_1^2\to(\omega_1^2,3)^2$, which no
source records, and as proved by a proof of the negative relation in ZFC
alone.

**Formulation.** The catalog's statement quantifies over finite $k<\omega$,
but the displayed relation does not use $k$; the booklet item [Va99, 7.85]
asks the same question without the quantifier. Read with $k$ triangle
colors, the relation at $k=0$ is $\omega_1^2\not\to(\omega_1^2)^2_1$, which
is false, since with one color every set is homogeneous; so the statement
quantified over all finite $k$ is false outright under that reading, which
supports taking the displayed relation without $k$ as the target. The
standing recorded here concerns the two-color relation as displayed.

**Source.** A. Hajnal, A negative partition relation, Proc. Nat. Acad. Sci.
U.S.A. 68 (1971), no. 1, 142--144, doi:10.1073/pnas.68.1.142. The issue is
dated January 1971 and the record carries no day, so this page's date is the
first of that month. The statement above follows the paper's abstract.
Nothing on this page is independently reviewed by this project.
