# Problem 1170

[[problems/set_theory/_index|..]]

***

**Statement.** Is it consistent that

$$
\omega_2\to (\alpha)_2^2
$$

for every $\alpha <\omega_2$?

**Status.** Open.

**Source.** [erdosproblems.com/1170](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1170),
