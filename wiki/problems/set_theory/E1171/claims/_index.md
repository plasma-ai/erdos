# problems/set_theory/E1171/claims

[[problems/set_theory/E1171/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_theory/E1171/claims/1987_01_01_baumgartner_hajnal|1987_01_01_baumgartner_hajnal]]: Baumgartner and Hajnal (Contemp. Math. 65, 1987) prove that (kappa^+)^2 ->
(kappa^+ kappa, 3, 3)^2 for regular kappa with kappa^(<kappa) = kappa; for
kappa = omega this is the ZFC case k = 2 of Problem 1171, containing k = 1.

[[problems/set_theory/E1171/claims/1989_01_01_baumgartner|1989_01_01_baumgartner]]: Baumgartner (Lecture Notes in Math. 1401, 1989) proved that Martin's axiom
for aleph_1 dense sets makes omega_1 omega a partition ordinal; the catalog
relation follows for every finite k, so it holds in a model of ZFC.

[[problems/set_theory/E1171/claims/2026_09_05_gao|2026_09_05_gao]]: An unrefereed, AI-assisted Zenodo deposit proving the exact relation for
every finite k from Martin's axiom for aleph_1 dense sets by a color-reduction
lemma; filed on the site as a partial claim, then withdrawn by its author.
