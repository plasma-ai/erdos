**Claim.** Theorem 3 of Saharon Shelah and Lee Stanley, A theorem and some
consistency results in partition calculus, Ann. Pure Appl. Logic 36 (1987),
no. 2, 119--152, stated on p. 119 and proved in §3 (pp. 139--144), reads: if
ZFC is consistent, then so is ZFC +
$\aleph_{\mathfrak c^+}\not\to(\aleph_{\mathfrak c^+},\aleph_1)^2$. The
cardinal $\lambda=\aleph_{\mathfrak c^+}$ meets both hypotheses of the
Statement of [[problems/set_theory/E1220/_index|Problem 1220]] in ZFC. It is
singular, of cofinality $\mathfrak c^+$, and its cofinality is
$\aleph_0$-inaccessible, since
$\mu^{\aleph_0}\le\mathfrak c^{\aleph_0}=\mathfrak c$ for $\mu<\mathfrak c^+$.
The cardinal itself is $\aleph_0$-inaccessible by Shelah's ZFC theorem that
$\mu^{\aleph_0}<\aleph_{\mathfrak c^+}$ for every
$\mu<\aleph_{\mathfrak c^+}$, which the paper's historical remarks (p. 125)
record from S. Shelah, A note on cardinal exponentiation, J. Symbolic Logic 45
(1980), 56--66. So in the model of Theorem 3 a singular cardinal $\lambda$
with $\lambda$ and $\mathrm{cf}(\lambda)$ both $\aleph_0$-inaccessible fails
$\lambda\to(\lambda,\aleph_1)^2$, the universal statement is false there, and
ZFC, if consistent, does not prove it. The paper treats the instance at
$\aleph_{\mathfrak c^+}$, which its p. 125 traces to Problem 35.5 of the book
of Erdős, Hajnal, Máté and Rado, and states no conclusion about the universal
question; the step to it is the bound above.

**Covers.** The not-provable side of
[[problems/set_theory/E1220/_index|Problem 1220]], relative to the consistency
of ZFC: ZFC does not prove that every singular $\lambda$ with $\lambda$ and
$\mathrm{cf}(\lambda)$ both $\aleph_0$-inaccessible satisfies
$\lambda\to(\lambda,\aleph_1)^2$. It settles that side only; it does not show
that ZFC fails to refute the statement, and one side alone leaves the question
open. The not-disprovable side is on
[[problems/set_theory/E1220/claims/1965_03_01_erdos_hajnal_rado|Erdős, Hajnal and Rado's page]],
and the two pages together settle the problem as independent of ZFC.
[[problems/set_theory/E1220/claims/2026_09_25_bae|Bae's pending claim]]
asserts the same not-provable side.

and Applied Logic, volume 36 (1987), received 3 March 1986 and communicated by
