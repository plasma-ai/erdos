***

**Claim.** M. W. Alomari's note *A simple direct proof of the Erdős–Straus
conjecture* (Authorea, 6 February 2023, version 2 of 21 February 2023; also
OSF, 10 February 2023, and Research Square, 15 February 2023) claims that
for every $n\ge2$ the fraction $4/n$ is a sum of three unit fractions; its
abstract says that the note proves the conjecture and that Sierpiński's
conjecture follows. Through the Formulation of
[[problems/unit_fractions/E0242/_index|Problem 242]], which records how a
representation becomes one with three distinct terms, this is a claim of the
whole problem.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki.

**Standing.** Claimed. The note reached the site's discussion thread through a
comment of 18 November 2025 that links its ResearchGate posting and strongly
doubts it; a reply of the same day calls the paper very mistaken, with unclear
or false steps from p. 2 on, and the site's owner adds that none of the proofs
of the conjecture the site's owner has looked at seemed credible. These are
opinions, not a refutation record, so the claim stays pending rather than
rejected. The note is not refereed, it was not submitted to the site's
proof-claim tab, no one has accepted it, and the site labels the problem
FALSIFIABLE. The page rests on the abstract and the posting records; the
argument is unexamined.
