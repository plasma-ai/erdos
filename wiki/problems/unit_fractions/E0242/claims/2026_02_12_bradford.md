***

**Claim.** Kyle Bradford's preprint *A solution to the Straus-Erdős
conjecture* (arXiv:2602.11774v1, 12 February 2026) claims that every prime
$p$ has $4/p=1/x+1/y+1/z$ with positive integers; its abstract (p. 1) says it
"outlines a solution". Since solutions scale to multiples, this is a claim of
the whole of [[problems/unit_fractions/E0242/_index|Problem 242]].

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki.

**Why it is rejected.** The paper proves sufficient conditions and never
proves that every prime meets one.

1. *The lemmas are correct but conditional.* With $t=4k+3$ ($k\ge0$),
   $1\le\ell\le2t$, $\gcd(\ell,t)=1$ and $h=4t-\ell$, Lemmas 1–2
   (pp. 2–3) give

   $$
   \frac4p=\frac{h}{tp+1}+\frac{\ell}{tp+1}+\frac4{p(tp+1)},\qquad
   \frac4p=\frac4{p+t}+\frac{h}{p(p+t)}+\frac{\ell}{p(p+t)},
   $$

   whose terms are unit fractions when $tp\equiv-1$, respectively
   $p\equiv-t$, modulo $\operatorname{lcm}(4,\ell,h)$. This is a restricted
   Type I/II construction: it splits $4t$ into two divisors of a fixed
   integer. Saying every prime satisfies one of these congruences is itself
   an existence statement at least as strong as the open case of the
   conjecture.
2. *The covering is never proved.* After examples for $k=0$ (pp. 3–4) the
   paper ends: "The last thing that we must show is that this is a covering
   system" (p. 4). No proof follows.
3. *The listed classes leave every hard class uncovered.* The displayed
   classes are $29,41\pmod{44}$, $13,17\pmod{20}$, $5\pmod8$ and
   $93,137\pmod{140}$. Each of Mordell's residual classes
   $1,121,169,289,361,529\pmod{840}$ avoids those modulo $20$, $8$ and
   $140$, and adding $p\equiv1\pmod{44}$ avoids the rest. By the Chinese
   remainder theorem and Dirichlet's theorem each combination contains
   infinitely many primes. This concerns the listed $k=0$ classes only, not
   the whole infinite family.

**Standing.** Rejected: **proof defective; statement unresolved**. The two
identities are valid and are retained at their scope; no counterexample to
Problem 242 and no verification tier are asserted.

**Read depth.** pp. 1–4 of v1, the only arXiv version, checked against the
PDF; the identities are checked above. Propositions 1–2 and the cited earlier
papers are not verified.
