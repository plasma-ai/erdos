***

**Claim.** Martin's
[[../library/unit_fractions/martin_2000_denser_egyptian_fractions/theorem_2|Theorem 2]]
(Denser Egyptian fractions, Acta Arith. 95 (2000), no. 3, 231--260;
arXiv:math/9811112, 18 November 1998): for every positive rational $r$ and
every integer $t\ge t_0(r)$, the least largest denominator $M_t(r)$ in a
$t$-term representation of $r$ by distinct unit fractions satisfies

$$
M_t(r)=\frac{t}{1-e^{-r}}+O_r\Bigl(\frac{t\log\log3t}{\log3t}\Bigr),
$$

and the order of the error term cannot be lowered. At $r=1$, with
$t_0(1)=3$ and $1/(1-e^{-1})=e/(e-1)$, this is
$f(k)=\frac{e}{e-1}k+O(k\log\log k/\log k)$ for the $f$ of
[[problems/unit_fractions/E0285/_index|Problem 285]], so the answer to the
question is yes for every $k\ge3$, with an explicit error term in place of
the $o(1)$.

journal (the arXiv listing's journal reference and the Crossref record of
