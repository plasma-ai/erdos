***

**Claim.** The theorem `erdos_289` of the linked file states, in Lean 4 over
Mathlib, that for all sufficiently large $k$ there are $k$ pairs
$(a_i,b_i)$ of natural numbers with $a_i<b_i$, pairwise separated in the
sense that $b_i+1<a_j$ or $b_j+1<a_i$ for $i\ne j$, whose interval
reciprocal sums $\sum_{n=a_i}^{b_i}1/n$ add to exactly $1$ in $\mathbb Q$.
This is the restricted statement of
[[problems/unit_fractions/E0289/_index|Problem 289]] as formal-conjectures
renders it: distinct, non-overlapping, non-adjacent intervals of at least two
integers. The file (about 9,500 lines, no `sorry`) builds the proof from
four anchor intervals $[2,3]$, $[14,15]$, $[84,85]$, $[492,493]$ whose
reciprocals sum to $1188/1189$, packets of four short intervals around
multiples of a parameter, and a thinning argument for a simultaneous
independent choice of intervals; by its docstring the proof follows a path
independent of the three solutions on the site's proof-claim tab.

system of Kung and coauthors (arXiv:2606.03303), which the docstring names as
