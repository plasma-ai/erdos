# problems/unit_fractions/E0300/claims

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0300/_index|..]]

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0300/claims/2003_03_01_croot|2003_03_01_croot]]: Croot's 2003 coloring paper is credited, by the site and by Liu and
Sawhney, with showing that A(N) is below cN for some constant c less than
one and all large N, refuting the expected A(N) = (1 + o(1))N.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0300/claims/2024_04_10_liu_sawhney|2024_04_10_liu_sawhney]]: Liu and Sawhney prove that every subset of one through N of size at least
(1 - 1/e + epsilon)N has a subset with reciprocal sum one, which with the
trivial top-segment example gives A(N) = (1 - 1/e + o(1))N.
