[[problems/unit_fractions/E0301/claims/2025_09_16_van_doorn|2025_09_16_van_doorn]]: An elementary argument credited to Wouter van Doorn in the site's commentary
that f(N) <= (25/28 + o(1))N for Problem 301, by counting disjoint dilates of

divisor-dilation argument on the divisors of 720 with a finite certificate.

would answer the particular question of Problem 301 in the negative.

over the divisors of 5040; AI-assisted, unrefereed, formalized only at M = 60.
