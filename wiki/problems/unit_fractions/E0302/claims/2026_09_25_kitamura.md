**Claim.** Let $f(N)$ be the extremal function of
[[problems/unit_fractions/E0302/_index|Problem 302]], defined, as in the
formal-conjectures statement file, through the predicate
`IsMaxNoTripleCard`. The linked repository declares the Lean theorems
`Erdos302ReflectiveUpper.limsup_le_0_8461739827964010` and
`Erdos302ReflectiveUpper.eventually_le_0_8461739827964010`, which state

$$
\limsup_{N\to\infty}\frac{f(N)}{N}\le\frac{8461739827964010}{10^{16}},
$$

and, equivalently, $f(N)\le(8461739827964010/10^{16}+\varepsilon)N$ for every
$\varepsilon>0$ and all large $N$. The README gives the underlying constant as
an exact rational $C=0.8461739827964009\ldots$ and the route: $[1,N]$ splits
into disjoint scaled blocks of smooth numbers over a finite set of primes; a
priority recurrence over a chain of $20$ prime sets, from $\{3\}$ up to the
primes to $71$, bounds from below the number of elements a triple-free set must
omit from each block, with corrections justified by $1{,}298$ branch-and-bound
certificates checked in the kernel by `decide +kernel`; summing over the
blocks gives $|A|\le CN+53{,}896{,}239$ for every triple-free
$A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$. The constant is below $25/28$, Schuh's $373/420$
and Khanukov's $140803024/163562355\approx0.86085$, and is the smallest
claimed.
