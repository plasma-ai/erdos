# problems/unit_fractions/E0307/claims

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0307/_index|..]]

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0307/claims/2012_03_25_kovic|2012_03_25_kovic]]: Kovič (J. Integer Seq. 2012) proves that a two-cycle of the arithmetic
derivative, which a solution of Problem 307 gives, is not two semiprimes, has
both members at least 10^4, and has nine primes in an odd smaller member.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0307/claims/2026_06_13_bonfioli|2026_06_13_bonfioli]]: An unrefereed, AI-assisted manuscript by Vico Bonfioli, first posted on 13
June 2026 with the 60-prime bound added on 2 July 2026, claiming that two
sets of primes whose reciprocal sums multiply to one have at least 60 primes
in all and products above 3.5 times 10^57.
