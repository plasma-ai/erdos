# problems/unit_fractions/E0318/claims

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0318/_index|..]]

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0318/claims/1975_01_01_erdos_straus|1975_01_01_erdos_straus]]: Erdős and Straus's 1975 solution to Problem 387 of Nieuw Archief: every
non-constant sign sequence on the positive integers has a finite zero-sum
of signed reciprocals, the case A = N of the first question.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0318/claims/1975_01_01_sattler|1975_01_01_sattler]]: Sattler's 1975 solution to Problem 387 of Nieuw Archief: every non-constant
sign sequence on the denominators 2i+1 has a finite zero-sum of signed
reciprocals, the case of the odd numbers in the first question.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0318/claims/1982_01_01_erdos|1982_01_01_erdos]]: Erdős's observation, published in Sattler's 1982 squarefree-numbers paper,
that an infinite set with exactly one even element has a sign pattern with
no zero-sum of signed reciprocals; the second question is answered no.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0318/claims/1982_01_01_sattler|1982_01_01_sattler]]: Sattler's 1982 theorem that every infinite arithmetic progression has
property P1, so that each non-constant sign assignment on it has a finite
subset whose signed reciprocals sum to zero; the first question, answered yes.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0318/claims/2026_02_01_larsen|2026_02_01_larsen]]: Larsen's Theorem 6, that every two-part partition of the perfect squares
greater than one has finite subsets of both parts with equal reciprocal
sums; the squares other than 1 have property P1, the third question yes.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0318/claims/2026_08_16_alexeev|2026_08_16_alexeev]]: A Lean development in Alexeev's repository, with Codex and GPT-5.6 Sol as its
formal authors, proves that every infinite arithmetic progression has
property P1, the first question; it names no published proof as its source.
