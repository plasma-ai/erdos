# Solymosi--Zahl: Improved Elekes--Szabó type estimates using proximity

***

József Solymosi and Joshua Zahl, arXiv:2211.13294 (2022).
[Full primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.13294).

Theorem 1.4 bounds the intersection of an irreducible non-special
algebraic surface with a real Cartesian product. For three sets of
size at most $Q$, the bound is $O_d(Q^{12/7})$, with constant depending
only on the polynomial degree. Axis-parallel cylinders and surfaces
with local additive-group structure are the exceptions.

Section 1.1 explicitly applies the theorem to distances from three noncollinear
planar anchors, giving at least $cN^{7/12}$ distinct distances to $N$ points.
The full theorem and application were checked. This improves the earlier
$N^{6/11}$ bound.
