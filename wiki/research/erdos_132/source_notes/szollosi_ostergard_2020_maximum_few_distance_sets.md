# Szöllősi–Östergård: Constructions of maximum few-distance sets

***

Ferenc Szöllősi and Patric R. J. Östergård, *Constructions of maximum
few-distance sets in Euclidean spaces*, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics
27(1) (2020), P1.23. DOI: 10.37236/8565.

[Primary full paper](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v27i1p23/pdf/).
Checked passage and scope.

Theorem 16 classifies maximum planar six-distance sets: their size is 13,
and the three types, up to similarity, are a regular 13-gon, a regular
12-gon with its center, and a regular hexagram with its center. The theorem
and its following discussion were checked in the full paper, pp. 15–16.
It is a computer-assisted classification using graph generation and
Gröbner bases; its exhaustive computation was not rerun here.
