These records are the independent focused reviews of the fourteen
reconstruction pages of the

reviewer in a fresh context who checked the page's statement and essential
deductions against the held source and took the page's input pages at their
Statement sections. The
[[research/erdos_354/evidence/verify/grade|distinct grade]], by a grader
separate from the author and from every reviewer, records pass for each
of the fourteen reports, adjudicates every finding against the sources
and the pages, and lists the accepted corrections C1--C10 with their exact
replacements. No record here assigns a tier or changes a status: the
pages remain author-recorded reconstructions.
