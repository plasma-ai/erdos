This folder holds the
[[research/erdos_49/evidence/verify/_index|review records]] of the six
reconstruction pages of [[research/erdos_49/_index|the folder]]: an
independent focused review of each page as it stood on 2026-09-28T05:03:27Z and
one distinct grade. No executable evidence is required or held: the pages
reconstruct a source proof whose steps are an $S$-unit count, a sieve bound, a
candidate-set construction and a counting argument, checked by reading; the one
explicit computation, the preimage sets of the reversed totient pair on the
Lemma 5.1 page, is recorded there as an author-recorded check and was recomputed
by the reviewer and by the grader. The source PDF is held by its library source
card
[[../library/primes/pollack_et_al_2013_sets_monotonicity_euler_totient_function/_index|Pollack, Pomerance and Treviño (2013)]].
Rendered page images are disposable reading aids, not proof inputs. This
evidence confers no tier and changes no status.
