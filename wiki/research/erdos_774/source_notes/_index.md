These notes summarize the sources used in the research. Follow the links to
canonical library records for retained reading copies and bibliographic details.

## Core problem sources

- [pisier](pisier.md): equivalence between Sidonicity and proportional
  extraction of quasi-independent subsets.
- [harrison](harrison.md): finite-block reduction and separated assembly.
- [grow](grow.md): the finite obstruction at extraction ratio one half.
- [nesetril](nesetril.md): fixed-order counterexamples and carry-free integer
  encoding.
- [hare](hare.md), [lewko](lewko.md), [alon](alon.md): nearby positive results
  and boundary cases.

## Roots-of-unity and matroid sources

- [ramsey-graham](ramsey_graham.md): closest source; spike/shadow machinery and
  the explicit $T_{105},T_{165},T_{195}$ test cases.
- [ramsey-graham-extension](ramsey_graham_extension.md): coordinatewise
  symmetries and prime-extension results.
- [martin-reiner](martin_reiner.md), [duval](duval.md): cyclotomic/simplicial
  matroid dictionary and the coefficient obstruction to treating dissociation as
  ordinary matroid independence.
- [conway-jones](conway_jones.md), [lam-leung](lam_leung.md),
  [christie](christie.md), [steinberger](steinberger.md),
  [coppersmith-steinberger](coppersmith_steinberger.md),
  [tan-zhang](tan_zhang.md): short and positive vanishing sums, minimal
  circuits, cyclotomic arrays, and certificate methods.
- [dadderio-moci](dadderio_moci.md), [onn](onn.md), [anderson](anderson.md),
  [edmonds](edmonds.md): arithmetic/signed dependence, primitive integer kernel
  moves, and the exact matroid partition analogue.
- [lovitz-petrov](lovitz_petrov.md): splitting bounds for minimally dependent
  product tensors.
- [poonen-rubinstein](poonen_rubinstein.md): Mann localization and recursive
  structure for short minimal vanishing sums.
- [bzdega](bzdega.md): coefficient bounds and jumps in ternary cyclotomic
  polynomials.
- [hindman-strauss](hindman_strauss.md): central sets, idempotents, and
  multiplicative recurrence.
- [moser-tardos](moser_tardos.md): the constructive asymmetric local lemma.
- [tao-notes](tao_notes.md): elementary prime-sum and Mertens estimates.
- [Révész](revesz_open_problems.md): the 2007 historical formulation of the
  roots-of-unity Sidon and finite quasi-independent-cover questions.

## Ramsey and canonization tools

- [graham-rothschild](graham_rothschild.md), [promel-voigt](promel_voigt.md),
  [voigt](voigt.md), [karagiannis](karagiannis.md), and
  [graham-leeb-rothschild](graham_leeb_rothschild.md) provide possible
  amplification and normalization theorems.  None controls the proportional
  extraction constant on its own; any application must prove that side
  separately.

## Sources checked but not held

- Canfield et al., on the asymptotic enumeration of correlation-immune
  Boolean functions (arXiv:0909.3321), abstract, Theorem 1.2 and
  Corollary 1.1: the stated real-Walsh correlation-immunity enumeration
  range is only of order $d/\log d$, not the order near $d/2$. Not held in
  the library.
