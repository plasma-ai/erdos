# Anderson: vectors of matroids over tracts

***

[Library
card](../../../../library/additive_combinatorics/anderson_2016_vectors_matroids_over_tracts/_index.md).

Laura Anderson, "Vectors of matroids over tracts," arXiv:1607.04868
(2016).

This paper develops vector and covector theories for matroids over tracts, a
framework encompassing ordinary fields, partial fields, and hyperfields such
as the sign hyperfield. It relates vectors and covectors to circuits and
cocircuits through orthogonality, and shows that the Composition and
Elimination axioms for vectors of oriented matroids, and some duality and
minor properties, fail for general tracts.' Replace '[Held copy and library
card](...)' with '[Library card](...)

The possible value for E0774 is conceptual: dissociation is sensitive to the
sign pattern and coefficient restriction of a relation, so an ordinary matroid
forgets too much.  A tract-valued model might retain the missing signed data and
clarify whether an Edmonds-type partition principle can survive.  The paper
does not directly model the hard coefficient cutoff $\{0,\pm1\}$, so any
claimed application must specify the tract and prove that its independent sets
coincide with dissociated subsets.
