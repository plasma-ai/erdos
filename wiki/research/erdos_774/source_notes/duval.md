# Duval–Klivans–Martin: cuts and flows of cell complexes

***

[Library card](../../../../library/additive_combinatorics/duval_et_al_2012_cuts_flows_cell_complexes/_index.md),
especially Theorem 4.11 and Remark 4.15.

Art M. Duval, Caroline J. Klivans, and Jeremy L. Martin, "Cuts and flows
of cell complexes," arXiv:1206.6157 (2012); J. Algebraic Combin. 41 (2015),
969--999.

This paper extends graphic cut/flow duality and critical groups to finite cell
complexes.  Cellular circuits can carry integral coefficients larger than one;
the discussion around torsion and the explicit coefficient examples are the
main reason to keep this source nearby.
