# On partitioning Sidon sets with quasi-independent sets

[Library card and held copy](../../../../library/analysis/harrison_ramsey_1996_partitioning_sidon_sets_quasi_independent_sets/_index.md).

***

K. J. Harrison and L. Thomas Ramsey, "On partitioning Sidon sets with
quasi-independent sets," *Colloquium Mathematicum* **69** (1996),
117--131.

## What the research consumes

The card carries the digest: the $N$-independence terminology, the random
positive examples of Theorem 1, and the finite-determination results
(Lemma 11 and Theorems 7 and 8). The paper's quasi-independence is
dissociation in [Problem 774](../../../problems/integer_sequences/E0774/_index.md).

The research uses the finite-determination principle. With $\mu(E,m)$ the
least number of $m$-independent classes covering $E$, Lemma 11 gives
$\mu(E,m)=\sup\{\mu(F,m):F\subset E\text{ finite}\}$, and Theorems 7 and 8
assemble finite examples of unbounded cover number at rapidly increasing
scales while preserving a common Sidon bound. The random blocks of
Theorem 1 have a uniformly bounded cover by design and are evidence for the
positive side, not counterexamples. Reading depth is claims checked.
