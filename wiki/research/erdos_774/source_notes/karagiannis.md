# Karagiannis: a combinatorial infinite Hales–Jewett proof

***

[Held copy and library card](../../../../library/ramsey_theory/karagiannis_2013_combinatorial_proof_infinite_version_hales_jewett_theorem/_index.md).

Nikolaos Karagiannis, "A combinatorial proof of an infinite version of the
Hales--Jewett theorem," *Journal of Combinatorics* 4 (2013), no. 2,
273--291.

Karagiannis gives a combinatorial proof, resting on the classical
Hales–Jewett theorem, of the infinite extension due to Carlson and,
independently, Furstenberg and Katznelson (Theorem 2, p. 274), and of its
version for an increasing sequence of finite alphabets (Theorem 3).
