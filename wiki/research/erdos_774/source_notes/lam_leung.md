# Lam–Leung: vanishing sums of roots of unity

***

[Library card](../../../../library/group_theory/lam_leung_2000_vanishing_sums_roots_unity/_index.md).

T. Y. Lam and K. H. Leung, "On vanishing sums of roots of unity," *Journal
of Algebra* 224 (2000), no. 1, 91--109.

Lam and Leung determine the possible weights of vanishing sums of $n$-th
roots of unity with nonnegative integer coefficients: the weight lies in the
additive semigroup generated by the prime divisors of $n$.  They also show
that when $n$ has at most two distinct prime divisors every minimal vanishing
sum is, up to rotation, a prime cycle.
