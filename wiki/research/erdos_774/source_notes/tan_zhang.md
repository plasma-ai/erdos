# Tan–Zhang: nonnegative cyclotomic multiples

***

[Held copy and library card](../../../../library/number_theory/tan_zhang_2026_sharp_diameter_bounds_nonnegative_cyclotomic_multiples/_index.md).

Hu Tan and Ying Zhang, "Sharp diameter bounds for nonnegative cyclotomic
multiples," arXiv:2609.07646 (2026).

Tan and Zhang prove sharp support-diameter bounds for polynomials with
nonnegative real coefficients divisible by a cyclotomic polynomial (Theorem
1.1: $D(F)\ge(p-1)N/p$, $p$ the least prime divisor of $N$, with equality
exactly for $cX^b(1+X^{N/p}+\cdots+X^{(p-1)N/p})$).  Evaluated at a primitive
$N$th root of unity, such a polynomial gives a vanishing sum of $N$th roots of
unity with nonnegative weights, its exponent support recording where the mass
lies.  The extremal constructions and sharp bounds provide another way to
constrain the geometry of positive relations.
