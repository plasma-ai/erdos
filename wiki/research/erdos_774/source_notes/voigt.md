# Voigt: canonizing partition theorems

***

[Library card](../../../../library/ramsey_theory/voigt_1985_canonizing_partition_theorems_diversification_products_iterated_versions/_index.md).

Bernd Voigt, "Canonizing partition theorems: diversification, products,
and iterated versions," *Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A* 40
(1985), no. 2, 349--376.

Voigt sets up an abstract framework for canonizing partition theorems, built
on attribute functions and diversification, and uses it to derive canonizing
product theorems (generalizing results of Rado for Ramsey's theorem) and
iterated versions of the Erdős–Rado canonization theorem and of its
$q$-analogue for finite vector spaces (abstract, p. 349).  Instead of finding
only a monochromatic substructure, one passes to a structured subsystem on
which an arbitrary equivalence relation has a controlled canonical form.
