# Earlier approaches to the seven-cycle color bound

[[research/erdos_809/_index|..]]

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_adaptive_frames|c7_adaptive_frames]]: Obstructions, signed and positive repairs, and explicit
lower bounds for the adaptive frame operator.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_core_port_constructions|c7_core_port_constructions]]: An inequality excluding a broad family of clique cores connected
through bipartite ports as threshold counterexamples.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_correlated_ports|c7_correlated_ports]]: A squared-degree color bound handles arbitrary correlated attachments
and arbitrary cores completely joined to independent hubs.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_cut_palette_certificate|c7_cut_palette_certificate]]: An enlarged cut dual proves the savings bound for a triangular maximum
star, an independent nonneighborhood, or a saturated complementary cut.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_cycle_space_obstruction|c7_cycle_space_obstruction]]: Reasons that a direct binary cycle-space or color-map rank
argument does not establish the C7 color bound.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_dense_curve_obstruction|c7_dense_curve_obstruction]]: A proved three-branch construction disproves the C7 extension of the full
Bucić–Chen–Ma curve, but not the threshold conjecture.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_dominating_walk_cliques|c7_dominating_walk_cliques]]: A joint two- and three-walk clique dominating outside degrees proves
the savings bound; a heavy-degree corollary and selection obstructions.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_half_edge_reduction|c7_half_edge_reduction]]: An equivalent half-edge target and a physical-rectangle route, with
a proved Ore-heavy clique and the remaining localization gap.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_large_palettes|c7_large_palettes]]: Independent active palettes can have arbitrary fixed size above the
Turan density with minimum degree arbitrarily close to one half.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_linked_tripartite_core|c7_linked_tripartite_core]]: A degree-weighted three-walk clique proves the squared-density color
bound for arbitrary tripartite triangle networks attached to a
properly three-colored triangle-free core.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_local_rainbow_sets|c7_local_rainbow_sets]]: Rainbow sets arising from robust three-edge paths or from a
fixed triangle; neither supplies a full threshold reduction.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_ore_mass_obstruction|c7_ore_mass_obstruction]]: A super-Turan family has Ore-heavy endpoints on only four ninths
of its vertices, ruling out a canonical mass-localization shortcut.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_overlapping_ports|c7_overlapping_ports]]: A sharp resource inequality excluding clique cores with overlapping
independent neighborhoods in a triangle-free port graph.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_palette_stationarity|c7_palette_stationarity]]: LP duality and weight stationarity for density maximization
expose why ordinary vertex symmetrization has not closed the palette gap.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_private_core_hubs|c7_private_core_hubs]]: A proved density bound for private clique cores attached to the
vertices of an arbitrary triangle-free hub graph.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_rainbow_representatives|c7_rainbow_representatives]]: A super-Turan construction rules out even the square-root
edge-count spectral bound for rainbow representative subgraphs.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_random_blowup_lp|c7_random_blowup_lp]]: Fractional-coloring formulas for complete and random finite
blow-ups, and an induced-matching construction principle.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_reflection_norms|c7_reflection_norms]]: Checked limitations of direct reflection-folding, graph-norm, and
polynomial spectral-kernel approaches to the C7 color bound.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_residual_geometry|c7_residual_geometry]]: Optimal-dual neighborhood inequalities and conditional density bounds
narrow the savings problem, but their uncolored relaxation is false.
A full-capacity template also obstructs unconditional pair balancing.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_residual_spectral|c7_residual_spectral]]: An exact spectral minimax formula for color savings, concavity in
squared vertex weights, and a universal degree-deficit spectral bound.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_second_degree|c7_second_degree]]: A second-degree bound and the unresolved three-path clique
enlargement approach.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_semidefinite_approach|c7_semidefinite_approach]]: An algebraic approach through positive semidefinite edge kernels;
certificates, trace obstructions, and the unresolved global step.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_singleton_palettes|c7_singleton_palettes]]: A compatible-pair degree inequality forces singleton palettes above
the Turan threshold and gives a density-budget identity there.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_tensor_exclusions|c7_tensor_exclusions]]: Analytic obstructions exclude private-core tensors with product weights
and every power of a looped three-vertex path with arbitrary weights.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_tensor_palettes|c7_tensor_palettes]]: Categorical products can reuse both edge orientations for nontriangle
palettes, giving a proved amplification criterion but no counterexample.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_triangle_average|c7_triangle_average]]: A universal average bound for density at least five sixteenths,
and a reduction leaving at most five nontriangular types.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_triangle_component|c7_triangle_component]]: A sharp weighted partition bound localizes the triangle-vertex mass
inequality to one component of the triangular-edge graph.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_triangle_vertices|c7_triangle_vertices]]: A weighted symmetrization lemma for super-Turan graphs and its limited
relevance to the C7 color problem.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_triangular_support|c7_triangular_support]]: A two-star rectangle proves the squared-density bound when every
supported edge is triangular, including arbitrary thinned demands.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_two_nontriangular_types|c7_two_nontriangular_types]]: An average inequality for two adjacent nontriangular types, and
resulting rectangle bounds when all triangular vertices form a
three-walk clique.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_two_star_rectangles|c7_two_star_rectangles]]: Anchor optimization, a two-star family of three-walk cliques,
and an unconditional high-density color bound; threshold localization remains open.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_universal_components|c7_universal_components]]: The half-edge inequality holds when each triangular-edge component
has universal two- and three-walk connectivity, via a deficit inequality.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_universal_subsets|c7_universal_subsets]]: Palette and replacement inequalities for a short-walk universal subset
identify the attachment-triangle obstruction beyond isolated components.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/c7_walk_clique_pruning|c7_walk_clique_pruning]]: A maximum-degree separation inequality and a twin-box induction prove
the sharp mass bound for an odd-three-walk clique; color localization
and simultaneous two-walk connectivity remain unresolved.

[[research/erdos_809/archive/evidence/_index|evidence/]]: An exact symbolic certificate for the two-type identities and bounded exploratory palette searches supporting archived research notes.
