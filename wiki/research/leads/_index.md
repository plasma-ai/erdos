***

Each lead records a mathematical approach or unresolved task and the evidence
needed to assess it. A lead can connect several problems, and a problem can have
several leads. The records are intended to grow as sources are compiled and
investigations proceed.

Read each lead's target, evidence, obstacles, next investigation, and review
scope together. Its research state describes the work; it does not establish
the truth of a proposed conclusion or replace a problem's mathematical status.
Closed or unsuccessful routes remain useful when their limitations are recorded.
