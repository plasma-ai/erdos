My own rederivation of the weakest steps, in my words.
- W1, the two edge conventions. The library's number minimizes over graphs
  with at least e edges; the claim's over graphs with exactly e edges. A
  rainbow coloring of a graph G with at least e edges restricts to any
  e-edge subgraph K ≤ G: a copy of any pattern graph in K composes with the
  inclusion homomorphism `Hom.ofLE` into a copy in G, whose image edges are
  the same edges, so the pulled-back coloring is injective on them. Hence
  every palette size admissible for "at least e" is admissible for "exactly
  e"; the converse is immediate; the two admissible sets coincide and so do
  their ℕ infima, including the empty-set value 0 at infeasible orders. The
  probe proves this for every pattern graph (`admissible_iff_atLeast`,
  `antiRamsey_eq_maximalAntiRamsey`) and re-proves the statement from
  `Erdos809.statement_proved` through it (`claim_reproved`); L17.lean proves
  the cycle case by the indexed route. Both are kernel-checked in my rerun.
- W2, the copy convention. Mathlib's `Copy` is a homomorphism (adjacency
  preserved) with an injectivity field and no reflection field; a copy of
  `cycleGraph m` therefore sends the m vertices to m distinct vertices with
  consecutive images adjacent, and permits chords. That is the English's
  "cycle on 2k+1 distinct vertices of the graph"; an induced reading would
  quantify over fewer copies and the English does not take it. The probe's
  `copy_gives_indexed_cycle` and `fourCycleInK4` pin both halves.
- W3, the asymptotic and the value 0. `u ~[atTop] v` is `(u - v) =o[atTop]
  v`; with v = n²/8 and the nonzero constant 1/8 this is `u - n²/8 = o(n²)`,
  and by `isEquivalent_iff_tendsto_one` (v eventually nonzero) it is
  `u/(n²/8) → 1`, which the identity `u/(n²/8) = (u/n²)·8` (valid at every
  n under the division-by-zero convention) turns into `u/n² → 1/8`; the ε–N
  form is `Metric.tendsto_atTop`. The infeasible orders are exactly n = 0,
  1, 2 (`n*n/4+1 ≤ n.choose 2 ↔ 3 ≤ n`), where `sInf ∅ = 0`; a filter
  statement at `atTop` ignores them. The probe proves all of this.

An attack of my own, beyond the review's. The English's "r-coloring" could
be read as a coloring that uses exactly r colors rather than at most r. The
Lean uses at most r (a function into `Fin r`). The least r is the same under
both readings: a rainbow coloring into `Fin r` that uses s ≤ r colors is, up
to renaming, a rainbow coloring using exactly s colors, so the "exactly"
minimum is at most the "at most" minimum; and an "exactly r" rainbow
coloring is an "at most r" one, giving the reverse inequality. The reading
does not change the number. A second reading, that "graph with n vertices"
excludes isolated vertices, is not the catalog's and would not be the
English's; the Lean's vertex set is `Fin n` exactly, as the English says.
Neither attack reaches the statement.
